#### § 549. Bases with initial vowels:—

(a) If a root begins with a vowel and ends in a single consonant, the consonant is reduplicated and @[इ] added to it in the reduplicative syllable: @[अट्] = @[आट्] = @[आटट्] = @[आरिटत्]–@[त]; @[ऊह्] = @[आरिहत्]–@[त]; @[ऊह्] = @[ओजिहत्]–@[त]; &c.

(b) If it ends in a conjunct consonant with @[न्], @[म्] or @[ह्] for its first member, the second member of the conjunct is redupli-

cated: @[उन्द्] = @[उन्दद्]--@[उन्दिद्] and finally @[औन्दिदत्]–@[त] 3 sing, similarly @[आन्दिदत्]–@[त] from @[अन्द्] (considered as @[अद्], otherwise @[आदिदत्]), @[आम्हिदत्]–@[त] from @[अम्ह्]; @[आञ्जिजत्]–@[त] from @[अञ्ज्], &c.

(c) The roots @[अन्], @[अङ्क्], @[अश्], @[अन्ध्], @[अंस्], @[अर्थ्] Atm. and some others, substitute @[अ] for @[इ] in the reduplicative syllable; 3 sing. @[आननत्], @[आनङ्कत्], @[आशश्त्], @[आनन्धत्], @[आंसत्], @[आरथत्], &c.
