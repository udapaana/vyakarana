#### § 362. That is an Avyaya whose form remaining the same in

all the genders, numbers and cases, undergoes no change.

The Avyayas may be divided into simple and compound ones. The latter are treated of in the chapter on compounds (@[Avyayī]-@[bhāvas] and a few @[Bahuvrīhis] and @[Tatpuruṣas]).
