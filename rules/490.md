§ 490. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed of all roots
beginning with any vowel except अ or आ which is uaturally or
prosodially long, and of roots of more than one syllable ( roote
of the 10th class and other derivative roots included ). Excep-
tions:—3yuy and ऋरच्छ्‌ which take the Redaplicative Perfeots.
