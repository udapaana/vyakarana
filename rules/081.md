#### § 81. Nouns derived from roots with the affix @[तृ] (@[ण्वुल्] and @[तृच्], i.e. 133, and 2, 135), such as @[नेतृ] a leader, @[कर्तृ] a maker, &c. and the words @[स्वसृ] a sister, @[दुहितृ] a daughter, @[मातृ] a mother, @[दातृ], @[धातृ], and @[प्रशास्तृ]; also @[उद्गातृ] change their @[ऋ] to @[आर्] in the Nom. sing. and to @[आर्] in the first five inflections. The @[अ] is lengthened in the Acc. and Gen. plu. and changed to @[उ] before the Ab. and Gen. sing; the Voc. sing. ends in @[अः].
