#### § 519. @[caṣṭa], @[cakṛ] and @[cakṣa] are optionally substituted for @[cakath] in the perfect, and necessarily in the other Non-conjugational tenses. @[caṣṭa] and @[cakṣa] take both the Padas.

Par.
@:
1 ācaṣṭa ācaṣṭiva ācaṣṭima
ācakra ācakriva ācakrima
2 ācaṣṭa, ācaṣṭitha ācaṣṭathuḥ ācaṣṭa
ācakra, ācakritha ācakrathuḥ ācakra
3 ācaṣṭa ācaṣṭatuḥ ācaṣṭuḥ
ācakra ācakratuḥ ācakruḥ
:@

Atm.
@:
1 ācacakṣe ācacakṣvāhe ācacakṣmahe
ācaṣṭe ācaṣṭivāhe ācaṣṭimahi
ācakre ācakrivāhe ācakrimahi
2 ācacakṣiṣe ācacakṣāthe ācacakṣidhve
ācaṣṭiṣe ācaṣṭāthe ācaṣṭidhve
ācakriṣe ācakriyāthe ācakridhve
3 ācacakṣe ācacakṣāte ācacakṣire
ācaṣṭe ācaṣṭāte ācaṣṭire
ācakre ācakrāte ācakrire
:@
