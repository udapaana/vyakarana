#### § 42. @[षः] belonging to a substitute (@[आदेश]) or a termination (@[प्रत्यय]) and not at the end of a grammatical form is changed to @[षः] when preceded by any vowel except @[अ] or @[आ], a semivowel, a letter of the guttural class or @[ह]; e.g. @[रामे] + @[सु] = @[रामेषु]; but @[शस्व] as @[स] is preceded by @[श], @[सुभ्यः] @[गृहेभ्यः] etc., as @[स] belongs to the word @[सुप्]. This change takes place even if an @[अनुस्वार] substituted for @[म], a @[विसर्ग] or @[ष] or @[स] intervene; e.g. @[धनुस्] + @[सि] = @[धनुःषि] nom. pl. of @[धनुस्] neu. a bow. @[पिपदिष्] + @[सु] = @[पिपदिष्षु].
