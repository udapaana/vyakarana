#### § 396. The following seven roots form their bases like the Desiderative in the senses indicated. These are:—@[चिकित्स्‌] to administer medicine, to treat as a patient @[चिकित्सति-ते]; @[निन्दित्स्‌] to censure, @[निन्दित्सति]; @[तितिक्ष्‌] to bear, to forgive, @[तितिक्षति]; @[बीभत्स्‌] to abhor, to act loathsomely, @[बीभत्सते]; @[दीर्घ्‌] to make straight, @[दीदीर्घति-ते]; @[मीमांस्‌] to reason, to think, @[मीमांसते]; @[चिचिश्‌] to sharpen, @[चिचिशति-ते]। But @[चित्‌] to desire @[चेतति]; to dwell, @[चेतयति]; @[दिन्‌] to cut @[दानयति-ते], &c.
