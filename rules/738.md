#### § 738. Roots ending in @[उ], short or long, take the affix @[ण्यत्] in the sense of 'what ought or must necessarily be done;' @[द्रु]—@[द्राव्यम्] 'what must necessarily be cut off;' @[पू]—@[पाव्यम्] 'what must necessarily be purified; @[धू] with @[आ]—@[आधाव्यम्], 'to mix,' @[प्रेव्यम्], &c.

(a) The roots @[वप्], @[रुप्], @[लप्], @[नप्] and @[दह्] also do the same; @[वाप्यम्] 'what must necessarily be sown,' @[राप्यम्], 'what must be spoken of distinctly;' @[लाप्यम्], @[नाप्यम्], @[दाह्यम्].
