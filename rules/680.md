#### § 680-683 } CONJUGATION OF VERBS. 387

(5) ई is inserted after the अ of the reduplicative syllable (which then remains unchanged) in the case of the roots @[vañc], @[vraśc], @[dhvaṃs], @[bhī], @[sā], @[pat], @[pad] and @[skand]; @[vañc]—@[vavañjya-vavañcyate]; @[vraśc]—@[vavraścya-vavarścyate]; @[dhvaṃs]—@[danīdhvaṃsyate], @[bhī]—@[banībhīṣyate], @[sā]—@[sanīsasyate], @[pat]—@[panīpatyate], @[pad]—@[panīpadyate], @[skand]—@[kanīskandyate].
