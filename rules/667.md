#### § 667. The participle of the present Parasam. is formed by the addition of the affix @[अत्] to that form of a root, primitive or derivative, which it assumes before the third person plural termination of the Present Tense. If the base ends in @[अ], the @[अ] is dropped; e. g.

@[भू] (1 cl.)—@[भव् + अन्ति] Pres. 3rd pl. @[भव् + अत् = भवत्] Pres. P. being.
@[स्था] (1 cl.)—@[तिष्ठ + अन्ति],, @[तिष्ठ + अत् = तिष्ठत्],, standing.
@[द्विष्] (2 cl.)—@[द्विष् + अन्ति],, @[द्विष् + अत् = द्विषत्],, hating.

similarly from

@[अद्] (2 cl.) @[अदत्] eating. @[रुध्] (7 cl.) @[रुन्धत्] preventing.
@[इ] (2 cl.) @[यत्] going. @[कृ] (8 cl.) @[कुर्वत्] doing.
@[यज्] (1 cl.) @[यजत्] sacrificing. @[तन्] (8 cl.) @[तन्वत्] stretching, &c.
@[दीव्] (4 cl.) @[दीव्यत्] playing. @[क्री] (9 cl.) @[क्रीणत्] buying.
@[षु] (5 cl.) @[सुन्वत्] extracting. @[मुष्] (9 cl.) @[मुष्णत्] stealing, &c.
@[तुद्] (6 cl.) @[तुदत्] giving pain. @[चुर्] (10 cl.) @[चोरयत्] stealing.
@[बोधय] cau. of @[बुध्], @[बोधयत्] causing to know. ‖ @[जुबोधिष] desid. of @[बुध्], @[जुबोधिषत्] desiring to know.
@[दित्स] desid. of @[दा], @[दित्सत्] desiring to give.
@[चेकृष्] freq. of @[कृष्], @[चेकृषत्] throwing again and again,
&c. &c. &c. &c. &c.

(a) The term. @[अत्] is optionally changed to @[वत्] when added to the root @[विद्]; @[विदत्] or @[विदन्त्] 'knowing.'

(b) The affix @[अत्] when added to @[द्विष्] and @[षु] 'to extract Soma juice in a sacrifice' has the sense of an agent; as @[द्विषत्] m. 'an enemy,' @[यज्ञे सोमं सुन्वन्तः] 'they are the extractors of Soma juice in a sacrifice.'

H. S. G. 27

(c) When affixed to @[अर्ह्] it has the sense of 'fitness, respect'; @[अर्हत्] 'one deserving respect, respectable.'

(d) @[इ] 2 P. and @[धृ] can take the affix @[अत्] when the idea of difficulty is not present, @[अधीयत्] 'studying without experiencing any difficulty;' @[धारयत्] 'holding or bearing with ease;' but @[कष्टम् अधीते], @[कष्टं धारयति].
