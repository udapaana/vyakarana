#### § 124. These are the perfect participles derived from roots with the affix @[वस्] to which the intermediate @[न्] is prefixed in some cases. They have a @[म्] prefixed to their final @[स्] in the first five inflections, the penultimate @[अ] being lengthened before it. In the Nom. sing. m., the @[म्] is dropped and the Voc. sing. ends in @[वन्]. @[उ] is substituted for @[व], before which the preceding short @[अ], if any, is dropped, before the vowel terminations beginning with those of the Acc. plu. and before the @[उ] of the Nom. Voc. and Acc. dual of the neuter nouns. The final @[द्] of a root is changed to @[त्] before @[व]. @[न्] reappears before this @[उ]; final @[अ] is changed to @[इ] before the consonantal terminations and in the Nom. Voc. and Acc. sing. of the neuter.

@[विद्वस्] m. a learned man.
