#### § 44. @[छ] is necessarily inserted between @[छ] and the preceding vowel, short or long; and optionally when a long vowel at the end of a word, except that of the particle @[मा] and the preposition @[आ], precedes it; e.g. @[शिव] + @[छाया] = @[शिवच्छाया] Śiva's shadow, so @[त्वच्छाया]; @[वेत्यच्छिद्यते] what is cut again and again; @[लक्ष्मी] + @[छाया] = @[लक्ष्मीच्छाया] or @[लक्ष्मीछाया], @[मा] + @[छिदत्] = @[माच्छिदत्] may he not cut; @[आ] + @[छादयति] = @[आच्छादयति] he covers.
