@[§] 96. When a mono-syllabic root (or root-noun) begins with @[प्], @[फ्] or @[ब्] and ends with @[ज्], @[क्], @[ट्], @[त्] or @[थ्], the @[प्], @[फ्] or @[ब्] is respectively changed to @[भ्], @[भ्] or @[भ्] before @[झ्] or @[भ्] or when followed by nothing or when the final soft aspirate is changed to an unaspirate letter.†
