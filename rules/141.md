#### § 141. Indefinite pronouns are formed by the addition of @[चित्‌] or @[चन] or @[अपि] and sometimes of @[स्वित्‌] to the various cases of the pronoun @[किम्‌] in all the genders; e.g. @[कश्चित्‌], @[कश्चन], a certain one, @[कोऽपि], @[कांचित्‌], @[कांचन], @[कयाऽपि]; @[कास्वित्‌], &c.
