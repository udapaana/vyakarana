#### § 7. म has all its syllables long; न has all its syllables short, त has its first syllable long, य has its first syllable short; ज has its middle syllable long; र has its middle syllable short, स has its last syllable long and ल has its last syllable short.

The symbol ˘ stands for a short or light syllable; the symbol – denotes a long vowel or a heavy syllable. Symbolically represented these @[gaṇas] will stand as under:—

म – – – न ˘ ˘ ˘
त – ˘ ˘ य ˘ – –
भ – – ˘ ज ˘ – ˘
र – ˘ – स ˘ ˘ –
ल ˘ – –

Similarly the letter ग is used to denote a short syllable and ल a long one at the close of a line.
