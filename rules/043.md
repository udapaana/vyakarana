#### § 43. The @[सः] of @[पुंस्] is changed to an @[अनुस्वार] and @[विसर्ग] when followed by a form of the root @[कृ], the @[अनुस्वार] again is optionally changed to an @[अनुनासिक]; e.g. @[पुंस्] + @[कर्ता] = @[पुंस्कर्ता] and @[पुंस्कर्ता]; @[स] is affixed to the ending of the words @[युवन्], @[मघवन्] and @[कान्]; so @[पुंस्कर्ता] and @[पुंस्स्कर्ता].

N. 2. The word @[पुंस्कर्ता] is capable of having 108 different forms as several @[सूत्र]s are brought to bear upon it; but they are not given here as being tedious and of no material value.

Sections a, b, c, and d may be omitted by beginners.

(a) The @[स्] of @[पुंस्] is optionally nasalised and has @[स] added to it as above, when it is followed by a hard consonant except a sibilant, preceding a vowel, a semivowel, a nasal or @[ह]; @[पुंस्] + @[कोकिलः] = @[पुंम्] + @[स्] + @[कोकिलः] = @[पुंस्कोकिलः], @[पुंस्कोकिलः] a male cuckoo; so @[पुंस्पुत्रः] and @[पुंस्पुत्रः] the son, a grown up man; but @[पुंसे] milk for a man; @[पुंस्दासः] a man-servant. Exception @[पुंस्गतम्] the account of a man.
