#### § 539. This Variety is @[parasmaipada] with a few exceptions such as @[ऋ] with @[प्र], @[ख्या], @[पत्]; and @[क्षिप्] 'to throw' with a preposition. The roots @[पत्], @[वच्], and @[श] take this form in the Par. and optionally in the Atm., in which they also take the Fourth Form.
