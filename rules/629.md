#### § 629. The form in @[य] derived as above is reduplicated according to the general rules of reduplication:

(a) If a root begins with a vowel, the following syllable is reduplicated.

(b) The vowels @[इ] and @[उ] of the reduplicative syllable take

their Guṇa substitute and the @[अ] of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened; @[गम्]—@[गम्य], @[गगम्य], +@[ते]=@[गागम्यते]; @[भू]—@[बोभूयते]; @[पच्]—@[पापच्यते] &c.
