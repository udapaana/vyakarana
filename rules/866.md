or devoted to' are construed with the Locative or the Genitive: @[hariparāyaṇaḥ] @[vā] @[hariparāyaṇe] @[hariparāyaṇasya] @[vā] appointed to worship Hari; @[śrutalokye] @[vaṇijyāham] @[āyuktaḥ] @[dūtakavṛttim] (Bhaṭṭi, VIII. 115.)
