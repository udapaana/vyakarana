#### § 854. ‡ The roots @[व्यवहृ] (s. e. @[हृ] with @[वि] and @[अव]), @[पण्] and @[दिव्] when they all mean to transact business, or to stake in gambling, govern the Genitive of their object; @[शतस्य व्यवहरति] invests a hundred rupees in business; @[प्राणानामपणत] he staked his life; @[अदेवीद्भ्रातॄणामानन्दानां] lost his brothers and pleasures in gambling, &c.; but when @[दिव्] is preceded by a preposition, the Accusative may also be used; @[शतस्य वा शतं प्रतिदीव्यति] (Sid. Kau.)
