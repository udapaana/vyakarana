#### § 803. The root @[vas] preceded by the prepositions @[upa], @[anu], @[adhi] and @[ā] governs the Accusative case; @[upa-anu-adhi-ā-vasati] @[sas] @[hariḥ] Hari dwells in @[vaikuṇṭha]; @[durgam] @[anvavasad] @[vanam] he dwelt in a dreary forest; but @[vas] with @[upa] meaning 'to abstain from food' is used with the Loc., @[upavasati] @[vane] @[rāmaḥ] Rāma observes a fast in the forest.
