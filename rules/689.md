#### § 689. Roots ending in @[आ] (@[ए], @[ऐ] and @[ओ] changeable to @[आ]) and beginning with a conjunct consonant containing a semi-vowel also substitute @[न] for @[त]; @[द्रा] 'to run, to sleep,' @[द्राण]; @[ग्लै] 'to fade,' @[ग्लान]; @[स्त्यै]—@[स्त्यान] 'collected to a mass,' &c.

Exceptions:—@[ख्या] 'to name,' &c, @[ध्यै] 'to contemplate,' @[प्यै] and @[म्ला]; @[ख्यात], @[ध्यात], @[प्यात] and @[म्लात].
