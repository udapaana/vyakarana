#### § 370. Several nouns, substantive and adjective, may be prefixed to the roots @[kṛ], @[bhū], and @[asad] to form what are called in Sanskrit 'chvi-derivatives' (vide chap. IX. Sec. III.) @[kṛṣṇakaraṇam] = @[kṛṣṇīkaraṇam], @[ghanībhū] = @[ghanapū] = @[ghanībhāvaḥ]. Such nouns are also termed 'Gati.'
