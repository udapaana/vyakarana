#### § 416. Special rules of @[sandhi] of the finals of roots and the initial letters of terminations:—

(1) The ending @[अ] of a root takes its @[Vṛddhi] substitute when followed by a consonantal strong termination; as @[अद्‌] + @[अन्ति] = @[अदन्ति].

(2) The final @[इ] or @[उ], short or long, of a root is changed to @[इय्‌] or @[उव्‌] before a vowel weak termination.

(3) The ending @[ह्‌] of roots is changed to @[ध्‌] when followed by any consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, or by nothing; and that of roots beginning with @[द्‌] to @[त्‌] under the same circumstances.

(4) The initial @[ध्‌] or @[भ्‌] of a termination is changed to @[ब्‌] after a soft aspirate (4th letter of a class).

(5) @[स्‌] or @[ष्‌] followed by @[ध्‌] is changed to @[द्ध्‌].

(6) @[त्‌] and @[न्‌] when followed by a consonant are changed to the nasal of the class to which the following consonant belongs, and to an @[anusvāra] when followed by @[श्‌], @[ष्‌], @[स्‌] or @[ह्‌].

(7) The ending @[इ] of a root, short or long, not preceded by a conjunct consonant is changed to @[य्‌] before vowel weak terminations, when the base consists of more than one syllable.

(8) The ending @[द्‌] of a root is optionally changed to @[ट्‌] or @[visarga] in the Imperfect second pers. sing. and the ending @[ध्‌] to @[त्‌] or @[द्‌] before the termination @[ध्वम्‌] and optionally before @[व].

(9) When a conjunct consonant having @[त्‌] or @[द्‌] for its first member is at the end of a word or is followed by a consonant except a nasal or a semivowel, the @[त्‌] or @[द्‌] is dropped.

N. B.—The usual @[sandhi] rules and those given in the 2nd and 3rd Chapters should be observed.
