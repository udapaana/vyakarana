@[§] 884. The pronoun @[sa] has often the sense of 'well known,
renowned,' &c. @[tau Pārvatīparameśvarau] those (well-known) Pārvatī and Parameśvara; @[tānyeva vanasthalāni] those wellknown forest sites.

(a) When repeated, this pronoun has the sense of 'various,
several,' @[teṣu teṣu ramyatareṣu sthāneṣu] in those various highly delightful spots, @[chatairapi udyamaiḥ punaḥ] notwithstanding several efforts @[punaḥ pratyāhṛtaḥ] &c. (Bhag. VII. 20).
