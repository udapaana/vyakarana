#### § 968. @[tu] but, @[hi] for, because, and @[api] are also excluded from the first place in a sentence. @[ātmā vai putrasya sakhā bhāryā sā tu duhitā duḥkham] the son is one's own self, the wife one's friend, but the daughter a source of anxiety, @[apyājñayā svaśvasurātmanā na prāptoṣi saṃbhāvayituṃ vanānmām kālo hyeṣa saṃkramatuṃ gataḥ sāmprakārakṣamamāśramaṃ te]
