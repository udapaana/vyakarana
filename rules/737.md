#### § 737. (a) The roots @[याच्], @[याच्], @[रुच्], @[त्यज्], @[त्यज्], and @[वच्] do not change their @[ज्] or @[च्] to @[क्] before @[ण्यत्]; @[याच्यम्], @[याच्यम्], @[रोच्यम्], @[वाच्यम्] (@[ग्रन्थविशेषः]), @[अर्च्यम्], @[त्याज्यम्], @[पाच्यम्].

(b) @[वच्] does not change its @[च्] to @[क्] before @[ण्यत्] when the meaning is 'what ought to be spoken out, speech,' @[वाच्यम्]; but @[वाक्यः] 'a sentence.'

(c) @[वञ्च्] when it means 'to go' does not change its @[च्] to @[क्]; @[वञ्च्यम्]; when it means 'to bend' the @[च्] is changed to @[क्]; @[वङ्क्यं काष्ठम्].

(d) @[युज्] when it is preceded by @[प्र] and @[नि], takes the affix @[एयत्] in the sense of 'what is possible or capable of,' and does not change its @[ज्] to @[ग्]; @[प्रयोक्तुं शक्यः प्रयोज्यः], @[योक्तुं शक्यः नियोज्यः].

(e) @[भुज्] has @[भोज्यम्] meaning 'food,' and @[भोग्यम्] 'what is fit to be enjoyed.'
