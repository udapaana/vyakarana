#### § 351. (a) All the numerals from 20-99, monosyllabic words in @इ@ and such as are formed by the affix @ति@, @इति@; @श्रीः@; @तनुता@ &c.

(b) The synonyms of @अमि@, @अग्नि@, @कवि@ and @पिता@ and these words themselves.

Exceptions:—@आर्मणिः@ n. and @ताटस्@ n. both meaning a 'river'.
