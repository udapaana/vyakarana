#### § 198. The following rules should be observed in arranging the various members of a Dwandwa Compound:—

(a) A word ending in i or u should be placed first, and when there are more than one of such words one of these should be placed first, the rest being arranged according to the will of the speaker or writer; @[hariharau], @[hariharakṛṣṇaḥ], @[harīśvarurūdrāḥ], &c.

(b) Words beginning with a vowel and ending in ā ought to precede others; as @[ambarākṣarāḥ] or @[īśvarāmbarāḥ]. This rule should have preference even over the above rule in case both would be simultaneously applicable; @[iṣukārmukaḥ].
