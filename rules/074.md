#### § 74. Obs. (a) @[औडुलोमि] descendant of Uduloman (@[औडुलोमः] @[अपत्यं] @[पुमान्]) is declined like @[हरि] in the sing. and dual and like @[पति] in the plural, the original word @[औडुलोम] being substituted; e.g. @[औडुलोमि]—@[औडुलोमयः], @[उडुलोमाः] &c.

(b) Words of similar derivation (see § 2. II. 4, 62, 63, 65, 66 and IV. 1. 105) also substitute their originals in the plural, e.g. @[गर्गस्य] @[अपत्यं] @[गार्ग्यः] N. @[गार्गिः] @[गार्ग्यौ] @[गर्गाः] &c.

Words ending in @[ई] and @[ऊ] mas. and fem.
