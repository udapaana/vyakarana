#### § 566. (a) In the @[parasmaipada] the radical vowel takes its @[vṛddhi] substitute; @[nī]—अनैषीत्; @[kṛ]—अकार्षीत्; @[mṛj]—अमार्जीत्, &c.

(b) In the @[ātmanepada], @[guṇa] is substituted for final इ or उ, short or long; final ऋ and the penultimate vowel remain unchanged, final अ is changed to ई or इष् in accordance with § 394; @[ci]—अचेष्ट, @[nī]—अनेष्ट, @[cyu]—अच्योष्ट, अभरिष्ट, see below, @[bhid]—अभित्त, @[stu]—अस्तुत, अमृष्ट.

(c) The Penultimate अ of Anit roots is optionally changed to आ; @[kṛ]—अकार्षीत् or अक्रार्षीत्.
