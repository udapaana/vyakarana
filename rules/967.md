#### § 967. @[tathā] 'likewise' often supplies the place of @[ca]: @[rāmastathā lakṣmaṇaśca] Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa; @[anāgatavidhātā ca pratyutpannamatiśca tathā] both Anāgatavidhātā and Pratyutpannamati; @[tathā hi] means 'for instance, to be more plain;' @[tathā ca] 'likewise,' both are often used introducing quotations.
