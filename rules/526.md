#### § 526. The Periphrastic perfect is formed by adding @[आम्] to the root and then adding the forms of the reduplicated perfect of @[कृ], @[भू], or @[अस्] as terminations; when the forms of @[अस्] are added, a Parasmaipadi root takes the Parasmaipadi forms and an Atmanepadi one takes the Atmanepadi forms.
