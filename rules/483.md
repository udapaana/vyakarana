#### § 483. भू 2. हन् &c. @[Aniṭ] roots ending in consonants admit इ in the Second Future and the conditional: गम् 1 P. (also that substituted for इ 'to go' and with अधि 'to remember') also admits it in the desiderative; गमिष्यामि, हनिष्यामि, करिष्यामि, &c. 2nd. Fut. 1. sing. अगमिष्यम्, अकरिष्यम्, अहनिष्यम्, &c. Con. 1. sing.
