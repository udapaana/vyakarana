#### § 443. (a) The base is formed by reduplication of the root.

(b) The third personal plural termination loses its न्.

(c) The Imperfect third person plural termination is उः (न्), before which the final आ of roots is dropped and the final इ, उ and ऋ, short or long, are @[guṇated].
