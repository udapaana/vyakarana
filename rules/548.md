#### § 548. Roots of the tenth Class and causals:—

(a) The @[ayaṃ] of the base is dropped (the vowel changes ta- king place before it being retained) and a short vowel substi-

tuted for the long one (@[ऐ] and @[औ] being shortened to @[इ], and @[ऐ] and @[औ] to @[उ]).

The base so modified is then reduplicated according to the general rules; @[भावय] (Cau. B. of @[भू]) = @[भाव] = @[भव्]--@[बभव्] after reduplication; @[चेतय] (from @[चित्]) = @[चेत] = @[चित्] = @[चिचित्] &c.

(3) @[इ] is substituted for the @[अ] of the reduplicative syllable if the syllable following it be short, and not prosodially long; the @[इ] of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened if not followed by a long syl. or a conjunct consonant; thus @[बभव] = @[बिभव] = @[बीभव्]; @[चिचित्] = @[चीचित्]; @[स्खल्] = @[चस्खल्] = @[चिस्खल्], the @[इ] is not lengthened as it is followed by a conjunct consonant; @[स्पन्द्] = @[पस्पन्द्] only, as @[अ] is followed by a syllable prosodially long.

(4) Roots having a penultimate @[ऋ], short or long, optionally preserve it, the long being changed to the short one; @[वृत्] causal base @[वर्तय] = @[वर्त्] after dropping @[अय] and @[अ]; by this: @[वर्त्]--@[ववर्त्]; @[वृत्] = @[वर्त्] = @[वित्] = @[विवृत्]; @[कृत्]--@[कीर्तय] = @[कीर्त्] and @[कृत्] by this, @[कीर्त्]--@[चिकीर्त्]; @[प्रथ्]--@[प्रीप्रथ].

(5) To the base so prepared the augment @[अ] is to be prefixed and the terminations added, P. & A., as in the 2nd Variety:—@[अबीभवत्]–@[त] from @[भू]; @[अचीचितत्] from @[चित्]; @[अचिस्खलत्]–@[त] from @[स्खल्]; @[अपस्पन्दत्]–@[त] from @[स्पन्द्]; @[अववर्तत्]–@[त], @[अविवृतत्]–@[त] from @[वृत्]; @[अचिकीर्तत्]–@[त], @[अचीकृतत्]–@[त] fr. @[कृत्]; @[अपिप्रथत्]–@[त], @[अपीप्रथत्]–@[त] from @[प्रथ्], &c.

N. B. The Atm. form in @[त] when not given should be supposed to be understood.
