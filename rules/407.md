#### § 407. The final उ of the base is optionally dropped before अ and इ if it be not preceded by a conjunct consonant. It is changed to अव before a weak termination beginning with a vowel, if preceded by a conjunct consonant and to ओ in other cases. The हि of the imperative 2nd per. sing. is dropped after उ not preceded by a conjunct consonant.
