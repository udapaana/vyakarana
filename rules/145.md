#### § 145. Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the pronominal adjectives @[अन्य], @[इतर] and @[पर]; e.g. @[अन्योन्य], @[इतरेतरः] and @[परस्पर]. These are generally used in the sing. and also adverbially e.g. @[परस्परं स्पृहणीयोभयं] &c. (Rag. VII, 14); @[परस्परं विवदन्ते] &c. They are more commonly used as the first members of compounds; as @[अन्योन्यदोषोद्भावनाद्‌] &c. (Ku. I, 44); @[इतरेतरयोगाः] (Śi. i. 24.) &c.
