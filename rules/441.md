#### § 441. @[हन्] P. A. 'to Kill'—drops its @[न्] before a weak termination, beginning with any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel, and its @[अ] before a vowel termination, the @[न्] then changing to @[य्]. The Imp. 2 sing. is @[जहि].

Present. Imperfect.
