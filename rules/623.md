#### § 623. (a) The perfect is formed by adding @[आस्‌] to the base and appending the perfect forms of the auxiliary verbs @[अस्‌], @[भू], and @[कृ] (see § 490, 526).

(b) In the aorist the desiderative Bases take the terminations of the 5th Variety.

(c) In the benedictive the Parasm. terminations are added without @[इ] and the Atm. ones with the intermediate @[इ].

(d) The remaining tenses have no peculiarities.

- These roots do not change their @[अ] to @[आ].
