#### § 444. The first vowel of a root together with the initial consonant, if any, is reduplicated i. e. doubled, as पठ् = पप्ठ्, उज्झ् = उउज्झ्, &c. after reduplication.

---

Note—The first syllable of a reduplicated root is called 'the reduplicative syllable'; e. g. the first प in पप्ठ्, or first उ in उउज्झ्.
