#### § 56. @[Sup] (@[सुप्]) is the technical term for a case termination in Sanskrit. Declension consists in adding the case termination to the crude form or base.

@[ekavacanaṃ dvivacanaṃ bahuṣu bahuvacanaṃ] Pāṇ. I. 4. 22. 21.
