#### § 684. The root is reduplicated according to the general rules of reduplication; the vowels इ and उ of the reduplicative syllable take their guṇa substitute; and the अ of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened; @[dā-dadā-dādā]; @[bhī-bibhī-bebhī]; @[kup-cukup-cokup]; @[kṛ-cakṛ-carkṛ]; @[vid-vivid-vevid]; @[budh-bubudh-bobodh] &c.
