#### § 591. Formation of base:—

(a) @[य] is added to the root, which is weak i. e. no Guṇa or Vṛddhi substitute takes place before it; @[नी]—@[नीय], @[भिद्‌]—@[भिद्य]

(b) Before @[य] roots undergo the same changes as they do before the Par. terminations of the benedictive; @[जि]—@[जीय], @[इ]—@[इय], @[स्मृ]—@[स्मर्य], @[ऋ]—@[अर्य], @[कृ]—@[क्रिय], @[पॄ]—@[पूर्य], @[वध्‌]—@[वध्य], (@[निन्द्‌]—@[निन्द्य]), @[वच्‌]—@[उच्य], @[ब्रह्‌]—@[ब्रह्य], &c.

(c) The final @[आ] (original or substituted) of @[दा] 'to give,' @[दे], @[धे], @[मा], @[स्था], @[पा], @[हा] 'to drink,' @[सो] @[पा] @[हा] 'to abandon,' is changed to @[ई]; in other cases it remains unchanged; @[दा] and @[धे]—@[दीय], @[गे]—@[गीय], @[हा]—@[हीय]; but @[छा] 'to cut, to purify'—@[छाय], @[ज्ञा]—@[ज्ञाय], @[प्सा]—@[प्साय].
