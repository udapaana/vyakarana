§ 7. In the case of metres regulated by syllabic instants
each line is divided into groups of four mātrās called the Mātrā
Gaṇas; one युयूत्रुयू or instant is allotted to a short vowel and two
to a long one. The Mātrā gaṇas are five in number. These
may be symbolically represented thus:—

यू——, नु∪——, भु∪∪∪, सु∪∪∪, तु∪∪∪,

* Vide Vṛittaratnākara I. 3.

युङुयुयूनुविरुयुयूनुयूनु तयूयूयू गन्युयूयूयूर्युयूयुयूनुयु नु स्युयूट् ॥ Chandomanjari.

† The above verse is easier to remember than one given
above; आदिमुयूयूयूयूयूयूनुयू युयूदुयूयूयू युयूयूति ल्युयूयूवुरु । युत्तुयूयू गोत्तुयूयूं युयूयूति
युतो दु युयुलुयूयूयूयू ॥
