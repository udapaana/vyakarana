§ 4. A syllable is लघु 'light,' or गुरु 'heavy' according as
its vowel is short or long.

(a) The vowels अ, इ, उ, ऋ and लृ are short; and the
vowels आ, ई, ऊ, ॠ, ए, ओ, ऐ, and औ are long. ‡ When a short
vowel is followed by an anusvāra or Visarga or by a conjunct
consonant, it is converted into what is called 'a prosodially
long vowel;' as अं, अच्छ, &c.
