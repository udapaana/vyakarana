N. B. All these vowels which do not blend with others, and sometimes the words in which they occur are technically called @[pragrihya].

III. @[Pluta] vowels; e. g. एहि मे आ३ गौ३: here is the cow grazing.

A vowel becomes @[pluta] under the following circumstances:—

(1) The last vowel of a sentence uttered in reply to a salutation from a male person other than a Śūdra when the name or the family is actually mentioned; e. g. ओ आयुष्मान् एधि देवदत्त३ live long, O Devadatta, which is said in reply to "नमस्ते देवदत्तोऽहम्" I, Devadatta, bow to you; but the last ई in "भो आयुष्मती एधि देवदत्ते" or in "आयुष्मान् एधि" is not @[pluta]; for in the 1st we have the name of a female and in the 2nd no name at all.

But if the sentence end in the word भोः or in the name of a Kṣatriya or Vaiśya the last vowel becomes optionally @[pluta]; e. g. आयुष्मान् एधि भोः or भो३; आयुष्मान् एधि इन्द्रवर्मन् or र्म३न्; आयुष्मान् एधि इन्द्रगुप्त or प्त३.

(2) The final vowel of a sentence addressed from a distance to a person; and that of the particles आ and हे used in such a sentence; e. g. संस्कृतं पठ or ठ३; हे राम or म३; राम or म३.

§ 27 The particle उ, preceded by a letter of the first five classes except अ and followed by any vowel, is optionally changed to व्; किमु उक्तं किंवुक्तं and किमु उक्तं (by § 26 a.).

II. @[Halsandhi], OR THE COALESCENCE OF FINAL CONSONANTS WITH VOWELS AND CONSONANTS.

§ 28 When त् or a consonant of the dental class comes in contact with:—

(a) च् or a consonant of the palatal class, the corresponding letter of the latter class is substituted for the former, and श् for स्; हरिः + शेते = हरिश्शेते Hari sleeps, रामः + चिनोति = रामश्चिनोति Rāma collects; सत् + चित् = सच्चित् Reality and knowledge; कृष्ण + जय = कृष्णञ्जय Be you victorious, Oh Kṛṣṇa.

Exception:—(I) Dentals coming after र् are not changed into the corresponding palatals, e. g. विरिञ्चिः that which spreads lustre; पूर्त्यः.
