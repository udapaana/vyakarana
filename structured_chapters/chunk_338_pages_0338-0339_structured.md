(5) THE AORIST @[luṅ].

§ 529. There are seven Varieties or Forms of the Aorist.

The augment @[a] is prefixed to the root as in the Imperfect.

First Variety or Form.

§ 530. The terminations of the First Variety are the same as those of the Imperf., except that of third person plural which is @[us]:—
