§ 164. @[eka] (@[ekā] fem.), @[dvi] (@[dve]), @[tri] (@[tisraḥ] f.), and @[catur] (@[catasraḥ] fem.), are all adjectives and take the gender, number and case of the nouns they qualify.

§ 167. @[daśan], @[viṃśati] and other cardinal numbers up to @[navanavatī] are all feminine substantives, the numerals @[śata], @[sahasra], &c. are all neuter, with the exception of @[lakṣa] which is mas. and fem. @[koṭi] which is fem. and @[arbudan] and @[jaladhi] both of which are mas. and have no declensional peculiarities. They are declined in the sing. and so used with a plural noun; e.g. @[pañcaviṃśatir brāhmaṇāḥ] 25 Brahmanas; @[ekādaśādhikaśatena] or @[ekādaśottaraśatena naraiḥ] or @[nāribhiḥ] by 111 men or women; @[ekonasahasreṇa rūpakaiḥ] with Rs. 999 &c. They may be made to have duals and plurals by being used as fixed standards of counting; e.g. @[brāhmaṇānāṃ viṃśatayaḥ] many twenties of Brāhmanas; @[dve śate nārīṇām] two hundreds of women &c.

§ 168. The ordinals are declined regularly except:—

@[prathama] (see § 156) and @[dvitīya] and @[tṛtīya] which are optionally declined like pronouns in the Dat. Ab. Gen. and Loc. sing; e.g. @[dvitīyasmai]-@[dvitīyāya], &c. @[dvitīyasyāḥ]-@[dvitīyasyai] &c.

NUMERAL ADVERBS.

§ 169. (a) @[sakṛt] once, @[dviḥ] twice, @[triḥ] thrice, @[catuḥ] four times; from @[pañcan] onwards adverbs of frequency are formed by adding @[kṛtvaḥ] before which final @[n] is dropped e.g. @[pañcakṛtvaḥ] five times, @[saptakṛtvaḥ] seven times, &c.

(b) @[ekadhā]* or @[ekadhyam] in one way or part, @[dvedhā] or @[dvidhā] or @[dvayam] in two ways or parts, @[tredhā] or @[tridhā] or @[trayam] in three ways, &c.; @[catuṣṭayam] in four ways, @[ṣoḍhā] or @[ṣaḍdhā] in six ways, @[saptadhā], @[aṣṭadhā], &c.
