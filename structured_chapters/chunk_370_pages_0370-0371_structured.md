§ 595-596] CONJUGATION OF VERBS. 364

NON-CONJUGATIONAL TENSES AND MOODS.

(1) The Perfect.

§ 595. (a) The Reduplicative Perfect of a root in the Passive is formed in the ordinary way, every root being considered @[ātmanepadi]: @[nī]—@[ninye]; @[bhū]—@[babhūve]; @[nind]—@[nininde]; @[aś]—@[ānaśe]; @[gam]—@[jagme]; &c.

(b) The Periphrastic Perfect of the Passive does not differ from that of the Active, except in that the Auxiliary verbs take @[ātmanepadi] terminations necessarily; @[īkṣāṃ cakre], @[rakṣāṃ babhūve], @[bhavāṃ āsa]; @[kathayāṃ cakre babhūve], @[kathayām āsa] &c.

(2, 3) The two Futures, (4) the Conditional and (5) the Benedictive.

§ 596. (a) The forms of the two Futures, Conditional and the Benedictive of the Passive are made up in the same way as those of the Active, every root being supposed @[ātmanepadi]; @[budh]—@[bhotsyate], @[bodhiṣye], @[abodhiṣye], @[bodhiṣīya]; @[tud]—@[totthāhe], @[totsye], @[atotsye]; &c.

(b) Roots ending in a vowel and the roots @[han], @[grah], and @[dṛś] optionally form the two Futures, the Conditional and the Benedictive of the Passive by changing their vowel to @[vṛddhi] and appending the @[ātmanepada] termination of those tenses with @[i] prefixed to them invariably; in the case of roots ending in @[ṛ] (and @[ṝ], @[ḷ] and @[ḹ] changeable to @[ṛ]) @[y] is inserted between the root and this @[i]; @[dā]—@[dāyitāhe], @[dātāhe]; @[dāyiṣye], @[dāsye]; @[adāyiṣye], @[adāsye]; @[dāyiṣīya], @[dāsīya]; and similarly @[sthā]—@[sthāyitāhe], @[sthātāhe]; &c. @[nī]—@[nāyitāhe], @[netāhe]; @[nāyiṣye], @[neṣye]; @[anāyiṣye], @[aneṣye]; @[nāyiṣīya], @[neṣīya]; @[han]—@[ghāyitāhe], † @[hantāhe]; @[ghāniṣye], @[haniṣye]; @[aghāniṣye], @[ahaniṣye]; @[ghāniṣīya], @[haniṣīya], 1 sing.; @[grah]—@[grāhitāhe], @[grahītāhe]; @[grāhiṣye], @[grahīṣye]; @[agrāhiṣye], @[agrahīṣye]; @[grāhiṣīya], @[grahīṣīya] &c. @[dṛś]—@[darśitāhe], @[drakṣyāhe]; @[darśiṣye], @[drakṣye]; @[adarśiṣye], @[adrakṣye]; @[darśiṣīya], @[drakṣīya]. &c.

* @[ṛ] and @[ḷ] take @[vṛddhi] in this case though it is usually forbidden with them, see § 463. @[grah] takes @[guṇa] only.

† The @[n] of @[han] is changed to @[ṇ] immediately before @[t] and before an affix containing a mute @[ṭ] or @[ṇ], e. g. @[ya] of the Passive, &c.

(6) The Aorist.

§ 597. (a) The Passive of the Aorist of roots belonging to the 4th, 6th and 7th Varieties, is made up similarly by appending the @[ātmanepada] terminations to the base; @[budh]—@[abodhaye]; @[kṛ]—@[akāri]; @[dhā]—@[adhāyi]; @[pac]—@[apacye]; @[div]—@[adīvye]; @[diś]—@[adiśye], &c.

(b) Roots belonging to the first, second, third and sixth Varieties take the fourth, fifth or seventh Variety in the Passive, in accordance with the general rules; @[sthā]—@[asthīye] 1 sing.; @[khyā]—@[akhyāye]; @[jū]—@[ajūye]; @[śri]—@[aśrāye]; @[jñā]—@[ajñāye]; @[nam]—@[anāmye], &c.

(c) The third per. sing. of the Aorist Passive of all roots is formed by adding @[i]:—

(1) Before this @[i] the penultimate (prosodially) short vowel takes its @[guṇa] substitute and penultimate @[a] except that of @[jan] and of @[seṭ] roots ending in @[am] except @[cam]. With @[ā], @[kam], and @[gam], and the final vowel take their @[vṛddhi] substitute. @[bhid]—@[abhedi]; but @[nind]—@[anindi]; @[tud]—@[atodi]; @[kṛṣ]—@[akārṣi]; @[vad]—@[avādi]; @[pad]—@[apādi]; but @[jan]—@[ajani]; @[gam]—@[agāmi]; but @[dam]—@[adami]; &c. @[cam] with @[ā]—@[ācāmi]; @[kam]—@[akāmi], &c. @[nī]—@[anāyi]; @[stu]—@[astāvi]; @[dū]—@[adāvi]; @[kṛ] or @[kṝ]—@[akāri].

(2) Roots ending in @[ā] original or substituted (i. e. of roots in @[e], @[ai], @[o]) insert @[y] before this @[i]; @[dā]—@[adāyi]; @[pe]—@[apāyi]; @[bhī]—@[abhāyi]; @[śo]—@[aśāyi], &c.

(3) @[bandh], @[gam] and @[rabh] insert a nasal before their final consonant, so that their penultimate @[a] cannot take @[guṇa] or @[vṛddhi] substitute; @[abandhi], @[agami], @[arambhi].

(4) @[lam] without a preposition does the same optionally and with a preposition necessarily; @[alambi], @[alami]; but @[prālambi].

(5) @[mañj] 'to break' forms @[amañji] or @[amāji]; @[śam]—@[aśāmi] and @[aśami] in the sense of 'to observe (10 A.).'

(6) @[mṛj] takes @[vṛddhi], @[gā] lengthens its vowel; @[amārji], @[agāri].

(7) @[i] 'to go' has @[āyi], with @[abhi] Ā., @[abhyāyi] or @[abhyavi].
