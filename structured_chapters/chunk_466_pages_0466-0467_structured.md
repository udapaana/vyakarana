**@[vaka]**—is added to @[śru], @[ju] and @[lu] in the sense 'skilful in,' @[śru]—@[śravakah] 'skilful in hearing,' @[ju]—@[javakah] 'skilful in walking,' @[lu]—@[lavakah] 'skilful in cutting:' this may be added to any root when the idea of a blessing is to be conveyed; @[jīvakastvam ciram] @[kālam] 'mayest thou live for many years,' @[modakapradaḥ] @[bhūyāḥ] 'mayest thou be the giver of delight.'

**@[ana]**—(@[lyuṭ], @[ṇvul], @[lyu], @[lyup])—

**@[ṇvul]**—is affixed to @[gai] and @[hā]; @[gāyanaḥ] 'a singer,' @[hāyanaḥ] 'a year,' 'a kind of rice.'

**@[lyuṭ]**—affixed to verbs meaning 'to go or to sound,' @[cal]—@[calanaḥ] 'one who moves,' @[rav]—@[ravanaḥ] 'one who makes a sound,' so @[ghasanaḥ] &c. It is also affixed to verbs meaning 'to ornament, to deck, and to be angry,'—@[bhūṣ]—@[bhūṣaṇam] 'serving as an ornament:' @[maṇḍ]—@[maṇḍanaḥ], @[kruś]—@[krośanaḥ], @[ruṣ]—@[roṣaṇaḥ] 'angry, irascible.'—to @[ji], @[śī], @[kṣi], @[pṛ], @[bhakṣ], @[pat]; @[gam]; @[śī]—@[jayanaḥ] 'a swift walker:' @[kṣi]—@[kṣaraṇaḥ] 'one who flows:' @[pṛ]—@[pūraṇaḥ] 'a glutton;' @[jval]—@[jvalanaḥ] 'that which blazes, fire;'—to some other roots ending in a consonant, @[vṛt]—@[vartanaḥ], @[vyadh]—@[vedhanaḥ] &c.:—to the freq. of @[kram] and @[gam], @[aṅkramaṇaḥ], @[daṅgamanaḥ] 'one who goes again and again': it also forms fem. abstract nouns with the causals of roots and the verbs @[aṇḍ], @[ghaṭ], @[vand] and @[iṣ] not meaning 'to wish,' @[kṛ]—@[kāraṇā] 'doing action,' @[bhāsanā], @[bhāṣ]—@[bhāṣaṇā], @[aṇḍ]—@[aṇḍanā], @[ghaṭ]—@[ghaṭanā], @[vand]—@[vandanā], @[iṣ]—@[eṣaṇā], @[viḍ]—@[vedanā]; @[anu]—@[anveṣaṇā] 'searching.'

**@[lyu]**—is affixed to @[nand] and other roots; @[nand]—@[nandanaḥ] 'one who delights, a son,' @[mad]—@[madanaḥ] 'he who exhilarates, the god of love,' @[sādh]—@[sādhanaḥ] 'one who accomplishes,' @[sah]—@[sahanaḥ] 'one who bears,' @[han]—@[madanudanaḥ] 'the killer of Madhu,' @[ard]—@[janārdanaḥ] 'the chastiser of the sinful,' @[bhī]—@[vibhīṣaṇaḥ] 'terrifier, name of Rāvaṇa's brother.'
