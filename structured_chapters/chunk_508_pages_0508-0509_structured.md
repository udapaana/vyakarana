§ 859. * In the case of Potential passive participles the agent of the action is put in the Genitive or in the Instrumental case; @[mayā bhavān vā sevyo hariḥ] Hari ought to be served by me; @[rākṣasendrasya rakṣyaṃ mayā nāśyam idaṃ vanaṃ] (Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 129.) this forest which the lord of the demons ought to preserve must be destroyed by me; @[antyā te vasatir alakā] &c. (Meg.) Thou should go to Alakā.

§ 860. † In the case of words denoting equality or likeness, such as @[tulya], @[sama], &c., the person or thing with whom or which any object is compared is put in the Genitive case, except in the case of @[tulā] and @[upamā]; @[tulyaḥ samaḥ samo vā sadṛśaḥ kṛṣṇena] equal to or like Krishna; @[ko'nyo'sti sadṛśo mama] who else is equal to me? but @[kṛṣṇasya tulā upamā vā nāsti] (Sid. Kau.).

Obs.—The words @[tulā] and @[upamā], however, are found used by good authors with the Instrumental against Pāṇini's rule; @[yat yadārohavati dantavatāṃsayā] (Kum. 5. 34.) which rises to the high position of being compared with your lip, @[sphuṭopamam iti bhasitena dharmyā] (Śiś. I. 4.) clearly deserving to be compared with Śambhu white with ashes; see Ragh. VIII. 15.

§ 862. The Genitive is used with* indeclinables like @[antare], @[pāre], @[ubhayataḥ], &c.; @[gaṅgāyā antare pāre vā] the middle or on the other side of the Ganges; @[ameṣāṃ rājñāṃ artham] for the sake of this life.

§ 863. With superlatives, and words having the sense of the superlative, the Genitive is used; @[teṣāṃ brāhmaṇaḥ śreṣṭhaḥ]; @[agrantmantravāḍāṃ] (Ragh. I. 4.) the chief of sages, the authors to the Mantras.

Note:—Words having the sense of comparatives are used with the Ablative and sometimes with the Instrumental; @[ayaṃ asmād balena hīnaḥ] or @[adhikaḥ] this person is superior or inferior to him in strength; similarly @[devadatto viṣṇudattād hīnaḥ vā]; @[kaḥ bhaved matto dhanyataraḥ] who will have a happier end than I? The word @[adhika] is used with the Genitive, the Locative or the Instrumental; @[putraiḥ putrebhyo vā'dhikataras teṣām] @[so'bhavat] he was more (i. e. dearer) to them than their sons; @[ṣaṭ ṣaṭṣv adhikeṣu varṣeṣu māsaḥ pañca rātayo dvādaśa] they passed five months and twelve nights more than (those years); @[kuḍave'dhikaḥ prastha] a Prastha is larger than a Kuḍava.

THE LOCATIVE CASE.

§ 864. The place where an action takes place with re-ference to the subject or object is called Adhikaraṇa (@[ādhikaraṇa]) and is put in the Locative case; † @[svapiti girigartre] (Bham. I. 50); @[ramate nandanavākanāne] (Ibid 64); @[sthālyāṃ odanam pacati] cooks food in a cooking utensil, @[karṇe kathayati] tells (something) into the ear; @[khaḍge tejas asti], &c.
