IV. NOMINAL VERBS.

§ 640. Verbs are formed from nominal bases by means of certain affixes. Those are not very much in common use and are generally used in the Present Tense. They have various meanings. They sometimes convey the notion of performing, practising, or using, or treating like the thing or quality expressed by the noun and are used transitively; sometimes they express the idea of acting, behaving or becoming like the person or thing expressed by the noun; and at others they yield the sense of desiring or wishing for the thing expressed by the noun. These will be arranged here under four heads, according to the affixes by which they are derived.

(α) By means of the affix @[य] (@[क्यच्]) and Conjugated in Parasm.

§ 641. When the sense of 'wish' is to expressed, a nominal verb may be derived from any Subanta or nominal stem by affixing @[क्यच्]. The derivative verbal base derived in this manner is conjugated in the Parasm. only.

§ 642. Before this @[य],

(1) final @[अ] and @[आ] are changed to @[ई]; e.g. @[आत्मनः इच्छति-आत्मीयति] ( @[आत्मन्+य=आत्मन्+य+ति] ) 'he wishes for a son.'

(2) final @[इ] and @[उ] are lengthened; @[कवि–कवीयति] ' he wishes for a poet,'

(3) final @[ऋ] is changed to @[री]; @[पितृ-पैतरीयति].

(4) final @[ओ] and @[औ] are changed to @[अव्] and @[आव्]; @[गो-गव्यति]; @[नौ-नाव्यति].

(5) a final nasal is cropped and the preceding vowel is changed like an original final vowel; @[राजन्–राजीयति] 'he wishes for a king.'

(6) in other cases the final consonant remains unchanged; @[वाच्–वाच्यति] 'he wishes for words,' @[दिव्–दिव्यति] ( @[दीव्यति] according to some ) 'he wishes for heaven,' @[समित्–समित्यति] ' he wishes for holy sticks,' &c.

§ 643. The consonant @[स्] and the syllable @[अस्] are inserted between any nominal stem and the affix @[य]; e.g. @[मधु इच्छति मधुस्यति] or @[मध्वस्यति] 'he wishes for honey'; so @[गोस्यति], @[दध्यस्यति], &c. Final @[अ] is dropped before @[अस्] ; @[पुत्रस्यति].

(a) @[अस्] is inserted after @[वृष] and @[अश्व] when the sense to be conveyed is that of longing for them, after @[क्षीर] and @[लवण] when the one is desired to be drank and the other to be licked; @[वृषस्यति गौः] 'the cow longs for the ox,' @[अश्वस्यति वडवा] 'the mare longs for the horse;' @[क्षीरस्यति बालः] 'the child desires to drink,' @[लवणस्यति उष्ट्रः] 'the camel wishes for licking the salt.'
