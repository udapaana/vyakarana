§ 693. (a) @[ṛ] when it means 'to incur debt' substitutes @[a]; @[ṛṇa] 'debt'; but @[īrṇa], 'gone.'

(b) @[kṣi] when the P. P. Participle has an active sense, substitutes @[na] and lengthens its @[i]; @[kṣīṇa], reduced, emaciated; but when a curse is implied, or pity expressed, the change is optional; @[kṣīṇāyuḥ] or @[kṣitāyuḥ] i.e. 'die,' @[kṣīṇaḥ] or @[kṣitaḥ ayaṃ tapasvī] 'oh, the poor ascetic has grown thin' but @[kṣitaḥ kāmo bhava].

(c) @[dīv] takes @[na] when it does not mean 'to gamble,' @[dīna] 'a sport in which nothing is staked,' but @[dyūta] 'gambling.'

(d) @[vā] with @[nir] takes @[na] except when it has @[vāta] for its subject; @[nirvāṇo hutāśanaḥ] 'the fire is extinguished,' @[nirvāṇo muniḥ] the sage is absolved; but @[nirvātaḥ pradeśaḥ].

(e) @[div] takes @[na] when the notion of touch is not present; @[saṃdīvāno vṛścikaḥ] 'a scorpion contracted through cold;' when it means 'to be coagulated or cold' its @[i] takes @[saṃprasāraṇa] (@[y]), @[adhyūḍhaṃ ghṛtam], but @[sīta udakam] and not @[dīna] as the idea of touch is present here; also when preceded by @[prati], @[pravidīna iva], &c.; with @[abhi] and @[ava] the change is optional; @[abhidīvānaṃ] or @[abhisītaṃ ghṛtam], @[avadīvāno] or @[avasītaḥ gṛdhrakaḥ]; but @[samavadīvātaḥ].

§ 695. @[pyā] has @[pīna] when it is used with 'one's own limbs,' @[pīnaṃ sukham], and @[pyāna] or @[pīna] in other cases, as @[pyānaḥ] or @[pīnaḥ aṅgaḥ]; when it is preceded by a preposition, @[i] is not substituted for its vowel; @[prapyānaḥ]; but when it is used with @[aṅga] or @[gātra] with the preposition @[ā], @[i] is necessarily substituted; @[āpīnaḥ aṅgaḥ], @[āpīnaṃ gātram].
