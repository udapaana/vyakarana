@[क्रीड्]—'to play' is Atm. after the prepositions @[अनु], @[आ], @[परि] and @[सम्], @[अनुक्रीडते-आक्रीडते-परिक्रीडते-संक्रीडते], but not when @[अनु] governs a noun, as @[माणवकमनुक्रीडति] i.e. 'plays with Māṇavaka.' @[क्रीड्] with @[सम्] is Par. when it means 'to creak as a wheel,' as @[संक्रीडति] the wheel.

@[क्षिप्]—'to throw' is Par. when preceded by the prepositions @[अभि], @[प्रति] and @[अति]; @[अभिक्षिपति] 'throws up,' @[अतिक्षिपति] 'throws out,' @[प्रतिक्षिपति] 'throws back.'

@[क्षण्]—with @[सम्] is Atm; @[संक्षणते शस्त्रम्] 'whets or sharpens his weapon;' @[संक्षणते चिन्ताम्] 'cherishes anxiety.'

@[गम्]—with @[सम्] is Atm. in the sense of 'to be proper, unite with, join'; @[सुखेन संगच्छते], @[सखीभिः संगच्छते], &c., but @[ग्रामं संगच्छति] 'goes to a village.' In the Causal it takes the Atm. in the sense of 'having patience or waiting,' @[आगमयस्व प्रथमम्] 'have patience first'.

@[गर्ध्]—'to be greedy' is Atm. in the Cau. when the sense is 'to deceive,' @[माणवकं गर्धयते] 'he deceives Māṇavaka,' but @[श्वानं गर्धयति] 'he makes the dog greedy.'

@[गॄ]—with @[सम्] is Atm. when the meaning is 'to pledge one's word, to promise, to proclaim;' @[संगिरते शपथम्] 'he pledges his word;' @[शतं संगिरते] 'he promises 100 (Rupees, &c.),' @[संगिरते स्वामिनो गुणान्] 'proclaims the merits of his master,' but @[संगिराति ग्रासम्] 'he swallows down a mouthful.' If it (sc. @[गॄ] Con.) be preceded by @[अव], it is Atm. @[अवगिरते रुधिरं पिशाचः] 'the fiend drinks blood,'

@[धर्ममुच्चरते] 'he transgresses his duty.' @[मार्गाविचरतः सिद्धान्तमुच्चरतः राजनम्] | Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 31; but @[वाष्पश्चरति] 'vapour rises up.' With @[सम्] or @[प्रति] it is Atm. when used with the instrumental of a vehicle; @[रथेन संचरते] 'he moves in a chariot' (See Bh. VIII. 32); @[कचिद्दिव्येन पथा संचरते] Ragh. XIII. 19. @[जन्]—in the Causal takes the Parasmai. @[जनयति].

@[जि]—when preceded by @[वि] and @[परा] in the sense of 'to conquer,' and 'to defeat' or 'to find unbearable,' respectively, is Atm. @[विजयते], also @[पराजयते], @[अध्ययनात् पराजयते] 'gets tired of study;' @[खं पराजयमानस्य] 'filling up the sky, &c.' @[तां पराजयमानां स प्रीतेः] 'her who was getting disgusted with,' &c. Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 9. 71.

@[ज्ञा]—'to know' used intransitively by itself is Atm. @[सर्पिषो यजते] (@[सर्पिषा उपायेन जानीते] said Sid. Kau. s.c. 'proceeds to perform a sacrifice having obtained ghee for it'); with the preposition @[अप] it is Atm. in the sense of 'denying;' as @[शतम् अपजानीते] 'denies a hundred,' with @[प्रति] in the sense of 'acknowledging or promising,' and with @[सम्] in the sense of 'expecting,' it is also Atm.; @[शतं प्रतिजानीते] 'acknowledges a hundred,' @[हरचापारोपणेन कन्यादानं प्रतिजानीते] 'promises the hand of his daughter by (i.e. on the condition of) the drawing of Hara's bow,' @[शतं संजानीते] 'looks for a hundred, @[मातरि मरणं संजानाति] 'thinks of his mother.' When this is used without a preposition and when the fruit accrues to the agent it takes the Atm. @[यत् जानीते]; when a preposition is prefixed to it and it is used transitively, it takes the Par. @[स्वर्गलोकं न प्रजानाति] says. In the Desiderative this root takes the Atm.
