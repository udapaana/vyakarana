@[आ]—forms abstract nouns from derivative bases; @[कृ]—@[चिकीर्षा] 'a desire to do'; @[पुत्रकाम्या] 'a desire to have a son, &c. Also from roots ending in a consonant having a long vowel for their penultimate; @[इष्]—@[ईषा] 'desire, @[ऊह्]—@[ऊहा] 'a guess; reasoning, &c.'

@[अ]—similarly forms abstract nouns; it is added to roots marked with an indicatory @[क्] and to @[लिप्] and others; @[जॄ]—@[जरा] 'old age' @[त्रप्]—@[त्रपा] 'shame, &c.; @[भिद्]—@[भिदा] 'distinction, separation, @[चिन्त्]—@[चिन्ता] 'contemplation,' + @[रजक्]; @[सृज्]—@[सृजा] 'creation' &c.—to @[कृप्] which changes its @[ऋ] to @[आ]; @[कृपा] 'mercy'; to roots ending in @[आ] if a preposition or the words @[प्र] and @[अन्तर्] precede, @[दा]—@[प्रदा] 'giving' 'gift', @[ना]—@[प्रज्ञा] 'lustre, &c. @[धा] with @[श्रद्]—@[श्रद्धा] 'faith; with @[अन्तर्]—@[अन्तर्धा] 'disappearance'.
