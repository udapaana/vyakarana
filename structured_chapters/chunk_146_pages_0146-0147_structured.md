§ 226. * The word @[ईषत्] 'little' is compounded with any other word except a verbal derivative, and even with a verbal derivative when it expresses a quality; as @[ईषद्रक्तम्] 'a little brown', @[ईषत्रक्तः] 'reddish', &c.

§ 227. † Words ending in the terminations @[तव्य], @[अनीय], @[य] and @[तुल्य] and words having a similar meaning, may be compounded with any @[subanta] not importing a particular kind; e. g. @[भोज्योदनम्] 'any hot eatable', @[गौरवर्णः] 'of a similar white colour', @[श्वेतवर्णः], &c.; but @[भोज्यः भोदनः] as the latter word refers to a kind of food here.

§ 229-231 ] DVIGU COMPOUNDS. 141

§ 229. In some @[Karmadhāraya] compounds the last member of the first word which is itself a compound word is dropped*; e. g. @[शाकप्रियः पार्थिवः शाकपार्थिवः] 'a king who likes vegetables', @[देवपूजको ब्राह्मणः देवब्राह्मणः] &c. These compounds, though they should be properly called "@[Uttarapadalopi]" are popularly designated "@[Madhyamapadalopi]". Any compound requiring a similar explanation is classed under this group (@[यथासंख्यम्]) which is an @[अव्ययीभावः].

DVIGU OR NUMERAL APPOSITIONAL COMPOUNDS.

§ 230. † When the first member of a @[Karmadhāraya] compound is a numeral it is called '@[Dvigu]'.

§ 231. A @[Dvigu] compound is possible (a) under the circumstances mentioned in § 223 (a) i. e.-

(1) when a @[Taddhita] affix is to be added to the compound so formed, @[षण्णां मातृणाम् अपत्यं षाण्मातुरः] 'the son of six mothers, Kārttikeya' (vide Ku. IX); @[पञ्चकपालः] &c; or (2) when the compound itself becomes the first member of another compound, as @[पञ्च गावः धनं यस्य असौ पञ्चगवधनः], @[पञ्चगुः] &c.

(b) And when the compound denotes an aggregate, in this case it is singular; ‡ as @[त्रयाणां लोकानां समाहारः त्रिलोकम्] the three worlds (taken collectively); so @[पञ्चनपाटम्], @[पञ्चगवम्], &c.

* @[शाकपार्थिवादीनां सिद्धये उत्तरपदलोपस्य उपसंख्यानम्] । Vārt on Pāṇ. II. 1. 60.

† @[संख्यापूर्वो द्विगुः] । Pāṇ. II. 1. 52.

‡ @[द्विगुरेकवचनम्] । Pāṇ. II. 4. 1.
