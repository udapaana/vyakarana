5—Reflexive Pronouns.

§ 140. The sense of the reflexive pronoun is, in Sanskrit, expressed by the word @[आत्मन्‌] 'self', which is invariably used in the masculine gender and singular number, and by the reflexive adverb @[स्वयम्‌] e.g. @[ताः सर्वाः स्वप्नेषु वामनैः]
"they all (the wives of Daśaratha) saw in dreams that they were guarded by dwarfs," @[स] or @[सा कृतापराधः] (or @[कृतापराधा]) @[राजा स्वयं क्षमां याचते] or @[जगाम], &c.

6—Indefinite Pronouns.

§ 141. Indefinite pronouns are formed by the addition of @[चित्‌] or @[चन] or @[अपि] and sometimes of @[स्वित्‌] to the various cases of the pronoun @[किम्‌] in all the genders; e.g. @[कश्चित्‌], @[कश्चन], a certain one, @[कोऽपि], @[कांचित्‌], @[कांचन], @[कयाऽपि]; @[कास्वित्‌], &c.

§ 142. The terminations given above are also added to interrogative adverbs in the sense of indefiniteness; as @[कदाचित्‌] at some time, @[कदाचन], @[किंचित्‌] some, @[क्वचित्‌] somewhere, &c.

7—Correlative Pronouns.

§ 143. Correlatives are formed by the affixes @[वत्‌] added to @[तद्‌], @[यद्‌] and @[एतद्‌], and @[अत्‌] added to @[इदम्‌] and @[किम्‌] in the sense of 'as much as;' and @[दृश्‌] or @[दृक्ष] in the sense of 'similar to;' @[तद्‌], @[एतद्‌] and @[यद्‌] become @[ता], @[एता] and @[या] respectively before these, and @[इदम्‌] and @[किम्‌] become @[ई] and @[की] before @[दृश्‌] and @[दृक्ष] and @[इ] before @[यत्‌] or @[वत्‌]; e.g. @[तावत्‌] (@[तत्परिमाणम्‌ अस्य]), @[इयत्‌] (@[इदं परिमाणम्‌ अस्य]), @[तादृश्‌] like that, @[ईदृश्‌], like this, @[कियत्‌] how much, &c.

§ 144. The affix @[अति] is added to @[तद्‌], @[यद्‌] and @[किम्‌] to show number or measure e.g. @[तति] 'so many', @[यति] 'as many,' and @[कति] 'how many'. These are declined in the plural only, and take no termination in the Nom. and Acc.; e.g. Nom. and Acc. @[कति] Inst. @[कतिभिः] the rest like @[हरि].

8—Reciprocal Pronouns.

§ 145. Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the pronominal adjectives @[अन्य], @[इतर] and @[पर]; e.g. @[अन्योन्य], @[इतरेतरः] and @[परस्पर]. These are generally used in the sing. and also adverbially e.g. @[परस्परं स्पृहणीयोभयं] &c. (Rag. VII, 14); @[परस्परं विवदन्ते] &c. They are more commonly used as the first members of compounds; as @[अन्योन्यदोषोद्भावनाद्‌] &c. (Ku. I, 44); @[इतरेतरयोगाः] (Śi. i. 24.) &c.
