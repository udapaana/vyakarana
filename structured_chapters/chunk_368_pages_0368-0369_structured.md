§ 593. (a) The roots @[खम्], @[अन्], @[तन्], and @[सन्], optionally drop their @[न्] and at the same time lengthen their @[अ], @[खन्]--@[खन्यते], @[खायते], &c.

(b) @[शी] 'to lie down' and @[स्वि] form their bases as @[शये] and @[स्वे].

(c) @[दिव्] shortens its @[ई] when a preposition is prefixed to it.

(d) @[ह्री], @[ध्री] and @[वे] drop their final vowel before @[य].

(e) @[अश्] and @[अस्] substitute @[श्य] and @[स्य] for themselves. @[वश्] and @[वस्] also substitute @[उश्] and @[उस्] respectively.
