Exceptions:—

(1) @[stu] and @[su], when Parasmaipadi, belong to the Fifth Variety.

(2) Roots ending in अ preceded by a conjunct consonant may take Fourth or Fifth Form in the Atmanepada.

§ 566. (a) In the Parasmaipada the radical vowel takes its Vrddhi substitute; @[nī]—अनैषीत्; @[kṛ]—अकार्षीत्; @[mṛj]—अमार्जीत्, &c.

(b) In the Atmanepada, Guna is substituted for final इ or उ, short or long; final ऋ and the penultimate vowel remain unchanged, final अ is changed to ई or इष् in accordance with § 394; @[ci]—अचेष्ट, @[nī]—अनेष्ट, @[cyu]—अच्योष्ट, अभरिष्ट, see below, @[bhid]—अभित्त, @[stu]—अस्तुत, अमृष्ट.

(c) The Penultimate अ of Anit roots is optionally changed to आ; @[kṛ]—अकार्षीत् or अक्रार्षीत्.

§ 567. After short vowel, and after a consonant except a nasal or semi-vowel, the स् of terminations beginning with स्त and साथ् is dropped; @[kṛ]—अकृत 3 sing.; @[kṛ]—अकृथाः 2 dual; @[sic]—असिक्थाः 2nd; @[stu]—अस्तुत 3rd sing. &c.
