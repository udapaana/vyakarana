§ 576. Roots of the 8th class ending in @[क्] or @[म्] drop their nasal and substitute optionally @[ष्ठाः] and @[ष्ट] for the terminations @[स्थाः] and @[स्त] of the 2nd and 3rd person sing. Atm. @[तन्] lengthens its vowel after dropping @[न्].

§ 577. The vowel of @[अनु] takes Vrddhi optionally in the Parasmai, i. e. optionally takes Guna substitute, also it optionally remains unchanged before @[ई] (Vide §§ 466, 518).

§ 578. The @[द्रा] of @[दरिद्रा] is optionally dropped in the Aorist; consequently it takes the Sixth and the Fifth Varieties.
