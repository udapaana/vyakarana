206 SANSKRIT GRAMMAR. § 340

@[त्य] (@[त्यप्]) is added in the sense of 'living in, belonging to,' &c. to the words @[दक्षिण], @[पश्चात्], @[पुरस्], @[अमा], @[इह], @[क], @[क्व], @[द्युत्], and indeclinables ending in @[तस्]; @[दाक्षिणात्यः] 'a southerner,' @[पाश्चात्यः], @[पुरस्त्यः] 'an inhabitant of the east'; @[अमात्यः] 'one who accompanies a king, a minister,' @[इहत्यः], @[कत्यः], @[द्युत्यः], @[ततस्त्यः] &c.; also to the preposition @[नि]; @[नित्यः] 'eternal'.

@[व्यक] (@[व्यकन्]) is added to @[उच्च] and @[अधि]; @[उच्चत्यका] 'land at the foot of a mountain,' @[अधित्यका] 'a table-land.'

@[तृ]—is affixed to @[गो] only; @[गवां समूहो गोत्रं] 'a herd of kine'.

@[त्व]—forms abstract nouns; @[गोत्वं].

@[द], @[इयस्] and @[मात्र] (@[दप्रच्], @[इयसुन्], @[मात्रच्]) are added in the sense of 'measuring as much as'; @[जानु प्रमाणं अस्य जानुदप्र-इयस्-मात्रं वारि] 'water reaching as far as the knee', &c.

@[न] and @[य] (@[ञञ्], @[यञ्])—are added to @[स्त्री] and @[पुम्] respectively in different senses:—@[स्त्रैण] 'womanly, feminine, suited to women, (n.) womanhood' &c.; @[पुंस] 'manly, heroic, fit for a man, (n.) manhood, virility'.

@[अज्ञ]—is added as a depreciatory termination; @[चिकित्सकाज्ञः] 'a bad doctor, a quack'; @[वैयाकरणाज्ञः] &c.; added to @[कुल] it has the sense of 'a collection of', as @[कुलापाज्ञः] (@[तृण] and @[हस्त] are also added to @[कुल] in the same sense).

@[मय] (@[मयट्])—@[मय] is added in the sense of (1) 'made of' = @[विकारः]; @[मृन्मयः] 'made of earth,' @[काष्ठमयः] 'made of wood' &c.; and (2) in that of profusion or excess = @[प्रचुर-]

* @[प्रमाणे द्वन्द्वे इयसुन्मात्रचौ] Pan. V. 2. 37.
† @[स्त्रियां ङीप् नञस्नञ् पुंवत्] Pan. IV. 1. 87.
§ @[अकच् वा एतयोः मायाख्याममस्थ्याण्डाभ्यः] Pan. V. 2. 3, 143.
