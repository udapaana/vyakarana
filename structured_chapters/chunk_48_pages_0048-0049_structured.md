§ 77. Root nouns in @[i] or @[u] M. F.

Rule of Sandhi—(a) The ending @[i] or @[u], short or long, of nouns derived from roots with the affix @[kvi] (°) and of @[go], is changed to @[iy] or @[uv] before the vowel terminations*; the fem. nouns of this description are optionally declined like @[nadī] in the Du. Ab. Gen. and Loc. singulars and the Gen. plu.

(b) But if the ending @[i] or @[u] of a many-voweled noun having a root at the end be not preceded by a radical conjunct consonant or the root noun has a preposition termed @[gati] (i.e. a @[s] as added to the root) or a word governed by the root preceding it, it is changed to @[iy] or @[uv], except in the case of @[sudhī] and roots ending in @[jū].
