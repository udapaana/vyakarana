§ 93. Bases ending @[च्], @[ज्], or @[ह्] and @[श्], @[ष्], @[स्].

§ 94. (a) @[च्] or @[ज्] is changed to @[क्] when followed by a hard consonant or by nothing, and to @[ग्] when followed by a soft consonant.*

(b) The ending @[ज्] or @[च्] of root-nouns and the final of @[व्यच्], @[प्रच्], @[दिश्], @[द्विष्], @[विश्], @[राज्] and @[भ्राज्] are changed to @[क्] when followed by any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel or by nothing†; the @[ज्] is changed to @[ट्] or @[ड्] when at the end of a word, and to @[ग्] when followed by a soft consonant. The @[ज्] of @[परिव्राज्] is similarly changed to @[ट्] or @[ड्].

(c) But the @[च्] of the root-nouns @[धृष्], @[कृष्], @[त्वष्] and @[सृज्] is changed to @[ट्] as also the @[ष्] of @[दधृष्] 'a bold or impudent man' and the @[श्] of such words as @[विश्व] &c. The @[ह्] of @[नह्] is optionally changed to @[ट्] or @[ड्], as also the @[श्] of @[तक्ष्] and @[द्रश्]. The @[ज्] of @[ऋत्विज्] is changed to @[क्].
