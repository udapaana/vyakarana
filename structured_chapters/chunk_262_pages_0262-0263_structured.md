258 CONJUGATION OF VERBS. [§ 405-406

§ 405. Before strong terminations the penultimate short and the final vowel of the base take their @[guṇa] substitute.

Fifth, Eighth and Ninth classes

§ 406. @[nu] and @[u] are added on to the roots of the 5th and 8th classes respectively before the terminations.

§ 407. The final @[u] of the base is optionally dropped before @[ma] and @[va] if it be not preceded by a conjunct consonant. It is changed to @[uv] before a weak termination beginning with a vowel, if preceded by a conjunct consonant and to @[un] in other cases. The @[hi] of the Imperative 2nd per. sing. is dropped after @[u] not preceded by a conjunct consonant.

§ 408. In the ninth class @[nā] is inserted between the root and the terminations. @[nā] becomes @[nī] before the weak terminations beginning with a vowel, and @[nī] before the weak terminations beginning with a consonant.

§ 409. (a) The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped before @[nā] &c. as @[śṛṇāmi], @[śṛṇīvaḥ], @[śṛṇīmaḥ] &c. from @[śraṃ] 'to put together'.

(b) In the case of roots ending in a consonant, the imperative second person singular termination is @[āna] instead of @[hi] as @[stāna] from @[stu] 'to steal'.
