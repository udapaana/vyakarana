§ 404. The base of the roots of the 2nd group of Conjugation classes undergoes many modifications with regard to which the terminations are divided into sets, one set is called 'strong', the other 'weak'. The base taking the strong terminations may be called 'The strong base', and that taking the weak ones 'The weak base'.

(a) The strong terminations are:—

The singulars of all persons of the Present and the Imperfect, the third person singular and all numbers of the first person of the Imperative, in the Parasmaipada, and all numbers of the first person of the Imperative in the Ātmanepada.

(b) The rest are weak.

§ 405. Before strong terminations the penultimate short and the final vowel of the base take their @[guṇa] substitute.

Fifth, Eighth and Ninth classes

§ 406. नु and ओ are added on to the roots of the 5th and 8th classes respectively before the terminations.

§ 407. The final उ of the base is optionally dropped before अ and इ if it be not preceded by a conjunct consonant. It is changed to अव before a weak termination beginning with a vowel, if preceded by a conjunct consonant and to ओ in other cases. The हि of the Imperative 2nd per. sing. is dropped after उ not preceded by a conjunct consonant.

§ 408. In the ninth class ना is inserted between the root and the terminations. ना becomes नी before the weak terminations beginning with a vowel, and नी before the weak terminations beginning with a consonant.

§ 409. (a) The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped before ना &c. as यथ्नामि, यथ्नीवः, यथ्नीमः &c. from @[यञ्ज्] 'to put together'.

(b) In the case of roots ending in a consonant, the imperative second person singular termination is आन instead of हि as स्तेयान from @[स्तेय्] 'to steal'.
