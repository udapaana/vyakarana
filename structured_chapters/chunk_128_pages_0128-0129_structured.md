I. Tatpuruṣa.

§ 203. The first variety consists of such Tatpuruṣa compounds as have the attributive member in any one of the oblique cases when dissolved. There are six sub-varieties of this corresponding to the six oblique cases.

§ 204. (a) A noun in the Accusative case is compounded with @[prāpta], @[atīta], @[patita], @[gata], @[atyasta], @[prāpta], @[āpanna], @[gata], @[gāmin], and others; e.g. @[kṛṣṇam] @[śritaḥ] @[kṛṣṇābhitaḥ] one who has resorted to Kṛṣṇa (as his refuge); @[duḥkham] @[atītaḥ] @[duḥkhātītaḥ] one who has overcome pain, @[sukham] @[āpannaḥ] @[sukhāpannaḥ] one who has obtained happiness, @[grāmam] @[gacchati] @[grāmagāmin] going to a village, a passenger; @[annam] @[prāptaḥ] @[annārthaḥ] &c.
