@[ka], @[kaṇa], @[caṇa] (@[kan], @[kaṇap]) and @[cuñcu] (@[cuñcup]) are added in the sense of 'reputed or remarkable for'; as @[vidyāvān vidyākah-vidyākaṇaḥ] 'remarkable or known for one's knowledge', @[śastrāstre śīlaḥ śastrajñaḥ] 'renowned for his skill in the use of weapons' (vide Bhatti II. 32.)

@[tana] (@[tanaṃ], @[tanañ])—is added to adverbs of time in the senses of 'relating or belonging to', and @[bhavaḥ sāyaṃtanaḥ], @[adyatanaḥ], @[hyastanaḥ], @[prātaḥtanaḥ], @[divātanaḥ], @[doṣātanaḥ], @[cirantanaḥ], @[sanātanaḥ]—so far; as @[purātanaḥ] 'old';—to the word @[nava] changed to @[nū]; @[nūtanaḥ].

@[tā] (@[tal])—forms abstract nouns @[latā], @[kṛpaṇatā], @[samatā], &c.; added to @[grāma], @[jana], @[bandhu], @[sahāya], and @[bahu] it has the sense of 'a collection of', @[grāmatā], @[janatā], @[bandhutā], &c.
