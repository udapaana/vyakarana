§ 248. A @[Vyadhikaraṇa Bah.] is that whose members are not in apposition to each other i.e. are in different cases when dissolved. A @[Vyadhikaraṇa Bah.] is not allowed in any case except in the Genitive and the Locative:* as @[cakraṃ pāṇau yasya sa cakrapāṇiḥ hariḥ], @[candrasya iva kāntiḥ yasya sa candrakāntiḥ]; so @[padmabāhuḥ], @[śaṅkhapāṇiḥ], &c., @[śaśi śirasi yasya asau śaśiśekharaḥ] &c.; but @[aśvaḥ sthitaḥ pṛṣṭhe yasya śvapṛṣṭhakaḥ] is not allowed.

§ 251. †The particle @[saha] may be compounded with a noun in the Instrumental case in a Bahuvrīhi when there is equal participation in some action; in this case @[saha] optionally i.e. becomes @[sa]; as @[guruṇā saha gataḥ] or @[saguruḥ].

(a) @[saha] remains unchanged when the compound involves a blessing but is necessarily changed to @[sa] when followed by @[go] a cow, @[vatsya] a calf, or @[hala] a plough, even when a blessing is pronounced; as @[svasti rājñe sahaputrāya], @[sahāmātyāya], &c.; but, @[savatsāyaḥ], @[sahalāya].
