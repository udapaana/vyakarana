I. यस्मिन्‌ घृतमयो यज्ञः 'a sacrifice consisting for the most part of clarified butter,' अन्नमयः &c. It is not added to words denoting 'an article of food or a covering'; e.g. मोदकः चापः.

य ( यक, यत्‌, यञ्‌, ण्य )—is added to nouns with different meanings:—( 1 ) गवां समूहो गव्यं 'a herd of cattle,' वातानां समूहो वात्या, so खल्या, रथ्या, ( रथानां समूहः ), धूम्या, धूमानां समूहो 'a dense mass of smoke,' शार्करा, कर्कटा, &c. ( 2 ) सभायां साधुः सभ्यः 'a courtier'; ( 3 ) सतीर्थ्याः 'disciples of the same preceptor,' सोदर्य, समानोदर्यः 'a brother of whole blood'; ( 4 ) forms abstract nouns राज्यं, सेनापत्यं, पौरोहित्यं, सारथ्यं, आस्तिक्यं, &c.; it is added—( 5 ) to the words राजन्‌ and मनु in the sense of 'born of the race of.' राजन्यः 'a man of the Kshatriya class,' मनोर्जातः मनुष्यः ( in this case ष is inserted ) 'a man';—( 6 ) to श्वशुर in the sense of 'the son of': शौशव्यः; ( 7 ) to आर्य: आर्यः—'of noble descent;' ( 8 ) to वायु, ऋतु, पितृ, and उषस्‌ in the sense of 'having any of these for one's presiding or tutelary deity,' &c. वायुः देवता अस्य वायव्यं अस्त्रं 'a missile having Vayu for its presiding deity,' ऋत्व्यः 'worshipping the Seasons ( as divinities ),' पित्र्यः 'sacred to the manes, offered to the manes'; औषस्यः 'sacred to the Dawn;'—( 9 ) to दण्ड and words included under the दण्डादि group in the sense of 'deserving or meriting': दण्ड्यः 'deserving punishment,' वध्यः 'fit to be killed,' अर्ह्यः 'deserving worship,' &c.
