5—Reflexive Pronouns.

§ 140. The sense of the reflexive pronoun is, in Sanskrit, expressed by the word @[ātman] 'self' which is invariably used in the masculine gender and singular number, and by the reflexive adverb @[svayam] e.g. @[tāḥ sarvāḥ svapneṣu dṛṣṭavantaḥ] "they all (the wives of Daśaratha) saw in dreams that they were guarded by dwarfs," or @[sa] or @[sā kṛtāparādhibhiḥ svayamevetasmāt] @[rājā svayam eva janakāt jagāma], &c.

6—Indefinite Pronouns.

§ 141. Indefinite pronouns are formed by the addition of @[cit] or @[cana] or @[api] and sometimes of @[svit] to the various cases of the pronoun @[kim] in all the genders; e.g. @[kaścit], @[kaścana], a certain one, @[kopi], @[kācit], @[kenacana], @[kayāpi]; @[kāsvit], &c.

§ 142. The terminations given above are also added to interrogative adverbs in the sense of indefiniteness; as @[kadācit] at some time, @[kadācana], @[kincit] some, @[kutrāpi] somewhere, &c.

7—Correlative Pronouns.

§ 143. Correlatives are formed by the affixes @[vat] added to @[tad], @[etad] and @[etad], and @[yat] added to @[idam] and @[idam] in the sense of 'as much as'; and @[dṛś] or @[dṛśa] in the sense of 'similar to'; @[tad], @[etad] and @[idam] become @[tā], @[etā] and @[ī] respectively before these, and @[tad] and @[idam] become @[ti] and @[ki] before @[yat] and become @[tī] and @[ī] before @[dṛś] or @[dṛśa]; e.g. @[tāvat] (@[tatparimāṇamasya]), @[iyat] (@[idaṃ parimāṇamasya]), @[tādṛś] like that, @[īdṛś] like this, @[kiyat] how much, &c.
