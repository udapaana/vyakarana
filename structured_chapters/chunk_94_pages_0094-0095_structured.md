§ 134. (a) The optional shorter forms of the pronouns @[asmad] and @[yuṣmad] are never used at the beginning of a sentence or of a foot of a metre; nor can they be used immediately before the particles @[ca], @[vā], @[ha] or @[hā], @[aho] and @[vai]; as @[mama gṛham] my house, and not @[me gṛham]; @[vedaiḥ aśeṣaiḥ sevyaḥ asmān kṛṣṇaḥ sarvadā rakṣatu] (Sid. Kau.), 'may Kṛṣṇa, revered throughout or capable of being known properly from all the Vedas, always protect us; and not @[naḥ kṛṣṇaḥ] &c.; @[tava eva kriyam etat] 'this is surely your deed,' and not @[te vai] &c. But if these particles do not connect these forms they may be used with them; as @[haro hariś ca me svāmī] (Sid. Kau.) Hara and Hari are my lords, &c.

Obs.—(b) The shorter forms may be used in a sentence having one verb; @[śālīnāṃ te odanaṃ pacāmi]; but @[odanaṃ paca tava bhaviṣyati] as there are two verbs.

(c) These shorter forms are not also used immediately after the Vocative case except when a qualifying adjective follows it; @[he hare asmān] (and not @[naḥ]) @[pāhi sadā] (Sid. Kau.) 'oh God! do thou protect us always', but @[he hare dayālo naḥ pāhi] (Sid. Kau.) 'Oh merciful Hari, protect us.'
