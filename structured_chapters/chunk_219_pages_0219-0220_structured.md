@[ु] and @[गु] ( @[कुजो ह्रस्विहलुः] ); @[पाण्डुर] 'possessing paleness,' i.e. 'pale'; @[मधुर] 'sweet,' &c.

214 SANSKRIT GRAMMAR. [§ 343

SECTION III.
AFFIXES FORMING ADVERBS.

@[अक] (@[अकन्])—is inserted before the final vowel of indeclinables without any change of meaning; @[यदा]—@[यदाक]; @[नीचैः]—@[नीचैक].

@[एन]—is added to nouns indicating 'a direction' in the sense of 'not far from'; @[पूर्वेण ग्रामम्] 'to the east of the village not far from it'; @[अपरेण ग्रामम्], &c.

@[तस्] (@[तसिल्])—has the sense of the Ablative; @[आदितः] 'from the beginning', @[मध्यतः], @[स्वतः], @[पुरतः] &c.; sometimes this is added in the sense of the Gen.; @[देवा अर्जुनतोऽभवन्] 'the gods declared themselves on the side of Arjuna'; (@[सर्वम्])—@[परितः] 'on all sides'; @[उभयतः] 'on both sides'.

@[धा]—@[विधा] 'without', @[नाना] 'in various ways', &c.

@[वत्]—is affixed in the sense of 'equally with' or 'like to' when the equality or likeness refers to an action; @[शतेन बलेन शतवदागच्छति]; but @[शतेन तुल्यं बलम्] and not @[शतवद्बलम्]; similarly @[अश्ववत्]; or (2) 'as in the case of that' @[मथुरावासिव तत्र सर्वे वसन्ति]; @[देवदत्त इव कृष्णः] (@[भक्ष्यं करोति];)—(3) @[विधिमति विधिवत् पूज्यते].

@[शः]—@[अल्पशः] 'little by little' @[स्वल्पशः] &c.

@[स्यम्]—is added to nouns or indeclinables to express that a person or thing, not being like what is denoted by the base to which it is added, becomes or is made like it.
