§ 217. Additional instances of the @[aluk] of the Loc.

(a) @[aluk] is necessary in the case of the words—(1) @[gau] and @[yudh],
followed by @[sthira]; as @[gavi-sthiraḥ], "stationary in the sky, @[yudhi-sthiraḥ]
firm in battle; (2) @[hṛd] and @[div] followed by @[spṛś], @[hṛdi-spṛk], @[divi-spṛk]
(@[hṛdayaṃ divaṃ ca spṛśatīti]); (3) @[anta] and @[madhya] followed by @[śara], @[madhye-
śaraḥ], @[ante-śaraḥ] and (4) words expressive of parts of the body
except @[mūrdhan] and @[mastaka] and not followed by @[kāma]; @[kaṇṭhe-kālaḥ],
@[uro-jaraṭhī] (having the hair on the breast: Bah.), but @[ūrdhva-mastaka-
śikhaḥ], @[yasya kāmaḥ asya akṣa-kāmaḥ].

(b) * When a noun in the Loc. is compounded with a verbal
derivative and the whole is a name, @[aluk] takes place in most
cases; e. g. @[stambe-ramaḥ] (@[stambe] a kind of grass @[ramate asau]) an elephant;
@[karṇe-japaḥ] one who whispers into the ear, a spy; @[khe-caraḥ] a heavenly
being; @[jale-jaḥ] a lotus; @[kuca-kumbhaḥ], @[jale-śayaḥ] a fish; but @[kuru-kṣetraḥ],
@[saraḥ-jam] or @[sarojaḥ] &c.

(c) There is optional @[aluk] of the Loc. of a word expressive of
time when compounded with @[kṛta] and words ending in the
affixes @[aka], @[tana] and @[ima]; @[vasante] or @[vasanta-kṛte]; @[ahar-ahni] or @[ahar-ahnīya-tane].

(d) ¶ When the compound is not a name, @[aluk] is necessary
when @[ja] comes after @[hemanta], @[śarad], @[grīṣma] and @[divas]; and optional
when it comes after @[varṣa], @[māsa], @[ahar] and @[śara]; as @[prātar-hemanta-śarad-divas-jaḥ]; @[varṣe-jaḥ] or @[varṣa-jaḥ] produced in the rainy season, &c.

Exceptions:—No @[aluk] takes place in the case of verbal deri-
vatives ending in @[in] and the words @[siddha], @[baddha], @[kṛta] and @[stha] in the
ordinary language, as @[sthaṇḍila-śāyī] 'an ascetic,' @[sāgara-tat-siddhaḥ],
@[cakra-baddhaḥ]; @[samara-sthaḥ]; but @[śaye'sya akṣa-śaye-sthaḥ] &c.
