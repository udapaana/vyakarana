(4) THE PERFECT (@[लिट्])

§ 488. There are two kinds of Perfect, Reduplicative and
Periphrastic.

§ 489. The Reduplicative Perfect is formed of all mono-
syllabic roots beginning with consonants, as well as of those
beginning with the vowels @[अ] or @[आ], and @[इ], @[उ], @[ऋ] short.

Exceptions—@[अच्], @[अय्], @[कास्], @[गास्]. These take the Periphrastic
Perfect necessarily.

§ 490. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed of all roots
beginning with any vowel except @[अ] or @[आ] which is naturally or
prosodially long, and of roots of more than one syllable (roots
of the 10th class and other derivative roots included). Excep-
tions:—@[ज्यय्] and @[ऋरच्छ्] which take the Reduplicative Perfects.

§ 491. The roots @[ऊष्], @[विद्], @[ग्रह्], @[ग्लै], @[मी], @[री], @[ली], @[हु], and @[दरिद्रा] take
both the perfects.

§ 494. The Parasmaipada singular terminations are strong;
the rest weak. Before the strong terminations the penultimate
short vowel takes its Guṇa substitute. The final vowel and
penultimate @[अ] take Vṛddhi necessarily in the third and
optionally in the first person singular. In the 2nd person
singular the final vowel takes Guṇa and the penultimate @[अ]
remains unchanged.

§ 495. Special rules about the admission of the intermedi-
ate @[इ] before the terminations @[व], @[म], @[थ], @[से], @[वहे], @[महे], @[ध्वे]; see (§ 457).

(a) All roots, whether @[सेट्] or @[अनिट्], except @[छु], @[जि], @[हि], @[ध्यै], @[स्तु],
@[रु], @[शु], and @[षु] admit it. But @[वि] with @[उप], and @[स्तु] admit it before
@[थः]; as @[ससंचकरिथ], @[तस्तरिथ].

(b) Anit roots ending in short @[ऋ], except @[ऋ], reject it
before @[व]; as @[सस्मर्व] from @[स्मृ]; but @[आरिव] from @[ऋ].

(c) Anit roots with a final vowel or with a penultimate @[अ]
admit it optionally before @[म].

§ 496. When initial @[इ] or @[उ] of a root takes Guṇa or Vṛddhi
substitute, the reduplicative @[इ] or @[उ] is changed to @[इय्] or @[उव्];
otherwise the two vowels combine to form long @[ई] or @[ऊ]; as
@[इ] + @[एष] + @[अ] = @[इयेष], @[उ] + @[ओष्] + @[अ] = @[उवोष]; @[ईष], @[ऊष], etc.

@[इव] = @[निन्य] + @[इव] = @[निन्यिव]. @[रिक्त] + @[इरे] = @[रिक्तिन्यिरे], etc.
