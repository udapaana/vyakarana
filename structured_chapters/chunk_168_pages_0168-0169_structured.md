§ 265. The final @[ā] is optionally shortened before @[ka] as @[bahumālāḥ-mālakāḥ-mālakāḥ], &c.

§ 266. * No @[ka] is affixed:

(a) when the whole compound is used as a name or when it ends in @[īyas], as @[viśve devā yatra te viśvadevāḥ] 'whose deities are the Viswadevas', @[bahavaḥ bhartāraḥ yasyāḥ sā bahubhartā]. A Bah. ending in @[īyas] (e. g. @[śreṣṭha]) does not shorten its final vowel; e. g. @[bahavaḥ śreṣṭhāḥ yasyāḥ sā bahuśreṣṭhā] 'one having many excellent wives'. But @[atiśreṣṭhaḥ] as a Tatpurusha.

(b) to the word @[bhrātṛ] preceded by a term of praise. @[prakhyātaḥ bhrātā yasyā sā prakhyātabhrātā]; but @[mūrkhabhrātṛkaḥ] one whose brother is a fool.

(c) to the words @[nāḍī] and @[tantrī] both referring to the body of an animal; @[bahunāḍīḥ kāyaḥ] 'the body having many arteries'; @[bahutantrī grīvā] 'the many-veined neck'; but @[bahurekhaḥ stambhaḥ] 'a pillar with many ornamental lines'; @[bahutantrī] (many-stringed) @[vīṇā].

(d) to the final of the compound @[niṣpravaṇiḥ] (@[nirgatā pravaṇī] 'a weaver's shuttle' @[yasya]) @[vastraḥ] 'a new, unbleached garment.'

(e) to compounds formed according to rules §§ 251, 252, and 253; as @[saputraḥ], @[upabahavaḥ], @[pratyavekṣakaḥ], &c.

IV. Avyayībhāva or the @[avyaya] Compounds.

§ 268. An Avyayībhāva comp. consists of two members the first of which is, in most cases, an indeclinable (a preposition or an adverb) and the second a noun, the whole being treated like the Nom. sing. of neut. nouns, the comp. so formed is indeclinable; e. g. @[anuhari] 'in Hari'; @[upaparvataṃ] 'in a mountain', &c.

§ 269. In forming the Avyayībhāva compounds the following rules should be observed:—

(a) The final long vowel is shortened, and the ending @[ā] or @[ī] is changed to @[i] and @[ū] to @[u]: @[upāyāti gaḥ strīti vā upagoḥ]. @[upamakṣiketi upamakṣikam], @[upayajñam] near a sacrifice, @[upagavi] near a cow, &c.

(b) The final @[n] of mas. and fem. nouns in @[an] is dropped; and optionally that of neuter nouns ending in @[an]; @[uparājā]; @[upacakravāku-am].
