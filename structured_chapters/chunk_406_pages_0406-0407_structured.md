§ 662. When the sense of @[अत्यन्ताहार] i. e. doing what is not proper for one to do, or the exchange of duties is to be implied, the Atm. is used. @[ब्राह्मणः सस्यानि वर्धयते] 'the Brāhmaṇa reaps corn' (which is the work of a Śūdra and not his); @[धर्मः व्यातिष्ठते] 'the religious duty is changed' (as when a Śūdra discharges the duties enjoined upon a Vaiśya, &c.); @[संप्रहरन्ते राजानः] 'the kings exchange blows.'
