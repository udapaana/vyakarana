§ 225. Two adjectives may also be compounded into what is called the @[विशेषणपूर्वपदकर्मधारय] as @[शुक्लकृष्ण], @[कृष्णसारङ्ग].

(1). Two participles expressive of two consecutive actions of the same individual may be compounded together, the one expressive of the previous action being placed first, @[आदौ स्नातः पश्चादनुलिप्तः] = @[स्नातानुलिप्त] 'first bathed and then anointed'; similarly @[पीतवान्तः] 'first drunk and afterwards vomited out' in @[पीतपरिवान्तः], @[गृहीतप्रतिमुक्तः] (Ragh. II. 1. IV. 43), &c.

(2). In the case of the two sets of words given above (§ 222.2.) forming compounds with one another those beginning with @[पूर्व] are placed first. In the case of @[एक] compounded with @[वीर] it may precede or follow @[वीर]; as @[वीरैक] or @[एकवीर]; the former, however, is preferable. In the case of the first set of words @[viz.] @[एक]..., @[केवल] the one succeeding precedes the one preceding when compounded together; as @[पुराणजरत्], @[केवलपुराणम्], &c.

(3). A past participle is compounded with another with the negative particle prefixed to it; @[कृताकृतम्], 'what is done and not done, i.e. 'partially done'.

(4). The word @[युवन्] (m. f.) enters into a Karm. comp. with the words @[खलति], @[पलित], @[वलित] having wrinkles, and @[जरती], and is placed first; e.g. @[युवा] + @[खलतिः] = @[युवखलतिः] 'a young

§ 226. The word @[ईषत्] 'little' is compounded with any other word except a verbal derivative, and even with a verbal derivative when it expresses a quality; as @[ईषदुदकम्] 'a little brown', @[ईषद्रक्तः] 'reddish', &c.

§ 227. Words ending in the terminations @[तव्य], @[अनीय], @[य] and @[तुल्य] and words having a similar meaning, may be compounded with any @[subanta] not importing a particular kind; e.g. @[भोज्योदनः] 'any hot eatable', @[तुल्यश्वेतः] 'of a similar white colour', @[तुल्यकृष्णः], &c.; but @[भोज्यः ओदनः] as the latter word refers to a kind of food here.

§ 228. The following are the more important of the compounds given by Pāṇ. as irregular under the class @[मयूरव्यंसकादि]: @[मयूरः व्यंसकः मयूरव्यंसकः] 'a peacock', so @[छागलं महिषं च भवौ च छागलमहिषम्], so @[प्राग्रुचम्] 'high and low', 'great and small', @[निश्रितं च प्रचितं च निष्प्रचम्], @[नास्ति किञ्चन यस्य सः अकिञ्चनः], @[नास्ति कुतो भयं यस्य सः अकुतोभयः], @[अन्यो राजा राजान्तरम्], @[अन्यो ग्रामः ग्रामान्तरम्], @[अत्र चिन्मात्रम्] (these are @[nitya-samāsas]). @[अश्न त पिब त इति एषः सततं वदन्ति सा अश्नीतपिबता] that in which are constantly uttered the words 'eat and drink', so @[खादतपिबता], @[खादतमोदता]; @[अहं अहं इति यस्यां क्रियायामाभिधायते सा अहाहिका], that in which are uttered the words 'I alone; I alone'; hence hard competition, &c. @[अहं अहं एवं इति यस्यां क्रियायामभिधीयते सा अहंपूर्विका]; so @[आहोपुरुषिका] 'great self-conceit or pride' (Bhaṭṭi. V. 27); 'vaunting', (Bv. I. 84) &c. Also @[कान्दिशीकं] 'putting to flight', 'incident', &c.
