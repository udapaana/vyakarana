**Neu. Nom.** @[ekam] **N. A.** The rest like the mas.

@[eka] when it does not mean 'one' is declined in the dual and plural also.

§ 155. The various meanings of @[eka] are:—

@[ekoyam alpe pradhāne ca prathame kevale tathā।]
@[sādhāraṇe samāne 'pi saṃkhyāyāṃ ca prayujyate॥]

@[alpa] or little, @[pradhāna] or chief, pre-eminent, @[prathama] or foremost, @[kevala] or sole, only, @[sādhāraṇa] or common (as @[vyabhicāritve hi āpadāṃ eko hetuḥ]), @[samāna] or the same (as @[ayam ekānvayo mama]), and @[saṃkhyā] or number (one).

§ 156. @[prathama], @[dvitīya], @[tṛtīya], @[ardha], @[katipaya], and words ending in the termination @[maya] optionally form their Nom. plu. like that of pronouns; *e. g.*—@[prathame-prathamāḥ], @[katipaye-katipayāḥ], @[dvitaye-dvitīyāḥ] &c.

**II—Pronominal Adverbs.**

§ 157. The pronominal adverbs that are most commonly met with are derived from the pronouns @[tad], @[etad], @[yad], @[idam], @[kim], and @[sarva] and the pronominal adjectives @[para], @[pura] &c. by means of such suffixes as @[tas], @[tra], @[thā], @[dā] &c. added in the sense of the Ab. or the Loc., @[dā], @[dānīm], @[hi] &c. showing time, @[arha] &c. showing point of time, place or direction, @[ā], @[āt], @[āhi] &c. expressing direction, @[thā], @[vat] &c. expressing way or manner &c.; *e. g.*

@[tad]........................@[tadā], then; @[tadānīm], at that time; @[atha], then, therefore; @[tathā], so, @[tatra], there; @[tataḥ], thence, thereupon, therefore, &c.
@[idam]........................@[idānīm], now; @[ittham], thus; @[atra], here, @[ataḥ], therefore; @[itaḥ], from this, hence; @[adhunā], now; @[iha], here.
@[etad]........................@[etarhi], now, @[evam], thus; @[ataḥ], hence, therefore; @[atra], here.
@[yad]........................@[yathā], as; @[yarhi], when; @[yadā], when; @[yatra], as; @[yataś ca], where; @[yataḥ], whence, since, because.
@[kim]........................@[katham], when; @[kadā], when; @[kutra], how; @[kutra], where, @[kva], where, @[kvacit], where, whence; @[kuha], whence, how.
@[sarva]........................@[sarvadā], always; @[sadā], always; @[sarvataḥ], everywhere, on all sides; @[sarvatra], every-where, in all places.
@[para]........................@[paraḥ], farther on, beyond, &c.
@[pūrva]........................@[puraḥ], @[purastāt], before, in front, &c.
@[adha]........................@[adhaḥ], @[adhastāt] or @[adhastād], @[adharataḥ], @[adharāt], down, below.
@[avara]........................@[avaḥ], @[avastāt] or @[avarastāt], @[avarataḥ], behind, below, downwards.
@[apara]........................@[aparāt], from behind, afterwards, westward, &c.
@[dakṣiṇa]........................@[dakṣiṇā], @[dakṣiṇāt], @[dakṣiṇāhi] to or in the south, on the right side.
@[uttara]........................@[uttarā], @[uttarāt], @[uttarāhi] to or in the north, &c.
