214 SANSKRIT GRAMMAR. [§ 343

SECTION III.

AFFIXES FORMING ADVERBS.

@[aṭ] (@[aṭac])—is inserted before the final vowel of indeclinables without any change of meaning; @[svataḥ]-@[svatāṭaḥ]; @[nityam]-@[nityātam].

@[ena]—is added to nouns indicating 'a direction' in the sense of 'not far from'; @[prācena grāmam] 'to the east of the village not far from it'; @[aparena grāmam], &c.

@[taḥ] (@[tasi])—has the sense of the Ablative; @[ādataḥ] 'from the beginning', @[madhyataḥ], @[svarataḥ], @[uccaistaḥ] &c.; sometimes this is added in the sense of the Gen.; @[devā arjunataḥ abhavan] 'the gods declared themselves on the side of Arjuna'; (@[samantāt])-@[paritaḥ] 'on all sides'; @[abhitaḥ] 'on both sides'.

@[dhā]—@[vidhā] 'without', @[nānā] 'in various ways', &c.

@[vat]—is affixed in the sense of 'equally with' or 'like to' when the equality or likeness refers to an action; @[rājñāā saha vartate yathā icchatyevam eva]; but @[śakuntalam tulyo rāmaḥ] and not @[śakuntalaṃ vad rāmaḥ]; similarly @[ahivat]; or (2) 'as in the case of that': @[mathurāvām iva bhaved anenaiva vārtā]; @[uccaiḥ iva śabdāyet] (@[yasya tathā iti]); (3) @[vidhimati vidhivat pūjyate].

@[śaḥ]—@[alpaśaḥ] 'little by little' @[avaśaḥ] &c.

@[ṣyaṅ]—is added to nouns or indeclinables to express that a person or thing, not being like what is denoted by the base to which it is added, becomes or is made like it.
