## 96 SANSKRIT GRAMMAR. [§ 144-146

§ 144. The affix @[ati] is added to @[ka], @[ya] and @[ki] to show number or measure; e.g. @[kati] 'so many', @[yati] 'as many,' and @[kiyati] 'how many'. These are declined in the plural only, and take no termination in the Nom. and Acc.; e.g. Nom. and Acc. @[kati] Inst. @[katibhiḥ] the rest like @[hari].

8—Reciprocal Pronouns.

§ 145. Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the pronominal adjectives @[anya], @[itara] and @[para]; e.g. @[anyonya], @[itaretara] and @[paraspara]. Those are generally used in the sing. and also adverbially e.g. @[parasparaṃ smṛhanīyaguṇam] &c. (Rag. VII. 14); @[parasparaṃ vivadante] &c. They are more commonly used as the first members of compounds; as @[anyonyaguṇājalanāt] &c. (Ku. I. 44); @[itaretarayogāt] (Sie. I. 24.) &c.
