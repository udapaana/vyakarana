§ 14] Prosody. 27

(1) @[Vaitālīyam],

Def. @[caturdaśamātre same prathamatṛtīye same dvayaṣoḍaśāntarāḥ |
na samātra parābhṛtā kalā vaitālīyamante tu guruḥ ||]

i.e. The first and third quarters of this metre should contain fourteen syllabic instants, and the second and fourth sixteen each; of these the last eight mātras should consist of a @[bharaṇa] (- ∪ ∪) followed by a short and a long syllable (∪ -); the syllabic instants in the even quarters should not be at all composed of short syllables or long syllables; and the even syllabic instant in each quarter (i.e. the 2nd, 4th and 8th) should not be formed conjointly with the next (i.e. 3rd, 5th and 9th).

Ex. @[kuśalaṃ khe tubhyameva tat vacanaṃ kṛṣṇayadoryadhāmadam |
śrīrājyaṃ rāmavaraprada tava svavināśābhimukheṣu sādhavaḥ ||]

(2) @[Aupacchandasikam],

Def. @[prayante rayo tathaiva roṣamaupacchandasikaṃ sudhībhiraṅktam |]

This is the same as @[Vaitālīya] except that at the end of each quarter there must be a @[ra] Gana, followed by a @[ya] Gana instead of @[ra] and @[gu], i.e. it is the same as @[Vaitālīya] with a long syllable added at the end of each quarter.

Ex. @[śruterdharmeśvaraviracitāsvaupacchandasika hado vinodam |
kaṃsaṃ yo nijaghāna devo vande taṃ jagatāṃ hṛdi sthitaṃ dadhānam ||]
