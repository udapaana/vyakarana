226 SANSKRIT GRAMMAR. [§ 366

@[उप]—'up to, towards, all round, a little,' &c.; @[उपाच्छाद्] 'to cover all round,' @[उपाकारः] 'form or shape (within due bounds),' @[उपान्तः] 'that which shines all round' i.e. 'the ether'; @[उपकम्प्] 'to shake a little' &c.

@[उद्]—'upon,' &c.; @[उद्गम्] 'to go up,' @[उद्योगः] 'industry,' @[उत्सर्गः] 'pouring out, hence a gift, a general rule,' &c.

@[उप]—'near to, towards, by the side of,' &c.; @[उपगम्] 'to go near to,' @[उपकृतिः] 'an obligation,' @[उपरतिः] f. 'death,' @[उपस्थानम्] 'praise, worship,' @[उपमितिः] f. 'comparison,' &c.

@[दुस्]—'bad, hard to be done' &c.; @[दुराचारः] 'bad conduct,' @[दुष्कर] 'hard to be done,' @[दुःसह] 'difficult to be borne,' &c.

@[नि]—'in, into, great, opposed to,' &c.; @[निगर्ह्] 'to insult,' @[निकेतः] 'a house,' @[निचयः] 'a heap, a great collection,' @[निपीत] 'drunk in,' @[निदेशः] 'a command,' &c.

@[निस्]—'out of, away from, without,' &c.; @[निर्गम्] 'to issue out,' @[निर्गमः] 'a passage out,' @[निर्दोषः] 'out of i.e. free from blame,' @[निःसंशयम्] 'without doubt,' &c.

@[परा]—'away, back, opposed to,' &c.; @[पराकृ] 'to reject, to despise,' @[पराक्रम्] 'to act bravely,' @[परागत] 'gone away,' @[पराञ्च्] 'turned back,' @[पराजय] 'what is opposed to victory; defeat,' &c.

@[परि]—'all round, about,' &c.; @[परिधा] 'to place all round i.e. to put on or wear,' @[परिधिः] 'a wall &c. that surrounds,' @[परिपाकः] 'ripening, maturity,' @[परिगणना] 'counting all round, i.e. a complete enumeration,' &c.

@[प्रति]—'towards, back, in return, in opposition to,' &c.; @[प्रतिगम्] 'to go towards,' @[प्रतिवचनम्] 'a speech in return, an answer,' @[प्रतिक्रियाकारः] 'an act in opposition to i.e. a remedy' &c.

§ 366-369] INDECLINABLES. 227

@[वि]—'apart, separate from, reverse to,' &c. @[विचल्] 'to move apart,' @[वियुज्] 'to be separated,' @[विक्री] opp. of @[क्री] 'to sell,' 'to buy' &c.; sometimes this has an intensive force.

@[सम्]—'together with, excellent, full,' &c. @[संगम्] 'to be united,' @[संस्कारः] 'perfection, refinement,' @[संहारः] 'contraction,' &c.

@[सु]—'well, thoroughly,' &c. (in this sense it is opposed to @[दुस्]); @[सुकृत] 'done well,' @[सुशासित] 'thoroughly trained, well governed,' &c. It is also used in the sense of 'very, excessively': @[सुमहत्] 'very great.'

§ 367. Two or more of these prepositions may also be combined and prefixed; e.g. @[अधिसमावस] 'to enter into with resolution,' @[समुपगम्] 'to come in close contact with,' &c.

§ 368. When used in a compound the verb may be omitted after the prepositions @[अति], @[अपि], @[अनु], @[अप], @[अव], @[अभि], @[उप], @[परि] and @[प्रति]; @[अतिक्रान्तो नयः अतिनयः] &c. Vide § 232.

§ 369. There are several other words, also styled @[Gati] by Sanskrit grammarians, used prepositionally. These are prefixed to certain roots only. We give the more important of them below.

(a) @[अच्छ]* 'towards' is prefixed to @[वद्] and to roots implying motion; @[अच्छगम्य-गत्य] 'going near,' @[अच्छपतत्] 'flying towards,' @[अच्छोद्य] 'having spoken to.'

(b) (1) @[अन्वाञ्च्] 'giving strength to the weak,' @[आङ्] in the sense of 'decorating,' @[आम्], @[आङ्], @[ओम्], @[स्वाहा] all implying 'assent, acceptance or promise,' @[खाट्] or similar imitative sounds, @[तिरस्] and @[सत्] in the sense of 'dishonouring or honouring,' @[प्राध्व] denoting 'fastening,' &c. are prefixed to @[कृ]; @[अन्वाञ्चीकरणम्], @[आङ्करणम्], @[असत्कारः], @[सत्कारः], @[खाट्करणम्], @[प्राध्वंकरणम्], &c.

* @[अच्छ गत्यर्थवदेषु] | Pan. I. 4. 69.
