(f) Potential Participles (and Verbal Adjectives).

§711. The Potential Participle is formed by means of the affixes @[tavya], @[anīya], @[ya] and rarely @[ṇyat], added to a root or derivative verb. This is Passive when the verb is transitive and impersonal when the verb is intransitive. It is also used like an adjective when denoting fitness, &c.

(1) Participles in @[tavya] and @[anīya].

§712. The affixes @[tavya] or @[anīya] are added to roots or derivative bases in the sense of 'must be, fit to be,' &c. Before these the ending vowel and the penultimate short @[a] of a root take their Guṇa substitute. Before @[tavya] Seṭ roots take @[i], Aṇiṭ roots do not, and Veṭ roots take it optionally.
