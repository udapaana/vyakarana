PARASMAIPADA FREQUENTATIVES.

The Parasm. Frequentative is peculiar to the Veda. Its forms are very rarely to be met with in classical composition.

Formation of the Base.

§ 634. The root is reduplicated according to the general rules of reduplication; the vowels @[ṛ] and @[i] of the reduplicative syllable take their @[guṇa] substitute; and the @[a] of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened; @[dā-dadā-dādā]; @[bhi-ribhi-bebhi]; @[gam-gagam-jāgam]; @[ṛ-ar-ār]; @[vid-vivid-vevid]; @[budh-bubudh-bobudh] &c.

§ 635. The rules § 630 (a) (b) apply likewise to the Parasm. Frequen. Base; @[gā-jagā] or @[jāgā]; @[dah-dadah] or @[dādah]; @[vañc-vavañc] &c.

§ 636. A vowel syllable @[ri] or @[rī] is inserted between the @[a] of the reduplicative-syllable and the radical consonant of roots ending in short @[a] or having it for their penultimate; similarly vowel @[li] or @[lī] is inserted in the case of @[ḷ]; @[vṛt] = @[vavṛt-variṛt] or @[varīvṛt] or @[vavṛt] or @[vavṛt]; @[kṛ-cakṛ], or @[carikṛ] or @[carīkṛ]; @[kalp-cakalp], or @[cākalp] or @[calikolp] or @[calīkalp], @[dṛ] or @[dariḍṛ].
