**436 SANSKRIT GRAMMAR. [§ 723-728**

**By means of the affix @[kyap] (@[ya])**

§ 723. The roots @[iṣ] 4 P. 'to go', @[stu], @[mṛj], @[vṛ] 6 P. @[vṛṇoti],
@[sev], and roots having a short vowel for their penultimate, except @[hṛṣ]
and @[tṛṣ], take the affix @[kyap] (@[ya]) in the same sense as @[yat]. When
a root ends in a short vowel, @[tu] is inserted between the final
vowel and the affix @[ya]; e. g. @[upeyaḥ] 'worthy of being approached,'
@[stotyaḥ] 'deserving praise.' @[śikṣā]—@[śikṣyaḥ] 'fit to be instructed;' @[mṛj]—@[mārjyaḥ];
@[vṛ]—@[vāryaḥ]; @[sev]—@[sevyaḥ] 'worthy of being served,' @[pac]—@[pākyaḥ]; @[bhṛ]—@[bhṛtyaḥ]
'fit to be increased as wealth, &c.' but @[kalpyaḥ] (@[kḷp] + @[yat]) 'able,'
@[vṛddhi]—@[vṛdhyaḥ] (@[vṛdh] + @[ṇyat]) 'that ought to be teased or hurt.'

§ 724. According to Vāmana, the roots @[śaṃs], @[mad] and @[has] take
the affix optionally; @[śaṃs]—@[śasyaḥ] 'praiseworthy,' @[mad]—@[madyaḥ], @[has]—@[hasyaḥ];
optionally they take the affix @[ṇyat] 'to be given hereafter'; @[śaṃsyaḥ],
@[madyaḥ], @[hasyaḥ].

§ 725. @[mṛj] also takes this affix optionally, @[mārjyaḥ] 'what is fit
or ought to be cleansed;' optionally it takes @[ṇyat] before which
the final @[j] is changed to @[g]; @[mārgyam].

§ 726. (a) @[bhū] 'to be' when it has a Subanta prefixed to it and is
without a preposition takes the affix @[kyap] impersonally, @[brahmaṇaḥ]
@[ātmanā] @[bhavitavyam] @[brahmabhūyam] 'identity with Brahman.' When no Subanta is used
prepositionally with it, it takes the affix @[yat]; @[bhavyaḥ] or @[parabhavyaḥ].

(b) @[vad] under similar circumstances takes either @[kyap] or @[ṇyat]
in a passive sense or impersonally; @[vedavādyam] or @[vedavādyam] 'expounding
the Veda' (@[vedaḥ] @[vādyaḥ] @[tasya] @[idam] Sid. Kau.).

§ 727. @[khan] takes this affix, but drops @[vikaraṇa] @[na] and adds @[i] to its
penultimate vowel @[khan] +@[ya] @[kha] +@[ya] = @[kha] + @[ī] +@[ya] = @[kheyaḥ] 'what is fit
or ought to be dug or excavated.'

§ 728. @[bhṛ] 10 cl. takes this affix when the participle so derived
does not form a name, @[bhṛtyāḥ]: 'those who ought to be nourished
or maintained, hence servants,' &c.; but @[bhāryāḥ] (@[bhṛ]+@[ṇyat]) 'a
class of Kshatriyas'; when @[sam] precedes @[bhṛ], @[kyap] and @[ṇyat] are
added optionally @[sambhṛtyāḥ] or @[sambhāryāḥ].
