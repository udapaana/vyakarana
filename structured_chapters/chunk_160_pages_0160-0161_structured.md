§ 253. Names of directions may form a Bahuvrīhi compound and signify the point of direction between them; @[dakṣiṇāyāḥ pūrvāyāśca digoḥ antarālaṃ dakṣiṇapūrvā]; so @[uttarapūrvā], &c. But if the words so compounded are not the names of quarters, no compound is possible; as @[prācyāśca kauveryāśca antarāle dik] the point between the East and the North, and not @[prāgdakṣiṇā] as these are not the names of the Eastern and Northern quarters.
