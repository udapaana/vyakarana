II. Tatpuruṣa or the Determinative Compounds.

§ 200. When a compound consists of two members and the first determines the sense of the other, that is called Tatpuruṣa.

§ 201. According to the grammatical nature of the first or attributive member the Tatpuruṣa is divided into six varieties:— (1) Tatpuruṣa proper or the Inflectional, (2) Nañ or the Negative Tatpuruṣa, (3) Karmadhāraya or the Appositional including 'Dvigu', (4-5) Prādi and Gati or the Prepositional compounds, and (6) Upapada. These are either nouns, adjectives or adverbs.
