§ 21. When @[a] or @[ā] is followed by @[e] or @[o] and @[ai] or @[au] respectively, @[e] and @[o] are respectively substituted for both‡; e.g. @[ca] + @[eva] = @[caiva]; @[deva] + @[aiśvaryam] = @[devaiśvaryam] the majesty of god; @[sā] + @[eva] = @[saiva] she alone, @[bhava] + @[oṣadham] = @[bhavauṣadham] medicine curing birth and rebirth; @[vidyā] + @[autsukyaṃ] = @[vidyautsukyaṃ] longing for knowledge.

Exceptions:—

(a) If a preposition ending in @[a] be followed by a root beginning with @[e] or @[o], @[ai] or @[au] is substituted; @[pra] + @[ejate] = @[praijate] quakes very much; @[upa] + @[oṣati] = @[upauṣati] burns something near; but if the root be a denominative, vṛddhi is optional; @[upa] + @[eḍakīyati] = @[upaipḍakīyati] or @[upaiḍakīyati]; @[pra] + @[odhīyati] = @[praudhīyati] or @[paraudhīyati].
