(5) Participles of the Perfect.

§ 674. The terminations of the Perf. and the Past participles are weak, and therefore the radical vowel does not take its @[guṇa]@ substitute before these. The penultimate nasal is generally dropped (see § 684).

§ 675. The participle of the Perf. Par. and Atm. is formed by adding @[vas]@ and @[āna]@ generally to that form of the root which it assumes before the termination of the 3rd per. plural.
