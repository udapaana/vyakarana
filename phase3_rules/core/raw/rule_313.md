§ 313. Nouns ending in अ and not having य for their penultimate, except हय, गवय a wild ox, शुक्रय, मनुष्य and मत्स्य take ई when 'class' is implied e. g. शूद्री 'A Sudra female' (also the wife of a शूद्र, see § 311. above); similarly माद्रवी, महाराष्ट्री, &c., हरिणी, दृष्टी, औरणी 'a woman of the औपव class of Brāhmaṇas,' कटी 'a woman of the कट class of Brāhmaṇas', &c., हयी; गवयी, शुक्रयी, मनुषी, and मत्सी (vide § 306. d.); but देवदत्ता 'a woman of that name'; अश्वा as it is one of the words included in the Ajādi group; (see § 307. and foot-note); शूद्रा 'a woman of the Śūdra class (see § 307 above).'

(a) Words ending in पाक, कूर्च, पर्ण, पुष्प, फल, मूल and वाल form their fem. in ई when kind is implied; कोदनपाकी, शङ्कुकूर्ची, कालपर्णी, शिंशुपर्णी, दाशीफली, दर्भमूली and गोवाली all names of particular herbs.

(b) Also words ending in त and denoting mankind; दाक्षी 'a woman of the दाक्षि family,' औदमेयी (उदमेयगणपठितम्), but तिमिनिः.
