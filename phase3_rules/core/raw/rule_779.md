§ 779. There are no articles in Sanskrit corresponding to
the English Definite and Indefinite articles. The words कश्चित्
and एक, however, are often used in the sense of 'a certain' and
the numeral एकः, i. e. as equivalent to 'one;' एकस्य नरस्य a certain
man; एकः पान्थः a traveller, स राजा the king, &c.
