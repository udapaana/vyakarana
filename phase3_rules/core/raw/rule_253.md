§ 253. †Names of directions may form a Bahuvrīhi compound and signify the point of direction between them; दक्षि-णस्याः पूर्वस्याश्च दिशोऽन्तरालं दक्षिणपूर्वा; so उत्तरपूर्वा, &c. But if the words so compounded are not the names of quarters, no compound is possible; as ऐन्द्याश्च कौबेर्याश्चान्तरालं विन्दु the point between the East and the North, and not ऐन्द्रकौबेरी as these are not the names of the Eastern and Northern quarters.
