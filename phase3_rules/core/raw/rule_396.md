§ 396. The following seven roots form their bases like the Desiderative in the senses indicated. These are:—किण् to administer medicine, to treat as a patient, चिकीर्षति; तिकित् to censure, जुगुप्साते; तिज् to bear, to forgive, तितिक्षति; विद् to abhor, to act loathsomely, विविक्षति; दीष् to make straight, दीदिषति-ते; मान् to reason, to think, मीमांसते; शिष् to sharpen, शीषाषति-ते. But किण् to desire कैकषति; to dwell, किकीषति; दीष् to cut दीदिषति-ते &c.
