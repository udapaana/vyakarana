§320. Adjectives ending in उ not preceded by a conjunct consonant, except स्वक्, take ई optionally; e.g. मृदुः-मृदुः-दी. पटुः-वी. बहुः-वी:; but स्वकः 'a girl who chooses her husband' (पतिवरा कन्या Sid. Kau.); पाण्डुः and not पाण्डुई as the उ is preceded by a conjunct consonant; आयुः as it is a noun.
