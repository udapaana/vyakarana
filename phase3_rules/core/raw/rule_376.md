§ 376. The following are the principal conjunctions in Sanskrit:—

(a) Copulative अथ, अथो, उत, च, किञ्च, &c.

(b) Disjunctive वा, वा...वा, &c.

(c) Adversative अथवा, तु, किन्तु, किन्वा, &c.

(d) Conditional चेत्, यदि, यदापि, नेत्, नोचेत्, वेद् (used in sacrificial ceremonies), &c.

(e) Causal हि, तत्, तेन, &c.

(f) Interrogative आहो अहोस्विद्, उत, उताहो, कि, किन्नु, किन्तु, किञ्चित्, नञु, नघा, नु, &c.

(g) Affirmatives and negatives अहो, अथ किम्, आम्, अद्धा &c.

(h) Conjunctions of time यावत्—तावत्, यदा, तदा &c.

(i) अथ and इति are used to mark the beginning and the close of a work respectively.
