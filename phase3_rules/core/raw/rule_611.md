§ 611. The Causal base is conjugated like the base of a root
of the 10th or Churādi class in all the ten Tenses and Moods of



the Parasmaipadā, the Ātmanepadā and the Passive. The अय्
with the final अ dropped is retained, as remarked before, in
the General Tenses, except the Aorist and the Benedictive, and
is dropped before the य of the Passive. The formation of the
Aorist of causals has been fully explained along with that of the
roots of the Tenth class, at § 548-556.
