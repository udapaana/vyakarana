§ 285. The finals of compounds having for their first member सु or अति in the sense of 'praise' and द्वि in the sense of 'censure', remain unchanged; सुराजा 'a good king' अतिराजा 'a pre-eminent king': अतिगौ 'an excellent bull' अतिवृष &c: but परमराजः; भाववासय: अतिभुवः; कुत्सितो राजा द्विराजा 'a bad king', द्विवला 'a bad friend'; but द्विराजः, द्विवलः in other cases. This prohibition does not apply to Bah. compounds सुभक्ष्य; सुहृत्.
