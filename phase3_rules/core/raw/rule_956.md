§ 956. ‡ When the sense of 'I expect' is impuded, the
Potential or the 2nd Future may be used provided the word युयूच्
is not used; संभुवुयुयूयूति युयुवुनीत भोक्तुरुनै वा भुवुयुरु I expect you will
eat; but संभुवुयुयूमि युयुदुयु प्रोयुयोरुस्युयुयुयु (Sid. Kau).
