§ 568. दा, धा, and roots assuming the forms of दा and धा (Vide
§ 459) and स्था substitute इ for their final vowel in the Ātm.
This इ does not take its Guṇa substitute. In the Parasm. these
roots take the first Variety (vide § 532).
