§ 933. The Aorist is used with the prohibitive particle युयू
(युुयूडु) or युयू स्युयु, with the temporal augment अ cut off, and has
then the sense of the Imperative; हरिं ते चुन्युयुयुनं युयू युयूदु Me. Bhā V.
132. 16. have nothing to do with that; similarly युयू नोश्युयुयुङुनुयुः Bhā V
Rarely in ancient works the augment is retained युयू विरुयूयुदु
युविनुयुयुनै नयुयूयुयुयुः शुयुयुनुयूदितुयुः समुयुयुः may you not live, oh Nishāda for
many years. When a root is preceded by a preposition, the
अ is sometimes not dropped; युयू युयु यवुवुयूयूस्युयुन्युयूयुयुयुः do not submit to
