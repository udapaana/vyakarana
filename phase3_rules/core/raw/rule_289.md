§ 289. (a) When the first member of a compound ends in इ or उ not capable of changing into इय् or उव् and not belonging to a fem. termination or an indeclinable, the इ or उ is optionally shortened; ग्रामणिपुत्रः or ग्रामणिपुत्रः the son of a leader of a village; &c.; but गौरिपतिः, श्रीमत्, भ्रूकः, शुक्रोपास &c.

(b) But the word भ्रू followed by कृत and कुटि is an exception; भ्रू+कृत्=भूकृतः (कृता कृतिः भासनं कोपया वा यस्य स निषेधवातीं नटकः Sid. Kau.) an actor; भ्रूकुटिः the knit eyebrow; according to some, भ्रू optionally becomes भु when followed by कृत or कुटि: as भकृतः and भुकुटिः (see ft. note).
