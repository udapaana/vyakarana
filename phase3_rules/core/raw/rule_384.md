§ 384. With reference to the various moods the verbs are divided into ten conjugational classes by Sanskrit grammarians, each class being denominated after the root which begins it viz. (1) भ्वादि (2) अदादि. (3) जुहोत्यादि, (4) दिवादि, (5) स्वादि, (6), तुदादि, (7) रुधादि, (8) तनादि (9) क्र्यादि and (10) चुरादि.
