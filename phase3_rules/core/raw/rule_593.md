§ 593. (a) The roots स्तच्, जन्, तन्, and सच्, 'to sprinkle'
drop their न् and at the same time lengthen their vowel;
स्तान्यते, जान्यते, &c.

(b) शी 'to lie down' and चि form their bases as शय् and चय्.

(c) ऊह् shortens its ऊ when a preposition is prefixed.

(d) दरिद्रा, दौषी and वेणी drop their final vowel before य.

(e) हू and अस् substitute हुय् and अस्य् for themselves; यच् and
भञ्ज् also substitute इज् and भज् respectively.
