§ 802. † विश with अविनुति governs the Accusative; अविनुति-
विक्तोहं त्युन्भुयोमुङु he pursues the path of goodness; युन्भुयो सु गुण-
कुदुयोरुकुयो घोयुच्युचु भवन्मुनोऽभिनुविशुते happy is that harlot girl on
whom you have fixed your mind (see Bhaṭṭi. VIII, 80,).
Rarely this governs the Loc. अविनुति सुङु fondly resorts
to sin. विश with a preposition governs the Acc. but with आ
meaning 'to sit' takes the Loc. अत्युयुनेऽविस्युच्युत्युष्टुविच्युक्तुयु sit on this seat.
