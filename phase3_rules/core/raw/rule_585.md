§ 585. दा, यम् and roots assuming these forms, धा, स्था, पा,
मा, 'to drink', पा, 'to abandon' and शो, change their final to ई.
Final या (original or substituted; see § 459), if it be preceded
by a conjunct consonant, is changed to ई optionally: दा—दीयात्,
यम्—यीयात्, पा—पीयात्, निशा—निशीयात्, निर्शीयात् &c.; but पा to
'protect, पायात्.

Ātmanepada.
