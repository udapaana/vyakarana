§ 799. † Intransitive roots govern the Accusative nouns
denoting space or country, or duration of time and distance;
कुच्युच्युच् युरुङु वसति युरुतुदं he stays in the country of the Kurus; तत्रु कति-
पुयुयुम् दिवुसं अवसत् there he dwelt for some days, गोदोहिकुयुयुल्युयु
he sits down till a cow is milked; कोशं प्रतिष्यु he walks for a
Koṣa, कोशो कुट्युटुटुला नदी the course of the river is winding for a
Koṣa; but sometimes factive studies whose length दीर्घुकोशं सत्युच्युयुङुयुदे नरुरु
the hill is situated at a part of a Koṣa.
