§ 231. A Dwigu compound is possible (a) under the circumstances mentioned in § 223 (a) i. e.-

(1) when a Taddhita affix is to be added to the compound so formed, षण्णां मातृणामपत्यं कार्त्तिकेयः: 'the son of six mothers, Kārtikeya' (vide Ku. IX); पञ्चकपालः: &c; or (2) when the compound itself becomes the first member of another compound, as पञ्चगावः पणं यस्य असौ पञ्चगवधनः:, पञ्च: गतिषु: &c

(b) And when the compound denotes an aggregate; in this case it is singular; ‡ as त्रयाणां भुवनानां समाहारः: त्रिभुवनम् the three worlds (taken collectively); as पञ्चगवधम्, पञ्चगवधम्, &c.
