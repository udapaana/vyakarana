§ 653. Before this affix the final अ of a nominal base is
lengthened, आ remains unchanged, other final letters undergo
the same changes as they do before the other य (क्यच्). The
final अ of a noun is changed to आ optionally and that of अन्तवन्



and ओजस् necessarily. कृष्ण इव आचरति—कृष्णायते 'he acts like
कृष्ण;' यशस्—यशस्यते, यशस्यते 'he behaves like one who is
famous,' विद्वत्—विद्वायते, विद्वस्यते 'he acts like a learned man,
&c,; but ओजस्य्-ओजायते 'acts like one who is lustrous' (power-
ful); अप्सरस्-अप्सरायते 'she behaves like an Apsaras.'

(a) A feminine noun not having ऋ for its penultimate drops
its fem. term., कुमारिव आचरति—कुमारायते 'he behaves like a
girl,' द्वैतीय: स्त्रीयरति द्वैतीयायते 'she acts like a female dee. । योषिता
आचरति यष्ट्या 'acts like a stout woman' But पचिकाराया इव आचरति
