§ 617. To this स् the augment इ is to be prefixed after Seṭ
roots, optionally after Veṭ ones and not after those that are
Aniṭ, subject to the following exceptions:—

(1) Roots ending in इ or ई, ऋ and ॠ short, and the roots
मृज् and मृड् do not take इ; इ-इयिष् Vide § 618. (2); ऋ-ईरिष्, &c.

Exceptions:—ऋ 'to go,' वृ Ā. 'to repeat,' वृ 6 Ā. 'to hold,'
and वृ Ā. 'to purify,' admit of इ (see also 4 below)

(2) स्मि, अञ्जर्, भञ्ज्, अर्च् take इ necessarily.

(3) ईड्, वृप्, वृध्, स्पृह् and कृत्स् do not admit of इ in the
Par. (see § 484) In the Ātm. they admit इ, the last two do
so optionally; दाड्-विदृक्षति, विवर्तिषते, &c.

(4) Roots ending in long ऋ and ॠ and the roots हराष्, त्रि.
जड्, ड, ष, ढ, स्, हू, कृष् 'to prosper,' कृष्, भर्ज, इङ् (i. e. गम्, of
the 3rd cl. and optional Cau. Base of ज्ञा), सृ 'to give,' पन्, पुन्,
टुन, चुट्, हुर्, दुर्, and युक् (Vide § 485.) take इ optionally.

Exceptions:—कृ 'to scatter,' पृ 'to swallow,' take इ neces-
sarily The intermediate इ is not lengthened in the case of these
roots; चिकीर्षिष्, &c.

(5) The roots कृष्, गृह् and ढुक् take इ in the Par. and reject
it in the Ātm.
