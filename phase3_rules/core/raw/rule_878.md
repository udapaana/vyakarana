§ 878. * When the action done or suffered by a person or
thing indicates another action i. e. when the time of the happen-
ing of the one action which is known indicates that of the second
action, the Locative Absolute be used; गोयुयु दुयुयुयुनुयुयुयुङु गतुः
he went away while the cows were being milked; अवुरुच्युयुयुयो

whence can there be obstacles to our religious rites when thou
