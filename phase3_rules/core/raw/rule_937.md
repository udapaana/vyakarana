§ 937. † When there is the idea of hope implied in a
conditional form, the Aorist, the Present or the Simple Future
may be used in both the clauses to denote a future time,
दैयुयूयूदुयूरुयूल्युयूदु नुयूति वरुयुत्युयूति वा यायूरुयूयूयूस्युयू बुसुयूयूः वस्युयूयुः वा। (Sid.
Kau.) Ifit were to rain it would it would sow corn.
