§ 114. The penultimate र or उ of the words पिपठिष् wishing
to read, सजुष् m.f. a companion, चिकीर्ष् wishing to do, डहिष्
one stepping well, आशिष् f. a blessing, छतुष्, cutting well, and
also of गिर्, speech, पुर् a yoke, पुर् a town all fem. is lengthened
before consonantal terminations; their final is changed to visarga
in the Nom. Sing. e. g. पिपठिष्—
