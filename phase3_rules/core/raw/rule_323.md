§ 323. The fem. of उशकपि, Vishṇu or Shiva, आर्धि, कुसित or कुसिद 'a usurer' is formed in ई before which the final vowel of these is changed to ऐ; उशकऐपयी, भगायी, कुसितायी or कुसिदायी.
