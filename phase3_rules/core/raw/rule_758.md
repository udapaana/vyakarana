§ 758. Roots of the Tenth class and Causals preserve their
अय before य; it being separated by a short penultimate vowel if
not, it is dropped; चोरय—परचोर्य; बोधय—परबोध्य; कृ—cau. विकारय; नी
cau. with आ—आनाय, &c.; but गृण—विगृण्य, प्रकृपुम्य, प्रकल्पय;
प्रबोविदुर्य 'having again and again caused to be broken.'
