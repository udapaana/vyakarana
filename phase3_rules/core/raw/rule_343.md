§ 343. When added to words denoting qualities मत् is dropped;‡ e. g. शुक्लो रूपोऽस्यास्तीति शुक्लः पटः a white (i.e. possessed of the white colour) garment, so उष्णः &c.

य (यत्)—is added to रूप in the sense of 'a stamped coin, or beautiful'; हिम्यः 'possessing snow, snowy', गुण्यः 'possessing merits'.

यु—is added to ऊर्णा, शुभ, अर्ह, and शं; ऊर्णायुः 'woolen', शुभयुः 'possessing bliss, fortunate', अर्हयुः 'proud'; शंयुः 'happy'.

SECTION III.

AFFIXES FORMING ADVERBS.

अक (अकच्)—is inserted before the final vowel of indeclinables without any change of meaning ; उच्चैः—उच्चकैः; नीचैः—नीचकैः

एन—is added to nouns indicating 'a direction' in the sense of 'not far from'; पूर्वेण ग्रामं 'to the east of the village not far from it'; अपरेण ग्रामं, &c.

तस् (तसि)—has the sense of the Ablative; आदितः 'from the beginning,' मध्यतः, स्वतः, वर्णतः &c.; sometimes this is added in the sense of the Gen.; as देवा अर्जुनतोऽभवन् 'the gods declared themselves on the side of Arjuna'; (तसिल्)—परितः 'on all sides'; अभितः 'on both sides'.

ना—विना 'without', नाना 'in various ways', &c.

वत्—*is affixed in the sense of 'equally with' or 'like to' when the equality or likeness refers to an action; ब्राह्मणेन तुल्यं ब्राह्मणवदधीते; but पुत्रेण तुल्यः स्थूलः and not पुत्रवत् स्थूलः; similarly क्षत्रियवत्; or (2) 'as in the case of that' मथुरायामिव मथुरावत् तत्र प्राकारः; चैत्रस्य इव चैत्रवत् (चैत्रस्य गावः); (3) विधिवद्धोति विधिवत् पूज्यते.

शस्—अल्पशः 'little by little'; बहुशः &c.

च्विन्—is added to nouns or indeclinables to express that a person or thing, not being like what is denoted by the base to which it is added, becomes or is made like it.
