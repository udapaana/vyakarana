§ 444. The first vowel of a root together with the initial consonant, if any, is reduplicated i. e. doubled; as पत्=पपत्, उत्=उउत्, &c. after reduplication.



Note—The first syllable of a reduplicated root is called 'the reduplicative syllable; e. g. the first प in पपत्, or first उ in उउत्.
