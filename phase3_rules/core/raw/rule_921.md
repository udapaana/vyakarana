§ 921. *With interrogatives the Present is often used in the
sense of the Future and has also to be regarded as implying कुयु युयु
i. e. (कुरिष्युयूमि), कं गच्छुयूमि i. e. (गमिष्युयूमि) What shall I do?
Whither shall I go? एतुयूयुयुः कुतुनुयुं or एतुयूयुयुयो किमुयुयुं भोजुनुयुयुरि (i. e. भो-
ज्युयुयुयुयुयुरि or भोजुयूयुयुयूरुयूनुरि) which of these persons will you feed?
so को दु युदुयुयोति युयूयूनुति, &c.; but कुः युयूयुयुनं युनुदुयुयुयूति।

(a) It is also used in the sense of the Future in conditional
sentences as implying a condition of the occurrence of the
desired object; रोकुनं दुयूयूति (रात्युयु द्युयुस्युयूति वा) स स्युयुनै यात्युति (मातु
यास्युयूति वा) he, who offers or (will offer) food, goes (or will go)
to heaven i. e. if one offers &c.
