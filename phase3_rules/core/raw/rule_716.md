§ 716. The roots हस् 'to laugh at,' भाष् 'to kill,' पृच् 'to
go on,' or 'ask,' यत् 'to strive,' जय्, भक् and शक् take the
affix य; हस्यं 'what is fit to be laughed at,' भाष्यं 'what ought to
be killed;' &c.
