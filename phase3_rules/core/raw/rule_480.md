§ 480. इ is substituted for the ending स of a root when followed by any non-conjugational termination beginning with a conso-

---

## Footnotes

* These terminations, it will be seen, are obtained by adding to the Present tense स्य or स्य the suffixes mentioned before which ष् and य are dropped before a vowel.

† These are obtained similarly by adding the terminations of the Imperfect to स्य the usual rules of Sandhi being observed.
