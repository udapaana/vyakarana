§ 111. Obs. The सा of उक्थशास् uttering hymns, becomes स before the consonantal terminations, except that of the Nom. sing.; e.g. Nom. उक्थशाः sing. उक्थशासौ dual.; Instr. sing. उक्थशासा; dual उक्थशोभ्याम्; Loc. plu. उक्थशा:सु.

                Irregular bases.
