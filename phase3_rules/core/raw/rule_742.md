§ 742. A few transitive roots take the affix एलिमुर् having
the same force as the affix यत्; पचेलिमः fr. पच्, 'fit to ripen or to
be cooked; as पचेलिमा भावनः; भिदेलिमाः (fr. भिद्) स्तम्बाः 'the pine
tress ought to be felled,' &c.
