§ 136. The following couplet ( Kārikā ) gives a rule for the correct use of these pronouns:—

इदमस्तु सन्निकृष्टं समीपतरमभति चैतदो रूपम् ।
अदसस्तु विप्रकृष्टं तदिति परोक्षे विजानीयात् ॥

इदम् is used with reference to a person or thing near at hand, and एतद् to one nearer still; अदस् is used of a person or thing at a distance, while तद् is used of one that is absent.
