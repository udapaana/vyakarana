§ 328. If a Bah. end in ऊधस् it takes the suffix ई in the fem. before which न् is substituted for the final अस्; पीन ऊधः यस्याः सा पीनोधनी having a large udder; कुण्डोधनी (see Rag. I. 84.); also when it is preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable;

अद् बृहती, अत्यती having an udder exceedingly large; but ऊघः अतिकान्ता अत्यघा.

(a) Similarly a Bah. ending in द्वायन्, and हायन् denoting age, and having a numeral for its first member takes ई in the fem.; द्विहायनी, त्रिहायनी 'a girl two years old;', त्रिहायणी, &c; but द्विहायना शाला a building built two years since.

N. B.—the य of हायन् is changed to ण after द्वि and बहुर् only when it takes ई; बहुहैयणी बाला; but द्विहायना, बहुहैयना शाला.
