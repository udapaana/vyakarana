§ 89. (a) After final न् the सु of the loc. is changed to षु.
(b) र् ( optionally changeable to र् ) may be optionally inserted between न् and सु.

कमल् mas. fem. neu. naming the goddess of wealth or a lotus कमले कमलौ वा आवसताम्:—वा—पौ—वा )—

Decline similarly सुगण्, सुगणन्, m. f. one who counts well, द्वार् f. a door and other words ending in र् or न्. The loc. plu. of सुगण् is सुगण्षु-पटसु,-पट्षु. The Nom. sing. of द्वार् is द्वा:.
