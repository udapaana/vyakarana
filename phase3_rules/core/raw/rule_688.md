§ 688. न* is substituted for त् when it immediately follows a
final इ or ऋ; this final इ is also changed to ई; शि—शीन, ऋ—ऋण;
पुल्—पूर्ण (See § 693).

Exceptions:—

(a) शीग् has शीतं when it means 'a part, a portion,' शीनं in
other senses.

(b) विङ् 6. P. A. takes त् in the sense of 'fit for enjoyment or
famous,' विक्तः 'wealth, property,' विकः 'famous'; विनः in other cases.

(c) भृङ्, पूर् and चुस्छु; भृतः, पूर्णः 'filled,' (also पूतः when it means
the same as अत्), चुस्छुः.
