§ 735. Roots ending in ऋ, and those ending in a consonant
take the affix घ्यत् (य) in the same sense as क्यप्. Before this affix
the ending चु and व of a root are changed to ऋ and यु respecti-
vely and the final vowel and the penultimate अ take Vṛiddhi
substitute; any other penultimate vowel generally takes Guṇa.

कृ—कार्यं 'what ought to be done,' धृ—धार्यं 'what ought to be
worn,' दा—देयः; हृ—हार्यं; पद्—दृदु—पार्यः 'what is to be thought of,' &c.;
वच्—वाच्यं 'what is meant in a sentence,' पच्—पाच्यं 'what is to
be cooked,' वुज्—भोज्यं 'what is to be purified,' &c.
