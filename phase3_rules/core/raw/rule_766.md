§ 766. The gerund in अम् of the root कृ is used*

(a) with the noun governed by it, if it be compounded with
it prepositionally when censure is implied; चौरुकारुते आक्रोशुते
(चौरुकृत्युय उत्युचिसुथुर्येः) 'cries out (he is) a thief, a thief.' In this
case य is added to the noun governed.

(b) with the words स्वाद्, छुण, and संस्यद्, य being added to
these as before; आस्वादुकारु स्वादु छत्युवा पुकैं स्वादुकारुणुकारुं-संस्यदै—कारुं पुकैं
'he eats having sweetened or seasoned his food.'

(c) with the words अत्युया, एव, किंयुं, and कुथुं, provided the
root य takes its sense; अत्युयाकारुं हुत्वो 'he speaks in a coherent
manner;' एवुं कृत्वा the acts thus;' किंकारुते, कुथंकारुते हुयुभुरामु,
but शिनैत्युयुत्या हुत्वा मुश्चैं.

(d) with the particles युथुं and युथा when an angry reply
is given; युथुमुकारुं शोध्युं युथुमुकारुं शोध्युं किं तवौनैन (Sid. Kau.);
'I will eat in this manner, I will eat in that manner, what
have you do with that?'
