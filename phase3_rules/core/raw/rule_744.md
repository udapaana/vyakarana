§ 744. The Indeclinable Past Participles are of the nature
of gerunds. They fall under two heads:—(1) Those derived
by affixing क्त्वा to the simple root, and (2) Those derived by
means of य affixed to the root compounded with prepositions or
words used prepositionally; गम्—गत्वा 'having gone,' भू with
परिदेवस्त्र 'having' 'having served;' &c.

I. Indeclinable Participles formed with क्त्वा.
