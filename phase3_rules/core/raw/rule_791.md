§ 791. When an adjective is preceded by a word used in
the sense of 'a certain' or when it qualifies an adjective used
as a noun, it is treated as a substantive and generally put in the
neuter; किंचिद्रूपम् something beautiful, किंचिद् गम्भीरम् some-
thing deep, अल्पं गुरु something important, दृढतरम् something very
hard.

(a) But such expressions as सुरूपोऽयं, दृढरूपोऽयं are also
used.
