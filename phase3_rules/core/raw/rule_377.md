§ 377 "The interjection is not properly a part of speech, as it does not enter into the construction of sentences. It is a sudden exclamation prompted by some strong feeling or emotion. There are various utterances suited to the different emotions of the mind":—Prof. Bain.

(a) These are—अः, इ, उ, ए, ऐ, ओ, अहो, अहहो, अहो, बत, ह, हा, हाहा, &c expressive of wonder, grief or regret, &c.

(b) धिक्, धिक् &c. expressive of contempt.

(c) हा, बत, &c. expressing sorrow, dejection, &c.

(d) हा, हाहा, हन्त showing grief.

(e) आ, हुम्, हुम्, &c. expressive of anger, contempt, &c.

(f) हन्त, &c expressive of joy.

(g) There are some interjections used to call attention. Of these,

(1) some show respect; such as अरे, अये, अहो, अहो, बत, उ, ए, ओ, भोसु, भोः, हंहो, हे, हे, हो, &c.

(2) and others, disrespect or contempt; as अड, अरे, अबे, रे, रेरे, अरेरे &c.

(3) The exclamations श्रौषट्, वौषट् and वषट् are used when offering oblations to gods or manes; and

(4) स्वाहा and स्वधा when offering oblations to gods and manes respectively.
