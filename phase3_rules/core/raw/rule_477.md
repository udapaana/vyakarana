§ 477. गै is substituted for अञ्च् 1 P. 'to go' necessarily before any non-conjugational termination, and optionally before such as begin with any consonant except य; एता, अञ्चिता; एताम, अञ्चियाम &c.

(2) Second Future (लुट्) and (3) Conditional (लृङ्).
