§ 760. Desiderative bases add य immediately and Freq.
bases add it after dropping their य, when it is preceded by a
consonant and dropping the final अ when it is preceded by a
vowel; बुबू—परबुबुधिष्य; Fre. परबोबुध्य; भू Fre. परबोभूय &c.
