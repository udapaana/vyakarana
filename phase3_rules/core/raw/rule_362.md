§ 362. That in an Avyaya whose form remaining the same in all the genders, numbers and cases, undergoes no change.* The Avyayas may be divided into simple and compound ones. The latter are treated of in the chapter on compounds (Avyayībhāvas and a few Bahuvrīhis and Tatpurusha).
