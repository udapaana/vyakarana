§ 154. एक when a numeral is declined in the sing. and द्वि in the dual only, द्वि. is considered as द्वि, and they are both declined like सर्व in all the three genders; e g.

एक                             द्वि.
Mas.        fem.        mas        fem. &c. neu.
N.    एकः         एका         द्वौ         द्वे
V.    एक          एके         "          "
