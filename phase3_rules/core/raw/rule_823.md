§ 823. * The roots कुयुयु, द्युट्, ईर्युच्, and असूयु, and others hav-
ing the same sense govern the Dative of the person against whom
the feeling of anger, hatred, &c, is directed; द्रुयुरे कुयुयुस्युति द्युड्युटिङु-
हुयुयुतिश्युयुदुरुतु वा (Sid Kan) 'he is angry with Hari, or bears
hatred towards him, or is jealous of him or finds fault with
him;' रातोयुयु मोङुक्तुयुच्युस्युयुयुयुरुद्युत (Bhatt. VIII 75.) 'he did
neither get angry with Śibā nor find fault with her;' but भार्यु-
मीर्चुति ' he keeps a jealous watch over his wife' (that others
may not see her प्रेमुयुर्ती:असूयुर्त्योऽधेति Sid. Kau)

(a) † But कुयुयु and द्युट् preceded by a preposition govern the
Accusative case; किं मां सकुयुयुयुसि ' why do you get angry with
me?' निरुयुयुयुस्युच्छुटुरीसुर्नुवितुरुयुङु वनते (Mud. I.) 'he always endea-
vours to do harm to my body.'

Obs:—द्युट् with अवि is also found used with the Dat.; मया
स्युयुम्युदुवुट्युस्योयुयुवुरुयुर्नों (Rag. XII. 52.)
