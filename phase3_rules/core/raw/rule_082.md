§ 82. Nouns expressive of relationship like पितृ m. a father,
मातृ f. a mother, देवृ m. a husband's brother, &c. with the excep-
tion of नप्तृ a grandson, भर्तृ a husband and स्वसृ a sister, as also
the words स्तोतृ one who praises (Un. II, 92), नृ a man (Un.II. 98)
and सप्तेतृ a charioteer, do not change their ऋ to आर् in the N.
dual and plural and Acc. sing. and dual; e. g.
