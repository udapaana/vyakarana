§ 37. An Anuswāra followed by any consonant except म्‌, य,
स् or ह् is changed to the nasal of the class to which the follow-
ing letter belongs, necessarily when in the middle, and
optionally when at the end of a word*; e. g. अनुक्+हत:=अं+क्
+हत: (by the preceding rule and then) आङ्कित: marked (by this);
so अनुञ्च+हत:=अर्हित: honoured; छण्डित: obstructed; शान्त:
calmed; युक्तित: woven; त्वम्‌+ करोषि=त्वं करोषि or त्वङ्करोषि thou
doest; similarly संयन्ता or सय्यन्ता a restrainer; संवत्सर: or
संव्वत्सर: a year; यं लोकम्‌ or यल्लोकम्‌ to which people.
