§ 548. Roots of the tenth Class and Causals:—

(a) The अय् of the base is dropped (the vowel changes tak-
ing place before it being reta'ned) and a short vowel substi-



tuted for the long one (ऋ and ऋ being shortened to इ, and औ
and ऐ to उ).

The base so modified is then reduplicated according to the
general rules; शोच् (Cau. B. of छु) =शोच-शुच-शुशोच् after
reduplication; चकम् (from कम्)=कम-चकम्-चचकम्.

(b) इ is substituted for the अ of the reduplicative syllable if
the vowel following it is short, or of the base if the vowel long; the
इ of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened if not followed by
a long syll. or a conjunct consonant; thus चकम्-चिकम्-चीकम्;
चिचित्-चिचित्; त्रकद्-चत्रकद्-चित्रकद्, the इ is not lengthened
as it is followed by a conjunct consonant; स्पुर्-स्पुस्पुर् only,
as प is followed by a syllable prosodially long.

(c) Roots having a penultimate ऋ, short or long, optionally
preserve it, the long ऋ te ng changed to the short one; वृत्
causal base वर्तय-वर्तय after cropping अय and वृत् by this: वर्त्-
चवृत्-चवृत्-चिवर्त्-चीवृत्; कृत्-कोत्तय-कोत्तद् and कृत् by this: कात्-
चिकोत्; कूत्-चाकद्.

(d) To the base so prepared the augment अ is to be prefixed
and the terminations added; अचीकमत् from कम्; अचीकमत् from
कम्; अचीचित् from चित्; अचिवर्त् or अचीवृत्त् from वृत्; अचिकोत्त् from
कृत्; अस्पुस्पुरत् from स्पुर्; अचिवर्त्त्, अचीवृत्त् from वृत्,
अचिकोत्त्,अचीकृत्त्,वृत्;अस्पुस्पुद्,अचुछुदत् from छुद्,&c.

N. B. The Ātm. form in इ when not given should be
supposed to bs understood.
