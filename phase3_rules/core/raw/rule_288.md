§ 288. (a) उदक becomes उद् necessarily (1) when it forms a proper name, also when it is final; or (2) when followed by the words पूर्, वास, वाहन, and धि: उदमेघः 'a particular cloud filled with water'; उदधिः, क्षीरोदः the sea of milk; उडुपोदः &c.; उदवर्षं तिष्ठति, उदवासः standing in water; उदवाहनम्; उदधिः 'a vessel for holding water' घटः, संज्ञाये तु पूर्णेण सिद्धम् Sid. Kau.;

(b) and optionally when followed by a word beginning with a simple consonant and denoting 'a vessel to be filled up with water', and by मन्थ, औदन, सक्तु, विन्दु, वज्र, मार, हार, शीर्षप् 'a yoke with a pan at each end for carrying loads' (काठी Maráthi) and गार; उदकुम्भः or उदककुम्भः; but उदकस्थाली (as स्थाली begins with a conjunct consonant), उदकपाषम्; उदमन्थम् or उदकमन्थम् barley-water; उदौदनः or उदकौदनः rice boiled with water,
