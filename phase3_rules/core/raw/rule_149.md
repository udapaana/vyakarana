§ 149. Obs:—स्व and स्व (the 10th and 11th सर्वादिगणs) the one उद्दात्त (uddātta), the other अनुदात्त (anudātta), both synonyms of अन्य are declined like सर्व. The former is of the form स्वत् according to some and then it is declined regularly by adding the terminations; e. g. स्वत् स्वतौ स्वतः Nom. &c.
