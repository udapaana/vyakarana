§ 924. * When जातु or आचि is used in a sentence and con-
demns or censures or implies that the Present may be used in the
sense of the three tenses; आपि जानुयूयुं स्युयूनुपि जानुयु भुनिकुयुयुयुयूयुनुयूनै
where स्युयुनुपि and आनुयुस्युनै may have also the sense of the Past or
the Future tense; जातु तवुभुवुयुनु दुयूयूनु राजुयुयूनुनं (you will even
make a S'ūdra perform a sacrifice).

IMPERFECT, PERFECT AND AORIST.
