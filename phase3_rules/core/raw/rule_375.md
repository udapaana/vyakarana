§ 375. The following particles are used with certain words—

अह—अद्भुतम् 'a wonder'.

का—कापुरुषः 'a bad man,' कौष्णम् 'luke-warm,' काजलम् 'a little water'.

कु—कुकृत्यं 'a bad deed'.

म—is generally used as an expletive. Used with a form of the present tense of a verb, it gives it the sense of the past tense; as भवति स्म i. e. अभवत्. When used with the particle मा it has an intensive force; as मा स्म शोकं मनः कुर्याः &c.

स्विद्—is added to कि and other indeclinables and asks a question or implies a doubt; किन्विद्, अहोस्विद्, &c.

स्वी—is used with कृ and its derivatives like a preposition in the sense of 'acceptance'; स्वीकारः, स्वीकृतम्, &c.
