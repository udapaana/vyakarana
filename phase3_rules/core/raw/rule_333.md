§ 333 A Bah. ending in पाद् (substituted for पाद ) optionally forms its fem. in ई before which it is changed to पद्; व्याघ्रस्य इव पादौ यस्याः सा व्याघ्रपाद्-पदी, सिंहपाद्-पदी and in आ when पाद् means 'a foot of a Vedic verse'; द्विपदा छन्द्, एकपदा &c.

(a) But if पाद् be preceded by one of the words कुम्भ, शाल, तूण, हंस, काक, शकुन्, सूकर. &c. the fem. is necessarily formed in ई. पाद् changing into पद् as before; कुम्भपदी 'a woman whose feet are as big as a pitcher.'
