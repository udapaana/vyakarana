§ 646. This form of the Nominal verb has not always a
desiderative meaning.

(a) The affix य is added in the sense of 'treating or considering
like;' पुत्रीयति छात्रम् 'he treats the pupil as a son,' विष्णुयति विप्रं
'he treats the Brāhmaṇa like Vishṇu,' कुटीयति कुटिकया भिक्षुः 'the
beggar considers his hut a palace,' कुटीयति प्रासादं नृपः 'the king
in his palace considers himself to be in a hut'

(b) It is added to नमस्, वरिवस् and चिच् in the senses of 'ador-
ing,' 'serving' and 'striking with wonder' respectively, नमस्यति
देवान् 'he adores the gods', वरिवस्यति गुरुं 'he serves the preceptor',
चिच्यीयते लोकान् 'he strikes the people with wonder' It is also
