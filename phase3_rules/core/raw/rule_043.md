§ 43. The षु of सषु is changed to an Anuswāra and Visarga
when followed by a form of the root छ, the Anuswāra again
is optionally changed to an Anunāsika; e. g. सषु+कर्ता=संस्कर्ता
and सँस्कर्ता; षु is affixed to the ending of the words सषु, पुषु and
कासु†; so संस्कर्ता and सँस्कृता.

N. B. The word संस्कर्ता is capable of having 108 different
forms as several sūtras are brought to bear upon it; but they
are not given here as being tedious and of no material value.

Sections a, b, c, and d may be omitted by beginners.

(a) × The षु of पुषु is optionally nasalised and has षु added
to it as above, when it is followed by a hard consonant except
a sibilant, preceding a vowel, a semivowel, a nasal or हु; पुषु+
कोकिल:=पुषु+षु+कोकिल:=पुंस्कोकिल:; पुँस्कोकिल: a male kokila
bird; so पुंस्पुत्र; and पुँस्पुत्र; the son, a grown up man; but पुंसीरषु
milk for a man; पुंदास: a man-servant. Exception पुंस्त्वानषु the
account of a man
