§ 25. When य or ओ at the end of a word is followed by अ
the latter merges into the former § and the sign s is sometimes
written in its place; e. g. हरे + अय = हरेऽय protect, Oh Hari;
विष्णो + अय = विष्णोऽय.

(a) †If the word गो, ending in ओ, be followed by अ, the अ
is optionally retained, and if by a vowel, अव is optionally
substituted for ओ; गो + अग्र = गोग्र, गोऽग्र and गवाग्र a
multitude of cows or chief among cows; but if it be followed by
इन्द्र and अक्ष it necessarily changes its ओ to अव; गो + इन्द्रः =
गवेन्द्रः an excellent bull; गवाक्षः a lattice-window, similarly.
