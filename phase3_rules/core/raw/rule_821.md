§ 821. ‡The roots स्तुयुयुष् 'to praise,' गुट् 'to hide,' स्था 'to stand,'
and मुयु 'to swear,' govern the Dative of the person to whom
some object or feeling is to be conveyed, गोर्नों स्युसुयुद् कृयुयुयुयु
श्युयुरुते-नूनं-कृष्णुं-स्तुरीयु 'a cowherd; else Kṛṣṇa instilled courage into
flatters 'Kṛṣṇa or apparently conceals her feeling from him
or waits for him or swears before him;' (Vide Bhatti. VII. 73.
74,); but रानानं स्तुयुयुते मन्त्रो 'a minister praises his king.'
