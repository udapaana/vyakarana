§ 587. The Ātm. terminations, are strong. Before these
radical vowel takes its Guṇa substitute, but when the inter-



mediate इ is not prefixed to the terminations, the final ऋ
remains unchanged and ऋ is changed to ऋर्, or to उर् if a labial
or व् precedes. चि—चेषीष्ट, धु—धोषीष्ट, लृ—लिरिषीष्ट, स्तृ—स्तरिषीष्ट, or
स्तीरिषीष्ट, वृ—वुरिषीष्ट or वूरिषीष्ट, &c.
