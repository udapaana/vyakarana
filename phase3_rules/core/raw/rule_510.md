§ 510. The अ of the roots गम्, हन्, जन्, सद् and घस्, is
dropped before vowel weak terminations except those of the
2nd variety of the Aorist; the ह् of हन् is then changed to घ्;
and जन् and घस् become ज्ञ and क्ष् respectively.
