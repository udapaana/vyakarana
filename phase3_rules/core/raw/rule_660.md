§ 660. There are several roots* which are also Pratipadī-
kas and which may be regarded as Nominal verbal bases derived
from those Pratipadikas by means of the affix य (यङ्). The
more important of these are given below:—

दयुष-दयुषति-ते he scratches,   सुशूष्-सुशूष्यति he worships,
मन्द-मंदयति he offends against,   निवञ्ज-निवञ्जयति treats medically,
  he becomes angry, also   इषुध-इषुध्यति it contains arrows,
  मंदयते according to चन्द्र.  गद्द—गद्दयति he stammers,
क्ला—क्लायति is delighted; कैल, सैल-कैलायति, सैलायति धुसा
  once, mild or gentle, honours  he sports ?, he becomes merry,
अङ्ग—अङ्गयति-ते (शुस्यति) he is कुप्णि—कुप्णायति he is angry or
  jealous, he envies, &c.      feels ashamed.

## Footnotes

* In the Kaumadi these roots are classed separately under
the head of 'कुपुत्रादिगण' or the group of roots beginning with कुपुत्र.



          (h) No affixes added to certain nominal bases.
