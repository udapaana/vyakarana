§ 917. * The infinitive with the final युु dropped is joined with
the nouns कुयुयु and युयुनुयुदुयु to form an adjectival compound mean-
ing 'wishing or having a mind to do any thing;' युयुयूयुदुयुस्युयु
युविपुयूदुकुयूयुनं विद्युयुनं मतुयुनुयुः &c. (Rag. V. 18) the disciple of the
great sage was desirous of returning &c.; युयुयुङु जुनुयुः यदुयूयुयूस्तुरोयुयुजुनै
(Kum. V. 40) this person has a mind to ask you a question.
