§ 681. The affix त् is weak.

Exceptions:—

(a) The roots नी, विद् 1 cl., दिश्, विष्टर्, प्रुष् and भृष् admit of
the Guṇa change before त् when इ is prefixed to it; या I. A also
does the same when it takes इ (see § 686b),



(b) Such of the roots of the 1st class as have उ for their
penultimate change their vowel to Guṇa optionally, when the
affix त् is added with the Intermediate इ, and in P. P. Parti-
ciple as used impersonally, or when it conveys the sense of
beginning to perform the action or undergoing the condition
expressed by the root; मुद् 'to delight' मुदित, but 'to begin to
delight' मुदित or प्रमुदित: म्हुदित or प्रमोदित रामयुः: प्रहुद्दितः or
प्रमोदितः मायुः, &c. similarly; बुद-प्रबुदित or प्रबोदित &c.
