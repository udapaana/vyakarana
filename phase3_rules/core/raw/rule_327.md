§ 327. A Bahuvrīhi ending in अन् forms its fem. in आ optionally; as श्र+पर्वन्=श्रपर्वन्-नी, बहु+भजन्=न्जा, &c. but if the word ending in अन् be such as drops its अ before the आ of the Inst. sing. it takes ई also optionally; e.g. बहु+राजन्=बहुराजन्-राजा-राज्ञी &c.

(a) If a Bah. ends in वन्, the न् is optionally changed to र्; as बहुपूर्ववन्-बहुपूर्वा or बहुपीवरी a town in which there are many fishermen.
