§ 490. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed of all roots beginning with any vowel except अ or आ which is naturally or prosodially long, and of roots of more than one syllable (roots of the 10th class and other derivative roots included). Exceptions:—ऊड् and चक्ष् which take the Reduplicative Perfects.
