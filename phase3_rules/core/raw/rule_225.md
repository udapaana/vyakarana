§ 225. Two adjectives may also be compounded into what is called the विशेषणोभयपदकर्मधारयः as शुक्लकृष्णः, कृष्णसारंगः.

(a). * Two participles expressive of two consecutive actions of the same individual may be compounded together, the one expressive of the previous action being placed first; आदौ स्नातः पश्चादनुलिप्तः स्नातानुलिप्तः 'first bathed and then anointed'; similarly पीतोद्गीर्णश 'first drunk and afterwards vomited out' पीतप्रतिबद्धः, पहीतप्रतिष्ठक: ( Rag. II. 1. IV. 43 ), &c.

(b). In the case of the two sets of words given above ( § 222.g. ) forming compounds with one another those beginning with पूर्व are placed first. In the case of एक compounded with वीर it may precede or follow it; as वीरैक: or एकवीर:; the former, however, is preferable. In the case of the first set of words viz. एक......केवल the one succeeding precedes the one preceding when compounded together; as पुराणनवत्, केवलपु-राणम्, &c.

(c) † A past participle is compounded with another with the negative particle prefixed to it; 'कृताकृतम्,' what is done and not done i. e. 'partially done'.
