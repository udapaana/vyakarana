§ 819. * The primary sense of the Dative case is Sampra-

---

*चुत्युर्युर्नों संप्रदुनं Pāṇ II. 3 13. कुर्मुणा यसुप्रुधेहि स संयुत्युन्युष्
Pāṇ. I. 4. 32, किंयुयुयु यसुप्रुधेहि सोपि संयुत्युन्युष् Vārt.



dāna (संयुदुत्युनं). The indirect object of the root दा is called
Sampradāna; also the person or thing with reference to whom
or which an action is done; हस्तुयं वुरुयुति हरिं देव: Give water to
a Brāhmaṇa; शुद्युयु संगुद्युते 'prepares for battle;' न ब्रुदुयुयु भुर्तो
दुयुयुयुट् 'The Śudra should not be instructed in the Veda, &c'

*But in the case of the root युच्च् the proper object is put in
the Instrumental case and the indirect object in the Accusa-
tive; यड्युयुनु रुदुरं यजुते 'he sacrifices a bull to Rudra.'

Note:—Though the root दा ought to govern the Dat of the
indirect object, it is sometimes found used with the Gen. or the
Loc. यस्य त्युत्युर्नों दुवुयुने वै वुदुरुङ्युयुट् सर्युकैचुरु 'Oh sky-wanderer, we
will give rain at the prosperous kingdom of the Śibis;' मुस्युवं रामं
मुधिविरी दाद्युयुरिकुच्छुटुति 'thou who art thinking of bestowing the earth
upon Rāma,' &c.
