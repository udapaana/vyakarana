§ 676 Roots ending in ऋ (ऋ and लृ included) form their
Perf P. irregularly; वत् is added to the root, which then
undergoes the changes given in § 394, and in § 537 a, which are
plicated· when a root is Ātम. it is first reduplicated and आन
afterwards added, the final ऋ changing as before; e g. कृ+वत्=
कृतुवत्, by reduplication चिकृतुवत्; कृ—by reduplication चकृ+आन
=चक्रुआन; similarly कृ—कृतुवत्, चिकृतुवत्; तितीर्ष; तितीर्ष्वात्; थु—तिथिवत्र् तथिति-
वत्, तीथुरिवत्, पश्यावत्, &c.
