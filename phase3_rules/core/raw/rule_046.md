§ 46. Visarga, followed by च, छ, ट, ठ and त्, थ, them-
selves not followed by a sibilant, is changed to श्* e. g.; विष्णु-
स्चाता Vishnu, the protector: हरिश्चरति, Hari walks; रामश्टङ्कते
Rāma goes; ( Vide § 23 ); but क: स्त: ( as the त is followed
by स् ); and optionally when followed by स, ष or श †; राम: स्यात्
or रामस्स्यात् Rāma who would stand; हरि: शेते or हरिश्शेते &c.

(a) Visarga, not belonging to an indeclinable is changed
to स् when preceded by अ, and to ष, when preceded by इ or उ,
short or long, and followed by the terminations पाश, कल्प, क
and काम्य ‡; e. g. पयस्पाशं Bad milk; यशस्कल्पम् little short of
fame; यशस्कं with fame; यशस्काम्यति he desires fame; but प्रात:-
कल्पम् almost dawn ( where प्रात: is an indec. ); सर्पिष्पाशम् bad
ghee, सर्पिष्कल्पम्, सर्पिष्कं, सर्पिष्काम्यति. If the visarga, how-
ever, is a substitute for a final र्, it is not changed to स् be-
fore काम्य§; गी: काम्यति speech desires.

(b) x The Visarga of नम: and पुर: when prefixed as indec-
linables to verbs beginning with क or ख and प or फ is
optionally changed to स् in the case of the former and neces-
sarily in that of the latter e. g. नमस्करोति or नम: करोति, पुरस्करोति
puts in the front; but पुर: प्रवेश्या: towns fit to be entered ( as
पुर is a noun ).
