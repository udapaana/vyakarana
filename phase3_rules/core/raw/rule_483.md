§ 483. गम् P. हन् and Aniṭ roots ending in अ admit इ in the Second Future and the Conditional; गम् P. (also गा substituted for इ 'to go' and अध्यै with अधि 'to remember') also admits it in the Desiderative. गमिष्याम, हनिष्याम, करिष्याम, &c. Sec. Fut. 1 sing. अगमिष्यम्, अकरिष्यम्, अहनिष्यम्, &c. Cond. 1 sing.
