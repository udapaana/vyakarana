§ 109. The Nom. sing. of अनेहस् time, पुरूहस् name of Indra, and उशनस् name of Śukrāchārya is अनेहा, पुरूहा and उशना respectively; the Voc. sing. of उशनस् is उशनस, उशन् and उशन; the other forms are regular.
