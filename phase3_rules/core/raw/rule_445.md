§ 445. If a conjunct consonant begins a root, the first consonant only with the following vowel is reduplicated; e. g. प्रच्छ्=पप्रच्छ्.

(:) If the first member of a conjunct consonant, however, be a sibilant (श, ष or स) and the second a hard consonant, the hard consonant is reduplicated. स्तुभ्=तुष्टुभ्; स्कन्द्=चस्कन्द्; but स्वन्=सुस्वन् &c.
