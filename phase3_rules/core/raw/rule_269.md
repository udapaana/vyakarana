§ 269. In forming the Avyayībhāva compounds the following rules should be observed:—

(a) The final long vowel is shortened, and the ending इ or ई is changed to य and ओ to उ; गोपावति भा: पातीति वा गोपः। तस्मिन्निति भक्तियोगः, भगविष्णु after Vishṇu, उपगु near a cow, &c.

(b) †The final त् of mas. and fem. nouns in अत् is dropped; and optionally that of neuter nouns ending in अत्; उपराजत्; उपब्रह्मत्-सं.
