§ 648. To express the sense of wishing for that which is denoted
by the noun; the affix काम्य is also added like the above and the
base so derived conjugated in the Par. as before. पुत्रकाम्यति 'he
wishes for a son,' यशस्काम्यति 'he wishes for fame,' लाभकाम्यति 'he
wishes for ghee,'
