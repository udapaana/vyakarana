§ 476. त is inserted before the ending consonant of the root मस् before which स is dropped, and after the vowel of नश्, पुरु and दिवु followed by any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel: मग्धासि &c.; नत्तासि, मंस्तासि. The स of मस् is changed to य when not dropped.
