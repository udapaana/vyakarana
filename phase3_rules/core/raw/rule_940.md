§ 940. When the word युयूच् is not used with roots meaning
'to remember,' such as स्युट्, छुदु, &c, the Second Future is used in
the sense of the Imperfect; स्युरुयूति छुयूयूयू गोकुलुयूनै यस्युयूयूयूः Kṛishṇa,
do you remember that we dwelt in Gokula?
