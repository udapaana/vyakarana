§ 370. Several nouns, substantive and adjective, may be prefixed to the roots कृ, भू, and अस् to form what are called in Sanskrit 'chvi-derivatives' (vide chap. IX. Sec. III.) कृष्णीकरणं = कृष्णीकरणं; धनी=भूत=धनीभूतः. Such nouns are also termed 'Gati.'
