§ 654. The suffix य (णयच्) is added to a few nouns such as शूल
'male,' भूरि 'slow,' भूतिधं 'learned,' शुयस् 'famous,' in the sense of
उन्मनस् 'agitated,' &c. in the sense of 'becoming what it was not
before,' or 'what it was not like to before,' शूल: भवति शूलायते 'what
was not much now becomes much,' उन्मनायति 'he (who was not
agitated before) becomes agitated,' similarly शुयसायते &c.
