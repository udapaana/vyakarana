§ 714. The affix य (यत्)* is added on to roots ending in a
vowel in the sense of 'fit to be, or ought to be.' Before
this य the radical vowel undergoes Guṇa substitute and final
आ (इ, ऋ and ओ changeable to आ) is changed to ए.

    दा—देयः what is fit or ought to be given.
    पा—पेयः       „       „       sucked.
    गै—गेयः       „       „       sung.
    भो—भोयः      „       „       cut.
    ग्रथ्—ग्रैयः      „       „       collected.
    नी—नेयः       „       „       led or carried.
