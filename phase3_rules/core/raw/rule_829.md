§ 829. † The particles नमुः, स्युहु, स्युयुहु, and सयुयु (exclama-
tions used in offering oblations to gods and manes respectively),
अलं meaning 'equal to, a match for' and वयुयुट् (an exclamation
accompanying an oblation to a deity) govern the Dative case;
तस्युयुं नमुः संभुवं 'bow to that Sambhu;' पुञ्युयुस्युः स्युहु 'may it be
well with the subjects,' स्युस्युरुयुतु (Rag. V. 17.) 'a farewell to
thee;' अग्रुयुयुङु स्युयुहु 'this offering to Agni,' similarly पितृयुयुयुुः स्युयुयुः
देवोभुयो हरिं अलं 'Hari is a match for the demons;' so अलं मद्युयो
मद्युयु (Mah. Bhā,) 'one wrestler is a match for another; (see
Bhat. II 39, Bhatti. VIII. 98.) इस्युरु वयुयुट् 'this oblation
to Indra,'

(a) But when कुच् is used with नमुः, it becomes the principal
verb ‡ and therefore governs the Accusative case;' नमुस्युकुरोति
देवुयुयुच् 'bows down to gods' (when the sense of an infinitive is
suppressed, of course the Dative will be used; see § 828).

(b) Words having the sense of अर्युं, such as यघुट्, सम्युर्युं, शुट्युंग,
&c. and also the verb युच्यु, are used with the Dative (Sid Kau.)
देवोभुयो हरिं यघुः संनुयुयुः शुकुङो वा; यघुट्—समुयुर्युं-शुट्युंग: मुद्युयो मद्युयुयुः
यभुवति मद्युयो मद्युयुयु। विविरुरिनं न यघुट्युः यभुवति (Bhar. II, 94.); यघु and
युच्यु are also found used with the Genitive (Sid. Kan.)
