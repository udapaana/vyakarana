ADVERBS.

§ 963. The neu. singulars of the Nominative and other cases
of several nouns are used as adverbs; चिरुयूं or चिरेणु or चिरुयूयु
स्युयूसुयुयो having contemplated for a long time; दुःखुयूं or दुःखेनु विद्युयूपि
he is in distress; so दुयूसुयूं or दुयूसुनै &c.

(a) The word नियुयु is used adverbially in combination with

CONJUNCTIONS.

§ 965. The use of Conjunctions has not many syntactical
Peculiarities and needs no special notice here. They are used
in their proper senses in sentences.

§ 966. The most important of these conjunctions and the
one very frequently used is च. It can never stand first in a
sentence, nor can it be used like 'and' in English. It is has a
sentence, we can it be used like 'and' in English. It is has a
with such as the words or assertions it connects or is placed
after the last of the words or assertions it joins together, रामुयु
लुक्ष्युयूणुयु or रामुः लुक्ष्युणुयूयूः कुयूयूयूनुयुयुं युयूविनुयूयूणुयो गवुयुनैनुयूनुयूं च love with
its excellences expanded, and fresh youth; कुदुलुयूनु कुन्युयूयु बयुयूयुयो
नवुनुनै युयूणोयूनु नैतीविरुयूयूयूयूयुनै।

(a) Sometimes this particle has a disjunctive force; ज्ञुयूयूनु-
तिदुयूनुयूभ्युयूयूदु स्युयुरुयूति च बाहु:—the hermitage is tranquil yet my
arm throbs.

(b) Rarely this particle is used in the sense of 'if';
जीविदुयूं चैच्छुसि यट् हेदुयूं युनै गहुरु: युण्युयुं oh fool, if you wish to live, &c.



(c) Sometimes it is used as an expletive; सोतुयूयू: यायुनैयूस्युयुनैयुयु च.

(d) Sometimes it is used to connect a subordinate fact
with a main one निश्युयूयूयूच् यु चाराणु wander for alms and bring
a corn; कुयुदैनुयूनों च ज्युयूविनुतुयू गोरोयू च नि संराहुतैयुः कुदैदुयूहुयूयूयूयू युयूरुष्कुयुयुः the
procuress was chastised, the ox-wardess was expelled and
Kardarakam was honoured.

(e) When the particle is repeated it has sometimes the
sense of 'on the one hand,' 'on the other hand,' 'and yet,' छु च
हरिष्युकुयूनुयूयुनों जीविदुयूयूनुयूयूं चुयूतुहुरुर्णोहुनै कं च विप्युर्नोतुयूनुयूयूयूयूयू बयुयुजुरुयूयूः शुरुस्युयुनै where,
on the one hand, is the extremely frail life of fawns, and
where, on the other, are thy arrows hard like adamant and
falling sharply; न युयुदुनैयुयू सुकुयोयूनुयूयूयूनुयूयुदुदो च स्युयू र्विकुयोनुयुयूं युदुयुयूयूयुयूयूयुनैर्युच्युयूयुर्णो-
यस्य न च the one hand is full moon-faced wife is not easy to
obtain and yet there is this unaccountable sport of love.

(f) Sometimes the repetition of च shows the simultaneous
or undivided occurrence of two events; युनै च यायुदुरुयूदुनुयूयूनुयूयूं युयुवुयुनु
चुयूदिवुयूयूयुयूः they reached the ocean and at the same time the
primeval Being arose awoke.

§ 967. तयू 'likewise' often supplies the place of च; रायूयूयूयूयू
लुक्ष्युयूणुयु Rāma and Lakshmana; अयूयूयूयूयूदुयूयूयूयूयू च युयूत्युयूयूयूयूतिरुयूयूयूयू
both Aṅgastravishtā and Pratyutpannamatī; तयूयूयू हि means 'for
instance, to be more plain;' तयूयूयू च 'likewise;' both are often used
introducing quotations.

§ 969 The यदि and इयु 'if' are usually used with the Potential
or the Conditional; as यदि मोयु संलिहितो गौयुदु तर्युनुयूं ननु युयूहुयूयुनुयूं
कुयुदैयुर्युनिं if he were here he would assist me; यदि दैयुदुयुनोरुयूयूयूविरुयुरुयुरु-
नयुदैतुवुकुयूरुयुयुदुनुयूच् had Devadatta been here, he would have undoubtedly
done this; but they are also construed with the Present Indica-
tive; यदि देदुवित युयूहुर्णि युयुयूरुयूनुयूं if he lives till to-morrow; याज्युयूयुयुनु;
यदि युयूयुयू देयुयूद्युयूनुयुयुयुनुयूं युयूयोकुनुरुयूनुयूनि if Your Majesty has any thing
to do with me, &c; कुयोतुयूसि मुयु जीविनुनुनै यदि बाच्युयू न कुयुयूरुयोयू
I conjure you by my life if you will not tell this in words. च्युदु
is rarely used at the beginning of a sentence, &c.; युयूयुयूदुयुयूयुनुयू
युयूरुंयू नुयुयुविरुयूयूयूट् (S'ak. VII. 4) if the thousand-rayed one (sun)
did not place him at the yoke of his car, आदि रोयुदैरुकुरोतोयू
(have recourse to) नो च्युदु &c Dha. V. I. 44.

The Particles अयु and इति.

§ 970. * अयु is used in the following senses:—(1) as a sign
of auspiciousness †; अयुयूतो बुरुदुमज्युयूतुयू now begins the inquiry
about Brahma; cf. the Bhāṣya on this sūtra (2) marks the
beginning or commencement of a work; आरुयूयुत आयुत ऋग्युदैयुयुनुयूयूनुयूयू:
now is begun the Ist Tantra; so अयु गीरुयूयूवुरुतुनुयूयूच्, &c; (3) 'then'
after that,' &c; अयु युत्रुजुयूयूयूयूनै, &c. (Meg. II. 1.) After that
i. e passing of the night) the lord of the earth, &c. (4) asks
a question; युयु कुयूयुर्यु शुकुयूदुरुयूर्युनुयुयुनुयूयूनुयुयूयूनुयुयुं oh the venerable
Kaśyapa all right that he may oblige the world? अयु कुयुदुनुयोयू युोयुदैयुनुयूं
are you able to eat? (5) 'and, including'; युोतुयू: अयु अजुर्युनुयुः Bhīma
and also Arjuna; (6) 'if;' अयु युयूयुयूयुयुच्युरुयूनुनै जन्तोयुः if death is
sure to befall a creature, &c.
