§ 669. The participles of the Present Ātम. is formed by
adding आन to the root which undergoes the same changes
before it, as before the termination अते or अन्ते of the 3rd pl.
pre. आन is changed to मान, in the case of roots of the 1st, 4th,
6th and 10th classes (i.e. and in derived verbs; see § 482). If the
base ends in अ, before which the अ of the base remains un-
changed; e. g एध् (1. cl.) एधमान 'growing;' वन्द् (1 cl.) वन्दमान
'saluting'; शये (2. cl.) शयान 'lying down;' द्विष् (2. cl.) द्विषाण,
दूव with आ (2. cl.) आप्रुवाण 'killing;' धृ (3. cl.) दधान 'holding;
हु (3. cl.) जुह्वाण 'sacrificing,' जु (4 cl.) दृष्यमाण 'desirous of
'extracting;' तुद् (6. cl.) तुद्यमान 'giving pain;' रुज् (7. cl.)
रुज्यान 'obstructing;' कृ (8. cl.) कुर्वमाण 'doing;' तन् (8 cl.) तन्वमान
'stretching;' क्री (9. cl.) क्रीणान 'buying;' चुर् (10, cl) चोरयमाण
'stealing,' &c. बुध् or चुस् (1. cl.)—cau. बोध्यमान—बोधयमाण 'causing
to know;'—desiderative बुबोधिषमाते—बुबूधयमाण 'being desirous
to know,' &c.
