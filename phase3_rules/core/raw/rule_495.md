§ 495. *Special rules about the admission of the intermediate इ before the terminations व, म, वहे, से, वहे, महे; see (§ 457).

(a) All Seṭ roots admit it, whether seṭ or aniṭ, except ई, ऊ, ऋ, लृ, हु, वृ; वृ, ग्रु, क्षु, permit इ or अ; But इ with सम्, and वृ admit it only as सचक्रिव, ववरिव.

(b) Aniṭ roots ending in short अ, except अव्, reject it before व; as तस्थिव from स्था; but आरिव from ऋ.

(c) Aniṭ roots with a final vowel or with a penultimate अ admit it optionally before व.
