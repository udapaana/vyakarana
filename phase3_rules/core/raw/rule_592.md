## Footnotes

"It might even be suspected, that the occasional assumption of
an Intransitive signification and a Parasmaipada inflexion by a
passive verb, was the cause which gave rise to a 4th class of Primi-
tive verbs as distinct from the Passive. Instances of passively
formed of passive verbs taking Parasmaipada terminations, and some
passive verbs (e. g. jāyate 'is born,' tr. jan; pūryate, 'is
filled,' tr. pṛ; and upyate 'is heated,' tr. tap) are regard-
ed by native grammarians as Âtmanepada verbs of cl. 4. Again,
many roots appear in class 4 as Intransitive verbs, which also
appear in some one of the other nine as Transitive. For example,
yuj, 'to join' when used in a Transitive sense, is conjugated
either in cl. 7, or in the Causal; when in an Intransitive, in cl.
4. So also puṣh, 'to nourish,' kṣubh, 'to agitate;' Kliśh 'to vex';
Ślāh, 'to accomplish,' &c.

                                            Monier Williams.



भू 'to be'

Present.

1. भूये              भूयावहे          भूयामहे
2. भूयसे             भूयेथे           भूयध्वे
3. भूयते             भूयेते           भूयन्ते

Imperfect.

1. अभूये             अभूयावहि         अभूयामहि
2. अभूयथाः           अभूयेथाम्         अभूयध्वम्
3. अभूयत             अभूयेताम्         अभूयन्त

1. भूये              भूयावहै          भूयामहै
