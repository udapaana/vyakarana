§ 931. † The Aorist ought to be used when the idea of the
continuousness or nearness of an action is to be implied; यावुजीतु-
त्युयुयूयूयूदुयुयूट् (Sid. Kau.) gave food throughout his life; तेयुनुयुं यायु-
युयूयुयुयुकुयूल्युयो तत्युयुयुयूयुनैरुयुयूयूयुर चोनुयूयुत्युयु (Sid. Kāu.) he consecrat-
ed the fire on the last Paurṇimā (i.e. full-moon day) and
offered Soma, &c.
