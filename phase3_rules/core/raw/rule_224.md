§ 224. The particle कु forms a Karm. compound with any other word, कुपुरुषः ( कुत्सितः पुरुषः ) a bad man; कुयवः &c.

(α) † कु is changed to ( 1 ) कद् when followed by a noun with an initial vowel in a Tat. by त्रि and the nouns रथ and वद and by ऋण ' denoting a species;' कुत्सितोऽश्वः कदश्वः: ' bad horse;' so कदन्नं 'coarse food;' but कुद्रः ' having bad camels' (a Bah. comp.) राजा; कुत्सिताः त्रयः कद्त्रयः: ' three inferior things;' कद्रथः ' a bad chariot,' कद्व्रः: 'speaking ill, a bad speaker,' कनृणम् ' a kind of fragrant grass.'

(2) To का when followed by पश्चिम् and अक्ष and when meaning 'a little,' and optionally when prefixed to पुरुष; कापश्यम्, काक्षः ' a sidelong look or a frown of displeasure;' see Bh.V. 54 (अक्षसादेश्च
