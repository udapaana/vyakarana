§ 258. †† हृदय is changed to हृद् when preceded by सु or दुस् meaning 'a friend', or an 'enemy' respectively; शोभनं हृदयं यस्य स सुहृद् 'a friend', दुहृद् 'an enemy'; but सुहृदय: 'one who has got a good heart' i.e. 'one who is able to appreciate merits', &c.; दुहृदय: 'a man of wicked intentions.'
