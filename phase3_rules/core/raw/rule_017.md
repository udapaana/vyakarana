§ 17. The numerical figures in Sanskrit are:—
    १, २, ३, ४, ५, ६, ७, ८, ९, ०.
    1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 0.
These are combined to express larger figures precisely in the
same way as in English; १२५ 125, ५४० 540, &c.
