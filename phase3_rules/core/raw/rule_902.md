§ 902 The past passive participle active and passive, may be
used with the auxiliaries अस् and भू in any tense, the mean-
ing of the participle changing accordingly; गतोऽस्युयु I have or am starting;
I have or am gone; so गतवुयूनुभुवं or गतवुयूनुयूनुयुं or गतोभुवुयुं I had
or was gone; इच्युच्युन्तुयूस्तु. गतो वनु युयुयो यविष्युत्युति रायुः that Rāma
is to go to the forest to-morrow; संबुसुः कोपिमुनुटुयुयुयुं भविष्युयुति
thou wilt obtain great glory, &c.
