§ 498. Roots ending in आ take औ instead of आ in the 1st and 3rd per. sing. and drop their final आ before vowel weak terminations, and before such as take the augment इ; as ददौ, दद्विव, दद्विम 1st Per. ददौ, दद्व 2. Per. sing. of दा.
