§ 380. In § 378 (b) the first four are marked विकरण; the last six are called the Special Tenses (विकरणलकाराः) and the rest the Common Tenses or Lakāras (सकारान्तलकाराः). The special Tenses undergo peculiar modifications (विकरण) and these are therefore called Vikaraṇa-lakāras; the others Sakārānta-lakāras (सकारान्त).
