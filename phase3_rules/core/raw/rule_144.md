§ 144. The affix आति is added to तद्, यद् and किम् to show number or measure e. g. तति 'so many', यति 'as many,' and कति ' how many'. These are declined in the plural only, and take no termination in the Nom. and Acc.; e. g. Nom. and Acc. कति Inst. कतिभिः the rest like हरि.
