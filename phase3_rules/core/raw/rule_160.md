§ 160. The cardinal numbers between विंशति and त्रिंशत्, त्रिंशत् and चत्वारिंशत्, it will be seen, are made up by prefixing एक, द्वि, &c. up to नवन् to the lower. The ninth intermediate number may also be made up by prefixing एकोन, ऊन or एकान् to the higher. In these compounds द्वि, त्रि and अष्ट are changed to द्वा, त्रयः, and अष्टा necessarily before विंशति and त्रिंशत्, and optionally before the rest except अशीति before which they remain unchanged.†
