§ 256. At the end of a Bah. compound—

(a) *जानु is changed to ञु necessarily when preceded by प्र or सम् and optionally when preceded by उच्चै; प्रजत्ते जानुनी यस्य स प्रञ्नुः ' one having the knees wide apart ' i. e. ' bandylegged; ' सञ्नुः ' one having well-turned or beautiful knees, ' उर्चैर्जानुः—ञुः ' long-shanked. '

(b) † जाया becomes जानि; युवती जाया यस्य स युवजानिः 'one whose wife is young; ' भूजानिः lit. ' one whose wife is the earth, i. e. a king, ' &c.

(c) † गन्ध becomes गन्धि when preceded by उत्, प्रति or दुः; उद्दतः गन्धः यस्य स उद्गन्धिः 'whose smell is spread above,' प्रतिगन्धिः 'having a repulsive smell,' दुर्गन्धिः. This change of गन्ध is possible only when the smell referred to is inseparably connected with ( looks like a part and parcel of ) the object denoted by the other member; as दुर्गन्धि पुष्करं सन्निहं च ( which receives its odour from the lotuses &c. growing in it; ) दुर्गन्धिवारिहः; but शोभना गन्धा अस्य इति गन्ध आपणिकः 'a perfumer dealing in sweet smells.' गन्ध is also changed to गन्धि § when it means ' a particle of. ' 'a little of, ' or when the compound involves comparison;
