§ 916. The root अर्ङु 'to deserve' when used (in the second
person) in combination with the infinitive expresses 'a request,
a respectful entreaty,' and is used the same way in
English 'I pray, be pleased, &c.;' अग्रिंङु शुयूपिट्युयूनुहुयूरि (Meg. 55)
please put out the fire; न चुयुस्युयुनै युतुयूपिट्युयूनुहुयूरि (Kum V. 40)
if you have nothing to conceal from me, please answer me:
विद्युयुयुयुदुयुनुयूनुहुयूरि सोदुयूयुनैदुरु (Rag. V. 25) you be pleased to tell these
days; किं अनुमुन्तुवुयूले one &c. Sometimes it is equivalent to a
gentle command: युयूयुयुनै यवुयूपिट्युयूनुहुयूरि (Rag. I. 89) you ought to
please her; न तं चुत्तुयूपिट्युयूनुहुयूरि you ought not to bewail him. In
the third person and under the same circumstances it expresses
power or ability and may be translated by 'can;' शुयुयुदु हि समुर्युनै
कोऽज्ञुयो सोदुयूयूनुहुयूरि फुयूल्युयुयुयूट् Māh. Bhar. IV. 58. 27. वेदुदुं युयुत्युयुविशुषेणै
को नितियुदुयूनुहुयूरि Ibid I. 1. 243.
