§ 712. The affixes तव्य or अनीय are added to roots or
derivative bases in the sense of 'must be fit to be,' &c. Before
these the ending vowel and the penultimate short of a root
take their Guṇa substitute. Before तव्य Set roots take इ, Anit
roots do not, and Veṭ roots take it optionally. Before अनीय
penultimate ऋ is always changed to अर् and not to ऋ (as it
