§ 651. The penultimate अ of a noun ending in a nasal is
lengthened, The base is treated like a root of the last gaṇa-
tion, its last vowel only taking Guṇa before त &c; (termina-
tion of Vishṇu) इव आचरति—अति 'he acts like Vishṇu,'
कृष्ण—कृष्णाति 'he acts like कृष्ण' (कृष्णाति 1st sing.), कवि—कव-
यामि 'I act the poet or behave like a poet,' विद्—विद्यति 'he be-
haves like a bird,' माला-मालाति 'he or it acts like a garland'
(Perf. मालाञ्च &c.), पितृ-पिताति 'he acts like a father.' भू-भूवति
'acts like the earth' (Perf. चुभूव, &c. as भूव is the base),
राजन्-राजनाति 'he acts like a king,' पथिन्-पयातति 'it serves as
a road,' &c So इदमति from इदम्, ऋत्विगस्त्रुति from ऋत्विगश्व
name of Indra.

(b) The words असुगल (a bold man), शीड (a child) and
विडाल take the affixes यूष् and णिच् optionally and are conjugated
in the Ātmane. असुगलीते or असुगलायते, शीडते or शीडायते, विडाते
or विडायते.

       (d) By means of the affix य (णयच्) and
                 conjugated in the Ātm.
