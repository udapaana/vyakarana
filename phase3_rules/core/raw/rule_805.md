§ 805. * The following prepositions are used with the
Accusative.

(a) अति 'superior to, higher than' अनु 'just after, after by
the side of, inferior to,' अपि 'close to' and उप 'near, inferior
to;' e. g. अति देवुरुद् कृष्णुः Kṛṣṇa excels gods in might; अतिरुयुम्
गोविन्दुः Govinda is superior to Rāma; जलुयुम् प्रावुर्तुत् it rained
just after the muttering of prayers; त्युवं मानुष्युतं ते every thing

(b) The prepositions आविस्, अनु, परि and प्रति are used with the
Accusative when they refer to particular things; पिपिः—आविस्—
अनु—परि—प्रति—विवोत्युते विद्युयुत the lightning flashes towards the
mountain; also when they mean 'to every one,' &c. वृक्षं युट्युयुन्त्युवि—
अनु—परि—प्रति—सिच्युति waters each and every tree; so अवि—
अनु—परि—प्रति स्त्री स्त्री जातमन्मुयुः ।

(c) अनु, परि and प्रति also govern the Accusative when they
imply 'one's own share;' छुक्तुमीः हुरिस्युच्-परि-प्रति Lakṣmī is the
proper share (property) of Hari.
