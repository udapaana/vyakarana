§ 566 (a) In the Parasmaipada the radical vowel takes
its Vṛddhi substitute; कृ-अकार्षीत्; इ-ऐषीत्; मञ्ज-अमाञ्जीत्, &c.

(b) In the Ātmanepada, Guṇa is substituted for final इ or
उ, short or long; final ऋ and the penultimate vowel remain
unchanged; final ऋ is changed to ऋर् or ऊर् in accordance with
§ 594; चि-अचेष्ट, नी-अनेष्ट, च्यु-अच्योष्ट, क्-अकोष्ट, छ् see below,
गिर्-अगरिष्ट, धूर्-अधोरीष्ट, हूर्-अहोरीष्ट.

(c) The Penultimate ऋ of Anit roots is optionally changed
to ऋर् or ऊर् सञ्जाघर्सुर् or सञ्जाघोरुत्.
