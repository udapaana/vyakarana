§ 238. *The word अङ्गि at the end of a Tatpurusha compound changes its final to अ when preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable, as द्वे अङ्गुली प्रमाणमस्य द्विङ्गुलं दारु 'a piece of wood measuring two fingers,' निर्गतमङ्गिभ्यो निरङ्गुलम् &c.
