§ 631. When a root contains a penultimate ऋ (ऋ) original
or brought in by samprasaraṇa the syllable री is inserted between
the अ of the reduplicative syll. (which remains short against
§ 629 b) and the radical consonant; पृट्-पूर्यंय=पुपूर्यंयते,
भञ्ज्-भुञ्जय=पुञ्जयते, पृञ्-परीयते, मृड्=मरीयते.

             Conjugation of the Ātm. Freq. Base.
