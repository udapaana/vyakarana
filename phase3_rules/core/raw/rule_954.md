§ 954. * With interrogative words such as किंयु, कुतुरु, कुयुयु, &c.
the Potential or the Simple Future may be used when censure
is implied (see § 937); कुः कुतुरो वा हुरिं निन्युदेत् निनिन्युयूयूति वा।

(a) When wonder is implied the simple Future is used in
preference to the Potential if the word यदि be not used; आश्युयुयुर्युनै
अन्युयूयुः नारं दुयुच्युयुनुयूं दुयुच्युयुयूति it is e wonder that a blind man should see
Hari; but अयुश्युयुयुयूर्युनं यदि निन्युदेत् it is wonder if he should.
