§ 859. * In the case of Potential passive participles the agent
of the action is put in the Genitive or in the Instrumental case;
मया मम वा हेयुयो हरिः Hari ought to be served by me; राक्तुयुसुनुयु-स्युयु
नुयुयुरुमु तृयुतुरुयुः (Bhaṭṭi VIII. 125) the forest together with the
lord of the demons ought to preserve must be destroyed by me;
गन्तव्युयु ते वसुतिरुटुकुयु हे. (Meg.) Thou should go to Alakā.
