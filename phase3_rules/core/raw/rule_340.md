§ 340. The following is a list of such of the Taddhita affixes as are commonly to be met with.

SECTION I.

MISCELLANEOUS AFFIXES.

अ—is added in the sense of:—(1) 'the son of'; as उपगोः अपत्यं उपगव्यः 'the son of Upagu'; so वाह्नेयः from बह्नेय; पर्वतस्य अपत्यं स्त्री पार्वती 'the daughter of the mountain,'



&c.
