§ 212. ‡ पूर्व, अपर, अधर उत्तर and अर्ध n. are compounded with nouns in the Genitive expressive of the whole and are placed first; e. g. पूर्वं कायस्य पूर्वकायः the forepart of the body; अपरकायः अधरकायः &c. अर्धं पिप्पल्याः अर्धपिप्पली; but ग्रामार्धः as ( अर्ध is mas. here ).

N B. In order that the above rule should hold the noun expressive of the whole must denote a single object; hence पूर्वः
