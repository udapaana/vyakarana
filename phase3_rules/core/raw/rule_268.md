§ 268. An Avyayībhāva comp. consists of two members the first of which is, in most cases, an indeclinable (a preposition or an adverb) and the second a noun, the whole being treated like the Nom. sing. of ses. nouns. the comp. so formed is indeclinable; e. g. आदिहरि 'in Hari'; अन्ताद्रि 'in a mountain,' &c.
