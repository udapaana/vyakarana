§ 618. The radical vowels undergo the following changes
before स्—

(a) The स् with इ is strong and without इ weak.

The usual rule as to Guṇa substitute holds good in the
one case and does not in the other; दुह्-विदूहिष्, विदुह्:
इ-दिदीषिष्, &c.

(b) The final इ and उ and the penultimate अ of गह् and
गह् (substituted for इ 2 Par. 'to go,' and for इ with आधि 'to



remember' or 'to study') are lengthened and final ऋ, short or
long, changed to ई; or to ऊ after labials or व when the स् is
unaugmented; जि-जीजिष्, इ-दूदूर्, कृ-चिकीर्ष्, तृ-तितीर्ष्, इ-दीयिष्,
इ-इईर्ष्, &c

(c) The roots दुह्, तुर् and मुष् do not change their vowel
to Guṇa; and the roots मृज्, स्मर् and भृज् take Samprasāraṇa;
दुदूहिष्, मीवर्तिष्, ममर्जिष्, जिमुर्क्ष् डुमुर्क्ष्, जुमुच्छिष्.

(d) Roots with an initial consonant, and having इ or उ
short for their penultimate, and ending in any consonant, except
ष् or क्, change their vowel to Guṇa optionally or स is
prefixed to स्; शुन्-दिशुनिष्, दिशोनिष्: द्रुह्-मद्रोहिष् or दुद्रोहिष् &c
