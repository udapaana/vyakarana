§ 942. When the idea of wonder is to be expressed and
the words युयूच्, युयूनु and युदि do not occur in a sentence, the
Simple Future should be used; युरुयूदुन्युोऽदुयुन्युोऽपि हरिं युयूयुरुयूति हरिं युयूक्ष्युयूति it is
a wonder that a blind man sees Hari.

(a) The Simple Future is also used when the particles
उनु and आपि expressing a doubt are used; उनु दुयूयुयुयुः युतियुयूयूति will
the stick fall? आपि कुयूयूरुयूनुयूयूति द्युयूरुं will be close the door?

(b) This Future is also used when the particle अड्युयू mean-
ing sure or able is used; अहुयुयुं हुतुयूयु निश्युयूयु Kṛishṇa is sure
sure or able to kill the elephant.
