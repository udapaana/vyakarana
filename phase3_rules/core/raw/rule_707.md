§ 707. The P. P. Participles of roots meaning 'to think or to
wish,' 'to know,' 'to adore,' and of the roots हन्, भू etc. have
the sense of the present Tense; राज्ञा मतः 'he is honoured by the
king;' सत्त्रैष आरुत्रेः द्युतः तथा the root is kindled;' so भूतः etc.

(d) Past Active Participles.
