§ 906 Occasionally this participle is used impersonally in
the neuter gender and singular number; तवुभुवुतु तपोवुनं यन्तुयुयुनं
his honour should go to the penance grove; मयु चाणुयुदुयूहे: सह
स्युयूस्युयुच् I should have to dwell in the company of Chāṇḍālas &c.
