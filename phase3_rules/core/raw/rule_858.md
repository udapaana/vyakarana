§ 858. † The Genitive is not used with present participles
except that of द्विष्, verbal derivatives ending in क् and उकुच्
except that derived from कुयुच्, verbal indeclinables, past parti-
ciples, passive and active, nouns formed with the affix युयुनु (see
p. 456) and with such as mean 'in the habit of, or having the
properties of or doing any thing well;' क्तुर्नों क्तुयुर्नं or कुयूनुयुयुयुयुः but
युरै दुयुस्युयु वा हिद्युद् हरिः Hari, the enemy of Mura; हुरै विद्युयुयुट्युः desir-
ous of seeing Hari; युरै अट्युदुहुरियुयुट्, दैन्युयुरु यावुदुकुयो हरिः Hari is the
killer of demons; कुयुच्युः कुरुणुयुयुङु : युविदुयुस्युयु युयुवै तुयु युङु न पुयुवुयु द्युयु
दैन्युयु; दैन्युयुरु हुवुनुयुरु: हुयुकुन्तुः प्रयुच्चो हुरेणु worldly life goes asray
with Hari; आतुयुनुनों अट्युकुत्तुयुरुष्णुयुट्युः in habit of decorating oneself;
अवुनं विद्युयुट्ु: a habitual beggar; क्तुर्नो कुट्युं one who prepares a mat;
युनै पुनुयुनुयु 'killing the fruit' means the fruit by terminations
