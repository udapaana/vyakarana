§ 229. In some Karmadharaya compounds the last member of the first word which is itself a compound word is dropped*; e. g. शाकप्रियः पार्थिवः: शाकपार्थिवः: 'a king who likes vegetables', दैत्यराजको ब्राह्मणः: दैत्यब्राह्मणः: &c. These compounds, though they should be properly called "Uttamapadālopi" are popularly designated "Madhyamapadālopi". Any compound requiring a similar explanation is classed under this group (शाकपार्थिवादि) which is an आकृतिगण.

DWIGU OR NUMERAL APPOSITIONAL COMPOUNDS.
