§ 947. The Imperative has sometimes the force of the pre-
sent when used in combination with the particle युयु; युयु भुवुदु no,
it is not so; युयु च तै तियुयुयुः शुयूच्युयुन्युयुयूयुवुयुदु पुयूथिवु।
