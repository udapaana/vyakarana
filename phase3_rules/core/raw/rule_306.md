§ 306. Before the affix ई—

(a) nouns ending in consonants assume that form which they take before the termination of Inst. sing.; प्रत्यच्-श्-प्रतीची, राजन्—राज्ञी, भवत्—भवती, श्वन्—शुनी, अर्यमन्—आर्येणी, विद्वस्—विदुषी, &c. There are some exceptions, अर्हन्—अर्हती &c.

(b) the preceding अ or ई is dropped; as गोर—गोरी आटी श—दूत्ती, पाम्वती. &c.

(c) if a Prâtipadika end in य, part of a Taddhita affix, that य is dropped गार्ग्य + ई = गार्ग्याः 'the daughter of Garga.' &c.

(d) the final य of the words सूर्य, तिष्य the constellation of stars called पुष्य, अगस्त्य and मत्स्य is dropped; as सौरी, मत्सी, &c.

(e) in the case of the participles of the present and the future न is inserted, as in the Nom. dual of the neu. (vide § 311. a. b.); for instances, see § 236.
