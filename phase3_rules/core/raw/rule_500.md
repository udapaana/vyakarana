§ 500. Monosyllabic roots, having a short अ for their penultimate, change it to इ and drop the reduplicative syllable before the weak terminations and before व when it takes इ, provided their initial letter undergoes no change in the reduplication, e. g. पत् 1st dual पेतुव्+इव=पेत्+इव=पेतिव, पेतिव 2 sing. शक्ष्व, शसक्ष्व 2. sing. of शक्, but निनेश्व 2nd dual of नन्द् as the अ here is prosodially long.

Exceptions:—Roots beginning with व and the roots शास and ग्रस्.
