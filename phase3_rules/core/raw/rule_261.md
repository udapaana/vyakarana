§ 261. "The words उरस्, सर्पिस्, अपनह्, द्वि, त्रि, शालि-ली and संध्, गन्धहु, पत् , गो and लक्ष्मी when used in the singular, have a क added to them, when they form the latter member of a Bah. comp.; उरुक (expanded, well-developed) उर्: यस्य उरुकोरस्क:, सिप्रसर्पिक: 'one fond of ghee', &c. एक: उद्गात् यस्य असौ एकुद्गातृक: 'having one man only', &c.; when द्वि and the words following it are used in the dual and the plural they take क optionally. द्विशालक or द्विशाल: &c.

(a) अर्थ takes the final क, necessarily when preceded by अन and optionally in any other case; अनर्थक: 'useless'; but अपार्थ-र्थीक 'meaningless' बच्च:.
