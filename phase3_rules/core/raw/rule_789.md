§ 789 When an adjective qualifies two or more substantives
it agrees with them in their combined number; but when
substantives differ in gender, the adjective will be masculine
when the substantives are masculine and feminine; and neuter
when they are masculine, feminine and neuter राजा रानी च
नित्यारुचितौ तुतौः the king and his queen are of laudable
conduct; सर्त्तः शामवश्चै धृर्तश्च कोशः भूमिं वशः । अर्थुद्धात्मिति चन्तापि
प्रवर्तते न लोपयुक्तः fulfilment of duty, satisfaction of desires, pride,
anger, happiness and long life, all these proceed undoubtedly
from wealth.

(a) Sometimes the adjective takes the gender of the majority
of the substantives; पितृयुः पत्युद्विधात्रग्र्यौ निरोन्य भार्या भूतः स्त्रियः ।
अप्युकुरुशन कर्तच्यौ भवन्तुमा भयुरुवुदिता ॥ aged parents, a good wife and
a young son should be maintained even by doing a hundred
foul deeds; so has Manu spoken (laid down).
