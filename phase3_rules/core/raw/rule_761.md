§ 761. There is another Indeclinable Participle having the
same sense as that in क्त्वा, formed with the affix अम्. Before
this the vowel of the derivative verbal base generally undergoes
the same changes as it does before the इ of the Pass. Aorist
3rd sing.; नी—नयाम् 'having carried or led,' दा—दायाम् 'having
given,' भू—भावाम्, भिद्—भेदाम्, भाह्—भाह्राम्, गम्—गमाम्, &c.
