§ 862. The Genitive is used with· indeclinables like मुयुध्युयुनै,
परिरै, छुतै, &c.; गङ्गुयुयुयु यस्युयुनै  यरै वा in the middle or on the other
side of the Ganges; अर्युनों ग्रायुयुनों छुते for the sake of this life.
