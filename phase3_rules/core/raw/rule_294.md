§ 294. सह forming the first member of a compound, is changed to स—

(a) when the whole compound is a name, e. g. सभाजयः; but सहयुध्वा a comrade in battle ( Upapada comp. ).

(b) when it means 'including a certain literary work,' or 'in addition to;' as सज्योतिषम् ( Avya. comp. ) ज्योतिषमधीते 'he studies the science of astronomy, including that of finding out the auspicious times,' सकोणा वीणा 'a khāri with a drone in addition to it.'
