§ 863. With superlatives, and words having the sense of
the superlative, the Genitive is used; सुणुयो ब्राह्मुयुणुः अद्युयुः; अयुयुयुणुयुयुयु-
हुनुयुयुट्युोयुयुयुट् (Rag. V. 4) the chief of sages, the authors to the
Mantras.

Note:—Words having the sense of comparatives are used
with the Ablative and sometimes with the Instrumental; अयुयु-
स्युरुयुयुट् वुलं हरिनुः or अविवुकुः this person is superior or inferior to
him in strength; similarly देवुदुत्तुयो यदुवुदुयूनुयुट्युङु: दूद्युहुयु वा; कौ द्युच्
स्युनुत्युरो भुयुयु who will have a happier end than I? The word
सुणुयुनै is used with the Locative as in the Locative or the Instru-
mental; द्युनुहुंङु नारायुणुविकुयुनुपि सोऽभुवुद् he was more (i. e. dearer)
to them than their sons; तेषुयुयुस्युयुजुङुनं यायुयुः यङुत्युनु द्युयुयुयुङु क्तुयुयुयुः
they passed five months and twelve nights more than (those
years); कुदुचुदुयुङुअकुच् यत्युयुः A Kadru is larger than a Kūdava.
