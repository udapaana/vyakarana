§ 499. Guṇa is substituted for the final vowel of roots ending in ऋ preceded by a conjunct consonant, and in long ऋ and of the roots ऋ, चक्ष and जाग्; as तस्तरिव 1 dual of स्तृ, &c. ऐकृव 1 dual of कृ; ववक्षिव 2 sing. of वक्ष्; रुरुव 1st dual of रु; सुषुप्व, शुश्रुविव 1 dual of स्वप् &c. weak terminations, and shorten their vowel when they do not; as शशरिव, शुश्रुविव 1 dual of शृ, &c.
