§ 364. Besides these there are in Sanskrit a few nouns having one inflection only which are treated as indeclinable (nipātas); e. g. अन्यत् another (reason), अस्तम् setting, अस्ति that exists, ओम् the well-known sacred syllable om, चनस् satisfaction, food, चाहु coaxing, नमस् a bow, नास्ति non-existence भूर् the earth, भुवर् the sky, बदि the dark fortnight, यशस् happiness, शुदि or शुक्ति the bright fortnight, संवत् a year, स्वाहा food offered to gods, स्वधा food offered to the manes, स्वर् heaven, स्वस्ति happiness &c.
