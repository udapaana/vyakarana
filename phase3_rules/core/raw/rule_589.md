§ 589. Every root in every one of the ten conjugational
classes may take a Passive form conjugated like an Ātmanepadi
root of the 4th Class *

---

## Footnotes

* The only difference is in the accent; in the Passive it falls
on य, whereas in the case of the primitive verbs of the 4th class,
Ātm., it falls on the radical syllable.
