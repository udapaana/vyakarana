§ 663. Verbs may be formed optionally from the words,
स्फातयि, चयायति, भूरि, and अर्थ by means of regular affixes, or
without the help of any affixes as given in the last para.
The special affixes are क्यच्, चङ्कच् or णिच्; so these four words
may be used in three ways:— 1. as nominal verbs with the
affixes added, 2. as verbs used transitively without affixes,
and 3. as nominal verbal bases not requiring any affixes for
their use. Thus स्फातयि भवते, स्फातयति, स्फातययति,' to use salt.' so
from आत्मन् we have आत्मनि, आत्मायति, आत्मायते respectively.
In the same way from other three also stems may be formed
Similarly from अम्बस्, पयस्, शुचि we get अम्बयन्ति, पयस्यति,
&c. In all these cases the root affix may be taken to be णिच्
or णिच्.
