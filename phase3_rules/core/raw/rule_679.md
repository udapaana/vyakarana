§ 679. The Past Passive Participle is formed by the addition
of the affix त् to the root, e. g. स्ना–स्नात 'bathed,' जि–जित 'con-
quered,' नी–नीत 'carried,' श्रु-श्रुत 'heard,' भू-भूत 'become,' हृ-हृत
'taken away,' त्यज्-त्यक्त 'abandoned,' चिन्त्-चिन्त्, 'thought or me-
ditated upon,' &c.
