§ 623. (a) The Perfect is formed by adding आम् to the base
and appending the Perfect forms of the auxiliary verbs अस्, भू,
and कृ (see § 490, 526).

(b) In the Aorist the Desiderative Bases take the terminations
of the 5th Variety.

(c) In the Benedictive the Parasm. terminations are added
without इ and the Atm. ones with the intermediate इ.

(d) The remaining tenses have no peculiarities.
