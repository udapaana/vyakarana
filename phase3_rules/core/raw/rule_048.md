§ 48. Visarga preceded by आ is dropped necessarily when followed by a soft consonant, and optionally when followed by a vowel; it is also optionally dropped when preceded by अ and followed by a vowel except अ; when it is not dropped it is changed to य् in both these cases; देवाः + नम्याः:=देवा नम्याः; देवा + इह = देवा इह, देवायिह !
