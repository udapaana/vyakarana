§ 639. Paradigms:—The Third persons of the Parasmaipada
Frequentative of कृ 'to do,' पृच् 'to fill,' and कृष् 'to draw
furrows.' All those tenses of this Intensive in which the weak
form of the base is used (vide §§ 485, 486) substitute चकृ for
कृ 'to do'; पूर्य 'to fill up,' and कृष्य 'to draw furrows' respect-
ively. If in any weak form of an Intensive the root has a final
consonant, र् is inserted between the Guṇa of the root and the
termination (see § 640)
