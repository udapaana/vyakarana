§ 882. The pronouns of the first and second person eti.
अस्युयुदु and युयुयुयुदु have no gender. The other pronouns follow the

---

* यट्युयुङु वानुयुदुरे । Pāṇ, II. 3. 38.



gender of the nouns they refer to. For the uses of the shorter
forms of अस्युयुदु and युयुयुयुदु see chapter IV.
