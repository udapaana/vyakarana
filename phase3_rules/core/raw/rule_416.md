§ 416. Special rules of Sandhi of the finals of roots and the initial letters of terminations:—

(1) The ending आ of a root takes its Vṛddhi substitute when followed by a consonantal strong termination; as आ+ति=आति.

(2) The final इ or उ, short or long, of a root is changed to इय् or उव् before a vowel weak termination.

(3) The ending ह् of roots is changed to ढ when followed by any consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, or by nothing; and that of roots beginning with द् to त् under the same circumstances.

(4) The initial त or थ of a termination is changed to च after a soft aspirate (4th letter of a class).

(5) द् or ध् followed by स is changed to त्.



(6) द् and ध् when followed by a consonant are changed to the nasal of the class to which the following consonant belongs, and to an anusvāra when followed by श्, ष्, स or ह.

(7) The ending इ of a root, short or long, not preceded by a conjunct consonant is changed to य before vowel weak terminations, when the base consists of more than one syllable.

(8) The ending इ of a root is optionally changed to र् or Visarga in the Imperfect second pers. sing. and the ending द् to त् or ढ् before the termination स् and optionally before थ्.

(9) When a conjunct consonant having श् or ष् for its first member is at the end of a word or is followed by a consonant except a nasal or a semivowel, the श् or ष is dropped.

N. B.—The usual Sandhi rules i. e. those given in the 2nd and 3rd Chapters should be observed.
