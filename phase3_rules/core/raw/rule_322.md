§ 322. If a compound has for its last member ऊरु and for its first, words signifying a standard of comparison, or one of the words सहित, शक्, क्षमण, वाम, महित and सह, it forms its feminine in ऊः; रम्भोरुः रम्भे इव ऊरु यस्याः plantain-thighed; करभोरुः having thighs ( beautifully tapering ) like the fore-arm or the trunk of an elephant; दृढितोरुः having well-turned thighs; शक्तो खुरौ वातृविव संनियच्छत्यादूरु यस्याः सा शक्तोरुः; [ हितेन सह सहितो ऊरु यस्याः सा ] सहितोरुः; [ सहते इति सहो ऊरु यस्याः सा ] सहोरुः 'a woman whose thighs are capable of enduring great fatigue or pain'; or 'one having excellent thighs.'
