§ 914. †The infinitive is also used with:—

(a) verbs meaning to be able, to make bold, to know, to
be wearied, to strive, to begin, to set about, to bear, to be
pleased, and to be proficient; चाण्डुयूत् उत्तुयू वचुनुवुटुनुयू he is not able
to support his neck; जुयुनुपि कोयुनै निवुयुदुयोतुदुयू you know how to re-
strain anger; आङुगुद्युनु समं युयूदुयुयुनैयुदुयु (Bhattṛi. XV. 77) to strove
to fight with Aṅgada; गन्तुदुयू युयुयुस्युयुदुयूयुयुट् (Meg. 22) thou wilt
try to go; तुरुकुच् युविहुतुर्युयुयु (?Meg. 103) begin to speak; भोज्युनुयुतुसित-
दिययुते वा भोजुनुयुयुरु (Sid. Kau.) there is food to eat, &c.

(b) ‡words like अर्ङु and others meaning sufficient or able,
proficient or skilled in, &c.; युयुयुयोयुति युज्ञुयु: यात्युदु (Rag. X. 25)
thou art able to protect the creation; कः समुयुर्युयो देवुयुन्युयुयुयु कुटुदुयुङु
युर्यु is able to change destiny; यरुयुयुयुवुयुनो मनुनुरुयुयुनुदु (Meg. 66)
the palaces arc able to stand comparison with thee; भोकुच् युयूनुयुयुयुनुयुः
कुयुशुलुः यदुयुर्युनै (Sid. Kau.) skilled in eating.

(c) §words having the sense of 'it is time to do anything?'
कुयूलुः समुयुर्युयो वेलुयु अवुद्युहुयु वा भोजुयूदुयुरु (Sid. Kau.) it is time to
take food.
