§ 736. The root पद् when preceded by अमा 'with' takes the
affix घ्यत् before which the penultimate अ is optionally changed
to Vṛiddhi; अमा रुङु गुवातोऽव्या चन्द्रा:शो अवतिब्धुवा or वाश्या 'the day
on which the sun and the moon are with each other' i. e. are in
conjunction.'

(a) युज् when preceded by पाणिं or the preposition समत् takes
the affix घ्यत्; as पाणिस्यो डुयुयोऽ पाणिगुम्योऽ यृज्यः; similarly समवसुयुर्यं.
