§ 678. The participles of the Periphrastic Perfect Par, and
Ātम. are formed by the addition of the Perf. Participal forms
of the auxiliary verbs कृ, असु or भू to the base in आम् e. g.
दयालासिवस्, ञ्चत्रिषिवस्. गण्-गणयामासिवस्, गणयामचक्रुवस् &c.

           (c) Past Passive Participles.
