§ 217. Additional instances of the aluk of the Loc.

(a) Aluk is necessary in the case of the words—(1) गो and तुर followed by दिव्; as गविदिव्:, 'stationary in the sky', तुरदिव्: firm in battle; (2) हृद् and दिव् followed by स्पृश्; हृदिस्पृश्, दिविस्पृश् (हृदयं दिवं च स्पृशतीति); (3) अन्त and मध्य followed by गुरु: मध्ये-गुरुः, अन्तेगुरुः and (4) words expressive of parts of the body except कृषीव and सक्थन् and not followed by काम; कण्ठेकाम:, उरसिलोमा (having the hair on the breast: Bah.), but मूर्धसक्थनि-शिरसि, मूर्धे कामो अस्य मुखकामः·

(b)* When a noun in the Loc. is compounded with a verbal derivative and the whole is a name, aluk takes place in most cases; e. g. स्तम्बेरमः (स्तम्बे a kind of grass रमते अत्र) an elephant; कर्णेजपः one who whispers into the ear, a spy; स्वर्चरः a heavenly being; पङ्केरुह a lotus; कुक्षिशय्य, जलेशयः a fish; but कुशवसः, सतदिवस् or सरोवस &c.

(c) There is optional aluk of the Loc. of a word expressive of time when compounded with काल and words ending in the affixed तर, तम and तन; पूर्वाह्णे-हे-काले; पूर्वाह्णे-हे-तरः &c. पूर्वाह्णे-हे तनः·

(d)† When the compound is not a name, aluk is necessary when ज comes after प्राहृद्, शरद्, काल and दिव्; and optional when it comes after वर्ष, हर, शर and धर; as प्राहृदि-शरदि-काले-दिवि-जः; वर्षेजः or वर्षजः produced in the rainy season, &c.

Exceptions‡:—No aluk takes place in the case of verbal derivatives ending in इन् and the words सिद्ध, चन्द्र, and स्थ in the ordinary language; as स्थलशायिन् 'an ascetic,' साद्वद्यवसिद्धः; चक्रचन्द्रः; समस्थः; but छुप्णोऽध्यासिनः &c.
