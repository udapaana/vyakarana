§ 615. There are a few primitive roots, which, though they
take a Desiderative form, do not convey a desiderative sense
(See § 39. b). As has been looked upon as primitive roots,
the Desiderative bases may be derived from them according
to the rules given below; as जुगुप्सिषत 'he wishes to censure'
from जुगुप्स् &c.
