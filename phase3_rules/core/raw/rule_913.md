§ 913. * The infinitive is used with verbs and verbal nouns
meaning 'to wish or desire' provided the agent of it is the same
as that of the verb; को मृग्यहुच्युनुति हरे: दुयूहुयु (Mṛid. I.) who
wishes to snatch away the jaws of the lion; मायुयुयुनै यदुयुविकुन्तुयुयु रयु-
दिद्युनै युयूरुयूत्रुयुङुरोतिदुयुः Bhattṛi. II. 6. But तेषुतुकुदुयुनुयूद्युहुयुतीयु । wish
him to do this, is wrong.
