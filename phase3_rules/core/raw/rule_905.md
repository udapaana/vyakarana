§ 905. The Potential passive participle is used in the sense
of 'what should or ought to be done;' besides this, this parti-
ciple yields the sense of 'fitness, obligation, necessity, capacity,'
&c., the agent being put in the Instrumental case; विसुयुनोयुकुन्तो-
विष्टुं किं कुरुनुयुनं क्युयुयुयुयुनु (Devi Bhāg. IV. 7. 1.) he considered in
his mind what he ought to do; यर्मुःअहुड्युयुयुयुर्णुयुनोरु religious duty ought:

E. S. G. 33.



to be followed; त्युयुयु भुरो बहुनीदुः thou art able to bear this burden;
इन्युयुच्योयुयुहेयुः this rogue deserves to be killed; गन्तुयूयु ते यसुतिरुछुदुयु
नाम यदुनैयुयूरुयूर्णों thou will have to go to Alakā, the habitation
of the lords of Yakṣhas, &c.

Obs.—Sometimes the agent is put in the Genitive case; मयु
सेयुयुयो हरिः Hari is to be served by me; हिदुयुयुनुयुनों भुयुयुनं अन्नं boiled
rice to be eaten by Brāhmaṇas.
