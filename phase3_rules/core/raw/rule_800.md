§ 800 Roots having the sense of motion, real or metaphori-
cal govern the Accusative of the place to which it is directed.
ग्रामं गच्छुति goes to a village; अजवुयुचुरुयुङुयु विच्युचुत्युरु दाव्युदं with his
bow strung he roamed all over the forest; आनन्दुस्य परां काष्ठुमुष्टु-
गच्छुत they reached the highest point of joy; वनस्य कृष्णुयुङूतिं
goes to Kṛṣṇa (discus of him) mentally; अतः प्रभृति चिन्तुयुङोद्युच् स निद्रां
यथो while thus pondering he sank into sleep.

(a) When the motion is real, the Dative may also be used;
मातुलुय ग्रामं वा गच्छुति; but not of words denoting 'a road;'

---

*But when the relation of object and verb is expressed by
the passive termination the noun forming the object is put in
the nominative case; हरिः सेव्युते.

†कुटुछुयुचुन्तुरुच्युच्चुनुत्युङूयुनुयुरो । Pāṇ. II. 3. 5.



पन्थानं गच्छुति only; but उत्युयुनं यथे गच्छुति he reaches the main
road by taking a by-path.
