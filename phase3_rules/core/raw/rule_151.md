§ 151. अन्तर is a pronoun ( and therefore declined like सर्व m. n. f. ) when it means " exterior, outer; or what is to be worn out-side, as a garment"* except when followed by पुर; it is optionally declined like nouns ending in अ in the Nom. pl. and Ab. and Loc. sing. of the mas. e. g. अन्तरे अन्तराः वा पटाः; अन्तरे-अन्तराः वा झटकाः ( garments ). But अन्तरायां पुरि.
