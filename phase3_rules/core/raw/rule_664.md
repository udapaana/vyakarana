§ 664. By means of the suffixes कुङ्, किय, णिच् and कुण
the sense of reproach is conveyed; e. g. स्तेनकुयति, स्तेनकियति
means 'to call a person a thief,' भृत्येयति means 'to call a
person a servant;' so किंकरेयति.
