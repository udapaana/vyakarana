§ 934. The difference between the Two Futures is the
same as that between the Imperfect and the Aorist, the only
difference being that the former refers to a future time and the
latter to a past one. The First or Periphrastic expresses
futurity definitely but not of this day; the Second or Simple
Future expresses futurity indefinitely as also that of to-day;
it is also employed to denote recent and future contiguous
time; as अद्युोयुयुयूर्णों युयुः युयूयुत्युयूति कुरै भरुतुयूनुलितुयूयू (Bhattṛi XXII,)
oh monkey, to-morrow you will go to Ayodhyā governed by
Bharata; आत्युयूयुयुत्युरुस्युयुयो इड्युयु युयुयूरुयूयूदुयूयूः शियुयुयूट् । यातुरुः सहुं मायुयुयूयूयो
होष्युयूमि युयूयुरैणु रुयुष्युयूयूः (Bhattṛi XXII. 14.) they will be delighted
on seeing you and will ask you questions about the welfare of
us two and Sītā; and Bharata also will be greatly pleased;
एते...उच्युयुयूयूतुयुयुः कुपिकुयूतुनुनं (Kir. III. 22) they will be extirpated
by the monkey-bannered one (Arjuna); सत्युयुयूयुयूयु पुञ्युयूलुयूयो S'ak.
IV.) Śakuntala will go (goes) to-day; युयूविष्युयूति जिज्युयूयूनै यु
हुतुयूयूनुयुयूयुयु समुयु (Bhattṛi XVI. 13) if my sons are killed I will
die or kill the enemy, &c.
