§ 959. Sometimes the Potential is used without a subject
when it is used in the sense of 'a precept or advice'; आयुयूयुर्युनुयूं यनुयुं
रक्तुनुयूरुयु रक्तुनैन्युनुरुयूति । मुयूत्युयूयूयुनुयूं युयूतुनुयूं रक्तुनुयूरुयूयूति युयूनुयूति ॥ ('a man)
should save money for adversity, he should serve his wife at the
cost of his wealth and himself even at the expense of his wife
and wealth; युयूयुनोजुनुनं विश्युयुयूयूत्तुदुयूयूनुयूनुयूरुयुः (Obs) should serve
Brāhmaṇas with all that they like, without being jealous.

THE BENEDICTIVE MOOD.
