§ 501. त is inserted after the reduplicative syllable in the case of roots beginning with अ and ending in a conjunct consonant, in that of अह् 'to pervade' and चक्ष् 'to go'; consequently त् after is changed to आ; अञ्ज् अनज्+अ=आनज्+अ+अ=आनञ्ज, similarly आनन्द 1 sing. of अन्द्, आनर्द of अर्द्, &c.
