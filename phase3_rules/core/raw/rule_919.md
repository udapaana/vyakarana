§ 919. Besides the general sense given above the Present
tense from the context has the following senses:—

(a) † It is sometimes used in the sense of 'immediate futu-
rity,' कुयु गमिष्युयूमि when wilt thou go? एयु गच्छुयूमि Here I go
(i. e. shall go); ऊरुध्युनै मियुनै छुहुनोत्तुस्युच् I shall die an hour after.

(b) It may also be used to denote an action which is recently
completed; कुयु त्युनै नगुरुयुयूनुयूतेस—यमुयूनुयुच्छुयूमि when didst thou
come from the city? Here I come (have come just now).

(c) In narrations it is used for the Past tense; युयुयुनो द्युयुनै
कुस्युरु the vulture says, 'who art thou'?

(d) Sometimes it is used to denote a habitual or repeated
action; यदुयुयुयूनुत्तुयो जीवति.
