§ 236. Sometimes the second member of an Upapada Tat. my be a Namulanta or the verbal indeclinable in अप्; as स्वादुकृत्य भुङ्क्ते 'he eats having sweetened the food;' अग्रेभोजं 'having dined first;' sometimes this compound is optional. मूलकोपदंशं or मूलकेन उपदंशं भुङ्क्ते 'he eats the condiment with a raddish,' &c.
