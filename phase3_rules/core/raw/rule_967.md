§ 967. तयू 'likewise' often supplies the place of च; रायूयूयूयूयू
लुक्ष्युयूणुयु Rāma and Lakshmana; अयूयूयूयूयूदुयूयूयूयूयू च युयूत्युयूयूयूयूतिरुयूयूयूयू
both Aṅgastravishtā and Pratyutpannamatī; तयूयूयू हि means 'for
instance, to be more plain;' तयूयूयू च 'likewise;' both are often used
introducing quotations.
