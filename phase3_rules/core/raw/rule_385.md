§ 385. The roots contained in the first nine classes and a few of the tenth are primitive roots, while almost all roots of the tenth class, the Causals, Desideratives, Frequentatives, Denominatives, and the roots णुच्, लुट्, किट्, पुट्, पुट्, चुट्, and चुण्, are comprised under the head of Derivative roots.
