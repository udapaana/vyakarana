§ 311. The feminine of nouns denoting 'a male' is formed by adding ई when the wife of that male is meant; गोपस्य स्त्री गोपी; शूद्री (sometimes शूद्राणी also) 'wife of a Sudra'.

(a) but not of nouns ending in पालक; as गोपालिका 'the wife of a cowherd' (but गोपाल has गोपाली); अश्वपालिका 'the wife of a horse-groom'.

(b) सूर्य has सूर्या 'the divine wife of Surya;' but सौरी i.e. कुन्ती who was a mortal.
