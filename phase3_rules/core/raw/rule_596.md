§ 596. (a) The forms of the two Futures, Conditional and
also the Benedictive (especially भावे—Passive, briefly
stated below.

(b) The Aorist optionally forms the Futures, Conditional as
well as the Benedictive of the Passive by changing their vowels to Vṛddhi
and then by आ regarded to them invariably; in the case of roots ending
in a vowel, the vowel is lengthened before इ; and the य् inserted
between the root and this इ; दा-दायिष्यते, दायि, दायात्; वच्य
च्यते, युज्यते-युज्+य, चाकरे 'to do'—कारिष्यते, कारि, कार्यात् इस्-सम् &c. करि-
कारिष्यते, चिनो वृहीस्यति, दूषते; चरिते, मिनो, सूयसाते,
वधैत्, सूत्रायते, अवच्छुम्, स्वाच्छुम्, वद्धित etc. हूल्-चूहीवर्षतु,
सूयोत्सवस्य &c, सूयते 'to fill, to accomplish'—सूर्यते, सूयिष्ट; but strictly
forbidden with them; see § 468; वु these docl only.

(c) The First Future, Conditional and the 5th Variety of the
Aorist containing a nasal or a र्, त्, स् of the Passive, &c.
