§ 227. † Words ending in the terminations तलय, अभीय, य and धन्य and words having a similar meaning, may be compounded with any subanta not importing a particular kind; e. g. भोज्यो-पाय् 'any hot eatable', तुल्यश्वेतः 'of a similar white colour,' सदृशसुभ्रतः, &c.; but भोज्यः भोजनः as the latter word refers to a kind of food here.
