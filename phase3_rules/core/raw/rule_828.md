§ 828. * The object governed by an infinitive mood not
actually used but implied, is put in the Dative; फुलुत्युयो यात्युति i. e.
(फुटुयु) हुयुरु 'goes for (in order to bring) fruits;' हुहुकुयुयु
प्रयुछुगुयुः (i. e युट्युदुयुमुयुट्युदुट्युट्युलुयुदुदुङु) 'We bow down to Nṛsiṁha
(so propitiate him)'

(a) The Dative of an abstract noun formed from a root may
be used to express the sense of the Infinitive of the same root;
शुयुयुयु यात्युति 'goes to perform a sacrifice;' त्युयुयुयु संचुयुत्युयुर्णों
(Rag. I. 7.) 'who had amassed wealth in order to give it
away,' &c.
