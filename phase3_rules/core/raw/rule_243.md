§243. †अष्टन् becomes अष्टा when followed by कपाल and गो and the compound so formed conveys the sense of 'an oblation' and 'yoked to' respectively; अष्टाकपाल: 'prepared or offered in eight pans' (पुरोडाश:); अष्टागवं (शकटम्) 'a carriage with eight bullocks yoked to it'.
