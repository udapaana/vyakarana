§ 708. Past Active participles are derived from the P. P.
Passive Participle in त् or न by changing it to them the affix वत्; e.g.

Roots.          P. P. Ptc.      P. Ac. Ptc.

भू to be            भूतः           भूतवत् one or that has been
कृ to do            कृतः           कृतवत् „     „      done
कृ to scatter       कीर्णः          कीर्णवत् „     „      scattered
दिद् to cut          दिन्नः          दिन्नवत् „     „      cut
etc.                etc.           etc.                etc.

(e) Participle of the Future Tense.
