§ 715. Roots having अ for their penultimate and ending in
a consonant of the labial class take the affix य; शाप्—शाप्यः; दुष्—
दुष्यः; रप्—रप्यः, &c.

(a) When वध् is preceded by अपि, य् (changed to इ) is inserted
between इ and प्; अपिवधो—आपेधयः 'what ought or is fit to be
killed;' य is also inserted when द्रुष् is preceded by उप if the
meaning be 'to praise;' उपद्रुष्यः साधुः 'a Sādhu ought to be
praised;' but उपकुष्यं धनं 'wealth ought to be acquired.'
