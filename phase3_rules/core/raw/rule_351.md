§ 351. (a) All the numerals from 20-99, monosyllabic words in ई and such as are formed by the affix ता; विंशतिः, श्रीः; नम्रता &c.

(b) The synonyms of भूमि, सरित्, लता and वनिता and these words themselves.

Exceptions:—स्रोतस् n. and वाहस् n. both meaning a 'river'.
