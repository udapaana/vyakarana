§ 4. The consonants are divided into sparś'a or mutes (those involving a complete closure or contact and not an approximate one of the organs of pronunciation), Antastha (or intermediate, e. the Semivowels,) and úshman or sibilants.

These are represented by thirty-three syllabic signs arranged as below:—

                    gh, ङ n.

(d) Sonant Aspirate—ह h.

Besides these we have two more characters occurring in the
Veda, viz. ळ and ऴ (often substituted for ल and ळ; as कॆळ for
कॆलॆ, सीळूरॆ for सीलूरॆ &c.). In Maráthi ळ is generally substi-
tuted for the final ल of Sanskrit words.
