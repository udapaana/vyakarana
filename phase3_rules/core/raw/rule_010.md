§ 10. A svara or vowel is that which can be pronounced without the help of any other letter; a vyañjana or consonant is that which is pronounced with the help of a vowel. The consonants, therefore, are written with a slanting nether stroke to denote their imperfect character.

(a) Hence the consonants are given, in the system of Tāṇṇi, with an अ added to them for the sake of pronunciation.
