§ 762. This Participle is generally used at the end of com-
pounds; श्वोन्यस्य हतः 'he was pelted to death,' बन्दिनाथं गृहीतः
(Vīks. I. 1.) She was taken a captive;' प्रभङ्क्तुं प्रसमाम्
(Bhaṭṭi. I. 2.) 'He totally exterminated his enemies, &c.'
