§ 549. Bases with initial vowels:—

(a) If a root begins with a vowel and ends in a single con-
sonant, the consonant is reduplicated and इ added to it in the
reduplicative syllable: अद्-आद्-आदिद्द्त्; आप्-आप्-आपिप्त्; &c.

(b) If it ends in a conjunct consonant with ध्, र् or द् for
its first member, the second member of the conjunct is redupl-



cated. उन्द्-उचन्द्-उचिन्द्द् and finally औचिन्द्त् 3 Sing, similarly
आविन्द्त् from अञ्ज् (considered as अजद्, otherwise आविद्त्),
आविञ्छत् from अञ्च्; आविज्जत् from अञ्ज्, &c.

(c) The roots उच्, आञ्च्, अञ्च्, अञ्, आद्, अर्ध् Ātm. and some
others, substitute अ for इ in the reduplicative syllable; 3 sing.
अौजत्, आवच्त्, आजघत्, आञ्चवत्, आअर्त्, आर्द्त्, &c.
