§ 719. The root वह् not denoting an instrument for carrying,
and ऋ in the sense of 'a master or a Vaishya' take य; वह्यं 'a
carriage or a vehicle;' but वाह्यः (वह्+एयत्) 'what can be borne,'
आर्यः 'a master or a Vaishya;' but आर्येः (ऋ+एयत्) 'fit to be
approached or adored.'
