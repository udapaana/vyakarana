§ 845 ~ The words स्तोकं 'a little' अल्युयु 'a little,' ङुच्छु 'diffi-
culty,' and कुतिपुयु 'some' when used in an adverbial sense with
verbs, are used in the Ablative with a sense of 'beginning
कुतिपुयुनं युयुदु let off with little; similarly अल्युनुतु अलुयुयुन्युच्युयुच्युः ङुच्युयुयुनुयु
कुयुष्टुयुहुयो छुत्तुं dose with difficulty; कुतिपुयुनं कुतिपुयुयुयुयु मायुयु. but
स्तोकुनं विषुयुनं हतुः killed with a little poison; when used adver-
bially they take the Accusative also; स्तोकं गच्छुति goes a little.
