§ 904. Besides showing simple futurity participle expresses
intention or purpose; अनुयुयुस्युयुच् मुतितुनुयुयूरु wishing to follow
the daughter of the sage; दुयूस्युयुच् wishing to give, वुन्युयुयुरु
हरिकुयूनुनु intending exercising, as it were, to tame the wild beasts.

POTENTIAL PASSIVE PARTICIPLES.
