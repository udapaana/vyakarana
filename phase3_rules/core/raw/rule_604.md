§ 604. Roots ending in आ (घ्, घ् or ऐ changeable to आ; see
§ 459) and the roots ऋ 'to go,' हृ 'to be ashamed,' हु 'to go,'
(9 cl.), 'to flow' (4. cl. Â.) and प्लू 'to choose, to go,' insert
the augment व् before अय् and their vowel takes Guṇa substitute;
दापयति-दा, धा, हा; पावयति हृ-हेपयति, हू, &c. कू—क्लाव-
यति; ऋति-एपयति, प्लू—प्लोपयति, प्लू—प्लोपयति.
