II. INDECLINABLE PARTICIPLES

(a) Indeclinable Past Participles.

§ 744. The Indeclinable Past Participles are of the nature
of gerunds. They fall under two heads:—(1) Those derived
by affixing क्त्वा to the simple root, and (2) Those derived by
means of य affixed to the root compounded with prepositions or
words used prepositionally; गम्—गत्वा 'having gone,' भू with
परिदेवस्त्र 'having' 'having served;' &c.

(b) THE ADVERBIAL INDECLINABLE PARTICIPLE

OR

The Gerund in अम्.

§ 761. There is another Indeclinable Participle having the
same sense as that in क्त्वा, formed with the affix अम्. Before
this the vowel of the derivative verbal base generally undergoes
the same changes as it does before the इ of the Pass. Aorist
3rd sing.; नी—नयाम् 'having carried or led,' दा—दायाम् 'having
given,' भू—भावाम्, भिद्—भेदाम्, भाह्—भाह्राम्, गम्—गमाम्, &c.

§ 762. This Participle is generally used at the end of com-
pounds; श्वोन्यस्य हतः 'he was pelted to death,' बन्दिनाथं गृहीतः
(Vīks. I. 1.) She was taken a captive;' प्रभङ्क्तुं प्रसमाम्
(Bhaṭṭi. I. 2.) 'He totally exterminated his enemies, &c.'

§ 763. The participles in क्त्वा and अम्* when repeated yield
the sense of repetition of the action or condition expressed by
the verb; स्मृत्वा स्मृत्वा or स्मरं स्मारयुम् 'having repeatedly remem-
bered; पीत्वा पीत्वा or पाय पायाम् 'having drunk again and again;'
similarly अच्युत्वाच्युत्वा or अचुयाचुयाम् or पोजुत्यपोजुत्य or पोयुपयम्, छोरयुछोरयुम्
आपुद्य, गम्—गन्त्वा गन्त्वा or गम् गमाम् or गत् गतम्, छत्युस्—छत्युस्त्वा छत्युस्त्वा
or छत्युं छत्युम् or छत्युं छत्युम्, पठत् पठतम्, जागृ—जागरं जागरम्, † &c.
