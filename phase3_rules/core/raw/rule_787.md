§ 787. When an indeclinable used with an adjectival force
takes the place of the verb, the object governed by it is put in
the Nominative case.* विषवृक्षोऽपि संवर्ध्यं घातं छेत्तुमर्हसि (Kum.
V. 71) it is not fit to cut down even a poisonous tree having
first reared it up; here the indeclinable अलमुद् is equivalent to
न इच्छते and the whole sentence to न इतं संवर्ध्यं छेत्तुयुद् अर्हति (न
इच्छते) यस्ति विषवृक्षः अपि।

Concord of the Adjective with the Substantive.
