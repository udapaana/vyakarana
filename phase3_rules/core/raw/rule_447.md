§ 447. The guttural of the reduplicative syllable is changed to the corresponding palatal (subject to the above rule) and ह to ज; e. g. कृ=चकृ; गम्=जगम्; पवि=तिपवि; सक्त्=तसक्त्=चसक्त्, हृणु &c.
