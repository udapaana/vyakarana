§ 670. (a) The आ of आन is changed to इ in the case of
the root आस् (2. cl.) 'to sit;' Pre. P. आसीन.

. (b) आन added to the roots यु and यज् forms nouns; e. g.
पवमानः 'that which purifies, hence, the wind' (cf. Rāg. VIII. 9,)
also, 'one of the sacred fires;' यजमानः 'one who sacrifices.'
