§ 922 With the words यावुट्, तावुट् and others having a
similar sense, the Present is sometimes used in the sense of the
Future perfect; यावुस्युरु त्युयुयुनों न युयुयूति तावुयुदुयूयुयूनुरु before he sees
you, move away (before he shall have seen you, &c.).

(b) † With the particles युयुयु and यावुयु the Present has the
sense of the Future when certainty is indicated; यावुयूयूते साराभिदुयूट्
स्युयुरुयू (Rag. X. 25.) it will endeavour to accomplish your
object. युयूयु वसुदुयूरोयो जयुति बहुयूयूयु (S'ak. VII. 33) he will
conquer the earth consisting of seven continents. युरुयूहुयूते तवु
वुच्युकुनै नमुः। Kir. VIII. 8.
