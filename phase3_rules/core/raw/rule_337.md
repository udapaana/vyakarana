§ 337. The terminations used to form derivative bases, in Sanskrit are distinguished into two classes; (1) Kṛit (कृत्) or Primary Affixes and (2) Taddhita (तद्धित) or Secondary affixes. Kṛit are those affixes that are added to verbs, and the Nominal Bases formed by their means are called PRIMARY NOMINAL BASES; while TADDHITA affixes are those that are added to substantives, primary or derived from roots, and the bases formed with them are called SECONDARY NOMINAL BASES (vide § 179).
