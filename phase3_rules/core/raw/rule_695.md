§ 695. पीयः has पूतः when it is used with 'one's own limbs.'
पूतं क्षौमं, and पीयः or पूनः in other cases, as पीयः or पूनः रथः;
when it is preceded by a preposition, इ is not substituted, and the
vowel; प्रस्यातः; but when it is used with अङ्गै or ज्चृपतु with the
preposition आ, इ is necessarily substituted; आपीतः अस्यः; भूयौनं
ज्चृषै.
