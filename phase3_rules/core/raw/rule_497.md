§ 497. The final ऋ, short or long, is changed to अर before a vowel weak termination and ई to इय or य as it is preceded by a conjunct consonant or not; as ऋ→अर→इरुव ! dual; तितिर्+इरे=तितिरिरे, लिभिर्+इरे=लिभिरिरे, &c.
