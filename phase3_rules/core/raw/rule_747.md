§ 747. Veṭ roots, except प्रच्छु which takes इ necessarily, and
रुच्, पूर् which reject इ, the five roots given under § 472 and
the roots marked with an indicatory उ* admit of इ optionally
before क्त्वा e. g.

Roots.                  Ind. P. Ptc. or Ger.

मृज् to cleanse           मृजित्वा, मृष्ट्वा
गो to enter              गाहित्वा, गाढ्वा
