§244. ‡All the rules given above for the change of the finals of the Tat. compounds do not hold good in the case of the Neg. Tat.; e. g. न राजा अराजा one not a king; न सखा: असखा, &c.

(a) But in the case of the word पथिन् the final इन् is optionally dropped and अ added; अपथं or अपथ्या: 'absence of a road'. पथिन् when so modified in a Tat. is neu.; §(but अपथ्य: देश:).
