§ 153. पूर्व 'prior, eastern', अपर 'posterior, western', दक्षिण 'southern', उत्तर 'superior, northern, subsequent', अपर 'other', and अधर 'inferior, lower,' when they denote relation in space, time or person with reference to a particular thing or point of time &c. and are not a name, are pronouns† and are then declined like सर्व; but they are optionally declined like राम in the Nom. plu. and Ab. and Loc. sing. when mas. e. g. Nom. पूर्वे पूर्वौ पूर्वे or पूर्वाः Ab. पूर्वात्-पूर्वेस्मात् पूर्वेभ्याम् पूर्वेभ्यः; Loc. पूर्वे-पूर्वेस्मिन्; &c., but दक्षिणाः गायकाः ( skilful musicians ) only, and not दक्षिणे, for दक्षिण does not show any relation here; so उत्तराः कुरवः and not उत्तरे, as उत्तराः is a name here.
