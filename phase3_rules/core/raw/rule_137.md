§ 137. The optional forms of इदम् and एतद् in the Acc., Inst. Sing. and Gen. & Loc. Duals are to be used when there is anōddeś'a* i. e. their proper forms have already been used in a previous clause; e. g. अनेन व्याकरणमधीतं एनं छन्दोऽध्यापय 'he has studied grammar, teach him prosody,' अनयोः पवित्रं कुले, एनयोः प्रभूतं स्वम् , 'The family of them both is pure, and their wealth ample.'
