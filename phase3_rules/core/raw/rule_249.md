§ 249. Obs.—The Bah. compounds are also further divided into two kinds; तद्गुणसंविज्ञानबहुव्रीहि: and अतद्गुणसंविज्ञानबहुव्रीहि:. That in which the sense of the attributive member is also present is called Tadguṇasaṃvijñāna. पीताम्बरं हरिमाहुय is an instance of the first; here such a हरि is meant as has the yellow silk garment actually on. चित्रगुं गोपं आनय is of the other;
