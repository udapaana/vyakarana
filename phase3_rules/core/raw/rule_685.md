§ 685. Set roots (subject to § 684), and all derivative bases
admit of इ; roots of the Tenth Class and causals reject their
final अय, Par. Frequentatives their final अ, and Ātम.

Frequentatives their य:—

Roots. P. P. Ptc.                       Roots. P. P. Ptc.

शङ्क्—शङ्कित 'suspected.'                कथ्—कथित 'told.'
उद्—उदित 'spoken.'                      प्रथ्—प्रथित 'spread.'



Roots. P. P. Ptc.          Roots. P. P. Ptc.

एष्—एषित 'increased.'       बोधय् cau. Base of बुध्, बोधित
                            'made to know.'
कम्प्—कम्पित 'shaken.'        चिकीर्ष des. b. of कृ; चिकीर्षित
                            'desired to do.'
चुर्—चोरित 'stolen, deceived.' बोबुध् Atम. Fre. Base of बुध्,
                            बोबुधित 'frequently known.'
मृश्—स्पृशित 'taken.'         बोभूय—Par. Fre. Base of भू,
                            बोभूत &c.

Exceptions:—इष्, उष्, चष्, 'to go, to till,' &c., चित् 'to know
to observe,' &c., विद्, मिद्, स्तृ, भृत्, भृष्, भी, पी, हीय, स्यै, प्रिह्य,
चुष्, डूष्, दुष्, सुष्, रुष्.

N. B. There are many Set roots which reject the intermediate
इ but as some of them form their P. P. participles in न and
others irregularly, they will be given in their proper places.
