§ 49. (a) Visarga, preceded by any vowel except अ or आ and followed by a vowel or soft consonant, is changed to र्: हरिः + जयति = हरिर्जयति Hari conquers; so भानुरुदेति the sun rises; गौरागच्छति a cow or bull comes.

Exception:—The Visarga of the particles भो:, भगोः and अयो: obeys § 48; e. g. भो + अच्युत = भो अच्युत or भोयच्युत Oh Achyuta; भगो नमस्ते Bhago! a bow to thee; अयो यान्ति Oh you go.
