§ 879. The locative or Genitive Absolute may be used to
express the sense of the English particles 'when, while, since,
although,' &c. (and may thus supply the place of a pluperfect
tense); एवं तयोः परुस्युरं वदुतोः while they two were thus talking;
द्युद् युयुयुनं अवुसुरि भुवुयुभुयुद्युयुद्युच्युकुयोयुयु (Meg. 40); Thou wilt accomplish
the rest of thy journey when the sun rises again.
