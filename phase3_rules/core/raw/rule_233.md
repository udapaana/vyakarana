§ 233. Compounds of the following words with verbal indeclinables are called Gati.

(a) † The particles उरी, उरती, बोल्ड, वष्ट, स्वाहा, स्वधा, प्राद्, आविष् and अस् and the word कारिका meaning an action; as उरीकृत्य, उरतीकृत्य 'having accepted; वषट्कृत्य 'having uttered the word Vashat;' प्रादूर्भूय, कारिकाकृत्य 'having done the act.'

(b) ‡ Onomatopoetic words not followed by इति, as खाट्कृत्य; but खाटिति कृत्वा (निरुक्तिवत्).
