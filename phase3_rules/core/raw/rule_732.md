§ 732. The following words are derived from the root यह्
with the affix क्यप्—अवयह्यः; प्रयह्यः, पदे 'two technical terms in
grammar;' पयुक्तः 'birds restrained from free motion, such as
parrots, &c.' प्रवायुरेव्येन परोहत्राक्ता उपक्षये। Sid. Kau.
ग्रामयुद्धे सेना 'an army stationed outside a village;' आर्युवाहास्ते
आयुयुद्धे: तिर्व्यामुद्धीव उत्यर्थः (Sid. Kau.) 'siding with the noble.'
See Rāg. II. 43.
