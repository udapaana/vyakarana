§ 388. अ (शप्) is added on to the roots of the First or भ्वादि class* before the terminations. Before this अ the penultimate short and the final vowel of a root take their Guṇa substitute; e. g.

बुध्+ति=बुध्+अ+ति=बोधति; जि+अ+ति=जे+अ+ति=जयति, &c.
