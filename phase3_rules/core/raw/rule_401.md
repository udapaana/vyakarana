§ 401. Some roots of the 10th class exclusively take the Ātmanepada i. e. even when the fruit of the action expressed by them does not accrue to the agent. These are:—अर्थ् to request, to desire, कुह् to astonish, to deceive, चिन्त् to be conscious of, to think, दंश् to bite, दृश् (or दर्श् according to some) to see, to bite, दुह् or दुह् to accumulate, तन्त्र् to support a family, मन्त्र् to counsel secretly, मूष् to search, to hunt, to seek, ग्रथ् to take, to string together, तर्ज् and तर्क् to reprove, वद् and गल्व् to injure, to hurt, विच्छद् to kill (विछ्द् according to some), चित्र् to measure, घृष् to desire, to contract, मृत् to kill, तुद् to fear, सूच् to praise, यज् to worship, रभ् to guess, मृष् to strike, सुव् to look at, to inspect, कुत्स् to reproach, छुत् (according to some खुट्) to cut, भुल् to drop down, भृट् to see, to expound, खुद् to abstain from giving, to muddle, खुट् to cut, वज्ज् to deceive, भूष् to have the power of generation, to be eminent,



उर्द् to gratify, दिन् to bewail, यु to know, विद् to know, to be conscious of, मुद् to stop, हुष् to censure, and हुत्स् to smile improperly.
