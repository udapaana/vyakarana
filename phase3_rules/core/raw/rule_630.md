§ 630. When a root ends in a nasal preceded by अ and the
nasal is not dropped, व (changeable to an Anusvāra or the
nasal of the class to which the letter following it belongs) is
inserted between the reduplicative अ and the first radical
consonant; the अ of the reduplicative syllable thus remains un-
changed (against § 629 b.).

गम्-गम्य, नयम्-नयंयते or नङ्गम्यते, जन्-जन्य-वंजन्यते
or जञ्जन्यते, but when जन्=ज्ञातुं, the Ā. Freq. base is जाजाय,
3rd sing. जाजायते.

(a) The same rule applies to the roots चर्, फण्, गुप्, जप्,
वंद्; दंश्, मञ्ज् and पद्. चर् and फण् after inserting व् change
the अ of the following syllable to अ; चर्—चर्य—वचर्य=वचर्यते
or अचर्यते; or चञ्चूर्यते by § 694. फण्=फल्य=वफञ्चयते or
पेफुल्यते or पफूल्यते; वंद्=वद्-वद्य=ववद्यते or ववन्द्वयते वुग्-
वञ्गयते or वङ्गायते.
