§ 168. The ordinals are declined regularly except:—

प्रथम ( see § 156 ) and द्वितीय and तृतीय which are optionally declined like pronouns in the Dat. Ab. Gen. and Loc. sing ; e. g. द्वितीयस्मै-द्वितीयाय, &c. द्वितीययाः:-द्वितीयस्याः: &c.

NUMERAL ADVERBS.
