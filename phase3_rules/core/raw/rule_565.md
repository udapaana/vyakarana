§ 565. (a) Anit roots not belonging to any of the preced-
ing Varieties take this Form. Anit roots that optionally
take any of the preceding Varieties and Veṭ roots optionally
belong to this Variety.

---

## Footnotes

take the 5th as Seṭ.

(1) ऋ and इ, when Parasmaipadi, belong to the Fifth Variety.

may take Fourth or Fifth Form in the Ātmanepada.
