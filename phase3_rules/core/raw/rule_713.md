§ 713. The final अय of roots of the tenth class and of cau-
sals, the final अ of Atम. Freq. Bases, when the य is preceded
by a vowel and the whole य when it is preceded by a consonant,
are dropped before अनीय. To Desiderative bases it is added
without any change; e. g.

कथ्—                  कथनीयः what must, or ought to be told.
चुर्—                  चोरणीयः „       „       stolen.
बोधय cau. of बुध्—बोधनीयः „       „       made to know.
बोबुध् Atम.

Freq. Base of „ बोबुधनीयः „       „       known frequently.
