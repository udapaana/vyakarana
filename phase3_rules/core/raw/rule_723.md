§ 723. The roots इ 1. 2. P. 'to go,' स्तु, शास्, इ 5. P. A. ह्व,
दुह्, and roots having ऋ short for their penultimate, except कृष्
and वृत्, take the affix क्यप् (य) in the same sense as यत्. When
a root ends in a short vowel, त् is inserted between the final
vowel of the root; e. g. इ+य=ए+त्+य='worthy of being served;'
स्तुत्यः 'deserving praise;' शास्—शिष्यः 'fit to be instructed;' दुह्—दुह्यः;
इ—आइत्यः; दुह्—दुह्यः 'worthy of being served;' भृ—भृत्यः; दृश्—दृश्यः
'fit to be increased as wealth, &c.' but कल्पः (कृष्+य) 'able;'
वद्—वर्यः (वृत्+एयत्) 'that ought to be told or said.'
