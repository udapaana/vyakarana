§ 955. † When hope is expressed without the use of the
word कुवितु the potential is generally used; कुयूयूयुयुं युनै युयुवुजीत भुवुयुरु
it is my desire (I hope) that it should grow; but कुविदु युयुवुजीति (I
hope he lives.
