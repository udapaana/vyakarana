§ 839. (a) ‡ The place or time from which the distance of another place or point of time is to be expressed is put in the Ablative case; the distance in space being put in the Nominative or the Locative and that in time in the Locative; वनात् ग्रामो योजनं योजने वा (Sid. Kau.) the village is a yojana from the forest; नवग्रुन्नतः सांकाश्यं द्वसति योजनानि चतुर्षु योजनेषु वा (Mb.); युयुनुदु शुत्युयु the distant life of Marjepakāla separated by five Margaṭhlas is a month (at the interval of a month) from that hundred separated by one, &c.

(b)  The  Ablative is  also  used in  'portion  and  subtracti
कुटुयुट्  of  two  classes  युयुयुट्  युयुयुयुयुयुयुनुयुयुनै  one  तुत्युयुयुयुयुनै,  &c.
