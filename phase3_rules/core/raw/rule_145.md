§ 145. Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the pronominal adjectives अन्य, इतर and पर; e. g. अन्योन्य, इतरेतर, and परस्पर. These are generally used in the sing. and also adverbially e. g. परस्परेण स्पृहणीयशोभौ &c. ( Rag. VII. 14); परस्परं विवद्न्ते &c. They are more commonly used as the first members of compounds; as अन्योन्यक्रोधाजननात् बभूव &c. (Ku. I, 44; इतरेतरयोगात् (Sis. X. 24.) &c.
