§ 194. (a) When the names of deities well known to be in constant association with each other are compounded, आ is substituted for the final vowel of the preceding word, except in the case of the word वायु×; e.g. मित्रावरुणौ, सूर्याचन्द्रमसौ, अग्निमरुतौ, &c. but अग्निवायू or वाय्वग्नी.

(b) The word अग्नि followed by the word सोम or वरुण lengthens its इ; as अग्नीषोमौ, अग्नीवरुणौ.
