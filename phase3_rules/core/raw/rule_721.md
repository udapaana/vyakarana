§ 721. जृ takes this affix with the negative particle prefixed
to it and forms अजर्यं 'what cannot grow old;' but this must
qualify संगतं 'friendship;' cf. तेन संगतमेण रामयोरयं कथं जरति।
Bhaṭṭi. VI. 53. In अत्रैरर्जर्यं जरसंबोध्यमानेन नित्यसंगत्वात् Aṅg.
XVIII. 7, the word अजर्यं can be construed as an adverb; but if
does not qualify संगतं, it will take the affix य; as अजरिता कन्या.
