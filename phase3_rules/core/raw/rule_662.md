§ 662. The notion of 'desiring' is expressed by adding
a root to a root; e. g. 'to eat salt,' लवणुप्यति = लवण+यु. यु
meaning 'to take' or 'to desire', so पुष्पयति, लवणगृह्यति =
लवण+गृह्, similarly सुकर्षति, &c.
