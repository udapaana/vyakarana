§ 590. There are three kinds of Passive Verbs:—(1) the
Passive properly so called (कर्मणि प्र०), as राघेण क्रयं दीयते;
(2) an Impersonal Passive (भावे प्र०), as गच्यते 'it is gone;'
and (3) a Reflexive (आत्मनेपदी प्र०), as ओदनं पच्यते 'rice
is cooked'
