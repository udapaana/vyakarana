§ 485. The roots कृत्, वृत्, गृध्, वृध् and वृश् take इ optionally when followed by an Ārdhadhātuka (Non-conjugational termination) beginning with स except in the Aorist; कृत्—कर्तिष्याम, कर्त्स्याम; अकर्तिष्यम्, अकर्त्स्याम Cond. &c.
