§ 24. ए, ऐ, ओ and औ, when followed by a vowel, are changed
to अय्, अय्, आय्, and आव् respectively†; e. g. हरे + ए = हरये for
Hari; विष्णो + ए = विष्णवे for Vishnu; ने + अक: = नायक: A leader;
गो + अक: = गावक: the purifier ‡, i. e. fire,

(a) य् or व् at the end of a word and preceded by अ or आ is
optionally dropped when followed by a vowel or a soft consonant§; e. g. हरे + एहि = हरयेहि or हरे एहि; विष्णो + इह = विष्णविह or
विष्णो इह; श्रिये + उदक: = श्रियोदक: or श्रिया उदक: intent on
getting money; धुरी + उत्क: = धुरावत्क: or धुरा उत्क: longing to
see the preceptor.

Note—Two vowels brought together by the disappearance of
an intervening consonant or visarga do not coalesce.

(b) गो and नौ, when followed by a termination beginning
with य्, become गव् and नाव् respectively ‡; e. g. गो + य् = गव्य
produced from a cow as milk, ghee &c. नो + य् = नाव्य accessible
by a boat, navigable

N. B. This rule holds good, in the case of roots, only when
the गो or नौ is brought in by virtue of a termination; × e. g.
छु + य् = छो + य् = छव्य fit to be lopped off. अवरुद्ध + य् = अवरुद्धो
+ य् = अवरुद्धव्य which ought necessarily to be cut off.
