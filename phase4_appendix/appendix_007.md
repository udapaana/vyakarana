§ 7. In the case of metres regulated by syllabic instants (@deva[मात्रा]) or @deva[जाति], each line is divided into groups of four @deva[मात्रा] called @deva[मात्रा-गण]. They are 8 in number viz. @deva[म], @deva[र], @deva[न], @deva[स], @deva[भ], @deva[य], @deva[ज], @deva[त].

The symbol ∪ stands for a light syllable equivalent to one @deva[मात्रा]; and ऽ stands for a long or heavy syllable equivalent to two @deva[मात्रा]. Symbolically they are represented as under:

- @deva[म]: ऽ ऽ
- @deva[र]: ऽ ∪ ∪
- @deva[न]: ∪ ∪ ऽ
- @deva[स]: ∪ ∪ ∪ ∪
- @deva[भ]: ऽ ∪
- @deva[य]: ∪ ऽ
- @deva[ज]: ∪ ∪ ∪
- @deva[त]: ∪
