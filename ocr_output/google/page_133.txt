N. B. Compounds like राजपूजितः, राजमतः, &c. ought to bo
considered as Instrumental Tatpurushas.
Exceptions.
(1) If the word denoting a quality ends in तर it does combine
with a noun in the Genitive, and the तर is dropped; सर्वेषां
श्वेततरः सर्वश्वेतः whiter than all others; similarly सर्वेषां महत्तरः
सर्वमहान् &c.
( 2 ) *The words द्वितीय, तृतीय, चतुर्थ and तुर्य are compounded
with nouns signifying the whole of which they are parts, and
are optionally placed first; द्वितीयं भिक्षायाः द्वितीयभिक्षा, मिक्षाद्विती-
यम् half of the quantity of alms; but द्वितीयं भिक्षायाः ( begging
alms & second time ) भिक्षुकस्य.
Note.—-Compounds like द्वितीयभिक्षा, पूर्वकाय (see further
on) &c. are not strictly Gen. Tat, as the case of the first
word determines the name, but mere Tat.; some call these
Nom. Tat. ( प्रथामतत्पुरुष ).
