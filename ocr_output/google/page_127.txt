II.
TATPURUSHA OB THE DETERMINATIVE COMPOUNDS,
§ 200. When a compound consists of two members and the
first determines the sense of the other, that is called Tatpurusha.
§ 201. According to the grammatical nature of the first or
attributive member the Tatpurushs is divided into six varieties:—
(1) Patpurusha proper or the Inflectional, (2) Nañ or the
Negative Tatpurusha, (3) Karmadharaya or the Appositional
including 'Dwigu'. (4.5) Prádî and Gati or the Prepositional
compounds, and (6) Upapada. These are either nouns, adjectives
or abverbs.
