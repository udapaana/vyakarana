GENDERS OF TATPURUSHA COMPOUNDS.
$245. As a general rule a Tatpurusha compound follows
the gender of the final noun.
Exceptions f:-
(a) Compounds with प्राप्त and आपन्न for their irst member
and the Prâdi compounds follow the gender of the words they
qualify; प्राप्तजीषिकः नरः, प्राप्तजीविका स्त्री, निष्कौशाम्बिः पुरुषः &c.
(b) A Tatpurushs ending in रात्र, अह्न and अह is masculins,
except when रात्र is preceded by a numeral and अह by पुण्य
and सुदिन 09 पूर्वराज, मध्याह्नः, सप्ताहः, नवरात्रम, गणरात्रम्,
पुण्याहम, सुदिनाहम; also that ending in पथ ( rubstituted for
पथिन्, vide $ 280 ) preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable;

(c) tA collective Dwigu is nes.; but that ending in अ in
