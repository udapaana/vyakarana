§ 387. The terminations used to form derivative bases, in
Sanskrit are distinguished into two classes; (1) Kṛit ( T☎ ) or
Primary Affixes and (2) Taddhita (af) or Secondary affixes.
Kṛit are those affixes that are added to verbs, and the Nominal
Bases formed by their means are called PRIMARY NOMINAL
BASES; while TADDHITA affixes are those that are added to
substantives, primary or derived from roots, and the bases
formed with them are called 8ECONDARY NOMINAL BASES
(vide § 179 ).
