(b) Oonjugation of the Desiderative Base.
Conjugational Tenser
S 622. अ is added to the base in the Conjugational Tenses
and then it is conjugated like the special base of the 6th class
in the Active and Passive.
Non-Conjugational Tenses.
9623. (a) The Perfect is formed by adding आम to the base
and appending the Perfect forms of the auxiliary verbs NT, L:
and (see § 490, 526).
(b) In the Aorist the Desiderative Bases take the terminations
of the 5th Variety.
(c) In the Benedictive the Parasm. terminations are added
without इ and the Atm. ones with the intermediate g.
(d) The remaining tenses have no peculiarities.
