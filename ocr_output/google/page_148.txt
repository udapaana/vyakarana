PREPOSITIONAL COMPOUNDS.
4. Pradi.
§ 232. Those Tatpurusha compounds whose first member is
a preposition are termed Prádi by Sanskrit grammarians*; some
of theso prepositions combine with a following noun in the
Nom., with a noun in the Acc. &c.; ० g प्रगतः आचार्य: प्राचार्यः
— an eminent teacher', संगतः अध्वानं समध्वः 'taken to a path-way'
( vide Bhstti. III. 45 ). प्रकृष्टो बातः प्रवातः ' strong or excessive
wind, ' ; अतिक्रान्तो मालां अतिमाल: ' excelling a garland (in
fragrance or beauty ), अतिक्रान्तो रथं रथिनं वा अतिरथः ' a chariot-
warrior who has no rival or equal '; so अतिमात्र ' exceeding
measure,' अतिसर्व &c. अवकृष्टः कोकिलया अबकोकिलः ' attracted by
the cuckoo; ' परिग्लानः अध्ययनान पर्यध्ययनः tired of studying; '
निष्क्रान्तः कौशाम्ब्याः निष्कौशाम्बिः 'gone out of Kausámbi; ' 80
निर्लङ्गः &c. A compound with prepositions which govern cases
( कर्मप्रवचनीय) is forbidden; वृक्षं प्रति.
5 Gati.
"
§ 233. Compounds of the following words with varbal inde-
clinables are called Gati.
(a) t The particles ऊरी, उररी, वौषट्, वषट्, स्वाहा, स्वधा, प्रादुः,
आबिद and श्रत् and the word कारिका meaning an action; as
ऊरीकृत्य, उररीकृत्य 'having accepted; वषट्कृत्य ' having uttored
the word Vashat; ' प्रादुर्भय, कारिकाकृत्य 'having done the act.
(b) † Onomatopoetic words not followed by इति, as खादकृत्य;
but खाडिति कृत्वा ( निरष्टीवत् ).
