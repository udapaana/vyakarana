Bases derived from the root अंबू 'to go to worship.
,
$ 104. Nouns derived form the root अञ्च् ' to go ' by means
of such affixes as fe, fany &c. such as Msq ( M-357) eastern,
प्रत्यञ्च् ( प्रति अञ्च् ) western, सम्यङ (सह-अञ्च् ) a companion,
तिर्यञ्च् ( तिरस्-अञ्च् ) going away, सम्यञ्च् ( सम्-अञ्च् ) going
with or well, बिष्वञ्च् ( विष्वञ्च् अञ्च् ) following, देवद्राञ्च्
( देव अञ्च् )adoring a deity, सर्वद्राज्चू (सर्व अञ्च् ) going every-
where, उदञ्च northen,, अन्वञ्च् following, उदयञ्च्, अदमुयञ्च्,
अम्मुमुयञ्च् going to that ( all those derived from अदस् + अञ्च् ),
मोअञ्च् &c.
(a) In the Nom sing. अञ्च् becomes अ‍. The nasal of
अञ्च् is dropped before all terminations beginning with the
Acc. plu. and the preceding semi-vowel is changed to its
corresponding long vowel before the vowel terminations after
which अ is dropped. When अञ्च is not preceded by a semi-
vowel, its अ is changed to ई it not preceded by a long vowel;
otherwise it remains unchanged तिर्यञ्च् becomes तिरश्च् under
similar circumstances. In other respects these words are
declined like words ending in चू.
