§ 216. A noun in the Locative is compounded—

( a ) †with शौण्ड, धूर्त, कितव a rogue, प्रवीण, संवीत adorned,
invested with, अन्तर, आवि, पट्, पण्डित, कुशल, चतुर, निपुण, सिद्ध,
शुष्क, पक्व, and बन्ध; as अक्षेषु शौण्डः अक्षशौण्डः skilled in dice;
ईश्वरे आवि ईश्वराधीनः dependent on God (when compounded
the affix स i. e. ईन is compulsory; so the compounded form is
always with अधीन); आतपशुष्कः dried in the sun; स्थालीपक्वः
cooked in a pot; वक्रबन्धः a kind of poetic composition; &c.

( b ) ‡with words meaning a 'crow' when contempt is
implied; तीर्थे ष्वाङ्क्षम इव तीर्थष्वाङ्क्षः said in contempt of one who
is very greedy; so तीर्थकाकः; similarly नगरकाकः:—वायसः &c. &c.
There are a few Loc. Tat. compounds given in the list
धावेसमिताद् having a similar sense; e. g. कूपे मण्डूक इव कूपमण्डूकः
a well-abiding frog i. e. one who knows nothing of the world
