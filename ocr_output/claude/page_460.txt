क—is added to roots having ऋ, उ, ऋ, or ल् for their penulti-
mate, and to the roots पी and कू and denotes the agent; लिख-
लिख: one who writes, क्षिप्-क्षिप: one who throws, प्रष्-प्रष: &c.
प्री-प्रिय: one who pleases; कृ-किर: one who scatters; it is also 
added to roots ending in आ with or without any preposition,
the final आ being dropped; ज्ञा-ज्ञ: or प्रज्ञ: one who knows,
wise; ह्वै-ह्व: or आह्व: one who calls; also when a subanta is
used prepositionally with it; गा-गोध: one who gives cows or
cuts the hair; गा-द्विप: (हस्तयौं पिबतीति) an elephant; when
added to स्था it has various meanings; समस्थ: happy; विषमस्थ:
placed in difficulties; प्रस्थ: a measure of corn, &c. This is
also added to गृह—गृहे a house, पृथ: a wife, a house.

इश—is added to दृश not meaning 'to see' when a pronoun pre-
cedes it; तत्+दृश=श=तादृश: like that; also when the words
समान and अन्य precede; सदृश: like to, अन्यादृश: like another
स is added similarly; सदृक्ष:, तादृक्ष: &c.

सच् and सर्—Before these the अ of the noun forming the object
of the root with which it is used prepositionally inserts the
syllable म् after it. सच् is added to the root वद् after प्रिय 
and वश; प्रियं वदतीति प्रियंवद: 'one who speaks sweetly;' वशंवद:
'subject to the influence of, obedient to the will of;—क after
the words क्षेम, प्रिय, भद्र, and भय; क्षेम—प्रियं-भद्र-कर: doing good,
&c.; भयंकर: causing fear, dreadful, अभयंकर:;—to the root नभ् 
after a subanta; विड्नभस्: passing through the sky, a bird;
—to the roots भू, द्, ई, जि, ष, सह्, तप् and दभ् when the
whole is a name; विश्वंभर: God; स्थैवतं a portion of the Sāma
Veda; पतिंवरा a birde who chooses her husband; राजंन्य: an
elephant, पुरंधर: name of a mountain; पतंग: name of a 
king; अरिंदम: name of a king;—to यम् after वाच्, वाग्यंयम:
one who restrains his speech for the observation of a vow;
—to the roots सह and हु after the words सर्व and पुर respec-
tively; सर्वंसह the earth; पुरंदर: Indra,—to कृ after the words
सर्व, कृत, अभ्र and क्षीर; सर्वंकर: all destroying, all powerful;
