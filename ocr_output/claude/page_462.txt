क्—added to roots ending in consonants has almost a universal
application and a variety of senses, before this the final द् or
त् are changed to ट् or ट; पच्—पाकः food; काम्—कामः desire;
श्रम्—विश्रामः rest; दृ—सारः strength, substance, with अति—अति-
or अतीसारः dysentery; हृ—हारः a necklace, पद्—पादः a foot;
भू—भावः being, a thing &c.; विज्—वेजः a house; रुज्—रोगः
a disease; स्पृश्—स्पर्शः touch; इन्ध्—एधः fuel; म्लस्थ्—प्रम्लेषः
laxity; क्षि—क्षायः (चीवतेरतिमक्षादिके) the body, with नि—
निकायः a house, &c.—to क when preceded by a preposition,
वितारः the warbling of birds (otherwise रवः). The roots
स्फुर् and स्फुल् change their vowel to आ before this; स्फारः or
स्फालः the throbbing of the hand, &c. इ and उ preceded by आ
take both पश् and अश्; आरावः or—रः a loud sound, आप्लाव-
पुः a deluge. Sometimes the affixes पश् and अश् are used in
different senses; धी—पायः chief, प्रजयः friendship, kindness;
परिणायः the act of moving a piece at chess, draughts, &c.
परिणयः marriage: दृ with वि—न्यायः justice, क्षयः ruin; षह् with
अप् or नि—अपभयः or निभयः impediment, separation; अवभयः
a grammatical mark; चौरस्य निभयः the confinement of a
thief, but अवभयः—षट्: draught, want of rain; चि after कुन्
take the affix पश् when the whole means 'gathering flowers
with the hands' उच्यचायः, but पुष्यचयः 'collecting flowers with
a stick' &c.; it is also added to भुज् and उच्च् with नि; करः
the hand; न्युब्जः hump-backed, the Nyagrodha tree.

र—is added—to the root कृ preceded by द्विषा, भास्, पद्, सत्, किम्,
a numeral, and nouns governed by it; द्विषा करोतीति द्विषकरः,
भास्करः the sun, पद्करः &c.:—to स preceded by पुरः, अमतः, अधे,
and पुर्व; पुरःसरः, अमतःसरः a leader, &c., and to चर् preceded
by भिक्षा, सेवा, वाय and words showing the place of action;
भिक्षाचरः a beggar, सेवाचरः a soldier, &c.
