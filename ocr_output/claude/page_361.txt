§ 576-578 ]    CONJUGATION OF VERBS.                         355

§ 576. Roots of the 8th class ending in न् or ण् drop their 
nasal and substitute optionally धातु and त for the terminations
ष्वा: and ह्वे of the 2nd and 3rd person sing. Atm. तन्
lengthens its vowel after dropping न्.

§ 577. The vowel of ऊर्णु takes Vṛddhi optionally in the
Parasm. i. e. optionally takes Guṇa substitute; also it option-
ally remains unchanged before ह ( Vide § § 466, 518):

§ 578. The आ of द्वरिक्षा is optionally dropped in the Aorist;
consequently it takes the Sixth and the Fifth Varieties.
