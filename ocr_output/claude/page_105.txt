§ 151. अन्तर is a pronoun ( and therefore declined like सर्वm.
n. f. ) when it means " exterior, outer; or what is to be worn
out-side, as a garment"* except when followed by पुर; it is
optionally declined like nouns ending in अ in the Nom. pl. and
Ab. and Loc. sing. of the mas. e. g. अन्तरे अन्तरा वा पटाः;
अन्तरे-अन्तरा वा झाटकाः ( garments ). But अन्तरायां पुरि.

§ 152. द्वेष meaning 'half' is a pronoun, but it is optionally
declined like राम in the Nom. plu. mas.; in other respects it is
declined like सर्व.

§ 153. पूर्व 'prior, eastern', अपर 'posterior, western', दक्षिण
'southern', उत्तर 'superior, northern, subsequent', अपर 'other',
and अधर 'inferior, lower,' when they denote relation in space,
time or person with reference to a particular thing or point of
time &c. and are not a name, are pronouns† and are then declin-
ed like सर्व; but they are optionally declined like राम in the
Nom. plu. and Ab. and Loc. sing. when mas. e. g. Nom. पूर्वे:
पूर्वौ पूर्वे or पूर्वाः Ab. पूर्वात्-पूर्वेभ्यात् पूर्वेभ्यम् पूर्वेभ्यः; Loc. पूर्वे-पूर्वेस्मिन्;
&c., but दक्षिणाः गायकाः ( skilful musicians ) only, and not दक्षिणे,
for दक्षिण does not show any relation here; so उत्तराः कुराः and
not उत्तरे, as उत्तराः is a name here.

in the dual only, द्वि. is considered as दु, and they are both
declined like सर्व in all the three genders; e g.
