1. Participles ( Declinable )

( a ) Participles of the Present Tense.

§ 667. The participle of the present Parasm. is formed by
the addition of the affix अत् to that form of a root. primitive
or derivative, which it assumes before the third person plural
termination of the Present Tense. If the base ends in अ, the अ
is dropped; e. g.
