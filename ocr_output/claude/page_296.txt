Seventh or कुपादि Class.

§ 455. In this class the base is formed by inserting न् bet
ween the radical vowel and the final consonant before the strong
and न् before the weak terminations.

§ 456. (a) The original nasal of a root is dropped.
(b) In the case of the root वह्, न् is changed to नै before
consonantal strong terminations.
