sometimes forms abstract nouns, sometimes shows the place
or the instrument of action denoted by the root; स्तु—स्तवः:
praise, यू—यवः: barley, दू—दवः;, भू—भवः; छू—छः: the instru-

fear, सू—सवः: a boon &c., स्थ with वि—विष्टरः: a tree or a seat,
विस्तरः: otherwise; इष with सम् takes this affix; संचः: a collec-

takes this affix and is changed to उद; निउदः;; प्रउदः;, विउदः:
&c.; food or eating, ( when no preposition precedes, it takes
घञ्—घासः ); the roots जप् and अप् not preceded by a preposi
tion take अप्; जपः: muttering prayers, आपः: the act of piercing;
( but when a preposition precedes, they take घञ्; as उपजापः:
secret whispering into the ear, separation, &c.); the roots
स्वप् and दूप् by themselves take अप् or घञ्; स्वप्-स्वनः: or स्वानः:
sound; दूप्-इषः: or इषः;; with a preposition they take the
latter only; प्रस्वानः; प्रइषः: &c.; यम् without any preposition
or with the preposition उप, वि, वि and सम् takes either अप् or
घञ्; यमः: or यामः: restraint, control, &c, उपयमः: or यामः:
marriage; similarly निगमः: or निगामः: &c., the roots गड्, नड्,
पड् and स्वद्, with नि take अप् or घञ्; निगदः: or निगादः: speech,
निनदः: or निनादः: sound, &c. क्रुध् with or without वि takes अप्
or घञ्; क्रधः: or क्राधः;, निक्रधः: or-क्राधः: the sound of a lute;
the root मद् when a word other than a preposition is prefixed
to it takes अप्; with a preposition it takes घञ्; धनमदः: the
pride of wealth, उन्मादः: madness, insanity; but when pre-
ceded by प्र or सम् it takes अप् when the meaning is joy; प्रमदः:
or संमदः; in other cases it takes घञ्; प्रमादः;, संमादः: careless-
ness, oversight, a blunder. The affixes अप् and घञ् are added
to several other roots too numerous to note here; the
difference between these is that घञ् occasions Vriddhi of the
preceding vowel, अप् does not.
