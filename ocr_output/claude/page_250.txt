§ 393. The augment अ is prefixed to roots in this [?].
This अ is replaced by आ in the case of roots beginning with a
vowel*; this आ forms Vriddhi with an initial vowel; e. g.
अ+इष्+त्=आ+इष्+अ+त्=ऐषत्; similarly ईक्ष्-ऐक्षत्; उक्ष्-
औक्षत्; ऊह्-औहत्; ऋच्छ्-आर्च्छत् &c.

(a) When a preposition (उपसर्ग) is prefixed to a root, the
augment अ or आ comes between the prep. and the root; e. g.
इ with प्र, प्राहरत्.
