§ 452. दृ and धा drop their आ after reduplication before 
weak terminations; दध् becomes दघ् before त, थ, स and ध. The 
Par. Imperative 2 per. singulars are देहि and धेहि respectively.

§ 453. भी optionally shortens its vowel before consonantal 
weak terminations.

(a) या and ह्वा 'to go' assume the forms सिध् and जिह् before 
vowel terminations and सिधा and जिहा before consonantal ter-
minations.

§ 454. हा 'to abandon' assumes the forms जहि or जही before 
consonantal weak terminations except in the Pot. and जह before 
vowel terminations and those of the Pot. The Imp. 2 sing. 
forms are जहाहि, जहिहि and जहीहि.
