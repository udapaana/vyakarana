§ 157-158 ] PRONOUNS AND THEIR DECLENSION.                     101

तद्...........................तदा, then; तदानीम्, at that time; तर्हि 
                          then, therefore; तथा, so, तत्र, there;
                          ततः, thence, thereupon, therefore, &c.

इदम्.........................इदानीम्, now; इत्थम्, thus; अत्र, here;
                          अतः, therefore; इतः, from this, hence;
                          अधुना, now; इह, here.

एतद्..........................एताहि, now; इत्थम् thus; अतः hence,
                          therefore; अत्र here.

यद्...........................यर्हि, when; यदा when; यथा as; यत्र
                          where; यतः, whence,, since, because.

किम्..........................कर्हि, when; कदा, when; कथम्, how;
                          कुत्र, where; क, where; कुतः, where,
                          whence; कुतः, whence, how.

सर्व्..........................सर्वदा, always; सदा, always; सर्वतः,
                          everywhere, on all sides; सर्वत्र every-
                          where, in all places.

पर...........................परतः further on, beyond &c.
पूर्व्..........................पुरः, पुरस्तात् before, in front, &c.
अधर.........................अधः, अधस्तात् or अधरस्तात्, अधरतः,
                          अधरात् down, below.
अवर..........................अवः, अवस्तात् or अवरस्तात्, अवरतः,
                          behind, below, downwards.
अपर..........................पश्चात्, from behind, afterwards,
                          westward, &c.
दक्षिण.......................दक्षिणा, दक्षिणात्, दक्षिणाहि to or in
                          the south, on the right side.
उत्तर.........................उत्तरा उत्तरात्, उत्तराहि to or in the
                          north, &c.

§ 158. When a pronominal word is used as the subordinate
member of a compound, or is at the end of an Inst. Tat., or of
sentence having the sense of the Inst. Tat., or at the end of a
Dwandwa, it is not declined like a pronoun*; e. g. अतिक्रान्तः

* संज्ञापूर्वंनियोगात्तु न सर्वादेः । Vārt. द्वितीयापसमाथे । इंद्रे च ॥
ān. I. 1. 30. 31.
