§ 593. (a) The roots स्वप्, सप्, तप्, and क्षप्, optionally drop their प् and at the same time lengthen their अ; स्वप्-स्वपते, सापते, &c.

(b) शी 'to lie down' and श्रि form their bases as शय्य and श्रय

(c) कुह् shortens its ऊ when a preposition is prefixed to it

(d) परिक्ष, द्रोणी and बेणी drop their final vowel before य.

(e) य् and अस् substitute वच् and भू for themselves; वद् and भूष् also substitute आह् and भी respectively.
