in fem. affix in the sense of 'the child or offspring of';
वैनतेय: 'the son of Vinatā i. e. Garuda'; भागिनेय: 'the
son of a sister'; when added to कुटिटा meaning 'a poor
woman', इन is inserted optionally. कौलटेय: or कौल-
टिनेय:; when कुटिटा means 'a harlot' or 'an adulteress'
एर; is optionally substituted for एय; कौलटेय: or कौल-
टेर: 'the son of an adulteress'; it is optionally changed
to एर when added to words denoting 'a female having

words of two syllables ending in इ but not formed
