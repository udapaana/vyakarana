460                        SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                  [§ 777

अक्—is added to वृ, षू and छृ in the sense 'skilul in,' वृ—अवक्:
छृ—सरक्: 'skilful in walking;' छवक्: 'skilful in cutting:' this
may by added to any root when the idea of a blessing is to be
conveyed; जीवक्स्त्वं सदा: 'mayest thou live for many years,'
मन्दुक्स्त्वं सदा: 'mayest thou be the giver of delight.'

अक्—is added to नृत्, खन् and रज् in the sense of 'one who knows
the art of,' नर्तक्: 'one who knows the art of dancing,' खनक्:
'a digger,' 'a miner,' रजक्: 'a dyer.'

अथु—(अथुन्)—वेप्-वेपथु: 'tremor;' श्वि—श्वयथु: 'swelling, a
tumour,' दु—दवथु: 'pain, anxiety,' &c.

अर्—(वसर्, उर्, स्रु, स्वर्)—

वसर्—is affixed to गै and धा; गायर्: 'a singer,' धायर्: 'a year,' 'a
kind of rice.'

उर्—is affixed to verbs meaning 'to go or to sound,' चर्—चरुर्:
'one who moves.' क्—रुर्: 'one who makes a sound,' so शब्दुर्: &c.
It is also affixed to verbs meaning 'to ornament, to deck, and to
be angry,'—सृ—सरुर्: serving as an ornament: मण्ड्—मण्डुर्,
कुध्—कोधुर्:, उर्—रोषुर्: angry, irascible.'-to यु, तु, धृ, ज्वर्, स्थुर्,
छर्, पद्, पर्; यु—अवर्: 'a swift walker:' तु—तरुर्: 'one who
goes ' तृष—तर्षर्: 'a glutton;' ज्वलुर्: 'that which blazes,
fire;'—to some other roots ending in a consonant, युद्—यर्तिर्,
दृश्—दर्श: &c.:‐to the freq. of भ्रम् and क्रम्, चंक्रमर्:, क्रीरमर्:
'one who goes again and again', it also forms fem. abstract
nouns with the causals of roots and the verbs अन्य, पद्, वन्द्
and इद् not meaning 'to wish,' कृ—कारर्ा 'doing action,'
ई—कारर्ा, आधु—आसर्ा, अन्धु—अन्यर्ा, पद्-पदर्ा, बन्द् बन्दर्ा,
पिद्—वेदर्ा. इष् with अनु-अन्वेषर्ा 'searching.'

स्रु—is affixed to तन् and other roots; नंदर्: 'one who delights, a
son,' मद्—मदर्: 'he who exhilarates, the god of love,',साध्—
साधर्: 'one who accomplishes,' सह्—सहर्: 'one who bears,'
धृष्—धृर्स्रुत: 'the killer of Madhu,' अर्ह्—अर्हर्त: 'the
chastiser of the sinful,' धी—विधीषर्: 'terrifier, name of
Rāvana's brother.'
