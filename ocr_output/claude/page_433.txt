§ 693. ( a ) ऋण* when it means 'to incur debt' substitutes न; ऋणा 'debt; but ऋत, 'gone.'

( b ) क्षि, when the P. P. Participle has an active sense, substitutes न and lengthens its इ; क्षीण, reduced, emaciated; but when a curse is implied, or pity expressed, the change is optional; क्षीणाय: or क्षिताय: भव i. e. 'die;' क्षीण: or क्षित: अयं तपस्वी 'oh, the poor ascetic has grown thin' but क्षित: कामो मया.

( c ) † दिव् takes न when it does not mean 'to gamble;' द्यून 'a sport in which nothing is staked,' but द्यूत 'gambling.'

( d ) † वा with निर् takes न except when it has वात for its subject; निर्वाणोऽग्नि: 'the fire is extinguished;' निर्वाणो छुनि: the sage is absolved; but निर्वात: वात:

( e ) श्चै takes न when the notion of touch is not present; संश्यानो वृश्चिक: 'a scorpion contracted through cold;' when it means 'to be coagulated or cold' it: अ takes संप्रसारण (इ), शीनं घृतं, but शीतं उदकं and not शीनं as the idea of touch is present here; also when preceded by प्रति, प्रतिशीनं घृतं, &c.; with अभि and अव the change is optional; अभिश्यानं or अभिशीनं घृतं, अवश्यानो or अव-शीनो वृश्चिक:; but समवश्यात:

§ 694. The roots जुष् , दिष् , 6. A., उष् , श्रै, ष्णा and श्री take न or त; जुन or जुत, दिष्ट or दित, श्राण or श्रात, श्राण or श्रात and श्रीण or श्रीत.

§ 695. व्याधृ has पीन when it is used with 'one's own limbs,' पीनं शरीरं, and व्यान or पीन in other cases, as व्यान: or पीन: स्नेह:; when it is preceded by a preposition, ई is not substituted for its vowel; प्रव्यान:; but when it is used with अन्यु or उपधि with the preposition आ, ई is necessarily substituted; आपीन: अन्यु:, आपीनं ऊरू:.
