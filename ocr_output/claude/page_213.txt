यरिमन् हुतमयो यज्ञः 'a sacrifice consisting for the most
part of clarified butter,' अजमयः &c. It is not added 
to words denoting 'an article of food or a covering';
e. g. सौद्रः खरः.

य (यक् , यत् , यञ् , यण )—is added to nouns with different mean-
    ings:—(1) गवां समूहो गव्या f. 'a herd of cattle,' धातानां 
    समूहो धात्या, so खल्या, रथ्या, (रथानां समूहः), धारय्या, धूम्या,
    'a dense mass of smoke,' तृण्या, नभ्या, &c. (2) सभायां 
    साधुः सभ्यः a 'courtier'; (3) सतीश्यः 'disciples of 
    the same preceptor,' सोदर्यः, समानोदर्यः 'a brother of
    whole blood'; (4) forms abstract nouns राज्य, सेनापत्यं,
    पौरोहित्यं, सारथ्यं, आदित्यं, &c.; it is added—(5) to
    the words राजन् and मनु in the sense of 'born of the 
    race of.' राजन्यः 'a man of the Kshatriya class,' मनो-
    जातिः मनुष्यः (in this case ष is inserted) 'a man.'—(6)
    to भृगुर् in the sense of 'the son of,' भार्गव्यः; (7) to कुलं;
    कुल्य—'of noble descent;' (8) to वायु, ऋतु, पितृ, and उषस्
    in the sense of 'having any of these for one's presiding
    or tutelary deity,' &c. वायुः देवता अस्य वायव्यं अस्त्रं 'a
    missile having Vāyu for its presiding deity,' ऋतव्यः
    'worshipping the Seasons (as divinities),' पित्र्यः 'sacred
    to the manes, offered to the manes'; उषस्यः 'sacred to
    the Dawn;'—(9) to दण्ड and words included under the
    दण्डादि group in the sense of 'deserving or meriting':
    दण्ड्यः 'deserving punishment,' बध्यं 'fit to be killed,;
    अर्ह्यः 'deserving worship,' &c. In the derivatives
