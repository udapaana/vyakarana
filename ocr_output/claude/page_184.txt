(a) When the nouns derived from the roots नह्, सद्, इष्, व्यध्, रुच्, सह, and तन् with the aff. क्विन् ( o ) follow prepositions termed Gati or nouns which they govern; उपनह्, नीरुद् an inhabited country, a realm, वारुद् the-rainy season, ममर्विद् piercing the vitals; so मृगविद् a hunter (cf. Bhaṭṭi II. 7). नीरुह्, अभीरुह्, कतीरद् overpowering an assailant परीतद्. But परिधावनम् as नह् is not followed by Kvip.

(b) When followed by the aff. वह, and the whole is a name; कषीवह: a husbandman.

(c) Words containing more than two vowels when followed by वत् (मत्) except in the case of अजिर, रविर, डलिन, इंद, कारणवत् and चक्रवाक and the whole is a name; अमरावती and इरावती which are names; but अजिरवती, क्रीडिमती, बहुमती which is not a name. Also in the case of हर, बंश, भूम, अति, कपि, दनि, शुचि, and इड; इरावती &c.

(d) When a verbal derivative with the aff. अ (घञ्) follows a preposition, in most cases, when the comp. does not signify a human being; परिधाय:-परीपाक:; but निषाद: one of a mountain-tribe. Similarly प्रति-सी-वंश:; प्रति-सी-कार: &c.

(e) When काष follows a preposition ending in इ; नीकाष:, नीकाष:; but प्रकाश:

(f) When अट्टन् is the first, and नर् the second member of a compound, and the whole is a name; अटापरन् gold, अष्टापर: a spider; but अट्टउत्त:, विद्यातर: an epithet of Savitṛi.

(g) When मित्र is the latter member and the whole is the name of a ṛishi; विश्वामित्र:; but विश्वामित्रो माषवक: ।
