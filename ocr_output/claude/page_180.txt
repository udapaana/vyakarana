174                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 292-294

§. 292. Obs.—* A nasal is inserted in the case of the follow-
ing words:—सत्य, अवद् and अद्य followed by कार; वेद्य by अक्ष; 
लोक by पृथि; अनभ्यास by हव; आङ् and अधि by इन्ध, तिमि by तिङ् 
or तिङ्गिल; and उन्न and भद् by करण; as सत्यङ्कार:, 'making true,
earnest money,' &c; cf. Kir. XI. 50. भवदङ्कार: 'a physician,'
सन्तङ्कार:, 'efficacious,' 'admittance' ( अन्युपगम: Tat. B. ),
वेद्यक्षया ( भविष्यती भय: Tat. B. ); लोकम्पृथ: 'pervading or filling
the world,' अनभ्यासाहित्व: 'not to be approached, to be shunned
from afar,' ( दूरत: परिहर्तव्य इत्यर्थ: Sid. Kan. ); आङ्गिन्धे:, 'one
who fries or roasts in a frying-pan,' अधिगिन्ध:, 'one who kindles
fire,' तिमितिङ्ग: 'a monstrous fish that swallows down Timi
(a large fish said to be 100 Yojanas in length ),' तिमितिङ्गिल:†
'a very large fish that swallows even a Timingila;' उन्नङ्करण
heating; भद्रङ्करण conferring prosperity.

§ 293. रात्रि followed by a kṛit affix inserts a nasal optionally;
रात्रिञ्चर: or रात्रिचर:, 'a night-rover,' 'a demon;' रात्रिष्टह: or
गष्टह: &c.

§ 294. † सह forming the first member of a compound, is
changed to स—

(a) when the whole compound is a name, e. g. सभाजय;
but सहयुध्वा a comrade in battle ( Upapada comp. ).

(b) when it means 'including a certain literary work,' or 'in
addition to;' as सहृदते ( Avya. comp. ) ज्योतिषमधीते 'he studies
the science of astronomy, including that of finding out the auspi-
cious times,' सकोणा सूती 'a khāri with a drone in addition to it.'
