§ 426. The इ of the root इ P.* 'to go' is changed to य
before a weak vowel termination: एति इत: यन्ति 3 pers. Pre.;
आयम् ऐव एम 1st Pers. Imperf.; य: 2 sing. Imperf.; अय्याते,
इहि, एतु 1. 2. 3. sings. Imp. यन्तु 3. pl. Imp.

इ with अधि A. † 'to study.' &c. is regularly conjugated; as

* इ P. 'to go' is conjugated regularly like दी-एति इत: इयन्ति
3rd Pers. Pre.; इहि 2 sing. Imp. इयन्तु 3 plural. Imp.

† इ with अधि P. 'to remember' should be conjugated like इ,
अधियन्ति 3rd pl. Pre. Some think that it is to be conjugated like
इ in the Non-conjugational tenses only. According to them the
third pers. plu. will be अधीयन्ति. The line of Bhatti 'मसीनयो
राजचरित्रमीयुः' III. 18. &c. is quoted in support of this opinion.
