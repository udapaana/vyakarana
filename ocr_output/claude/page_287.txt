                THIRD OR जुहोत्यादि CLASS.

§ 443. (a) The base is formed by reduplication of the root.

(b) The third personal plural termination loses its त्.

(c) The Imperfect third person plural termination is
उस् (P.). before which the final आ of roots is dropped and the
final इ, उ and ऋ, short or long. are guṇated.

Rules of Reduplication:—

§ 444. The first vowel of a root together with the initial
consonant, if any, is reduplicated i.e. doubled, as पद्=पपद्,
उष्—उउष्, &c. after reduplication.
