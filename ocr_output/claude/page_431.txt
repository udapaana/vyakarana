§ 687. (a) अच्च् in the sense of 'to worship' takes त; अर्चित
'worshipped;' but अक्त: 'gone;' with सम्-समक्त (समक्तो शकुन्तै पादौ)

(b) धृष् and ब्रष् reject त when they express the idea of 'immo-
desty or rudeness;' धृष् 'rude,' विश्रत: 'ill-mannered;' but धर्षित
'overpowered, trifled with,' &c., विश्रसित 'tormented or ill-treated'

§ 688. न* is substituted for त when it immediately follows a
final द् or त्; this final द् is also changed to न्: भिद्-भिन्न, पद्-पीर्ण;
तुद्-तृन्न (See § 698).

Exceptions:—

(a) भिद् has भित्त when it means 'a part, a portion;' भिन्न in
other senses.

(b) विद् 6 P. A. takes त in the sense of 'fit for enjoyment or
famous;' वित्तं 'wealth, property,' वित्त: 'famous'; विन्न in other cases.

(c) मद्, डुद् and सन्नु; मत्त, पूर्तं 'filled,' (also पूर्ण when it means
the same as त), मूर्त्.

§ 689. Roots ending in आ* (उ, ए and ओ changeable to आ)
and beginning with a conjunct consonant containing a semi-
vowel also substitute न for त; श्रा 'to run, to sleep,' श्राण; म्लै 'to
fade,' म्लान; स्त्यै-स्त्यान 'collected to a mass,' &c.

Exceptions:—ख्या 'to name,' &c. ध्यै 'to contemplate,' व्यै and
ह्वै; ख्यात, ध्यात, वीत and हूत.
