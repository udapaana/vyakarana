§ 126. अस्थि n. a bone, दधि n. curds, सक्थि n. a thigh and 
अक्षि n. an eye, become अस्थ्न्, दध्न्, सक्थ्न्, and अक्ष्न् respec-
tively before the vowel terminations beginning with the I. 
sing.* and then they follow the declension of nouns in न्; in 
other respects they are declined like वारि.

§ 127. अप् f. water, is declined in the plural; it lengthens
its अ in the N. and changes its प् to द् before consonantal
terminations. आपः-आपः-आप्भिः-आद्भ्यः-आद्भ्यः-आपाम्-आप्सु.
