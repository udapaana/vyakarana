§ 682. The Penultimate radical nasal is generally dropped.
(See § 674).

§ 683. The augment इ is prefixed to this त in the case of
certain roots, optionally in the case of some and not at all in
the case of others.

§ 684. As a general rule, all roots ending in a vowel, and
such as take the इ optionally before any termination, and Anit
roots ending in consonants generally, do not take इ; the rules
of Saṃdhi given in the foregoing chapters that are applicable in
particular cases ought to be observed.—
