PERIPHRASTIC PERFECT.

§ 526. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed by adding आम् to the root and then adding the forms of the reduplicated Perfect of कृ, भू, or अस् as terminations; when the forms of कृ, are added, a Parasmaipadi root takes the Parasmaipadi forms and an Atmanepadi one takes the Atmanepadi forms.

§ 527. Before आम् the final vowel and the penultimate short except that of विद्, take their Guṇa substitute.
