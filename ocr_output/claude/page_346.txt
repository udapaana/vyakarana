                    THIRD VARIETY OR FORM.

§ 545. Terminations:—

The same as those of the 2nd Variety.

§ 546. Roots of the tenth class, causals, some derivatives, the root कद् and the roots सि, दु, and सु when expressing the agent, take this Variety necessarily, and the roots धे and सि do it optionally.

§ 547. (a) The root is first reduplicated and then the augment अ and the terminations are added as in the 2nd Variety.
(b) Final इ and उ are changed to इय् and उव् before अ and final ओ is dropped.
