§ 700. दा 'to give,' and दे, form their P. P. Ptc. as दत्त; the त of दत्त is dropped after a preposition ending in a vowel; प्रत्-जस्त &c.; in this latter case the preceding इ or उ is lengthened; नीत, धूत, &c.; त may optionally be retained also; प्रदत्त, अवदत्त, उद्दत्त,* &c.
