458                SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                [ § 777

य—is added to roots ending in आ which insert त be-
fore it; दा-दाय: 'one who receives a share,' धा—धाय: 'one 
who holds,' &c.—to कै when preceded by अन् and प्रति, 
अन्सधाय: 'mist, frost,' प्रतिस्धाय: 'a catarrh or cold;—to इ, 
उ, सो and इ preceded by a preposition; अत्याय: 'violation,' 
संस्राव: 'oozing, trickling,' अवसाय: 'end', अवहार: 'a thief, a 
scamonster';—to लिह्, क्षिय, पह, ष्यय, भ्यय and य; लेह: 'a 
lambative, an electuary;' लेय: 'an embrace,' ग्राह: 'an alligator,'
व्याघ: 'a hunter,' श्वास: 'respiration,' भाय: 'a thing,—to धी 
and इ not preceded by a preposition; नाय: 'a leader', दाय: 'a 
forest-fire;'—to जब्ल, कल्, जल्, टल् 'to be confused', तल् 
'to smell,' बद्, पद्, बल्, सुल्, कुम्भ्, शब्द्, हुल्, पद्, कप्, पष्, 
गष्, बब्, ब्रभ्, सर, सह, छद्, कुश, स्य, and कत्, (these may 
also take अनु्); ज्वाल: (or ज्वाल:), flame or blaze,' &c:—
to क्रीत्, क्रम and भक्ष्, when their objects are prefixed to 
them; मांससलील: 'one who keeps flesh;' मांसक्रम: 'one who 
wishes for flesh;' मांसभक्ष: 'one who eats flesh';—to क्रम्, क्षम् 
and वद् with आ; उखप्रतीक्ष: 'one desiring happiness,' बहुक्षम: 
'forgiving much,' कल्याणीवाद: 'good-conducted;'—to दै, वे and 
मा after their objects; स्वर्गै ह्यते स्वर्गोहाय:, तंतुवाय: 'a weaver, 
धान्यमाय: 'measure of corn;'—to अद् with वि, व्यद्: 'food.'

i—is added to पा, घ्रा, स्था, धै, and दृश; पा-पिय: 'one who 
drinks,' घ्रा—जिघ्र: 'one who smells; दृश—पश्य: 'one who 
sees;—to दा and धा 3 cl.; दा-दद्: 'one who gives,' धा—
दध: 'one who holds;—to लिप् and विद्; लिप: 'one who plasters;'
विद्प्:'one who knows;' also when the former is preceded by वि 
and the latter by गो and other words: विलिप्र: 'a god;' गोविन्द: 
'name of Vishnu;' अरविन्द: 'a lotus;'—to the Cau. of क्षिप्, तृ,
एत् with उत्, and धृ; चेतय: 'one who thinks or knows;' पारय:
'one who fills'; उद्वेजय: 'one who makes another tremble.' cf.
Bhatti. 1. 25. धारय: 'one who holds'; added to all roots it 
forms abstract fem. nouns; कृ-क्रिया 'an act;' इष-इच्छा a 
'wish;' परिचर-परिचर्या 'service;' मृग-मृगया 'hunting;' अट् 
अटाक्या rambling;' जागृ—जागर्या 'wakefulness, &c.'
