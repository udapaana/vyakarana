नि—is affixed to जु with प्र, जि, ई, द्धि, त्रि with वि, बध्, जप् 
with आ, अच with अधि, यु with परि and ह with प्र; प्रजविन् 
swift-going; जयिन् a conqueror; धरिन् timid. &c., क्षयिन् wast-
ing away; it is also added to क्री with वि when the noun go-
verned by it is prefixed to it and when the idea of censure
or reproach is to be conveyed; तैलविक्रायी, सोमविक्रायी, &c.

वङ्णु—is affixed to स्यज्, रज, भज्, दप्, द्विष्, दुह् द्रुह्, उप्,
यम् with आ, पद्, क्रीड् and इष with आ; ह, विद्, भिद्, रुह्,
बध्, बृह्, वृह्, all with परि; हन्, पच्, उञ्च, all with सम्; विष्
and स्पृ with नि; लप्, सृ, सप्य्, बध्, वस् all with प्र; चर with
अति and अप; हन् with अभि, रुष् with अनु; and denotes the
agent; त्यज—त्यागिन् one who abandons, रागिन् -ull of passion,
impassioned, a lover ; भागिन् one who takes a share, दोषिन् one
who blames; similarly भाविन्, क्रोधिन्, &c.; this is also added to
शद् and the other roots of that group but without occasioning
any change in the roots; शम्—शमिन् tranquil; मद्—मदिन्; but
मद् with उद् or प्र—उन्मादिन्, प्रमादिन्.

आति—is added to the roots of the मद् group in the sense of
'the agent;' यत्नातति शाहिन् 'one who takes;' स्था—स्थातिन्,
ति with वि—विषयिन् 'a sensualist;' राध with अप—अपराधिन्
'one who is guilty;' जि with परि—परिजातिन् 'one who defeats;'
&c.,—to हन् when preceded by कुमार and श्रीष्; कुमारं हन्तीति
कुमारघातिन् 'one who murders a child,' शीर्षघातिन् 'one who cuts
off the head.' This is added to any root, when the subanta
other than one denoting a class, precedes it, in the sense of
'in the habit of,' 'disposed to'; उष्णकारिन् ( उष्णं भोक्तुं
इच्छमय ) 'one who eats hot things,' साधुकारिन् 'one who acts
well,' ब्रह्मवादिन् 'one who expounds the Vedas or the nature
of Brahman;'—to मन् after any subanta. पण्डितम्मानिन् 'one
who considers himself a Pandit,' दर्शनीयमानिन् 'one who con-
siders himself handsome,' &c.; this is added in the sense of
the Past Tense to यज् preceded by the name of a sacrifice;
