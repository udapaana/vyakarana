§ 664 ]          PARASMAIPADA & ATMANEPADA.          411

वह्—is both Par. and Atm. but with प्र is restricted to the-
Par.; प्रवहति.

विद्*—'to know' (2nd cl.) with सम् is Atm. when used
intransitively in the sense of 'knowing of, being aware of,' and
adds र to द optionally in the 3rd per. plural; संविद्रते or संविद्रते
'they know well.' cf. के न संविद्रते मायात्मनाकाद्रिप्रथमा सखा Bh. VIII.
17.; 'who do not know that the mountain Mainaka is the friend
of Vāyu?;' but संविद: सह्युञ्जानो तष्टधिक खद्गुणौ Bhaṭṭ. V. 37,
as it is used transitively. It is also Atm. with सम् in the sense
of 'recognising;' as संविद्ये.

विक्री†—with नि is Atm. निविक्रीते; किंकिंकपादि न्यविक्रत &c. Bhaṭṭ
VI. 143. Also when अभि is prefixed to नि, अभिनिविक्रते सन्मार्गं
(Sid. Kon.) 'takes to a good path,' see Bh. VIII. 80.

शप्‡—meaning 'to reproach or to abuse' takes the Atm.
even when the fruit of the action does not accrue to the agent;
कृष्णाय जपते ।

शिक्ष§—takes the Atm. in the sense of 'wishing to study;'
धनुषे शिक्षते 'wishes to study archery.'

श्रु—†† 'to hear' with सम् is Atm. when used intransitively;
संश्रूयते 'hears well.' संश्रुष्व कपे (Bhaṭ. VII. 16.) 'Listen with
heed, oh monkey;' cf. हिताय य: संश्रूयते स कि प्रभु: Kir. 1. 5; but
शब्दं संशृणोति 'he hears the sound.' This root takes the Atm. in
the Desiderative, except when आ or प्रति precedes it, शुश्रूषते; but
आ—प्रति—शुश्रुषति.

स्था§—with सम्, अव, प्र and वि is Atm., संतिष्ठते: इमे परिवष्टासाव
