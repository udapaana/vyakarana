( f ) Potential Participles ( and Verbal Adjectives ).

§ 711. The Potential Participle is formed by means of the
affixes *तव्य, अनीय, य and rarely एलिम, added to a root or deriva-
tive verb. This is Passive when the verb is transitive and
impersonal when the verb is intransitive. It is also used like
an adjective when denoting fitness, &c.

(1) Participles in तव्य and अनीय.

§ 712. The affixes तव्य or अनीय are added to roots or
derivative bases in the sense of 'must be fit to be,' &c. Before
these the ending vowel and the penultimate short of a root
take their Guna substitute. Before तव्य Set roots take इ, Anit
roots do not, and Wet roots take it optionally. Before अनीय
penultimate अ is always changed to अर् and not to र् ( as it
sometimes does ); e. g.
