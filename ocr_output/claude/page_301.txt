II. GENERAL
OR
NON-CONJUGATIONAL TENSES AND MOODS.

§ 457. In the General Tenses and Moods and in the
formation of verbal derivatives generally, the augment इ is
prefixed ( necessarily or optionally ) to terminations beginning with any consonant except य in the case of certain roots.
Such roots as take the augment इ necessarily are called Set
(स + इट् i. e. with इ); such as take it optionally are called
Wet ( वा + इट् ), and those that do not, are called Anit (अन् +
इट् i. e. without इ ).

§ 458. ( a ) Roots of more than one syllable, derived roots
and roots of the 10th class are always Set.

( b ) Of all monosyllabic roots ending in a vowel those given
in the following couplet ( Kārikā ) are Set and the rest Anit.

"ऊर्णुदूनेयोतिस्खनशोचुच्छौचुम्लुचुवृतौइनर्भीभिः ।
कूर्द्व्यौ च विनेजनौऽन्येषु निपातः स्मृताः ॥"

i. e. roots ending in long ऊ and long ॠ, the roots कू, कू, क्षु
