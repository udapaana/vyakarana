§ 706 P. P. Participles have sometimes the sense of neuter
abstract nouns; as जल्पितं 'speech', शयितं 'sleep', हसितं 'laughing,'
similarly स्थितं, गतं, etc; See Bhatt. VII. 125.

§ 707. The P. P. Participles of roots meaning 'to think or to
wish,' 'to know,' 'to adore,' and of the roots इच्छ्, भू etc. have
the sense of the present Tense; राज्ञः मतः 'he is honoured by the
king,' सतां प्रणितः इदं: अग्निः 'the fire is kindled,' so भीतः etc.

(d) Past Active Participles.

§ 708 Past Active participles are derived from the P.
Passive Participles in त or न by adding to them the affix वत्; e.g.

(e) Participle of the Future Tense.

§ 709. The Participle of the Simple Future, Active as well
as Passive, is formed from the third person singular form of
the Second Future of a root. The Pararsm. Ptc. is formed
simply by dropping the final इ, and the Atm. and the Passive
one by substituting मान for the final ते; e. g.
