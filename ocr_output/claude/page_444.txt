§733. The roots छ and वृ take both क्यप् and ण्यत्; छव्य,

§734. युज् in the sense of 'to be harnessed' takes the affix
क्यप् and changes its final to ग्; युग्य: गो: 'a bull to be harnessed
to the yoke;' in other senses it takes the affix ण्यत्-योज्य.

By means of the affix ण्यत्.

§735. Roots ending in क्, and those ending in a consonant
take the affix ण्यत् (य) in the same sense as यत्. Before this affix
the ending श् and ज् of a root are changed to क् and ग् respecti-
vely and the final vowel and the penultimate अ take Vriddhi
substitute; any other penultimate vowel generally takes Guṇa.

छ—कार्य 'what ought to be done,' वृ—धार्य 'what ought to be
worn,' &c.; यह्—याह्य, धम्—धाम्य 'what ought to be impelled,' &c.;
वच्—वाक्य 'what is arranged, a sentence,' पच्—पाक्य 'what is to
be cooked,' शुच्—शार्य 'what is to be purified,' &c.

§736. The root वस् when preceded by अमा 'with' takes the
affix ण्यत् before which the penultimate अ is optionally changed
to Vriddhi; अमा सह वसन्तोऽस्यां चन्द्राकौ अमावस्या or वास्या 'the day
on which the sun and the moon are with each other i. e. are in
conjunction.'
