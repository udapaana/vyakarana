206                             SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 340

त्व-(त्वच्) is added in the sense of 'living in, belonging to,' &c.
      to the words क्षत्रिया, पश्चात्, उत्तर, अमात्य, ङृद, कृ, शरद्, भवत्,
      and indeclinables ending in तस्; दाक्षिणात्यः a south-
      erner, पाश्चात्यः, पौरस्त्य:, an inhabitant of the east;
      अमात्यः; one who accompanies a king, a minister, ङृदत्वः
      कृत्वः; शरत्वः, भवत्वः &c.; also to the preposition नि, 
      नित्यः 'eternal'.

चक (चकट्) is added to उप and अधि; उपत्यका land at the foot
      of a mountain, अधित्यका 'a table-land.'

त्र—is affixed to गो only; गवां समूहो गोत्रा f. 'a herd of kine'.

त्व—forms abstract nouns; गोत्वं.

दृश, इयस् and मात्र* (दृशच्, इयसच्, मात्रच्,) are added in the sense
      of 'measuring as much as'; जानु प्रमाणं अस्य जानुदृश्-
      इयस्-मात्रे उदकं 'water reaching as far as the knee', &c.

न and स †(नञ्, सञ्)—are added to स्त्री and पुस् respectively in
      different senses:—स्त्रैण 'womanly, feminine, suited to
      women, (n.) womanhood' &c.; पौंस 'manly, heroic, fit
      for a man, (n.) manhood, virility'.

पाष—is added as a depreciatory termination; भिषक्पाषः 'a bad
      doctor, a quack'; वैयाकरणपाषः &c.; added to केश it
      has the sense of 'a collection of', as केशपाषः (पश and
      पस्त are also added to केश in the same sense)

मय (मयट्)—§ is added in the sense of (1) 'made of'; उभः विकारः
      इत्ययं 'made of earth,' काष्ठमयं 'made of wood' &c.;
      and (2) in that of profusion or excess' चूर्णे प्रचुरं.

* प्रमाणे द्वयसद्यत्तनमात्रचः । Pāṇ. V. 2, 37.
† स्त्रीपुंसाभ्यां नञ्स्नञौ भवनात् । Pāṇ. VI. 1, 87.
§ मयट् वा एतयोर्भाषायामभक्ष्याच्छादनयोः Pāṇ. IV. 3. 143.
