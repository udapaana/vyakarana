§ 718. The roots वह् implying reproach, पण् meaning 'to
transact business,' and वृ 9. Atm. not denoting limit or restraint,
take the suffix य; as अवद्यं पापं 'sin is reprehensible or merits con-
demnation,' but अवृद्यं ( अव्+वृद्+क्यप् i. e. य ) गुरुनाम 'the name
of a preceptor ought not to be uttered ( out of respect );' पण्या
गौ: 'a cow is saleable;' but पाण्य: ( पण्+ण्यत् i. e. य ) ब्राह्मण: 'a
Brâhmaṇa deserving praise;' वर्य 'that can be chosen or sought
after;' as शतेन वर्या कन्या 'hundred men ( i. e. any one ) can seek
the hand of a girl;' but द्वर्या ( वृ+क्यप् i. e. य ) कन्या 'to be married
by a particular man.'

§ 719. The root वह् not denoting an instrument for carrying,
and कृ in the sense of 'a master or Vaishya' take य; वह्य 'a
carriage or a vehicle;' but वाह्य ( वह्+ण्यत् ) 'what can be borne;'
अर्य: 'a master or a Vaishya;' but आर्य ( कृ+ण्यत् ) 'fit to be
approached or adored.'

§ 720. सू with उप in the sense of 'being conceived' takes the
affix य; as उपसरण्यं गौ: गर्भंधानार्थं सूयतेऽत्र उपजन्तुं योग्येत्यर्थ: ( Sid.
Kan. ) but उपसाद्यं ( उपसद्+ण्यत् ) ज्ञानी प्राप्तव्या इत्यर्थ: (Sid. Kan.).
