§ 488. There are two kinds of Perfect, Reduplicative and
Periphrastic.

§ 489. The Redaplicative Perfect is formed of all mono-
syllabic roots beginning with consonants, as well as of those
beginning with the vowels अ or a, and इ, उ, aud a short.

Exceptions—aq, अय्‌, कास्‌ , ATE. These take the Periphrastic
Perfect necessary.

§ 490. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed of all roots
beginning with any vowel except अ or आ which is uaturally or
prosodially long, and of roots of more than one syllable ( roote
of the 10th class and other derivative roots included ). Excep-
tions:—3yuy and ऋरच्छ्‌ which take the Redaplicative Perfeots.

§ 491. The roots उष्‌, faq, जाश, मी, ही, मु, हु, and दरिद्रा take
both the perfects.
