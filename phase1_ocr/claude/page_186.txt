Chapter VIII.

FORMATION OF FEMININE BASES.

§ 305. Feminine bases are derived from the masculine by the addition of the affixes आ (टाप्, डाप्, चाप्), ई (ङीप्, ङीष्, ङीष्), क (कङ्) and ति.

§ 306. Before the affix ई—

(a) nouns ending in consonants assume that form which they take before the termination of Inst. sing.; प्रत्यच्-छु-प्रतीची, राजन्—राज्ञी, भवत्—भवती, श्वन्—शुनी, अर्यमन्—अर्येणी, विद्वस्—विदुषी, &c. There are some exceptions, अर्हन्—अर्हती &c.
