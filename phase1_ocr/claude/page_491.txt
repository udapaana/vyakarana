§ 818. The following words also govern the Instrumental
case:—

(a) Words expressing the idea of use or need, such as कि,
कार्यं, अर्थः, प्रयोजनं and the like, and the root ह with कि used in
the same sense; धनेन कि यः &c. what is the use of his wealth who
&c.; तृणेन कार्यं भवति-धरणाय—'even the rich sometimes stand in
need of grass,' similarly कार्यं: पुत्रेण जातेन यो न विद्वान् धार्मिकः न
स्वामिपादानां मया किमपि प्रयोजनं, &c.

(b) The particles अलं and हन्त meaning 'enough;' अलं रुदितेन
'enough of your weeping ( do not weep );' हन्त अध्यादरेण 'away
with overpressing;' अलं is also used with a gerund अलं अन्यथा
संभाव्य 'away with misunderstanding.'

(c) Particles expressing 'accompaniment' such as साकं
साधं, समं, सह, &c.; आस्स साकं मया सौधे ( Bh. VIII. 70. ); वनं
मया सार्धमेषि प्रभो: Rag. XIV. 63: आसौ तिष्ठत्यते समं हरिणी-
गणाभि: । Śak I. 27. &c.

(d) Words meaning 'having or destitute of;' समलङ्कृतोव्यर्थः:
( परिभवपदं याति रूपवः ) 'though possessed of wealth,' &c.; अर्थेन
हीनः 'destitute of wealth.'

N. B. For the optional use of the Instrumental see the Abl.,
the Gen. and the Loc. cases.
