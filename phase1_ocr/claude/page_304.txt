§ 464. The root प्रस्थू assumes the forms प्रस्षू and गस्षू in the Non-conjugational Tenses.

§ 465. The penultimate क of वक्षू and कृषू is changed to र before a consonantal strong termination in the General Tenses.

§ 466. The intermediate इ is weak in the case of the root विक्षू 6. A. 7 P.; and optionally so in the case of कृषू.

§ 467. The roots दीधी 2 A. 'to shine' and वेधी 2 A. 'to go' do not take Guṇa or Vṛddhi before any termination. They also drop their final vowel before the intermediate इ and अ. दरिद्रा also drops its आ before a non-conjugational termination except in the Desiderative and the Aorist where it retains it optionally.
