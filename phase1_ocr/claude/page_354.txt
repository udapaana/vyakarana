Exceptions:—

(1) छ and ज, when Parasmaipadi, belong to the Fifth Variety.

(2) Roots ending in क्र preceded by a conjunct consonant may take Fourth or Fifth Form in the Atmanepada.

(3) भञ्ज् and ष् Par. take the Fifth Form only. ष् A. may take the Fourth or the Fifth Form.

(4) Of Set roots द्र and those ending in क्र (long) when Atm. belong to either (4th or 5th Variety). स्तु, and क्रु when A. belong to the Fourth.

§ 566 (a) In the Parasmaipada the radical vowel takes its Vṛddhi substitute; नी-अनैषीत्; कृ-अकारषीत्; भञ्ज्-अभाक्षित्, &c.

(b) In the Atmanepada, Guṇa is substituted for final ऋ or उ, short or long; final क्र and the penultimate vowel remain unchanged; final क्र is changed to ईर् or ऊर् in accordance with § 394; चि-अचेष्, नी-अनेष्, स्तु-अस्तोष्, सु-असोष्, छ see below, भिद्-अभित्, स्तु-अस्तोष्, दु-अदुष्.

(c) The Penultimate क्र of Anit roots is optionally changed to र; छद्-अकार्षीत् or अकाषीत्.

§ 567. After a short vowel, and after a consonant except a nasal or semi-vowel, the स् of terminations beginning with स्स and स्य is dropped; हु-अहुत 3 sing.; हु-अहुथाः 2 sing.; क्षिप्-अक्षिप्थाः जहित्; छुप्-अछुट 3rd sing. &c.
