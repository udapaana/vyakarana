अ-forms abstract nouns from derivative bases; कृ-सिकीर्षा a: 
desire to do; पुत्रकाम्या a desire to have a son, &c. Also from 
roots ending in a consonant having a long vowel for their 
penultimate; ईह-ईहा desire, ऊह-ऊहा a guess; reasoning, &c.

अह्-similarly forms abstract nouns; it is added to roots marked 
with an indicatory प् and to भिद् and others; जृं-जरा old age; 
व्रप्-व्रपा shame, &c.; भिद्-भिद्या distinction; separation; चिद्-
चिन्ता contemplation, anxiety; शुच्-शुचा cleaning &c.--to क्रुध् 
which changes its र् to क्-क्रपा mercy; to roots ending in आ if 
a preposition or the words प्रद् and अन्तर् precede, दा-प्रदा giving 
gift, भा-प्रभा lustre, &c. पा with श्रत्-श्रद्धा faith; with अन्तर्-
अन्तर्धा disappearance.

क्रुक्-( क्रुच्, पटुक्, इत्र, इक, त्रुन् )-

कुन्-is added to रज्जु्; रजक: 'a washerman,'

पटुलु-io affixed to all roots and denotes the agent of the action; 
कृ-कारक: one who makes, acting, &c.; पच्-पाचक: one who 
cooks; इष्-पातक: द्रा-द्रापक्; पा-पापक्; &c; it is added to 
डाह् and other roots of that group; but in this case the preced- 
ing vowel is not lengthened. शाप्-शापक:, वम्-वमक:, कपक्: 
a killer, जनक: a father, &c. Added to some roots it forms 
the name of diseases; छर्द्-छर्दिका vomiting; वह्-प्रवाहिका 
dysentery; diarrhœa; खर्ज्-विचर्चिका itch, scab, &c; sometimes 
it is added to denote the meanings of roots आस्-आसिका 
sitting, शी-शायिका sleeping, &c.; sometimes it has the sense 
of futurity,. इच्छन् दृष्टिको याति he goes desirous of seeing 
Krishna; सभां पालक: &c.

इत्र-is added to निन्द्, हिंद्, क्षिप्, सार्; नत्र with वि, क्षिप् with परि 
पद्, बद्, व्यै, भाष् 'and ह in the sense of the 'agent,' or 'in the 
habit of;' निन्द्-निन्दक: 'one who blames or is in the habit of 
blaming or censuring others,' हिंद्-हिंसक:, क्षिप्-क्षेपक: &c.; 
-to दिव् and कुश् preceded by आ; आदेवक: 'a gambler,' आक्रोशक: 
'one who calls out or vociferates, reviler.'
