§ 742. A few transitive roots take the affix पलिम् having the same force as the affix य: पचेलिम् fr. पच्, 'fit to ripen or to be cooked; as पचेलिमा मात्राः; छिदेलिमाः (fr. छिद्) सल्लाः 'the pine trees ought to be felled.' &c.

§ 743. The declension of these Participles follows that of nouns ending in अ.

II. INDECLINABLE PARTICIPLES.
(a) Indeclinable Past Participles.

§ 744. The Indeclinable Past Participles are of the nature of gerunds. They fall under two heads:—(1) Those derived by affixing त्वा to the simple root; and (2) Those derived by means of य affixed to the root compounded with prepositions or words used prepositionally; गम्—गत्वा 'having gone;' भू with अनु—अनुभूय 'having experienced,' &c.
