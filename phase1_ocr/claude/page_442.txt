436                                SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 723-728

By means of the affix क्व्यप् ( य )

§ 723. The roots इ 1. 2. P. 'to go,' स्तु, शास् , इ 5. P. A. इ, उप्, and roots having इक् short for their penultimate, except कृप् and दृप्, take the affix क्व्यप् ( य ) in the same sense as यत्. When a root ends in a short vowel, न् is inserted between the final vowel and the affix य, e. g. इ-गम्य 'worthy of being approached,' स्तुत्य 'deserving praise,' शास्—शिष्य 'fit to be instructed;' उप—उप्य; इ—आलम्य; उष्—उष्य 'worthy of being served,' कृत्-कृत्य; दृध—दृध्य 'fit to be increased as wealth, &c.' but कल्प्य ( कृप्+यत् ) 'able,' दृप्—दर्प ( दृप्+ण्यत् ) 'that ought to be teased or hurt.'

§ 724. According to Vāmana, the roots स्तोत्, इह् and यह् take this affix optionally; स्तोत्—स्तय 'praiseworthy,' इह—इह्य; यह—यह्य; optionally they take the affix ण्यत् ( to be given hereafter); स्तोष्य, दोह्य, गोह्य.

§ 725. शुच् also takes this affix optionally; शुच्य 'what is fit or ought to be cleansed;' optionally it takes ण्यत् before which the final च् is changed to क्; मार्जक्.

§ 726. ( a ) इ when it has a Subanta prefixed to it and is without a preposition takes the affix क्व्यप् impersonally; ब्रह्मणो भाव: ब्रह्मभूय 'identity with Brahman.' When no Subanta is used prepositionally with it, it takes the affix यत्; भव्य or प्रभव्य.

( a ) वच् under similar circumstances takes either क्व्यप् or ण्यत् in a passive sense or impersonally; ब्रह्मोच्य or ब्रह्मवच्य 'expounding the Veda' ( ब्रह्म वेद: तस्य वचनं Sid. Kan. ).

727. खन् takes this affix, but drops its न् and adds ई to its penultimate vowel खन्+य=ख+य=ख+ई+य=खेय 'what is fit or ought to be dug or excavated.'

728. भृ 1 cl. takes this affix when the participle so derived does not form a name, भृत्या:'those who ought to be nourished or maintained, hence servants,' &c.; but भार्या: ( भृ+ण्यत् ) a class of Kshatriyas'; when सत् precedes भृ, क्व्यप् and ण्यत् are added optionally संभृत्या: or संभार्या:.
