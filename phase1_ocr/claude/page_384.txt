§ 617. To this सू the augment इ is to be prefixed after Set roots, optionally after Wet ones and not after those that are Anit, subject to the following exceptions:—

(1) Roots ending in ऋ or ॠ, ऌ and ॡ short, and the roots अह् and वह् do not take इ; उ-उयते; Vide § 618.(d); र-सुयत्, &c.

Exceptions:—कृ 'to go,' इ A. ' to respect,' वृ 6 A.'to hold,' and पू A. 'to purify,' admit of इ. (see also 4 below.)

(2) लिह्, अज्ञ्, प्रच्छ्, ञप् take इ necessarily.

(3) इष्, दध्, धम्, स्थष् and कृष्य् do not admit of इ in the Par. (see § 484). In the Atm. they admit इ, the last two do so optionally; इष्-विधत्साति, विधत्सिते, &c.

(4) Roots ending in long कृ and कॄ and the roots दरिद्रा, श्रि, ऊर्णु, धृ, दृ, ह, स्फ, कृप् 'to prosper,' दम्भ्, भस्ज् ज्ञप् (i.e. ज्ञप् of the 10 cl. and optional Cau. Base of ज्ञा), सप् 'to give,' नद्, पद्, छद्, युध्, हृद्, वद्, and हन् (Vide § 485.) take इ optionally.

Exceptions:—कृ 'to scatter,' कृ 'to swallow,' take इ necessarily The intermediate इ is not lengthened in the case of these roots; चिकरिष्, &c.

(5) The roots क्रव्, नद् and हु take इ in the Par. and reject it in the Atm.

§ 618. The radical vowels undergo the following changes before सू—

(a) The सू with इ is strong and without इ weak.

The usual rule as to Guna substitute holds good in the one case and does not in the other; इष्-विधत्सिते, विक्त्स; इ-विपत्सि, &c.
