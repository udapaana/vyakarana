§ 331. * A Bah. ending in नख and मुख and used as proper names forms fem. in आ; सूर्णनखा, गौरमुखा; but ताम्रमुखी a girl having a ruddy face.

§ 332. † Names of the limbs of the body, preceded by words denoting a cardinal point in a compound, form their fem. in ई उदक्मुखी, &c.

§ 333 A Bah. ‡ ending in पाद् (substituted for पाद ) optionally forms its fem. in ई before which it is changed to पद्; व्याघ्रस्य इव पादौ यस्या: सा व्याघ्रपाद्-पदी, सिंपाद्-पदी and in आ when पाद् means 'a foot of a Vedic verse'; द्विपदा त्रक्, एकपदा &c.

(a) But if पाद् be preceded by one of the words कुम्भ, शाल, तूण, हंस, काक, छच्छ, सूकर. &c. the fem. is necessarily formed in ई. पाद् changing into पद् as before; कुम्भपदी 'a woman whose feet are as big as a pitcher.'
