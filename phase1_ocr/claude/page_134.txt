§ 213. A word expressive of a part is compounded with
another signifying a period of time and is placed first; मध्यं
अह्नः; मध्याह्नः mid-day; सायाह्नः; मध्यरात्रं &c.

§ 214.* A word expressive of a period of time that has
passed since a certain occurrence is compounded with another
expressive of the occurrence; as मासो जातस्य यस्य स मासजातः
'one since whose birth a month has elapsed'; so द्व्यहजातः;
संवत्सरजातः &c.

§ 215 † Instances of the Aluk of the Gen. The noun in the
Genitive may retain its case ending—

(a) when reproach is to be implied, as चोरस्यकुलम्, but
ब्राह्मणकुलम्. Also when देवानां is followed by प्रिय and the whole
means a fool; otherwise देवप्रियः.

(b) when the words वाच्, दिश् and पक्षत् are followed by पुत्रि,
दण्ड and हर respectively; as वाचस्पुत्रिः 'skilful speech,' दिशो-
दण्डः: 'a particular appearance of stars in the shape of a staff in
the sky;' पक्षतोहरः 'one who robs another in his very presence
while he is looking on,' hence 'a goldsmith, a rogue.'

(c) in the following cases:—विशेषासः: 'a king of Kāśhī,'
दिवस्पतिः 'Indra,' वाचस्पतिः lord of speech, Brihaspati; शुनःशेपः,
शुनःपुच्छः and शुनोलाङ्गूलः: 'sons of Ajigarta.'

(d) and optionally when followed by पुत्र and censure is im-
plied; दास्याः पुत्रः or दासीपुत्रः a bastard, a knave; but ब्राह्मणीपुत्रः.
