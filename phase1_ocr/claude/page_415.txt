युज्*—preceded by प्र or उप, or generally by a prep. beginning 
or ending with a vowel, and does not refer to sacrificial vessels, 
is Atm. प्रयुंक्ते, उपयुंक्ते, प्रयुञ्जानः सिद्धा भावः: ( Bh. VIII. 39): but 
यज्ञपात्राणि प्रयुनक्ति 'arranges the sacrificial vessels;' स इमां आश्रम-
चर्यै नियुङ्क्ते (S'ák.) 'who appoints her to the duties of the hermitage;' 
अन्वयुंक्त एकमीश्वरः: क्षितिः Rag. XI 62. 'the lord of the earth asked 
his preceptor;' षणब्दप्युपायान्युपायञ्जः षड्पारकः 'Aja employed the 
six expedients beginning with peace.' Rag. VIII. 21.

युध्—in the Cau. takes the Par.

रम्‡—'to sport' changes its Pada when preceded by the pre-
positions वि, आ and परि; विरमेतत्समादिरम् 'cease, oh child, from 
this' ( Uttar. I. 33.). रात्रिरेष स्मरेत्यपि Ibid. I. 27. आरमति, 
विरामोत्तिश्वति चारदेव Manu. II 79. पतिरमति; क्षणं पर्यरमतस्य दर्शनात् 
'was for a moment delighted with his sight.' With उप it is 
Par.; यज्ञदत्तं उपरमति रमवत्तीत्यर्थः ( Sid. Kau.) When intran-
sitively used it takes either Pada; उपरमति-ते. 'sports'; cf. स्वरे-
तीच् समप्रवचन; नाच् सौतिस्युपातात्, &c. Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 54. 55.

ली—'to melt, to embrace' is Atm. in the cau. in the sense of 
'adoring, defeating or deceiving;' जटाभिर्लाप्यते 'is adorable on 
account of his matted hair;' दण्डेन लाप्यते श्वा 'the dog is defeated 
by means of a stick;' इन्द्रेणो शतिकाम्लापयते बध्यमतीत्यर्थः ( over-
powers ); मोर्ख्येण लाप्यते ब्राह्मणः 'the Brāhmaṇa is deceived by 
reason of his folly.' बाहुबलाप्यते बध्यमतीत्यर्थः ।

वञ्च्‡—in the cau. is Atm. in the sense of 'deceiving;' माणवकं 
वञ्चयते 'deceives Māṇavaka;' but अहिं वञ्चयति 'avoids a serpent.'
