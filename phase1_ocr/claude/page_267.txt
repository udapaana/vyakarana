Second, Third and Seventh Classes.

§ 416. Special rules of Sandhi of the finals of roots and the initial letters of terminations:—

(1) The ending उ of a root takes its Vṛddhi substitute when followed by a consonantal strong termination; as उ+सि=नौति.

(2) The final ऋ or उ, short or long, of a root is changed to अर् or उर् before a vowel weak termination.

(3) The ending ऋ of roots is changed to र् when followed by any consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, or by nothing; and that of roots beginning with ऋ to घ under the same circumstances.

(4) The initial द् or ध् of a termination is changed to ध् after a soft aspirate (4th letter of a class).

(5) क् or ग् followed by ह is changed to क्.
