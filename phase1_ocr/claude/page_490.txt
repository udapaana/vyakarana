484                         SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.               [§815-817

§815. * A characteristic attribute indicative of the existence
of a particular state or condition is put in the Instrumental;
जटाभि: तापस: he is an ascetic (which is apparent) from his
matted hair (जटाद्वाच्यतापसत्वविशिष्ट इत्यर्थ: Sid. Kau.).

§816. † The Instrumental is also used to express the cause
or motive or the object of purpose of an action as distinguished
from the mere instrument of it; पुण्येन दृष्टो हरि: Hari was seen
by (virtue of) merit; तेनापराधेन दण्डनीयोसि thou art to be
punished for that fault; अध्ययनेन वसति dwells for the purpose of
studying; also when the object to be accomplished is simply
implied; अलं श्रमेण away with your efforts i. e. they will not
succeed; श्रमेण साध्यं नास्ति इत्यर्थ: (Sid. Kau.)

§817 The Instrumental is used with words expressing the
idea of—
(a) excelling; प्राज्ञं महाराज त्वयातिशेरे Oh fortunate one, you
excel your ancestors in that (devotion); भासातिशायबति भास्य
सुप्रभास: (Mu. 3. 17.) he surpasses, by his lustre, the lustre
of the sun; रविच्छटा खलु उपरिष्ठानलिता धनलतामि: । S'ak. I.

(b) resemblance, likeness, equality; स्वरेण पितरमनुकरोति resembles
his father in voice; देहबन्धने स्वरेण च राममनुकरोति (Utta. 4.);
अस्य मुखं मातृ: मुखेन संवदति his face resembles that of his mother;
विष्णुना सदृशो वीर्ये equal to Vishnu in valour.

(c) swearing; भरतेनात्मना चाहं शपे I swear by Bharata and
myself; शापितासि मम जीवितेन I conjure thee by my life.

(d) rejoicing, being pleased; प्रज्ञया गुरौ प्रत्युपकारया च प्रीतोस्मि
I am pleased with thee by thy devotion to your preceptor and
compassion upon me; कापुरुष: स्वल्पकेनापि तुष्यति a low person is
satisfied with little.

* गर्थविशेषणे । Pāṇ. II. 3. 21.
† हेतौ । Pāṇ. II. 3. फलमपि हेतु: । क्रयादिसाधारणं निर्व्यापार-
साधारणं च हेतुत्वम् । करणार्थं तु क्रियामात्रनिर्वर्त्यं व्यापारनियतं च ।
Sid. Kau.
