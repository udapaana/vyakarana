§ 485. The roots कृत्, चृत्, दृत्, वृत् and हृत् take इ optionally
when followed by an ārdhadhātuka (Non-conjugational termi-
nation) beginning with स except in the Aorist; कृत्-कतिष्यति,
कत्स्यति; अकतिष्यम्, अकत्स्यम् Cond. &c.

§ 486. In the case of इ with अति, गा is optionally substi-
tuted for इ in the Conditional and the Aorist. इ is substituted
for the final vowel of the verbs वृ 3 P. A. 1 P. षा, दो, दै, धै, ना,
ध्या, गा (substituted for इ 2. P and इ with अति,) वा, हा, and
सो before a consonantal weak termination. All terminations
added to गा substituted for इ are weak.
