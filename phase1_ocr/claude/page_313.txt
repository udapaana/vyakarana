§ 494. The Parasmaipada singular terminations are strong;
the rest weak. Before the strong terminations the penultimate
short vowel takes its Guna substitute. The final vowel and
penultimate अ take Vṛddhi necessarily in the third and
optionally in the first person singular. In the 2nd person
singular the final vowel takes Guna and the penultimate अ
remains unchanged.

§ 495. *Special rules about the admission of the intermedi-
ate इ before the terminations य, म, य, से, वहे, महे, ध्वे; see (§ 457).

(a) All roots, whether set or anit, except छ, चु, यु, ड्, क्नु,
शु, श्रु, and श्रु admit इ. But छ with सम्, and डु admit it before
य; as संस्करिय, बधरिय.

(b) Anit roots ending in short ऋ, except कृ, reject it
before य; as सत्सर्य from सृ; but आरिय from कृ.

(c) Anit roots with a final vowel or with a penultimate अ
admit it optionally before य.

§ 496. When initial इ or उ of a root takes Guṇa or Vṛddhi
substitute, the reduplicative इ or उ is changed to इय or उव;
otherwise the two vowels combine to form long ई or ऊ: as
इयप+य=इयेय, उओश्+अ=उवोश; ईरिव; &c.

§ 497. The final उ, short or long, is changed to उव before a
vowel weak termiation and इ to इय or य as it is preceded by a
conjunct consonant or not; as इष्+इव=इष्विव 1 dual; निनी+
इव=निन्य+इव=निन्यिव. विशि+इव=विशिशियिव, &c
