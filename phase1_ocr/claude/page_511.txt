§ 868. † The words साधु and निपुण, not preceded by the pre-
positions अनु, परि and प्रति, are used with the Loc. when the
sense of adoration is to be conveyed; मातरि साधुर्निपुणो वा reveren-
tially disposed towards his mother; but निपुण: राज्ञ: भृत्य: a clever
servant of the king. When preceded by the prepositions अनु, परि
and प्रति these are used with the Accusative; साधुर्निपुणो मातरं
प्रति पश्य वा.

upon Hari; पत्या प्रस्थितेन पत्यौ प्रस्थिते वा गोविन्दउत्सुका, a woman gets
anxious ( or restless ) when her husband goes out; तैजसिबिर्विक्रुष्ट-
