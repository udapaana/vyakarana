§ 663. The Causals of the roots सृ, डु, जर, र with अपि, ङ्, 
ङु, and षु are Parasm; बोधयति पश्यं, योषयति कान्तानि, नाशयति 
दुःखं, जनयति हसं, अध्याययति, धाययति धाययतीत्यर्थः ( Sid. 
Kau. ); दृन्दयति विलासयतीत्यर्थः (Sid. Kau.). भावयति स्वन्दयतीत्यर्थः 
( Sid. Kau. ).

664. The following is an alphabetical list of the roots that 
change their proper Pada after certain prepositions under the 
circumstances given in each case.

अप्-when preceded by a preposition is conjugated in either 
Pada; कन्यां निरस्यति-ते.

र with अपि-in the causal takes the Parasm. अध्याययति.

ऊह-when preceded by a preposition is conjugated in either 
Pada; पापं अपोहति-ते 'he destroys sin;' तदपोहति 'discards;' सम्-
हति-ते 'he gathers together.'

क् with सम् is Atm.; समाप्ता समाशीर्घाः Bh. VIII. 16; 'all 
my desires have come to me i. e. are gratified.'

क्च्छ with सम्-is Par. when used transitively, and Atm. 
when used intransitively; सम्च्छति 'he collects;' सम्च्छते 'is 
collected.'

कृ-without a preposition admits of either Pada. It is 
Parasm. with अनु and परा*; अनुकरोति भगवतो नारायणस्य । (Kâś.), 
तौ हृन्मानस्यकूर्देव् &c. ( Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 50. ); it is Atm. with 
preposition in following senses:-(1) मन्त्रण † or 'hurting,' 'kill-
ing,' as उत्कुरुते 'informs against ( with a view to injure );' 
( 2 ) अधिक्षेपण or 'censuring, overcoming;' उदाकुरुते श्येनो भूर्जिकाम् 
'the hawk reproves the snail;' ( 3 ) सेवन or 'serving, attending
