§ 654. The affix य is added to a few nouns. such as भूरा
'much,' मन्द 'slow,' पण्डित 'learned,' सुमनस् 'generous-minded,'
उन्मनस् 'agitated,' &c. in the sense of 'becoming what it was not
before,' or 'what it was not like to before,' भूरा: भवति भूरायते 'what
was not much now becomes much,' उन्मनायति 'he (who was not
agitated before) becomes agitated,' similarly सुमनायते &c.

§ 655. The following are the different senses in which the
affix स्यक् is used in the particular cases given below.

(a) It is added to सह, कह. कृच्छ्र and गहन in the sense of 'de-
sirous of committing sin,' पापं चिकीर्षति-सहायते, कहायते, &c.,
added to कह it has also the sense of 'prompt in', कहाय कुरुते-
कहायते (पापं कर्तुमुत्सहते इत्यर्थः; Sid Kau.).

(b) to रोमन्थ used objectively, रोमन्थायते 'is ruminating.'

(c) to वाष्प 'tears,' उष्मन् 'heat,' and फेन, in the sense of 'send-
ing forth, vomiting,' वाष्पायते 'he sheds tears,' उष्मायते 'gives
out heat,' फेनायते 'sends forth foam.'
