SECTION III.

AFFIXES FORMING ADVERBS.

अक (अकच्)—is inserted before the final vowel of indeclinables·
        without any change of meaning ; उच्चै:–उच्चकै:; नीचै:-
        नीचकै:

एन—is added to nouns indicating 'a direction' in the sense of
        'not far from'; पूर्वेण ग्रामं 'to the east of the village
        not far from it'; अपरेण ग्रामं, &c.

तस् (तसि)—has the sense of the Ablative; आदित: 'from the
        beginning,' मध्यत:, स्वत:, वर्णत: &c.; sometimes this is
        added in the sense of the Gen.; as देवा अर्जुनतस्भवन्
        'the gods declared themselves on the side of Arjuna';
        (तलस्)–परित:· 'on all sides'; अभित: 'on both sides'.

ना—विना 'without', नाना 'in various ways', &c.

वत्—*is affixed in the sense of 'equally with' or 'like to' when
        the equality or likeness refers to an action; ब्राह्मणेन
        तुल्यं शाक्यप्रव्रजिते; but पुत्रेण तुल्य: स्थूल: and not पुत्रवत्
        स्थूल:; similarly प्रह्रियवत्; or (2) 'as in the case of that'
        मधुरायामिव मथुरावत् तत्र प्राकार:; चैत्रस्य इव चैत्रवत् (वैत्रस्य
        गाव:); (3) विधिवद्होति विधिवत् पूज्यते.

शस्—अल्पश: 'little by little'; बहुश: &c.

च्वि—is added to nouns or indeclinables to express that a person
        or thing, not being like what is denoted by the base to
        which it is added, becomes or is made like it.

कुत्सितायामे सम्पवक्तरि च्वि: । Pāṇ. V. 4. 50. अशुततन्त्राव इति
