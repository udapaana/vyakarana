जन्—in the Causal takes the Parasm. जनयति.

जि*—when preceded by वि and परा in the sense of 'to con-
quer,' and 'to defeat' or 'to find unbearable,' respectively, is
Atm. विजयते, शत्रुं पराजयते, -अध्ययनात् पराजयते 'gets tired of
study,' सं पराजयमानोऽसौ 'filling up the sky, &c ' तां पराजयमानां
स भीतः 'her who was getting disgusted with,' &c. Bhaṭṭ.
VIII. 9. 71.

ज्ञा†—'to know' used intransitively by itself is Atm. सर्पिषो
जानीते ( सर्पिषा उपायेन प्रवर्तते Sid. Kan. i. e. 'proceeds to perform
a sacrifice having obtained ghee for it); with the preposition
अप it is Atm. in the sense of 'denying;' as शतं अपजानीते 'denies
a hundred,' with प्रति in the sense of 'acknowledging or
promising,' and with सम् in the sense of 'expecting,' it is also
Atm.; शतं प्रतिजानीते 'acknowledges a hundred,' हरचापारोपणेन
कन्यादानं प्रतिजानीते 'promises the hand of his daughter by (i. e.
on the condition of) the drawing of Hara's bow,' शतं संजानीते
'looks for a hundred,' मातरि स्नेहं संजानीते 'thinks of his
mother.' When this is used without a preposition and when
the fruit accrues to the agent it takes the Atm. गां जानीते;
when a preposition is prefixed to it and it is used transitively,
it takes the Par. स्वर्गलोकं न प्रजानाति कश्चित्. In the Desiderative
this root takes the Atm.
