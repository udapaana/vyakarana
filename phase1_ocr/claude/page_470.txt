उक—(उकन्)—is affixed to लष्, पद्, पद्, स्था, घ्र, हृष्, धृष्, कम्, गम्,
and स्रु and denotes the agent; लष्—लाषुकः sporting, glittering
पादुकः falling स्रु—स्राषुकः happening, living; हृष्—घाषुकः 
कम्-कामुकः amorous.

इर्—(इरन्)—is affixed to विद्, भिद्, and छिद्; विदुरः 'who knows,
knowing,' भिदुरः 'breaking, brittle;' छिदुरः 'cutting.'

अक—(इक) is added to जागृ and the Pre. Bases of यज्, जप्
and दंश; जागरूक 'watchful,' &c. (vide Bhatti. II. 22. Rag.
XIV. 85. Sis. XX 36); यजः यजः अतिशयेन वा यजतीतीलिः;
यायजूकः 'one who performs sacrifices frequently' (see Bhatti.
II. 20.); जपः जपः अतिशयेन वा जपतीति जंजपूकः 'one who
mutters prayers repeatedly, an ascetic;' दंशः दंशः अतिशयेन वा
दशतीति दंदशूकः 'biting frequently, a serpent, demon' (vide
Bhatti I. 26).

किन्, किप्, and विच्—Derivatives are formed with these affixes
    which are added to roots and then dropped; the difference
    between the first two is that in the case of the latter
    र् is inserted between it and the root, if it ends in a
    short vowel.

किन्—is affixed to स्पृश् preceded by a subanta; घृतस्पृश् 'one who
    touches ghee; मन्त्रस्पृश् 'one who touches anything after recit-
    ing a holy verse,' &c. except when the subanta means water;
    उदकस्पर्शी: 'one who touches water' and not उदकस्पृश्. The
    following words are to be accepted as they are; यजु्—ऋत्विज्
    (ऋतो ऋतो यजते) sacrificing regularly at every season;
    a priest who officiates at a sacrifice; धृष्—धृष्ट—proud,
    haughty; स्रज्—स्रज् a garland; दिश्—दिश् a direction; त्रिह्—
    त्रिष्टुभ् name of a metre, words like प्राक्षिन् derived from the
    roots अक्ष् and the words युज् and काञ्च् ought to be considered
    as derived by means of this affix.
