THE SECOND OR SIMPLE FUTURE.

§ 936. * When the close proximity of a future action is
intended, the Second Future or the Present may be used; कदा गमि
ष्यासि when will you go? एव गच्छामि or गमिष्यामि I shall just go,

§ 937. † When there is the idea of hope implied in a
conditional form, the Aorist, the Present or the Simple Future
may be used in both the clauses to denote a future time;
देवश्चेत्प्रवर्षीत वर्षति वर्षिष्यति वा धान्यमवाप्स्म वप्स्याम: वा । (Sid.
Kau ). if it were to rain we would sow corn.

§ 938. The Simple Future is sometimes used as a courte-
ous way of command; एष्यावसरे यदि गमिष्यसि (Vik. IV.) then
you will go (i. e. please then go) to the lake, &c,
