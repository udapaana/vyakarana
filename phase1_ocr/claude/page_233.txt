§ 367. Two or more of these prepositions may also be
combined and prefixed; e. g. अभिनिविश् 'to enter into with
resolution,' सहप्रागम् 'to come in close contact with,' &c.

§ 368. When used in a compound the verb may be omitted
after the prepositions अति, अधि, अनु, अप, अव, अभि, उप, परि and
प्रति; अतिकान्तो मालां अतिमाल: &c. Vide § 232.

§ 369. There are several other words, also styled Gati by
Sanskrit grammarians, used prepositionally. These are prefixed
to certain roots only. We give the more important of them below.

(a) अच्छ* 'towards' is prefixed to वृ and to roots implying
motion; अच्छगम्य-गम्य 'going near,' अच्छपतत् 'flying towards,'
अच्छूद्य 'having spoken to.'

(b)(1) अन्वाये 'giving strength to the weak,' अलं in the
sense of 'decorating,' ऊरी, उररी, उत्री all implying 'assent,
acceptance or promise,' खात् and similar imitative sounds, असत्
and सत् in the sense of 'dishonouring or honouring,' प्राश्व
denoting 'fastening,' &c. are prefixed to कृ; अन्वाजेकरणाय, उरी-
करणाय, भत्सकार:, खत्कृत, कात्कृत, प्राश्वकरणाय, &c.

* अच्छगत्यर्थवदेषु । Pāṇ. 1. 4. 69.
