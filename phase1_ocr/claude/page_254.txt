248                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.          [ § 394-395.

§ 394. * The ई (long) of a root, penultima'e or final, when
it does not take Guṇa or Vṛddhi, is changed to इय, and to उव्
if a labial or व् precedes; and the इ or उ is length·ned when इय् 
or उव् is followed by a consonant; e. g. जी 4. P. 'to become old,'
जीर्यति, अजीर्यत्, &c.; कृ 6. P. किरति, अकिरत्, &c; here कि does
not become long as किर् is followed by अ; कृत् 10. P. A.
कीर्तयति-ते, अकीर्तयत्-त, &c.

§ 395. The penultimate इ, उ, ऋ or ऌ of a root, followed
by र् or व् is lengthened when a consonant follows; †e. g. उर्वी
1. A. to measure, to play, ऊर्वीते; similarly कूर्वी, खूर्वी, धूर्वी
all A. and meaning to play, हूर्वी, to act dishonestly, to be
crooked, ढूर्वी to faint; स्फूर्वी to spread, to forget; स्फुर्जी to
thunder, to shine; उर्वी, ढुर्वी, धुर्वी, दुर्वी, घुर्वी all meaning to kill, धूर्वी
to try, मूर्वी, &c., all Parasm. and belonging to the 1st class,
lengthen their penultimate vowel; दिवृ 4. P. दीव्यति; similarly
शिवृ has सीव्यति, छिवृ, छीव्यति, &c.
