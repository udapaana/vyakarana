506                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.              [§ 871-875

§ 871. * Words expressive of the interval of time or space
are used with the Ablative or the Locative, अद्य भुक्त्वार्य द्वौ
दिवसाय भोक्ता having dined to-day he will dine again after two
days; इहस्थोऽर्धे कोशात् कोशौ वा लक्ष्यं विध्येत् standing here he will hit
a mark two miles distant.

§ 872. † The prepositions उप in the sense of 'exceeding'
and अधि in that of 'the master of' govern the Locative; उपपरार्धे
हरेर्गुणः the merits of Hari exceed a parārdha; अधि क्षिति रामः or अधि
रामे क्षः Rāma is the lord of the earth; in other senses these
prepositions are used with the Accusative, for which see § 805.

§ 873. The words दूर and अन्तिक and others having the same
sense are used in the Locative also; ग्रामस्य दूरे—दूरं—दूरेण—दूरात्
वाः तस्याः समीपे—समीपेन—समीपात् भवति.

§ 874. Verbs having the sense of 'love, regard for, attach-
ment to' such as स्निह्, अनुरञ्ज्, अभिलष्, रुच् &c. and their deriva-
tives generally govern the Locative; पिता पुत्रे स्निह्यति a father
loves his son; आति मे सोदरस्नेहोऽपि एतेषु ( S'ak. I ) I have a
sisterly affection for them also; न स्वच्छ तापसकन्यकायां ममाभिलाषः
I do not, indeed, love the ascetic's daughter, अशुद्धमन्त्रिणि राजनि
जनता नानुरज्यते people do not love a king whose ministers are
corrupt; प्रातुर्वृत्तस्य मार्यायां शैलुषस्येव कान्यकाः ( Manu. S. III. 179 ).
रमते रमते ( Māl. Māḍ. III. 2. ) takes pleasure in solitude; रतः
श्रेयसि ( Bhaṭṭ. I. ) devoted to his welfare.

Note:—अनुरञ्ज् and अभिलष् are sometimes used with the
Accusative also; समत्यमनुरञ्जन्ति ( Rāma ); मालालामभिलषन्ति
( Bhaṭṭ. IV. 22. )

§ 875. Verbs of acting, behaving towards,&c. such as वृत्,
व्यवहृ, &c. and of throwing, such as अस्, क्षिप्, क्षिर् &c. govern
the Locative; गुरुषु विनयेन वृत्तिः कार्या one should act modestly
towards respectable persons; कुञ्ज विपक्षभृगिणि क्षिपलीजले ( S'ak.

* द्वमासीयष्टो फालक्षम्ये । Pāṇ. II. 3. 5.
† उपान्वध्यवसाये च । Pāṇ. II 3. 9.
