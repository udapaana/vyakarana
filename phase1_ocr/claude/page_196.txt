(a)*युवन् forms its feminine by the addition of ति before which the final न् is dropped; युवतिः

§ 326. † If prātipadika end in वन् it changes its न् to र् before ई; शक्वन्-शक्वरी the strong one; पीवन्-पीवरी, शर्वन्-शर्वरी the night, सुत्वानमतिक्रान्ता अतिशर्वरी, अतिपीवरी, &c.

Exception.—‡ But if a base end in वन् applied to roots ending in a soft consonant; or if a compound has such a base for its latter member, the fem. is formed by adding आ only, before which the preceding अन् is dropped, e. g. अवाबन्+आ=अवाबा a Brāhmaṇa woman (ब्राह्मणी) or a female thief; राजगुप्ता.

§ 327. § A Bahuvrīhi ending in अन् forms its fem. in आ optionally; as शु+पर्वन्=शुपर्वन्-नी, बहु+भजन्=न्जा, &c. but if the word ending in अन् be such as drops its अ before the आ of the Inst. sing. it takes ई also optionally; e.g. बहु+राजन्=बहुराजन्-राजा-राज्ञी &c.

(a) × If a Bah. ends in वन्, the न् is optionally changed to र्; as बहुपूर्ववन्-बहुपूर्वा or बहुपीवरी a town in which there are many fishermen.

§ 328. ‖ If a Bah. end in ऊधस् it takes the suffix ई in the fem. before which न् is substituted for the final अस्; पीन ऊधः यस्याः सा पीनोधसी having a large udder; कुण्डोधसी (see Rag. I. 84.); also when it is preceded by a numeral or an indeclinable;
