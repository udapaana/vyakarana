§ 857. * When past passive participles are used in the sense
of the present tense the Genitive is used; राज्ञां मतो बुधः पूजितो
वा respected, known or honoured by kings; यो धर्मः स सतां मतः;
तमस्य संमते Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 124.

(a) Past participles showing the place of an action, as well
as those used as abstract nouns are used with the Genitive
मुकुन्दस्थासितानिहसिंहै धाते रमापतेः । मुक्तमेतदुर्नतस्थैरन्युगोव्यो विद्वसम् ॥
मयूरस्य नृत्तं, कोकिलस्य व्याहृतं, नटस्य कुक्तं, छात्रस्य हसितं, &c. (Mb.)
See. Bhaṭṭ. VIII. 125.

§ 858. † The Genitive is not used with present participles
except that of द्विष्, verbal derivatives ending in र and उक
except that derived from कृष्, verbal indeclinables, past parti-
ciples, passive and active, nouns formed with the affix तृन् (see
p. 466) and with such as mean 'in the habit of, or having the
properties of or doing any thing well'; कर्तुं कुर्वन् or कुर्वाणः; but
हरे द्वेष्य वा द्विष्य हरि: Hari, the enemy of Mura; हरिं विद्वेष्टुः desir-
ous of seeing Hari; हरिं अलङ्कुर्विष्णुः, दैत्यान् घातको हरिः Hari is the
killer of demons; लक्ष्म्याः कामुकः, जगद्गुरुः, इदं कर्तु &c. विष्णुना सुता
