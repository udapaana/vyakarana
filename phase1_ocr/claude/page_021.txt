§ 21. When अ or आ is followed by ए or ऐ and ओ or औ
ऐ and औ are respectively substituted for both;; छुप्य+एकन्त=
छुप्येकन्त्य; देव+ऐश्वर्य=दैवैश्वर्य the majesty of god; सा+एव=सैव
she alone; भव+औषर्य=भवौषर्य medicine against birth and
re-birth. विद्या+औत्सुक्यं=विद्यौत्सुक्यं longing for knowledge.

Exceptions:—

(a) † If a preposition ending in अ be followed by a root
beginning with ए or औ, ए or औ is substituted; प्र+एजते=प्रेजते
quakes very much; उप+ओषति=उपोषति burns something near;
but if the root be a denominative, vrddhi is optional; उप+एक-
कीयति=उपेककीयति or उपैककीयति; प्र+ओषीयति=प्रोषीयति or
प्रौषीयति.
