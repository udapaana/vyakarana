क is affixed to इन् preceded by जाया or पति, इन् changing to अ
the whole meaning 'bearing an inauspicious mark'; a hus-
band causing the death of his wife—जायक्न: bearing a mark
on his body indicative of the death of his wife; similarly
पतिक्न,—this is also added to इन् when the agent of the action
is not a man; पित्तक्न checking bile, such as ghee; पतिक्न (पाणि-
रेक्षा ), &c.;—to इन् preceded by इत्तिन् and क्षत in the sense of 
'having the power to do what is denoted by the root;' इत्तिक्न
'one able to kill an elephant,' &c.,—and preceded by पतिक् and 
ताल in the sense of 'one having the skill;' तालिक्न: 'one who beats
time with the hand, also a drummer ( beating a drum with
the hand );'—to पा 'to drink' and जै if not preceded by a pre-
position; सोमप्न: 'one who drinks the Soma juice'; साम वाक्षतिति
साम्न: 'one who chants the Sāma Veda', but सामसंवाद्य: (when
a prep. precedes it ); पा 'to protect' takes अ; क्षीरपा ब्राह्मणी, &c.

न—is affixed to चर् preceded by अन्त, अत्यन्त, अजस्र्, दूर्, पार, उर्वी,
अनन्त, सर्व, पद 'creeping on the ground,' उत्तर् and विहायस्
and denotes the agent; to चर् preceded by दूर and दु and shows
the place of action; before this the final consonant with the
preceding vowel or the final vowel of a root is dropped; अन्तं
अवस्थतिति अन्तन: 'one who goes to the end;' अत्यन्तन: 'a traveller,'
पद्मन:, उरगन: 'a serpent;' विहायस् is changed to विह—विहन: 'a
bird,' दुर्ग: 'a fortress,'&c.;—to हन् when a blessing is implied'
तत् पुत्र: शत्रुह: भवेत् 'may thy son be the destroyer of his enemies;'
—also to हन् with अप after the words कैश and तमस्; कैशापह:
'removing pain, consoling, a son;' क्षसोपह: 'the dispeller of
darkness, the sun;'—to जन् preceded by a word having the sense
of the Loc. or of the Ab. and not denoting a class, or when it
is preceded by a preposition and the whole is a name; गोकुलज:
'born in a stable;' सरसिजं 'a lotus;' संकालज: 'produced after
some operation;' अनुज: &c.; प्रजा:, अनुज: 'a younger brother;'
the words त्रिज:, अज:, ब्राह्मज: &c. are also formed by means
of this affix: this is also added to खन् with परि; परिखा 'a ditch'
