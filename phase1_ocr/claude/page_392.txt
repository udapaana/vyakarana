§ 630. When a root ends in a nasal preceded by अ and the 
nasal is not dropped, म् ( changeable to an Anuswāra or the
nasal of the class to which the letter following it belongs ) is
inserted between the reduplicative अ and the first radical
consonant; the अ of the reduplicative syllable remains un-
changed ( against § 629 b. )
