§ 945. When time after a घटाते (nearly equal to an hour) is expressed, the Imperative is used; घटाविजतां स्व offer the sacrifice after an hour.

§ 946. The Imperative with the particle स्व is used when a request is courteously expressed; बालमध्यापय स्व please teach the child.

§ 947. The Imperative has sometimes the force of the present when used in combination with the particle मा; मा भवतु no, it is not so; मा च ते निमत्तः शङ्कुमत्युपैक्षद् पाण्डिव ।

§ 948. इच्छामि भवान्भुञ्जीत or भुङ्क्ताम् I wish you should dine. See § 958.

§ 949. *There is a peculiar use of the Imperative which ought to be noticed. The Imperative second person singular is repeated when frequency of an'act is indicated and the whole used with the root in any tense; यादि यादि इति यादि ( Sid. Kau. ) he goes every now and then; so यात यातेति मुहुर् याय्य; यादि याद्यित्यध्ययीत्; अधीष्वाधीष्वेत्यधीते he studies steadily. The Imperative second person is also used when several acts are described as done by the same person; सक्तून् पिब, पानाः खादेत्यध्यवहरति ( Sid. Kau. ) he takes his food, now eating barley now eating fried rice; similarly अर्धं शुद्ध दाक्षिकमात्मादयत्येत्यध्यवहरते ( Sid. Kau. ).
