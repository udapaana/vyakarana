ह्र†—with अनु is Atm. in the senses of 'following the habits
of, acquiring the natural quality of;' वेदक्रमथा अनुहरन्ते 'horses
always follow the gait of their progenitors;' so मातरं भावः
अनुहरन्ते; in the sense of 'acquiring a new quality by imitation'
it takes the Par. पितरमनुहरति 'imitates his father.'

हे‡—उप, preceded by उप, नि, वि, and सम् and used intransiti-

Chānura to battle;' आहुत चैदिरागमताति Sis. XXI. 1, but
