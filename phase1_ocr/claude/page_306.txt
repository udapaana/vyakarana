§ 476. स is inserted before the ending consonant of the root
सच्, before which स् is dropped, and after the vowel of नश्,
when they are followed by any consonant except a nasal or a
semi-vowel; सक्षा &c.; नेष्टा, नशिष्टा. The स् of सच् is changed
to श when not dropped.

§ 477. ति is substituted for अच् 1 P. 'to go' necessarily
before any non-conjugational termination, and optionally 
before such as begin with any consonant except य; वेता, अजिता;
वेष्यति, अजिष्यति &c.

§ 480. त् is substituted for the ending त् of a root when followed by any non-conjugational termination beginning with स्.

* These terminations, it will be seen, are obtained by adding
those of the Present to स्य with its अ lengthened before initial 
य and थ, and dropped before a vowel.

† These are obtained similarly by adding the terminations of
the Imperfect to स्य the usual rules of Sandhi being observed.
