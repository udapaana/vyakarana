§ 753. Roots of the 8th class ending in a nasal, except धन् 
and the roots मन्, बन् and हन्, drop their nasal necessarily; गह्,
नह्, यम् and रम् do it optionally; तन् with वि-वितन्य; सन् with 
अव-अवसन्य; यम् with नि—निपम्य or निपय; रम् with वि—विरम्य or 
विरय; नह् with प्—प्रणम्य or प्रणय, &c.

§ 754. The roots स्तन्, जन् and सन् have respectively निषन्य-
निष्नाय; प्रजन्य-प्रजाय; and प्रसन्य-प्रसाय.

§ 755. स्थि lengthens its इ before the affix य and जागृ changes 
its final vowel to Guṇa; प्रष्ठीय; प्रजागर्थ.

§ 756. भै, ज्या and श्रै do not take Samprasāraṇa; प्रयाय;
प्रज्याय 'having become old,' उपज्याय 'having covered,' but श्रै 
with परि or सम्, takes Samprasāraṇa optionally; परिश्राय or 
परिश्रीय; संश्राय or संश्रीय.

§ 757. The roots mentioned under § 486 do not change 
their आ to ई; प्रयाय, प्रयाय, प्रसाय, &c.

§ 758. Roots of the Tenth class and Causals preserve their 
अय् before य, if it be preceded by a short penultimate vowel; if 
not, it is dropped; चोरय-प्रचोर्य; बोधय-प्रबोध्य; कृ—cau. विकार्य; गॄ
cau. with आ—आनाय्य, &c.; but गण-विगणय्य, गणमय्य, प्रकथय्य;
प्रवेशिपय्य 'having again and again caused to be broken.'

§ 759. (a) आप् cau. may retain its अय् optionally-याप्य 
or यापय्य.

§ 760. Desiderative bases add य immediately and Freq. 
bases add it after dropping their य, when it is preceded by a 
consonant and dropping the final अ when it is preceded by a 
vowel; बुध-प्रबुभुत्सिष्य: Fre. प्रबोबुद्य; सृ Fre. प्रबोस्य्य &c.

* धै changes its final to इ optionally; so अनुशिष्य also.
