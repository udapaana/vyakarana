§ 859. * In the case of Potential passive participles the agent 
of the action is put in the Genitive or in the Instrumental case:
मया मम वा सेव्यो हरिः Hari ought to be served by me; राक्षसेन्द्रस्य 
संरक्ष्यं मया छेत्तव्यमिदं वनं ( Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 129. ) this forest which the 
lord of the demons ought to preserve must be destroyed by me;
अन्तरा ते दर्शतिव्यका &c. ( Meg. ) Thou should go to Alakā

§ 860. † In the case of words denoting equality or likeness, 
such as तुल्य, सदृश, &c., the person or thing with whom or which 
any object is compared is put in the Genitive case, except in 
the case of तुला and उपमा; तुल्यः सदृशः समो वा छन्दस्य कृष्णेन वा 
equal to or like Kṛishṇa; कोऽन्योस्ति सदृशो मम who else is equal 
to me? but छन्दस्य तुला उपमा वा नास्ति ( Sid. Kau. ).

Obs.—The words तुला and उपमा, however, are found used by 
good authors with the Instrumental against Pāṇini's rule; 
तुलां वहतीति दन्तवासना ( Kum. V. 34. ) which rises to the high 
position of being compared with your lip; कुट्टोपर्यं श्रुतिसिद्ध 
शुक्ला ( Sis. I. 4. ) clearly deserving to be compared with S'ambhu 
white with ashes; see Rag. VIII. 15.

§ 861. ‡ The words आयुष्य, स्वस्ति, स्वस्ति, कुशलं, शर्म, अर्थः and हितं 
and words having the same sense, when used in a sentence con-
taining a benediction, govern the Dative or the Genitive case;
आयुष्यं चिरजीवितं छन्दाय छन्दस्य वा भूयात् ( Sid. Kau. ); may 
Kṛishṇa live long; similarly स्वस्ति, स्वस्ति, कुशलं, निरामयं, शर्म, शं, अर्थः,
प्रयोजनं, हितं, पथ्यं वा भूयात् ( Sid. Kau. ).
