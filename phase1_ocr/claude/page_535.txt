§ 951. † When the words 'after an hour' are used the Poten-
tial (also Imperative) or the Potential participle may be used; 
मुहूर्तादयं यजेत, यजतां, यष्टव्यं वा (Sid. Kau.).

§ 952. ‡ The Potential is used with the words काल, समय 
and वेला, when the word यत् is used; कालः समयः वेला वा यद्भुञ्जीत 
भवान् it is time now that you should dine.

§ 953. § When the idea of fitness is to be expressed the 
Potential or the Potential participle may be used; and some-
times the noun in य also; त्वं कन्यां वहेः; त्वं कन्यायाः वोढा or त्वया 
कन्या वोढव्या you are fit to marry the girl.

(a) The Potential or the Potential participle may be used 
when the sense of capability is implied; भारं त्वं वहेः or भारस्त्वया 
वोढव्यः thou canst (art able to) carry the load.
