§399. (a) The following roots belong optionally to the 1st
and 6th Classes:- हृष् 1. P. 6. U. to plough, to draw, डृ 1. A.
to return, 6. P. to strike against. णृत् 1.A. 6. P. to roll, to whirl;
1. A. to take, to receive, घृणु 1. A. 6. P. to reel, whirl round,
move to and fro, डृ 1. P. to divide, 6. P. to envelope, डृभृ.
डृभ्यृ P. to kill सद् P. to sit down, सिच् P. 1. to sprinkle, 6.
to open the eyes, स्तह् P. 1. to stir, to churn, 6 to cover, to
adhere, छद् 1. A. to cheat, 6 U. to release, to leave &c.

(b) The following roots belong to the 4th as well as to
the 6th class:- क्षिप् 4 P. 6. U. to throw, लुप् 4. P. to
confound, 6. U. to take away, लुभ् P.4. to covet, to be
perplexe, 6 A. to perplex, सृज् 4 A. to let loose, to send
forth. 4, 6. P to create.
