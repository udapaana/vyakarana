256                           SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.              [§ 405-409

§ 405. Before strong terminations the penultimate short and
the final vowel of the base take their Guna substitute.

                  Fifth, Eighth and Ninth classes

§ 406. * उ and उ are added on to the roots of the 5th and
8th classes respectively before the terminations.

§ 407 The final उ of the base is optionally dropped
before र and म् if it be not preceded by a conjunct consonant.
It is changed to उव् before a weak termination beginning with a
vowel, if preceded by a conjunct consonant and to उ in other
cases. The हि of the Imperative 2nd per. sing. is dropped after
उ not preceded by a conjunct consonant.

§ 408. † In the ninth class ना is inserted between the root
and the terminations ना becomes न् before the weak terminations
beginning with a vowel, and नी before the weak terminations
beginning with a consonant.

§ 409. (a) The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped
before ना &c. as ग्रथ्नाति, ग्रथ्निषे, ग्रथ्नीमः &c. from ग्रन्थ् 'to
put together.'

(b) I. the case of roots ending in a consonant, the impera-
tive second person singular termination is आन instead of
हि; as चुराण from चुर् 'to steal'.
