रु—is added to all nouns to form new. abstract nouns; सह्—
सहने endurance, हस्—हसनं laughing, शी—शयनं sleeping, पा-
पानं drinking, धुप्—धोऽनं, साध्—साधनं, &c. This is also added 
in the sense of 'the instrument of an action'; प्रच्छ्—प्रश्न: an
instrument for cutting; an axe, &c; दुह्—दोहदोहनी a milk
vessel ( here it shows the place of action of Adhikarana ).

आक्—( णाचु )—is affixed to जल्प्, भिक्ष्, छुट् लुट् and र in the
sense of 'in the habit of,' जल्पाक: ( जल्पितुं शीलमस्य ) 'a
prattler; भिक्ष—भिक्षाक: ' a beggar,' छुटाक: 'he who divides or
cuts;' लुंटाक: 'a robber;' राक: 'a poor man.'

आक्—ड्—डाराक destructive, hurtful; वंद—वंदाक praising, a
panegyrist.

आलु—is added to the cau. of स्पृह्, यह् and पद्, to वृद् and to
the words निद्रा, तन्द्रा and श्रद्धा; स्पृहयालु longing for, desirous
of; दयालु compassionate; निद्रालु disposed to sleep; तन्द्राल:;
श्रद्धालु; 'full of faith.'

इ—( इक्, इष्य्, इष्, कि )—
कृष—कष—कृषि: 'one who tills the ground'; यु-गिरि: a mountain.
इक्—is added to खन् and other roots; खानि: a well; धानि: a
dwelling.

इष्य्—is added to अज् and other roots of the group; आजि: a
battle, आति: &c.

कि—is added to the roots धा and पा and others assuming these
forms; धा—उपाधि: fraud, condition, &c.; निधि: a treasure,
संधि: a joint, peace, &c.; जलधि: the sea ( here it is used in
the sense of आधिकरण ).

त्र—( त्रच् ) is added to कृ, दृ, ध, दृ, जन्, सह्, and धर; कृ—
आरित्रं a rudder, helm; an oar; लवित्रं a sickle; धारित्रं a fan
made of the deer's skin; जनित्रं cause of generation; खनित्रं a
spade; सहित्रं patience, forbearance; धरित्रं.
