§ 901. The past passive participle is used as a neuter sub-
stantive; गतं departure; दत्तं a gift, खातं an excavation, भुक्तं,
खातं, &c.

§ 902 The past passive participle active and passive, may be
used with the auxiliaries अस् and भू in any tense, the mean-
ing of the participle changing accordingly; गतोऽस्मि or गतवानस्मि
I have or am gone; so गतवानभवं or गतवानासं or गतोऽभवं I had
or was gone; so छत्वानास्मिः गतो वनं श्वो भविष्यति रामः that Râma
is to go to the forest to-morrow; संप्राप्तः कीर्तिमतुलां भविष्यति
thou wilt obtain great glory, &c.

THE FUTURE PARTICIPLES.

§ 903 The future participle denotes that a person or thing
is doing, or is about to do the action or to undergo the condi
tion, expressed by the root; करिष्यद् going or about to do; करिष्य-
माण about to do or what is about to be done.

§ 904. Besides showing simple futurity participle expresses
intention or purpose; अनुयास्यद् प्रतितनयाद् wishing to follow
the daughter of the sage; दास्यद् wishing to give; बन्याद्
विनेष्यदिव दुःसत्त्वाद् wishing, as it were, to tame the wild beasts.
