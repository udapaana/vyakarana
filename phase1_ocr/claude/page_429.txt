Exceptions:-

(a) स्नी, जाग्, स्था and दरिद्रा take इ; the final vowel of the 
first two takes Guna substitute, and the last two drop their
final; स्नपित, जागरित; स्थित, दरिद्रित.

(b) पत् takes इ though it admits of इ optionally in the 
Desiderative; पतित.

(c) The Anit roots वच् and शुष् admit इ before त and सा;
उचित, शुषित.
