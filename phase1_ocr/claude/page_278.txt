§ 429 चकासू, P. 'to shine,'—चकासू, जभू, जागृ, दरिद्रा and
झारू drop the उ of the 3rd pers. plu. termination when added
to them. In the Imperf. they take उस् as 3. pl. termination.
Imp. 2 sing. of चकासू is चकासिद्धि.
