396. The following seven roots form their bases like the  
Desiderative in the senses indicated. These are:—चिक् to  
administer medicine, to treat as a patient चिकित्सति-ते; गुप् to  
censure, जुगुप्सते; तिष् to bear, to forgive, तितिक्षते; बध् to abhor,  
to act loathsomely, बीभत्सते; दास् to make straight, दीधासति-ते;  
मास् to reason, to think, मीमांसते; शास् to sharpen, शीक्षाति-ते. But  
चित् to desire केतति; to dwell, केतयति; दाय् to cut दायति-ते. &c.

§397 There are a few roots which add a penultimate  
nasal in the Special Tenses and Moods necessarily; e. g. चिद् to  
cut चिन्दति, अश् to go अंहते, विद् to roll into a ball विन्दते, शुद् to  
purify, to go शुंदति &c; and a few more which do so optionally;  
e. g. दृह् to be firm दृंहति-दृहति; क्रुच् or क्षुच् to go क्रोंचयति-  
क्रुंचति, क्रोचयति-क्षुंचति; लुञ्च् to pluck लुंचति, लुब्जति all P.; यम्  
A. to hum योंजते, यजते; गर्ज् P. to roar गर्जति, गेंजति and others  
less important.
