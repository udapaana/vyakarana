§ 959. Sometimes the Potential is used without a subject 
when it is used in the sense of 'a precept or advice'; आपद्यर्थं धनं
रक्षेद्दाराय् रक्षेद्धनेनपि । आत्मानं सततं रक्षेद्दारैरपि धनैरपि ॥ ( a man )
should save money for adversity; he should save his wife at the
cost of his wealth and himself even at the expense of his wife
and wealth; यद्यद्रोचेत विप्रेभ्यस्तत्तदद्याद्मत्सरः ( one ) should serve
Brāhmaṇas with all that they like, without being jealous.

THE BENEDICTIVE MOOD.

§ 960. The Benedictive Mood is used to confer a blessing
or to express the speaker's wish; चिरं जीयाद् भवान् may you
live long ! वर्षिणीहः स्वजातेषु वत्सात्मं तिरुहंततः । युग्मात्मं शणिनां
मान्यतेतेगं स्थेया व्यतिष्यतौ ॥ ( Bhaṭṭi. XIX. 26 ); कृतार्थः भूयासम्
may I be successful !
