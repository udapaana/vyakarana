धृ--this is added to a root with or without any preposition
prefixed to it; धृते असौ धृः or प्रधृः one who brings forth,
a mother; सद्--सुसदः those who sit in heaven, the gods;
द्विष्--प्राद्विष् a powerful enemy; युज्--अश्वयुज् the constellation
of stars called 'Aswini'; नी--सेनानी the leader of an army;
गम्--विवगम् the creator; वि--अग्निचित् one who consecrates
the sacred fire, a householder; जि--इन्द्रजित् the conqueror of
Indra, name of Rāvaṇa's son; स्तु--देवस्तुत् he who praises the
gods; सु--सोमसुत् the extractor of Soma juice; क्र--कर्मक्रत्,
भागक्रत्, टीकाक्रत्, &c. This is also added to इष्, स्पृश्, and
सृज् when the object governed by them is prefixed to them;
सर्वेष्ट् seeing all; मर्मस्पृश् touching the vitals; विश्वसृज् the
creator of the universe;--to अद् and हन्; क्रव्याद् a flesh-eater,
a demon; ब्रह्महन् the Killer of a Brāhmaṇa;--to छिद् changed
to छद्; तनुच्छद् a garment. Before this affix roots ending in
a nasal lengthen their penultimate; as शम--प्रशान् tranquil;
तन्--प्रतान् one who stretches, &c., except in the case of roots
गम्, नम्, यम्, and तम्, which drop their nasal and then obey
the general rule; अध्वानं गच्छतीति अध्वगत् a traveller; परि
तनोतीति--परितत् stretching on all sides; सुनत् yielding, poor;
संयत् well restrained, &c.; the आ of ज्ञात् is changed to इ
before this; मित्रं शास्तीति--मित्रशिष् one who gives advice to
his friend; आशिष् a blessing, गृ forms गिर् speech; the roots
श्रंथ्, मंथ्, and स्यंद् drop their nasal and insert त् before their
final; वाहस्यद् dropping down from a vehicle, उखास्यद् dropping
down from a vessel; पर्णस्यद् falling down from a leaf. before
this affix द्विष् changes its ष् to उ and other roots change their
त् to उ; अक्षद्यूत् (अक्षैर्दीव्यति) a gambler; वे--ऊः a weaver;
अव्--ऊः a protector; this forms Vṛiddhi with a preceding अ;
जन + ऊ = जनौः the protector of the people, ज्वर्--जूर् feverish,
त्वर्--तूर् swift-going; before this affix व् or छ् following र् is
dropped; स्वच्छ्--सूर् swooned, धुर्व्--धूर् hurting, injuring; अक्षर्
