त्र—with आ* is Atm. भाष्टे 'he praises.'

प्रच्छ*—with आ is Atm. in the sense of 'taking leave of—
"आयुष्टस्य प्रियसखमम्" Meg. 10. 'take leave of this thy dear
friend,' also with the preposition सम् when used intransitively,
संपृच्छते 'he ascertains.'

भुज्†—is Atm. except in the sense of 'protecting,' ओदनं भुङ्क्ते
'eats food,' भुञ्जे पृथिवीपालः पृथिवीमेव केवलाम् 'the protector of
the earth enjoyed (experienced the pleasure from the possession
of) the earth alone;' वृद्धो जनो दुःखशतानि भुङ्क्ते 'old people suffer
hundreds of miseries;' मह्यं भुनक्ति 'protects the world.'

स्पृश्—with परि is Par.; परिस्पृशति 'endures;' but आस्पृशते 'he
touches.'

तन्‡—with आ is Atm. either when used intransitively or has
a limb of the body of the agent for its object; आतच्छते तरुः 'the
tree spreads;' आतच्छते पाणिम् 'stretches forth the hand;' but
आतन्वन्ति कूपरज्जुं 'draws up the rope from the well'; preceded
by सम्, उत् and आ it is Atm. except when it has a literary
work for its object; वस्त्रं आतच्छते 'puts on a garment;' भारमुपच्छते
'lifts up a load;' व्रीहीन् संतच्छते 'gathers rice;' but उच्छति वेदम्
'tries hard to learn the Veda'. पर्य—with उप is Atm. in the
