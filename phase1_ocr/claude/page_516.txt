COMPARATIVE AND SUPERLATIVE DEGREES.

§ 887. Adjectives in the Comparative degree are used with 
the Ablative; सर्वेपाकरणों श्रेय: protection ( of one's subjects ) is 
better than aggrandizement. अर्जुनादुधिष्ठिरो ज्यायान् Udhisthira 
was older than Arjuna.

( a ) Sometimes the comparative is used with the Instru-
mental; प्राणेन प्रियतर: dearer than life. See also § 863 Note.

§ 888. The superlative may either be construed with the 
Genitive or Locative; अपत्येषु एतेषु वा गरिष्ठ: छुद्रस्मो वा.

§ 889. The sense of the comparative and the superlative may 
also be expressed by the particular case used; अस्य हृदयं पाषाणात्क-
ठिनं his heart is harder than stone; छात्राणां छात्रेषु वा श्रेष्ठ: पट्टु: 
Chaitra is the cleverest of all students.

§ 890. The words वर and प्रवर when used in the sense of the 
superlative govern the Genitive or the Locative, पुत्र स्पर्शवतां वर: 
'a son is the best of things possessed of touch;' चतुष्पदां गौ: प्रवरा 
लोहानां काञ्चनं वरं 'the cow is the best of quadrupeds and gold of 
metals'; the neu. sing of वर is used ( with a word expressive 
of negation ) in the sense of 'better and not, or but not;' शनैश्च्य-
च्चरणं वरं doing any thing slowly is better than not doing
