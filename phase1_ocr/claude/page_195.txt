§ 322. *If a compound has for its last member ऊरु and for
its first, words signifying a standard of comparison, or one of
the words सहित, शक्‌, क्षमण, वाम, महित and सह, it forms its
feminine in ऊः; रम्भोरुः रम्भे इव ऊरु यस्याः plantain-thighed;
करभोरुः having thighs ( beautifully tapering ) like the fore-arm
or the trunk of an elephant; दढितोरुः having well-turned thighs;
शक्तो खुरौ तात्रिव संनियच्छत्वादूरु यस्याः सा शक्तोरुः; [ हितेन सह सहितो
ऊरु यस्याः सा ] सहितोरुः; [ सहते इति सहो ऊरु यस्याः सा ] सहोरुः 'a
woman whose thighs are capable of enduring great fatigue or
pain'; or 'one having excellent thighs.'

§ 323. † The fem. of उशकपि, Vishṇu or Shiva, आधि, कुसित
or कुसिद 'a usurer' is formed in ई before which the final vowel
of these is changed to ऐ; उशकऐपयी, भगायी, कुसितायी or कुसिदायी.

§ 324. ‡ The feminine of मनु is optionally formed by adding
ई before which the final उ is changed to ओ or ऐ; मनायी.
मनायी or मनुः ।

§ 325. ¶ A prātipodika ending in short क or द forms its
feminine by the addition of ई; कर्क-कर्की, दण्डिय-दण्डिनी, छुनी,
धञ्जी, परिदेवय-परिदेक्नी; &c.

N. B. Words like स्वयु, ननान्दु, विधु, हरितु, स्वश्रु &c. being
themselves feminine do not take this termination.
