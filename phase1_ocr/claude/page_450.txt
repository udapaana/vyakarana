2. Indeclinable Participles formed with य.

§ 751. When a root is compounded with one or more prepositions or words prefixed to verbs like prepositions, the indeclinable participle is formed by affixing य immediately to it; the य is changed to त्य after a short radical vowel ( even when it combines with the final vowel of a preposition to a long vowel ); e. g.

दा with आ—आदाय          इ with प्र—प्रेत्य
द्वि with निस्-निर्द्विष्य    छ with सम्—संछत्य
जि with परा—पराजित्य    छ with द्विधा—द्विधाछत्य
नी with वि—विनीय        भिद् with निस्—निर्भिद्य
भू with अनु—अनुभूय      स्फुर् with उद्—उच्छूर्य
इ with अधि—अधीत्य      &c.    &c.    &c.

§ 752. The rules given at § § 394, 395; 459, 502 and 587 apply to the roots also in the gerund in य:—
