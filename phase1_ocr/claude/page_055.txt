Nouns ending in तृ mas. fem. and neu.

§ 31. Words derived from roots with the affix तृ ( तवृ and
तृन् [?]. f.f. 1. 133. and 2. 135 ), such as कर्तृ a maker, &c. and
the words स्वस्तृ f, नप्तृ, नेष्टृ, क्षत्तृ, होतृ, पोतृ, and प्रशास्तृ as also उद्गातृ
change their तृ to आर् in the Nom. sing. and to आर् in the first
five inflections. The तृ is lengthened in the Acc. and Gen. pl.
and changed to उर् before the Ab. and Gen. sing; the Voc. sing.
ends in अर्.
