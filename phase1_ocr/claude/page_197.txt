§ 329. * When a compound has for its second member a 
word denoting a limb of the body of an animal and having no 
conjunct consonant preceding its final vowel, the fem. is formed 
by the addition of आ or ई; अतिकेशा-शी; 'a women having abun-
dant hair;' सुकेशा-शी; चंद्रमुखा-खी; but सुगुल्फा having beautiful 
ankles; हस्तनी or हस्तना (आ वा प्रतिषा वा;) हट्टका शाला 'an edi-
fice with a beautiful front'.

(a) † If the word denoting the limb, however, be one of 
the words कोष, नस जंघ, उस्रा, शिखा, बाल, शाफ, शुक, भुज, कर &c. 
or have more than two syllables, the fem. is formed in आ alone; 
कान्यपाप्कोषा (अ:अप्राहुर: कोषा Sid. Kau.), पृथुजघना, having large 
hips; बहुलनयना, &c.

(b) ‡ Also when the first member is one of स, सह, or विष्व-
मात्, the fem. is formed in आ necessarily; सकेशा, अकेशा, विष्व-
मात्नासिका, सहनासिका &c.
