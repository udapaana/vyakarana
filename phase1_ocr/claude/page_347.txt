( b ) इ is substituted for the अ of the reduplicative syllable if the syllable following it be short, and not prosodially long; the इ of the reduplicative syllable is lengthened if not followed by a long syll. or a conjunct consonant; thus बधय्=बिधय्=बीधय्; चिचित्=चीचित्; स्स्वन्=चस्वन्=विस्वन्, the इ is not lengthened as it is followed by a conjunct consonant; स्पन्द्=पस्पन्द् only, as प् is followed by a syllable prosodially long.

( c ) Roots having a penultimate ऋ, short or long, optionally preserve it, the long ॠ being changed to the short one; वृत् causal base वर्तय=वर्तु after dropping अय and वृत् by this: वर्तु=वर्वर्तु; वृत्=वरृत्=विरृत्=वीरृत; कृत्=कीर्तय=कीर्ते and कृत् by this, कीर्ते—चिकीर्ते; कृत्—चीकृत्.

§ 549. Bases with initial vowels:—

( a ) If a root begins with a vowel and ends in a single consonant, the consonant is reduplicated and इ added to it in the reduplicative syllable: अट्=अटट्=आटिट्=आटिटत्-त; आप्=आपिपत्-त; उह्=ओजिहत्-त; &c.
