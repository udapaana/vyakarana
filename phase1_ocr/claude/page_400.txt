§ 646. This form of the Nominal verb has not always a

(a) The affix य is added in the sense of 'treating or considering
like,' पुत्रीयति छात्रम् 'he treats the pupil as a son,' विष्णुयति द्विजम्

विस्मीयते लोकान् 'he strikes the people with wonder.' It is also
added to तपस् in the sense of 'practising,' तपस्यति.
