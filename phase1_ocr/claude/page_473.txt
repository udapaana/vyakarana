न—( नह्, नञ् )—

नह्—is added to यज्, याच्, यत्, विच्छ्, प्रच्छ्, and रक्ष्; यज्ञ: a
sacrifice, याच्ञा beggary, यत्न: an effort, विच्छ: going, lustre,
प्रश्न a question, रक्ष: protector.

नञ्—स्वप्न: sleep, a dream.

नञ्य—( नञिष्णु )—is added to स्वप्, तृष्, and धृष्, in the sense of
'in the habit of;' स्वप्नु sleepy, तृष्णु thirsty, धृष्णु bold,
confident.

नु—( स्नु )—is added to त्रस्, रभ्, स्पृ and क्षिप् in the sense of
'in the habit of;' त्रस्नु timid, रब्स्नु covetous, greedy, धृष्णु bold,
क्षिप्नु throwing, casting.

सर—( क्सरच् )—च-क्सर going, a kind of deer, पद्—धसर and
अद्—अघर voracious, gluttonous, glutton.

य्—( क्यप् )—is added to त्रप्, यज् and कृ and forms fem.
abstract nouns; त्रप्या asceticism, an attack, कृप्या a sacrifice,
छाय्या doing; it also takes श्रा and त्रिक्य; त्रिक्या, छत्रि:; it is
affixed to अज्, ( not changed to धी in this case ) with रभ्,
सद् and पद् with त्रि, मन्, विद्, द्, क्षी, भू and इ in the sense of
either the place or the instrument of the action denoted by
the roots; समय्या assembly, निषय्या a conch, a market place,
the hall where an assembly meets, त्रिपय्या slippery ground,
मन्य्या the nape of the neck, त्रिय्या, दुय्या a sprinkling with the
Soma juice, शय्या a bed, भूय्या wages, इय्या a vehicle.
