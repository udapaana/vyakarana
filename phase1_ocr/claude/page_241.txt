III. PARTICLES.

§ 374. The particles are either used as expletives or intensives; some of these are किल, बत, च, उ, क, वै, हि, &c.

§ 375. The following particles are used with certain words:—

अद्—अद्भुत् ' a wonder'.

का—कापुरुषः 'a bad man,' कोष्ण 'luke-warm,' काजल 'a little water'.

कु—कुकृत्य ' a bad deed '.
