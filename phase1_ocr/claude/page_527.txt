IMPERFECT, PERFECT AND AORIST.

§ 925. In Sanskrit there are three tenses denoting a past 
action, viz. the Imperfect, the Perfect, and the Aorist. Ori-
ginally each of these three tenses had a signification of its own 
and was used in its proper sense in ancient writings. † After 
Sanskrit ceased to be a spoken language the exact senses of 
these tenses were lost sight of and writers began to use them 
promiscuously, so that now any of these may be used to denote 
past time with certain limitations. The original senses of these 
as well as their other peculiarities are noticed below.

IMPERFECT.

§ 926. ‡ The Imperfect according to Pāṇini denotes past 
action not done to-day i. e. done at some time prior to the cur-
rent day, तान्भगवत् पोऽब्रवीत्: ( Bhaṭṭi. ) Bibhishaṇa spoke to them.
