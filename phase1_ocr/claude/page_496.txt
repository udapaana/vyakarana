§ 830. With verbs of telling such as कथ्, श्वा, शंस्, वच्,
निविद् cau. &c. and of sending such as हि with प्र, दुष् with वि,
&c. the Dative of the indirect object is used; रामामिषेक्तुमनघं
नैतहुकं मैथिलाय कथयामबभूव सः । ( Rag. XI. 37. ); He told the king
of Mithilā that Rāma was eager to see the bow; आख्याहि
मे को भवानुग्ररूपः ( Bhag. XI. 31. ) Tell me, who thou art in
this fierce form, &c., उपस्थितां होमवेलां गुरवे निवेदयामि ( S'ak. IV. )
I will tell my preceptor that it is time to offer the morning
oblations; हरिस्तस्मै सुराङ्गनां प्रजिघाय ( Rag. VIII. 79. ) Indra sent a
heavenly damsel against him ( i. e. to disturb his contempla-
tions ); रक्षतस्तस्मै महौघं प्रजिघाय ( Rag. XV. 21. ).

§ 831. *The indirect object of the root मन् cl. 4. 'to think'
if not an animal, may be put in the Accusative or the Dative
case when contempt is to be shown; न त्वां तृणं मन्ये तृणाय वा I do
not consider thee a straw; but न त्वां तृणं मन्ये ( मन् cl.; 8 ) when
mere comparison is meant, the Accusative is used; त्वां तृणं
मन्ये ( Mb. )

* मन्यकर्मण्यनादरे विभाषाऽप्राणिषु । Pāṇ. II. 3. 17. On अप्राणिषु
Kātyāyana remarks 'अप्राणिषु इत्यवतीय नौकाकाष्ठुकडङ्गकाकशुन्येष्विति
वाच्यम्' i. e. "Instead of 'If not an animal' it should be said,
'If not one of a ship, or food or a crow or a parrot or a jackal,"
न त्वां मन्ये नावं अन्नं वा, न त्वां शुकं भानं वा मन्ये; in the former case
though neither नौ nor अन्न is an animal, the Accusative ought
to be used, in the latter though भद्र is an animal the Dative
may be optionally used.
