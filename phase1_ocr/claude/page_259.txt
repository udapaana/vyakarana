§ 400. The following roots of the 10th class preserve their
vowel unchanged; वदृ to sin, डृद् to tell, षृद् to send, to pass;
नृद् to count, पद् U. to filter, A. to throw, पद् to choose or
seek, to get, जनृद् to sound, सृह् to honour, रच् to compose, रस्
to taste, वह् to forsake षद् to speak ill of, deceive, रद् to scream,
पट् to weave, (but पाटयति when it means to tear &c.), तनृद्
to thunder, नद् to sound, पद् to go, कन् to count, स्वद् to sound,
पद् A. to go, अंस् to divide, बट् to separate, कञ्ज् to shine, कुन्
to bore, छद् to conceal, वध् to cheat, वध् to dwell, भ्रध् or क्रथ्
to be weak or lax, व्यय् to give, to spend, स्पृह to desire, स्पच
to seek, दृष् to bear, क्षृप् to pity, to be weak, कुप्, यूप् to converse
with, ग्रह् A. to take (also ग्राहयति when cau. of ग्रह्) कुह् A.
to astonish, to deceive, पट् to bind or string together, दस्कुद्
to become manifest, सुख् to make happy, and others less common.
