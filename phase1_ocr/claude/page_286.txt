§ 441. हन् P. A. 'to kill'—drops its न् before a weak termi-
nation, beginning with any consonant except a nasal or a semi-
vowel, and its अ before a vowel termination, the त् then chang-
ing to ध. The Imp. 2 sing. is जहि.
