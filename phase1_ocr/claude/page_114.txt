DECLENSION OF THE NUMERALS. (CARDINALS AND ORDINALS)

§ 164. एक (एका fem.), द्वि (द्वी fem.), त्रि (तिस्र* fem.), and
चतुर् (चतस्र fem.), are all adjectives and takes the gender, num-
ber and case of the nouns they qualify.

§ 165. एक is declined in the sing. (though it may have the
dual and plural also), and द्वि in the dual alone. For further
particulars see § 154. त्रि and चतुर् are declined in the plu.
alone. E. g.

§ 166. पञ्चन्—नवदशन्. These are also adjectives agreeing
in case with the nouns to which they are applied. They are
declined in the pl. and are uniform in all the three genders.
