II. TATPURUSHA OR THE DETERMINATIVE COMPOUNDS.

§ 200. When a compound consists of two members and the
first determines the sense of the other, that is called Tatpurusha.

§ 201. According to the grammatical nature of the first or
attributive member the Tatpurusha is divided into six varieties:-
(1) Tatpurusha proper or the Inflectional, (2) Nañ or the
Negative Tatpurusha, (3) Karmadhāraya or the Appositional
including 'Dwigu', (4-5) Prādi and Gati or the Prepositional
compounds, and (6) Upapada. These are either nouns, adjectives
or adverbs.
