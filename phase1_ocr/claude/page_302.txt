* The following couplet gives the endings of these roots and their number in each:—

कचछजवधमयाः पसयुः क्रमात् ।
१ २ ३ १७१३१२ ३: १०१३ ३ ४ १० ४३ २ गच्छन्त्येवञ्जिः स्थताः ॥

The first line gives the ending consonants and thus enables the students to know at once which root is Set and which is Anit; e. g. कुट् may be at once known to be a set root as ट् does not occur in the first line. The second line gives the number of roots ending in a particular consonant. Thus roots ending in क are क i. e. one (क being the first consonant) in number; roots ending in च are च i. e. 6, (च being the 6th consonant); roots ending in क् are क् i. e. one, in number, and so on.
