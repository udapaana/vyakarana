IRREGULAR BASES.

§ 509. The roots प्रच्य, मच्य, क्रच्य, and स्वच्जू drop their nasal
optionally before the terminations of the Perfect. श्रच्य, ग्रच्य,
दच्य obey § 500, even before the strong terminations when their
nasal is dropped.
