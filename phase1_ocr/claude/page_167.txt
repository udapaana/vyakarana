§ 261. "The words उरस्, सर्पिस्, अपनह्, द्वि, त्रि, शालि-ली and संध्, गन्धहु, पत् , गो and लक्ष्मी when used in the singular, have a क added to them, when they form the latter member of a Bah. comp.; उरुक (expanded, well-developed) उर्: यस्य उरुकोरस्क:, सिप्रसर्पिक: 'one fond of ghee', &c. एक: उद्गात् यस्य असौ एकुद्गातृक: 'having one man only', &c.; when द्वि and the words following it are used in the dual and the plural they take क optionally. द्विशालक or द्विशाल: &c.

(a) अर्थ takes the final क, necessarily when preceded by अन and optionally in any other case; अनर्थक: 'useless'; but अपार्थ-र्थीक 'meaningless' बच्च:.

§ 262. † A fem. Bah. ending in इन् takes this क necessarily; as बहुतपस्विका नगरी 'a city having many ascetics'; बहुवाग्मिका सभा 'an assembly having many eloquent speakers in it'; but बहुदण्डी or बहुदण्डिको ग्राम: (vide § 263 below), as it is mas.

§ 263. ‡ And as a general rule, when the final word of a Bah. comp. does not undergo any additions or alterations mentioned in the foregoing rules, it optionally takes the affix क. महायशस्क:=महायशा: 'of mighty glory'; but उत्तरपूर्वी, व्याघ्रपाद्, छगलिक: &c.

§ 264. But if the last word of a Bah. compound be a fem. noun ending in ई or ऊ not capable of being changed into इन् or उन् before the vowel case termination, or if it ends in आ, क is necessarily affixed; ईश्वर: क्षमा यस्य तत् ईश्वरक्षमक जगत्, बहुवर्णको देश:, रूपवती वधू: यस्य स रूपवधूक:, &c.; but सुधी: स्त्री is an exception; बहुस्त्रीक:, सस्त्रीक: &c.
