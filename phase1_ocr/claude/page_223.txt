1 MASCULINE WORDS.

§ 345.    Verbal derivatives formed by the affixes ष् and त,
and इ added to the roots धा and पा; e. g. पाक्:, स्वाद:, कर्:, भार:
any beverage or drink, poison, गोचर: range, scope, धक्र:, विक्र:;
आधि: mental pain or anguish, निधि: a treasure, &c.

Exceptions:—पाश्चा fem. and क्षय, लिप्, and प्रभु all neu.

§ 346. Words ending in उ and those having क्, ट्, ण्, थ्, च्,
ष्, ख्, घ्, र्, or ह्, for their penultimate; as प्रभु:, भानु:, इक्षु:,
स्तबक: 'a bunch of flowers' &c; घट:, पाषाण:, शोथ: 'a swelling,
फेन; दीप:; स्तम्भ:; सोम:; समय:; क्षुर: a razor,' अङ्कुर:, धर:, पायस: &c.

Exceptions:—Words ending in—

(a) उ-पेउ, रज्जु (except when it ends a compound, in which
    case it is both mas. and fem.) कुहु-हु 'the last day of a
    lunar month on which the moon is invisible,' सरयु 'name
    of a river,' नजु, करेणु and विपेणु 'a kind of creeper,' which 
    are all fem; क्षम्यु, जातु, वसु 'wealth,' अञ्जु, जतु 'lac,'
    त्रपु 'tin or lead', तालु, वाह, मधु 'honey,' स्वादु 'relish',
    वस्तु and मस्तु 'sour cream', which are all neuter.

(b) क-चिबुक 'the chin', झाटुक, प्रातिपदिक, ओङ्गुक 'a garment,'
    उल्मुक 'a fire brand.'

(c) ट and ण—त्रिकीट, षकूट. छलाट, चतुष्पट 'a place where four
    roads meet;' कृण, लवण, पर्ण, उष्ण.
