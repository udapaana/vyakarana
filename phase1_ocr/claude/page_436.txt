§ 702. (a) The roots सु and वष् take the affix वत् like त
in an active sense; सुनवत् 'one who extracted Soma juice,'
यज्वत् 'one who has offered a sacrifice;' अत् is added to जृ
optionally in the same sense; जीर्ण or जरत् ' one who has
become old' ( also जीर्णवत् ).

§ 703. The participles in त or न follow the Declension of
nouns in अ.

§ The P. P. participles have not always a passive force:-

§ 704.* The affix त added to roots having the sense of
'sitting or going, or eating' shows the place where the action
takes place; इदं मुकुन्दस्य आसितं 'this is the place where Mukunda
to sit,' इदं यातो रमापतेः 'this is the path by which the husband
of Ramā used to pass,' मुक्ते एतत् भुक्तस्य 'this is the place where
Ananta took his food,' &c.
