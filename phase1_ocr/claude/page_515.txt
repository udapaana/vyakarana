§ 883. भवत् is used in the second person like 'you' in English
as a courteous form of address though it is to be regarded as a
pronoun of the third person and ought to be treated as such,
भवान् अत्र पृष्टव्य: you ought to be asked here, भवान् अपि तत्र गच्छतु
you may also go there.

( a ) When respect is to be shown, अत्र and तत्र are prefixed to
भवत् according as the person with reference to whom it is used
is near, or at a distance or absent; अत्रभवान् काश्यप: the venerable
Kāshyapa ( who is near ); इदमासनं अलङ्कुरीतभवान् may you
occupy ( lit. ornament ) this seat; तत्रभवती इरावती lady Iravatī
( who is not present ). Sometimes तद् is used with भवत् to show
respect; as यत्नां विधेयविधये स भवान् नियुङ्क्ते । Mal. Mad. I.

§ 884. The pronoun तद् has often the sense of 'well known,
renowned,' &c. तौ पार्वतीपरमेश्वरौ those (well-known) Pārvatī and
Parameśwara; तान्येव वनस्थलानि those well-known forest sites.

( a ) When repeated, this pronoun has the sense of 'various,
several;' तेषु तेषु रम्यतेषु स्थानेषु in those various highly delightful
spots; छोतैरपि तैस्तै: प्रयत्नै: notwithstanding several efforts-कामैस्तै;
स्तैर्वदामा: &c. ( Bhag. VII. 20 ).

§ 885. The pronouns एक and अपर or अन्य are used in the
plural in the sense of 'some—others;' विधवाया: पुनर्विवाह: सशास्त्र
इत्येके शास्त्रप्रतिषिद्ध इत्यन्ये केचिन् निषिद्ध इत्यपरे some think that widow-
remarriage is sanctioned by the S'astras, some say that it is pro-
hibited by them, while others hold that it is not allowed in the
Kali age. केचिद् may take the place of एके.
