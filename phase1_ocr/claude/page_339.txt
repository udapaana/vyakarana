§ 534. स् takes अन् instead of उस् in the 3rd per. pl. and 
changes its vowel to उन् before the vowel terminations; e. g.,
अस्युवम्, अस्युव, अस्युम 1st pers.; स्युस्, अस्युताम्, अस्युवन् 3rd per.

§ 535. गा is substituted for स् in the Aorist; अगाम्, अगाव्, 
अगात 1st per.; स् with आवे 'to remember' अस्मगाम्, अस्मगाव्,
अस्मगात, &c.

§ 536. This Variety is exclusively Parasmaipada. दा, धा, 
and स्था take the fourth Variety in the Atm. स् when Atm. 
takes the fifth; and स् with आवे Atm. the fourth.

Second Variety or Form.

§ 537. In this Variety अ is added on to the root and then 
the terminations of the Imperf. of the first group of conjuga-
tional classes are added; viz:

§ 538. The preceding अ is dropped before अम्, अन्, and 
अन्त and lengthened before व and म. The radical vowel except 
a final ऋ, short or long, and the ऋ of ईश, does not undergo 
Guna or Vrddhi substitute.

§ 539. This Variety is Parasmaipada with a few exceptions 
such as कृ with सम्, स्था, बच्; and अस् 'to throw' with a preposi-
tion. The roots लिङ्, सिच्, and है take this form in the Par. 
and optionally in the Atm., in which they also take the 
Fourth Form.

§ 540. The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped; as क्रम्-
चक्रमत्, स्कन्द्-अस्कदत्, &c.
