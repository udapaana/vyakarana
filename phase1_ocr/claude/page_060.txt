SECTION II.

BASES ENDING IN CONSONANTS.

§ 87. Bases ending in any of the first four letters of a class, and in ण्, र्, श्, ष्, स्, ह् and ह्. The declension of these bases consists, for the most part, in adding the general case terminations and observing the rules of sandhi.

§ 88. Bases ending र्, न्, and स्.

§ 89. (a) After final न् the सु of the loc. is changed to षु.
(b) र् ( optionally changeable to र् ) may be optionally inserted between न् and सु.
