after a semivowel is optionally doubled; so तत्वकार: also be-

Exceptions:—Vṛddhi substitute takes place in the follow-
ing cases †

(a) When a word ending in अ is followed by ऊह, and य by
ऊढ, ऊढ and ऊहि; e. g. प्रध + ऊह: = प्रधौह:, chief guess (or this
may be the acc. pl. of प्रधवाह a young bull trained to the yoke);
प्र + ऊह: = प्रौह: principal reasoning. Similarly प्रौढ: full grown;
प्रौढि:; but प्रोहवान् (as ऊहवान् is not mentioned in the Sūtra ).

(b) अक्ष + ऊहिनी = अक्षौहिणी a complete army.‡ (The change
of य to ण् will be explained further on ).

(c) When स्व is followed by ईर and ईरित् both derived from
ईर् to go; e. g. स्व + ईर: = स्वैर:, one acting wilfully, selfwilled;
स्व + ईरिणी = स्वैरिणी; so स्वैर्, स्वैरी (स्वेन ईरितं कीलसदृशं इति).
