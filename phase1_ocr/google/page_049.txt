$ 77. Root nouns in ई or ऊ M. F. N.
Rule of Sandhi - (a) The ending इ or उ, short or long, of
nouns derived from roots with the affix क्विप् (०) and of भ्रू, is
changed to इस or उन् before the vowel terminations *; the fem.
nouns of this description are optionally declined like नदी in
the Dt. Ab. Gen. and Loc. singulars and the Gen. plu.
(b) But if the ending इ or उ of a many voweled noun having
a root at the end be not preceded by a radical conjunct conso-
uant or the root noun has a proposition termed गति (e. g as
added to the root) or a word governed by the root preceding
it. it is changed to य् or , except in the case of सुधी and nonns
ending in f.
