Another variety of उद्गता is mentioned wherein the third
quarter has भ, न, भ, ग instead of भ, न, ज, ल, ग.
Other kinds of metres in which every quarter of the stanza
differs in the number of syllables, are included under the general
name ‘Gáthá.’ The same name is applicable to stanzas consist -
ing of any number of quarters other than four.

Metres regulated by the number of syllabic instants.
§ 14. The most common variety of metres regulated by
syllabic instants is आर्या. It is of nine kinds :—
पथ्या विपुला चपला मुखचपला जघनचपला च ।
गीत्युपगीत्युद्गीतय आर्यागीतिश्च नवधार्या ॥
of these the last four are generally used & deserve notice here.
आर्या.
Def. यस्याः पादे प्रथमे द्वादश मात्रास्तथा तृतीयेऽपि ।
अष्टादश द्वितीये चतुर्थके पञ्चदश सार्या ॥
The first and third quarters have each 12 mâtrâs or syllabic
instants, the second 18 and the fourth 15.
