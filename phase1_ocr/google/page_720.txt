दण्डक.
Metres with 27 syllables or more in each pâda are designated
by the general name Dandaka. Many varieties of this are
mentioned ( as the number of syllables in each pôda may

लाकर, सिंहविक्रान्त, कुसुमस्तबक, अनंगशेखर, संग्राम &c; Mal. Mad. v.
23 is an instance of the species last named, having 54 letter
