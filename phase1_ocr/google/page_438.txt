(f) Potontial Participles ( and Verbal Adjectives ).
§ 711. The Potential Participle is formed by means of the
affixes "तव्य, अनीय, त and rarely एलिम, added to a root or deriva-
tive verb. This is Passive when the verb is transitive and
impersonal when the verb is intransitive. It is also used like
an adjective when denoting fitness, &c.
( 1 ) Participles in सव्य and अनीय.
§712. The affixes तथ्य or अनीय are added to roots or
derivative baeos in the sense of 'must be fit to be, ' &c. Before
these the ending vowel and the penultimate short of a root
take their Guna substitute, Before तव्य Set roots take, Anit
roots do not, and Wet roots take it optionally. Before अनीय
penultimate ॠ is always changed to अर् and not to र ( as it
sometimes does ); e.
