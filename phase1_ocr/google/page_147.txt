§ 229. In some Karmadhâraya compounds the last member
of the first word which is itself a compound word is dropped*;
०. 9. शाकप्रियः पार्थिवः शाकपार्थिवः ' a king who likes vegetables;,
देवपूजको ब्राह्मणः देवब्राह्मणः &c. These compounds, though they
should be properly called "Uttamapadalopi" are popularly
designated “ Madhyamapadalopi”. Any compound requiring
similar explanation is classed under this group ( शाकपार्थिवादि )
which is an आकृतिगण.
