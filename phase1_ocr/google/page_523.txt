§ 913. The infinitive is used with verbs and verbal nouns
meaning 'to wish or desire' provided the agent of it is the same
as that of the verb; को हर्तुमिच्छति हरेः दंष्ट्राम ( Mad. I. ) who
wishes to snatah away the jaws of the lion; माधुर्ये मधुबिन्दुना रच-
पितुं क्षाराम्बुधेंरीहते; Bhartr. II. 6. Bat तमेतत्कर्तुमहमिच्छामि I wish
him to do this, is wrong.

(6) words like and others meaning sufficient or able,
