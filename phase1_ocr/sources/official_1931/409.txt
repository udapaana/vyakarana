विद्§†—with हि is  निविदति; किल्विदनानि न्यविदम्न &c. Bhaț.

शप्‡—meaning 'to reproach or to abuse' takes the  Atm.
even when the fruit of the action does not accrue to the agent;
कृष्णाय शपते ।

शिक्ष्—takes the Atm. in the sense of ' wishing to study;'
यडौषे शिक्षते ' wishes to study archery.'
