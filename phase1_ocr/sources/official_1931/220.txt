SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                                                  [ § 340

त्य-(त्यक्‌) is added in the sense of ' living in, belonging to,' &c. to the words द्रदिष्णा, पश्चात्‌, पुरस्‌, अमा, इह, क, ह्यस, दसे, and indeclinables ending in तस्‌; द्राक्षिणात्यः a southerner, पाश्चात्यः. पौरस्त्यः, an inhabitant of the east; यक्षात्य, one who accompanies a king a minister, इह्यः क्रत्यः, ह्रास्त्यः, तत्स्यः &c ; also to the preposition वि, विस्य्यः ' eternal '.

यक ( स्यकन् ) is added to उप and अधि; उपत्यका land at the foot of a mountain, अदिन्यका ' a table-land. '

-is affixed to गो only; गवां समूहो गोवा f. ' a herd of kine '.

म—forms abstract nouns; शार्मं.

य, द्रयस and मात्र* ( दश, द्रयसं, मात्रं. ) are added in the sense of ' measuring as much as '; जानु प्रमाणं अस्य जानुद्रयं-द्रयसं-मात्रं उदकं ' water reaching as far as the knee ', &c.

त and न†( बञ्, सञ्. )—are added to स्त्री and पुंस respectively in different senses:—स्त्रीणं ' womanly, feminine, suited to women, ( n ) womenhood ' &c ; पैंर्म ' manly, heroic, fit for a man, ( n. ) manhood, virility '.

ष—is added as a depreciatory termination, निषद्य्याञ्र: ' a bad doctor, a quack '; वैयाकरणपाञ्रः &c ; added to केशा it has the sense of ' a collection of ', as केशपाञ्रः ( दश and इत्त are also added to केशा in the same sense ).

मय ( मयट् )—§ is added in the sense of ( 1 ) ' made of '; इष्ठः विकारः मुन्मयं ' made of earth, ' काष्ठमयं ' made of wood ' &c.; and ( 2 ) in that of profusion or excess ' यूते प्रचुरं.

* प्रमाणे द्युयसुञ्चदेङ्मात्राचः । Pán. V. 2. 37.

† स्त्रौंसाम्यां नञ्जेजौ भवनात्‌ । Pán. VI. 1, 87.

§ मयह् वा एतर्मभोषाषायाममुक्षाच्छदनयो Pān IV. 3 143.
