256                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 405-409

§ 405.  Before strong terminations the penultimate short and the final vowel of the base take their Guṇa substitute.

                  Fifth, Eighth and Ninth classes

§ 406.  * न and च are added on to the roots of  the 5th and 8th classes respectively before the terminations.

§ 407   The final न of  the base  is  optionally dropped before म् and य if it is not preceded by a conjunct  consonant. It is changed to ण् before a weak termination beginning with a vowel, if preceded  by a conjunct consonant and  to च in other cases.  The हि of the Impera ive 2nd per.  sing. is dropped after न not preceded by a conjunct consonant.

§ 408.  † In the ninth class न is inserted  between  the  root and the terminations  न becomes ण् before the weak terminations beginning with a vovel, and  नी  before the weak terminations beginning with a consonant.

§ 409.  ( a )  The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped before न  &c. as यूजाति,  यूजीत, यूजीन:  &c. from यूज् 'to arrange, put together.'

    ( b )  In the case of roots ending in a consonant, the imperative  second person singular termination  is आन  instead  of हि; as छुनाग from चुस् ' to steal '.
