parasavarṇa; and thus we get शुनम्, शुनः, शुनाम् । So also
in the ins. dat. and abl. sing., where श् is dropped by the
general rule, and न् is retained, and consequently we have
शुना, शुने, शुनः । In the loc. sing. न् may or may not be dropped,
and thus we have शुनि or शुनि । The fem. is formed by adding the
affix आ to शुन् and this gives शुनी, which is declined regularly. The
fem. may also be formed by adding ई to श्वन् → श्वी ( by शुनोष्य, Pan.
VI. 4. 116 ), which is also declined regularly after the analogy
of पृथिवी ॥ There are no neuter forms.
