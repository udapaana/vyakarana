( Pan. VI. 4. 3 ). So also we have युवानौ ( dual ), युवानः ( plu. ),
युवाने, युवानाम् and the loc. sing. युवानि which is like the form
made from the base युवन् ॥ So also युवना ( ins. sं० ) in which
न् is retained. Before the case-affixes beginning with consonants,
न् may be changed to ण् or otherwise ; thus युवभिः or युवणभिः,
युवभ्यः or युवणभ्यः, युवभ्याम् or युवणभ्याम्, युवसु or युवणसु, युवनाम्; युवनि
or युवानि ॥ Before न्, अम्, न् is generally dropped, and thus we
have युवानम्, युवनोः, युवनः ( gen. sं० ) which is also युवनः ( loc. sं०)॥
The fem. is युवती, which is declined like the fem. of भगवत्; and
this base is not used in the neuter.
