§7. Some consonants are pronounced with a slight aspiration and are designated as Alpa-prána, while others which are pronounced with a stronger aspiration are called Mahâ-prâna. The first and third letters of each class, the nasals and the semi-vowels belong to the first class; the rest belong to the second class. For the sake of convenience the first and third letters of each class are sometimes called "unaspirates."
