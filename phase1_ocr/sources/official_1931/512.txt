§ 856.  * The  Genitive  is  used  subjectively  and  objectively
with Kṛidantas or  Primary Nominal  Bases  derived  by  means
of the Kṛit affixes (i  e.  is  used  in  the  sense  of  the subject  or
the object of the action denoted  by  the  Kṛidantas); युयुणुयुस्युरु  छुति:
an act of Kriṣhṇa i. e. of which Kṛishṇa is the agent; जुगुतुः क्तुर्त्तुयुयु
the Creator  of  the  world,  which  is  the  object  of  the  action:
denoted  by  the  noun  क्तुर्त्तुयुयु;  similarly  सुतुनों  याट्युकुः  the  protector  of
the good; यवुयुस्  वरं  will  be  the  similies  of  mṛiti;  मुयु  कुवेः  किंनुयुयु a  work
of that  poet;  सारुयुर्णो  दुहितुरुनं  न  भायुट्.  (Rāmacharita  XII.  117.).
This is  not  a  common  creation  of  Brahman.

(a)  | In the case of verbs  governing  two  Accusatives  the
secondary  object  of  the  Kṛidanta  may be put in the Genitive or
in  the  Accusative;  नैत्युयुयुस्युयु  द्युयुयुयुं  दुयुयुस्युयु  वा  (Sid.  Kau.)  the  taker
of the  horse  to  Śrughna.

(b)  ‡ When  the  agent and the object  of  the  bases  derived
by  means  of  Kṛit  affixes  are  used  in a sentence,  the  object  is  put
in  the  Genitive  case  and  not  the  agent.  गुवुयुयो  गुवुयु  वैष्णुविकुरुयुरुयु
the milking  of  cows  by  one  who is  not  a  cowherd  is  a  wonder

Exceptions.—This  rule  does  not  apply  to  Kṛit  nouns  ending
in the affixes  अनुकु  and  अ  when  feminine;  विविस्युरुस्युयु  भेदिकुयु  वा  वरुस्युयु
जुगुतुः  (Sid.  Kau.)  the  desire  of  Rudra  to  split  the  universe  or
the  splitting of the universe  by  Rudra  According  to  some  when
the  Kṛit  affix is  घुञु,  i.  e.  of  feminine  gender,  and  according  to  others
