313. † Nouns ending in अ and not having य for their penultimate, except द्रय, गव्य a wild ox, द्रुक्य, मड्रुष्य and मत्स्य take ई when ' class ' is implied e. g. द्रुषली ' A Sudra female ' ( also the wife of a द्रुषल, see § 311. above ); similarly म्राहुषी, महाच्युदूद्री, &c., द्यरिणः. ह्युर्गों, औपगर्वो ' a woman of the औपगब class of Brāhmaṇas.' हत्त्रो ' a woman of the हन्त्र class of Brāhmaṇas ', &c., इपौ; गव्यौ. द्रुकौ, मह्युषौ, and मत्स्यौ ( vide § 306. d. ); but देवदत्ता ' a woman of that name '; अज्ञा as it is one of the words included in the Ajādi group ; ( see § 307. and foot-note ); सूद्रा ' a woman of the Sudra class ( see § 307 above ).'

(a) Words ending in पाद, कुण्, पर्ण, पुष्प, फल, स्त्रल and चाल form their fem. in ई when kind is implied ; शोवदागक्रो, शाकुकुर्णो, शालपर्णाँ, शंबेपुष्पाँ, दासीफलौ, दूभंकाली and गोदाली all names of particular herbs.

(b) Also words ending in ई and denoting mankind; द्राक्षी ' a woman of the द्राक्ष family,' औदमैयौ (उदमेधरस्याग्रल्यं), but तितित्रिरः.
