(a)—सम when it means 'equal' is not a pronoun and is
declined like nouns in अ; e. g. समः समौ समाः Nom., समाय D.
sing., समानाम् G. plu. as in the Sutra of Pāṇ. ग्रयासौस्ख्यमसुद्देशः
समानाम् । 1. 3. 10.

§ 149. Obs :—उद and उद ( the 10th and 11th सर्वादि० ) the one
उदात्त ( udātta ), the other अनुदात्त ( anudātta ), both synonyms of
अन्य are declined like सर्व. The former is of the form उत्त
according to some and then it is declined regularly by adding
the terminations; e. g. स्वत् स्वतौ स्वत् Nom. &c.
