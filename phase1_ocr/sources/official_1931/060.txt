ending beyond च्, before which, therefore, न् cannot be dropped
by the rule सोऽञ्चि, and so च् is not changed to ग्, being
already preceded by न् . If, however, we drop न् by the rule
कृदन्तेषु चान्येषु, then च् becomes ग् before the loc. pl. affix सु
and the word will take the form of प्रागसु ॥ Or we may drop
न् optionally after च् has become ग् by the rule सोऽञ्चि, the rule
कृदन्तेषु चान्येषु authorising the optional dropping; then the loc. pl.
may be either प्रागसु or प्राङ्सु ॥ In the rest of the cases, the
forms of प्राञ्च् will be like those of वाच् ( a word ) ॥ Thus
in the plural:
