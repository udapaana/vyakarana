§25. When ए or औ at the end of a word is followed by अ the latter merges into the former § and the sign ऽ is sometimes written in its place; e. g. हरे+अव=हरेऽव protect, Oh Hari; विष्णो+अव=विष्णोऽव.

(a) ‡If the word गो, ending in औ, be followed by अ, the अ is optionally retained, and if by a vowel, अव is optionally substituted for औ. गो+अग्रय=गोमय, गोअग्रय and गवाग्रय a multitude of cows or chief among cows; but if it be followed by इन्द्र and अक्ष it recessarily changes its औ to अव; गो+इन्द्रः:=गविन्द्रः an excellent bull; गवाक्षः a lattice-window, similarly.
