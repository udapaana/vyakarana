§ 216. A noun in the Locative is compounded—

(a)†with शौण्ड, धर्तं, कितव a rogue, प्रशीण, संसीत adorned, invested with, अन्तर, आवि, वद्, प्रणीत, कुशल, चल, निपुण, सिद्ध, गुष्ट, पक्व, and बन्ध्य; as अक्षेषु शौण्डः अक्षशौण्डः skilled in dice; ईश्वरे भक्ति ईश्वरभक्तिः dependent on God (when compounded the affix इन i. e. इन is compulsory; so the compounded form is always with अभीन); आतपञुष्कः dried in the sun; स्थालीपक्वः cooked in a pot; चक्रबन्ध्यः a kind of poetic composition; &c.

(b)‡ with words meaning a 'crow' when contempt is implied; तीर्थे स्वाइङ्घ इव तीर्थैस्वाइङ्घः said in contempt of one who is very greedy; so तीर्थैकाकः; similarly मगरकाकः:—वायसः &c. &c.

There are a few Loc. Tat. compounds given in the list परमेसमितादि having a similar sense; e. g. कुपे मण्डूक इव कुपमण्डूकः a well-abiding frog i. e. one who knows nothing of the world
