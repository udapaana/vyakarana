§ 787–789 ]                SYNTAX.                    473

§ 787.  When an indeclinable used with an  adjectival  force
takes the place of the verb,  the  object  governed  by  it is put in
the Nominative case.  * विपश्युयोपि  संवर्धुयं  घटं  छुट्टुयुमुतुक्तानुत्रु (Kum.
V. 71) it is not fit to cut down  even  a  poisonous  tree having
first reared it up; here the indeclinable  अलमुद्युतं  is  equivalent to
न इच्युयुते and the whole sentence to न्युयुं  संवर्धुयं च्युट्युद्यु  असन्मर्युतुयु (न
इच्युयुत्युर्)  योति विपश्युयुः  स्मात ।

Concord of the Adjective with the Substantive.

§  788.   An adjective, participial  or  qualitative,  must  agree
with the  substantive  it  qualifies  in  gender,  number  and  case;
रुपत्युर युद्युरुः  a  handsome  man; रुपत्युर्ती  स्त्री  a  beautiful woman;
गतदर्शुनं च  a  great  calamity;  एते  मर्त्यराः  गति  पुन्त्युक्तानि;  गच्छुन्तो
नरोः, &c.

But numeral adjectives of fixed  gender  and  number  remain
unchanged:—शतं  ब्राह्मणाः  a  hundred  Drāhmaṇas;  शतं  स्त्रियः  a
hundred women, विविंशति:  वाट्युकुनि  twenty children.

§ 789  When an adjective qualifies two or more  substantives
it  agrees  with  them  in  their  combined  number;  but  when
substantives  differ  in  gender,  the  adjective  will  be  masculine
when  the  substantives  are  masculine  and  feminine;  and  neuter
when  they  are  masculine,  feminine  and  neuter  राजा  रानी  च
युत्युच्युवुरुचितौ तुयुः  the  king  and  his  queen  are  of  laudable
conduct; सर्त्युः  शुमवुत्युस्  द्युर्युद्यु:  कोशुः  भूमिं  वयुः । अर्युद्युदितात्युति  चन्तोपि
प्रवर्तनुते न लोयुयुयुः  fulfilment of duty, satisfaction of desires,  pride,
anger, happiness and  long  life,  all  these  proceed  undoubtedly
from wealth.

(a) Sometimes the adjective takes  the  gender of the majority
of  the  substantives;  पुयुत्युयु  पत्युविभात्युग्र्यो  निरुर्नो  भार्या  भूतः  स्त्रियः ।
अप्युकुरुयुशुनं कुत्युच्युरु  भवुन्त्युयु  मयुत्युरुवुदिंत ॥ aged  parents, a good wife and
a  young  son  should  be  maintained  even  by  doing  a  hundred
foul deeds; so has Manu spoken ( laid  down ).

* निरोतितभिहिते क्तु स्न्ता न विभकुत्युद्युरुपोन्तुच्च प्राविकुच्युकुत ।  Vāman.
