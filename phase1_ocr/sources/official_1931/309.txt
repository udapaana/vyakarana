                       II. GENERAL
                           OR
            NON-CONJUGATIONAL TENSES AND MOODS.

§ 457.  In the  General  Tenses and  Moods  and in the formation of verbal derivatives generally,  the  augment  इ  is prefixed ( necessarily or optionally )  to  terminations beginning with any consonant except य in the case of certain roots. Such roots as take the augment इ  necessarily are called Set ( स+इट् i. e.  with इ ); such as take it  optionally are called Wet ( वा+इट् ), and those that do not,  are called  Anit ( अन्+इट् i. e.  without इ ).

§ 458.  ( a ) Roots of more than one syllable,  derived  roots and roots of the 10th class are always Set.

    ( b ) Of all monosyllabic roots ending in a vowel those given in the following couplet ( Kārikā ) are Set and the rest Anit.

       " ऊदूनौयौगितिदेयूङ्ज्ञोविदुद्राबिहारिभिमिभिः ।
         इडङ्ढुळभ्यां च विनेकाच्योऽजन्तेषु निहताः स्थूताः ॥

i. e.  roots ending in long  उ  and long  ऋ,  the roots ग्, रु, ध्यू
