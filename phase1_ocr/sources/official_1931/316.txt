§ 485.  The roots कृत्, वृत्, धृत्, वृध् and वृत् take इ optionally when followed by an Ardhadhatuka ( Non-conjugational termination ) beginning with स except in the Aorist; कुत्—कर्तिष्याम, कर्त्स्याम; अकर्तिष्यम्, अकर्त्स्यम् Cond  &c.

§ 486.  In the case of इ with आग्नि, गा is  optionally  substituted for इ in the Conditional  and  the Aorist. इ is substituted for the final vowel of the verbs चा 3 P. A  1 P, प्रा, दो, ते, घे, मा, स्या, भा ( substituted for इ 2 P  and स्था with आग्नि , ) म्रा, मा, and को before a  consonantal  weak  termination.
