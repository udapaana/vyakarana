The  Causal of कु is Atm.  when तितिया is prefixed to it;  पत्
तितिस्थाकारयते 'mispronounces the accent on the syllable.'

कू—'to scatter' with अपु ; is Atm.  in the sense of 'turning up
or scratching with joy,' or turning up with the intention of
making an abode or for maintenance ( by quadrapeds or birds );'
in this sense प् is prefixed to कू; अपकीकति दुर्गा ह्रद: 'the ball turns
up the ground in joy;' similarly समीकीकति कुलुङ्खेर: पश्यायौ ; मृग-
