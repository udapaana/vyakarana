Exceptions:—Vrddhi substitute takes place in the following cases †

(a) When a word ending in अ is followed by ऊह, and प् by ऊरु; ऊढ and ऊर्ढि; e.g. प्रछु+ऊहः:=प्रछौहः', chief guess (or this may be the acc. pl. of प्रछुवाह a young bull trained to the yoke); प्र+ऊहः:=प्रौहः principal reasoning. Similarly प्रोढः full grown; प्रीढः; but प्रोढवान् (as ऊढवान् is not mentioned in the Sûtra).

(b) अक्ष+उहिनी=अक्षौहिणी a complete army.‡ (The change of अ to ए will be explained further on).

(c) When स्व is followed by ईर् and ईरिन् both derived from ईर् to go; e.g. स्व+ईरः:=स्वैरः, one acting wilfully; selfwilled, स्व+ईरिणो=स्वैरिणो, so स्वैरं. स्वैरि (स्वेन ईरितुं शीलमस्य इति)
