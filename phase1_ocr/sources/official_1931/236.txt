म—is generally used as an expletive.  Used with a form of
        the present tense of a verb, it gives in the sense of the
        past tense ; as भवति स्म i. e. अभवत्.  When used with
        the particle मा it has an intensive force; as मा स्म शोकं
        मनः कुथाः &c.

इद—is added to कि and other indeclinables and asks a question
        or implies a doubt ; किन्विद्, अहोस्विद्, &c.

स्वी—is used with छ and its derivatives like a preposition in the
        sense of ' acceptance ' ; स्वीकारः, स्वीकृतम्, &c.

                        IV. CONJUNCTIONS.

§ 376.    The following are the principal conjunctions in
Sanskrit:—

( a ) Copulative अथ, अथो, उत, च, किन्च, &c.

( b ) Disjunctive वा, वा...वा, &c.

( c ) Adversative अथवा, तु, किन्तु, किन्वा, &c.

( d ) Conditional चेत्, यदि, यदापि, नेत्, नोचेत्, वेद् ( used in
        sacrificial ceremonies ), &c.

( e ) Causal हि, तत्, तेन, &c.

( f ) Interrogative  आहो अहोस्विद्,  उत,  उताहो,  कि,  किच्चु,
        किधुत, किन्चित्, नञु, नघा, नु, &c.

( g ) Affirmatives and negatives अहु, अथ किम् , आम्, अत्ता &c.

( h ) Conjunctions of time यावत्—तावत्, यदा, तदा &c.

( i ) अथ and इति are used to mark the beginning and the close
        of a work respectively.
