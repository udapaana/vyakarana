308                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 498-502

§ 498.  Roots ending in आ take ओ  instead  of  अ  in  the  1st and 3rd per. sing and drop their final  आ  before  vowel  weak terminations, and before such as  take the  augment  इ; as  ददौ. द्विव, द्विव 1st Per. ददौ; द्विव 2. Per. sing. of दा.

§ 499.  Guṇa is substituted for the final vowel of roots ending  इ अ preceded  by  a  conjunct  consonant,  and  in  long  अ and of the roots अ. चच्छु  and  जाग्; as तस्तरिव 1 dual of स्यु, &c. इ, 1 dual of गा; विधविव 2 sing.  of विध्; रुरुव 1st dual of रु; सूषुप्व, शुश्रुविव 1 dual of सू, &c weak terminations. and shorten their vowel  when  they do not; as शासरिव, सासविव 1 dual of शु, &c.

§ 500.  Monosyllabic  roots,  having  a  short  अ  for  their penultimate, change it to य and drop  the  reduplicaive  syllable before the weak  terminations  and  before  य  when  it  takes  इ, provided  their  initial  letter  undergoes  no  change  in  the reduplication, e. g   पत् 1st dual  पेतुव्+इव=पेत्+इव=पेतिव, पेरिव 2 sing. शोक्ष्व, शसक्ष्व 2. sing. of शक्, but निनेश्व  2nd  dual of नन्द् as the अ here is prosodially long,

Exceptions:—Roots  beginning  with  व  and  the  roots  शासु and ग्रस्.

§ 501.  त is inserted after the  reduplicative  syllable  in  the case of  roots  beginning  with  अ  and  ending  in  a  conjunct consonant, in that of अह् ' to  pervade ' and  चच्छु ' to  go ' consequently त् after is  changed  to  आ; अञ्ज् अञ्ज्+अ=आत्+अ+अ=आनञ्ज, similarly आनन्द् 1 sing. of अन्द्, आनत्र of अत्र, &c.

§ 502   The change of a  semi-vowel  to  its  corresponding vowel is called Samprasāraṇa.   Samprasāraṇa generally takes place before weak  terminations  in  the  case  of  the  following roots:—वद् वस् शप् वच् स्वप् वह् यज् वह् दै. वष्, ग्रध्, दो, स्यम् वप्, भच्, श्वन्च्, भञ्ज्, ग्रह्, सह् and स्यप् In  the  Perfect the roots पद्ध्यू, द्रक्षु and , मध्य form  an  exception.
