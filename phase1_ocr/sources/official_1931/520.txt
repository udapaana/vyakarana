§ 928. When the particle युयु in combination with स्युयु is used
with the Imperfect in the sense of the Imperative, the augment
अ is dropped; युयु स्युयु भुवुः युयु स्युयु करोतु, युयु स्युयु युटुवितुयुं युयूयूयु.

PERFECT.

§ 929. *The Perfect denotes an action done before the current
day and not witnessed by the speaker. It has reference to a very
remote time and should, therefore, be used in narrating events
of the remote past; तुयुनों तुयूटुकुयूस्युयुयुनों निजुयुयूनु रामुः। Rāma killed her
whose name was Tātakā. युयुयुयूयूविन्युदुट्युच्युयुयुदुच् (Bhattṛi. XIV.16.).

(a) In the 1st person the Perfect shows that the speaker was
in a distracted state of mind or was unconscious when the event
took place, or that he wants to say too story of something he
has done, बहु जुयूयुयुट् पुरुत्तुयुयुयुस्युयु यूयूयूक्तुछुयुहुयुयु (S'is XI. 30) being
frenzied I prattled much, I am told, before him; छुलिङुयूनुयूरुयूस्तो: किंयु।
didst thou dwell in the country of the Kalingas? यहुयुदु कुलिङुगुयुनुयूयू-
गायु I never went to Kalinga; With these exceptions the Per-
fect should not be used in the 1st person.
