अव्ययीभावः                                                           165

षष्ठ्यादनुकर्षे पौर्वापर्ययुप्पदे च स्याद्विभक्तित्वात् ।

प्रातिकूल्येऽनुषङ्गः स्यात्कार्यसिद्ध्यनुकारिता ।
आधिक्ये सप्तम्या योग उपमान्तः पुनर्गुणे ॥ २७१ ॥

Obs. This is a general observation on the Avyayībhāva compounds. षष्ठी has been mentioned in the preceding section. Now, as regards the other case-relations, the Genitive is found where there is अनुकर्ष 'superiority' and in compounds with पूर्व and अपर. प्रातिकूल्य means 'opposition' or 'contradiction'. अनुषङ्ग means 'connection' or 'conjunction'. सिद्धि means 'accomplishment', and अनुकारिता is 'imitation'. When there is आधिक्य i. e. 'excess' or 'superiority', the Locative is used. उपमा means 'resemblance' and गुण means 'quality'; these are also attended with the Locative.

§ 272. गमनार्थेभ्यो विभक्तितो रुट् । अनुपसर्गात् साद्यसम्पत्योर्लुक् ॥

Obs. रुट् is a technical name for the first member of a compound being in the Accusative. After roots meaning 'to go' with a preposition prefixed, the first member is in the Accusative. But when सा (with) is prefixed, the compound has the sense of accomplishment, and the Accusative is dropped. Similarly, when अनु is prefixed, the sense being 'conformity', the Accusative is dropped.

Examples:— अनुगङ्गम् 'along the Ganges', प्रतिकूलम् 'against the current', समुद्रम् (not समुद्रम्) 'along with the ocean' (सम्+उदन्), अनुरथम् 'after or according to the chariot'.

§ 273. विभक्त्यलोपे सम्पन्नार्थे लुक् ।

When the case-ending is not dropped, and the compound has the sense of 'accomplishment' or 'completion', the Accusative sign is dropped.

Example:— सजलम् 'together with water', समुद्रम् 'along with the ocean'.
