§ 714   The affix य ( यत् )* is  added on to  roots ending in a
vowel in the sense of fit to be, or ought to be.' Before
this य the radical vowel  undergoes  Guṇa  substitute and final
आ ( य, ऋ and ओ changeable to आ ) is changed to ए.

    दा—देय what is fit or ought to be given.

    भो—भोय      „       „       cut.
    विर्-वैय      „       „       collected.
    नी—नेय       „       „       led or carried.

§ 715.  Roots having अ for their penultimate and ending in
a consonant of the labial class take the affix य;  शाप्-शाप्य; छुषु-
छुष्य: रप्-रप्य, &c.

(a) When ळुप् is preceded by अप्, य् ( changed to इ ) is inserted
between इ and प्; अपेळाप्-भाउप्य: ' what  ought  or is  fit  to be
killed ;' य is also inserted  when छुप् is preceded by  उप  if the
meaning be 'to prise;' उपक्षुप्य: यायै: 'a Sādhu ought to be
praised ;' but उपकुष्युं धनं 'wealth ought to be acquired.'

§ 716.  The roots  तङु  'to laugh at,'  भाष्  'to kill,'  वत्  to
ङो.ये on; ' or ask,' यत् 'to strive,' जय , भुकु  and शक् take  the
affix य;  तङ्कुं 'what is fit to be laughed at,  भाषं  'what ought to
be killed ;' &c,
