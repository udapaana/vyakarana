§ 742.  A  few  transitive  roots  take  the  affix  एलिमुर्  having
the same force as the affix यु; पचेलिमु fr.  पच् , 'fit to  ripen or to

tress ought  to be felled,' &c.

§ 743.  The  declension of these  Participles follows  that  of
nouns ending in अ.

            II  INDECLINABLE PARTICIPLES

                ( a ) Indeclinable Past Participles.

§ 744.  The Indeclinable  Past Participles are  of  the  nature
of gerunds.  They  fall under two  heads:—( 1 ) Those  derived
by affixing  स्या to the simple  root,  and ( 2 )  Those  derived by
means of य affixed to the  root  compounded with prepositions or
words used prepositionally;  गस्-गत्वा  'having  gone,'  भू  with
यश्यस्त्र ' having ' having served;' &c.
