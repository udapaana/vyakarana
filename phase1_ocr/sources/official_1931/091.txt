स्वसारम्, नप्तारम्, होतारम्, भर्तारम् &c. ॥ So also in the nom. voc. and
acc. dual र् is changed to anusvāra before those case-affixes
( वा० V. 4. 106 ; Pan. VII. 2. 98 ) and we get स्वसारौ, नप्तारौ,

ning with भ् or स् the substitution of र्, for न्, by the rule नो णः
( Pan. VIII. 4. 2 ) takes place optionally, so that न् sometimes
becomes न् and sometimes ण् ; thus नप्तृभ्याम् or नप्तृभ्याम्, नप्तृभिः or
नप्तृभिः ॥ Before सु too, this becomes स्वसृषु or स्वसृसु, and by the
guna rule अत्र लोपः of Vār. quoted in the Kās. ( on VI. 4. 116 ),
we get पितृषु or पितृसु in which the former has प्रकृतिभाव ऋ, and
not ॠ for which is only an alternative rule ॥
