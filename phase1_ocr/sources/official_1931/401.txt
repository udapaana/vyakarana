When the root has its original sense, the Par. is used and
य् is not inserted:  कुरुपति अपकर्वति  त्री  'the woman scatters
flowers;' अयोदुपति fruit scatters it

कुत्*—when not preceded by a preposition is conjugated in both
the Padas.   But it is used in Atm. by itself when the meaning is
'free movement, energy, development or increase, '&c,. कचिच्य फमते
इतिर्: 'his intellect moves freely in ( i. e proves very powerful in
mastering ) the Rigveda;' फत्तामाणोरिसदति 'moving unobstructed in
the assembly of the enemy' (Bh. VIII. 22); अष्यचनाय फमते 'shows
ability or energy for studying ;' न त्स्नाय फमते जडानाम्। Vikra.I.16
फमनेऽन्तेष् शास्त्राणौ 'the Śāstras find enlargement in him or are
satisfactorily  mastered by him.'  In the same sense it is Atm.
when preceded by उप and परा only ( i. e. is Par. if preceded by any
other preposition) परक्रामति, परक्रीडति i. c. is Par. if ' शक्ति ( showed
might ); परिक्रामसुरातते  ( made bold ) पाङ्क्ती यस्य कुरुते ( किंचुग । Bh.
VIII. 22–23; but क्रामनिते ( शाकेबृद् इतिर्: ). When preceded by आति
is Atm. in the sense of 'the ascending or the rising of a heavenly
body;' आक्रमते सूर्य: 'the sun rises;' but आकामति धूमो इस्थीतेऽसात् 'the
smoke issues forth from the upper terrace.' Also when preceded by
क्ति, in the sense of 'a graceful movement of the feet, or ascending,'
सादुशु विक्रमते वाजौ 'the horse moves gracefully;' but विक्रामति सत्रय:
'the joint splits,'—and by य and उय in the sense of ' beginning,'
यक्रमते; as in सन्दृत्ते तिर: प्राक्रमनेवनूत्र । Kum. III. 2.  'thus began to
talk with him in private;' but प्रक्रामति 'goes,' उपक्रामति 'comes near.'

क्री†—'to buy' is Atm. when the prepositions अद्, परि  and हि
are prefixed to it; e.g. अदुक्रीत्ते वं means 'to sell'), अपक्रीणीते, परिक्रीते
cf. Bh.  VIII. 8. छुनेत्ोपह्युतं वाणौ: परिक्रीत्णातम्बुयितवत् ।
