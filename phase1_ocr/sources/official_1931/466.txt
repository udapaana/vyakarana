ऋ—is added to roots  having इ, उ, कु, or छु  for their penulti-
mate, and to the roots युट् and कू and denotes the agent;  लिख-
लिख 'one who writes, क्षिप्-क्षिप्; one who throws, कुप्-कुप्: &c.
रु-is added to roots ending in इट् with one who soothsays; it is also
added to roots ending in आ with or without any preposition,
the final आ  being dropped;  ज्ञा- ज्ञ: or प्रज्ञ: one who knows,
wise; द्वे—द्वे: or आद्वे: one who calls; also when a subanta is
used prepositionally with it;  दुग्-गोद्वे: one who gives cows or
cuts the hair;  हस्ति-हस्ति: ( अरतिहस्ति ) an  elephant;  when
added to युह् it has various meanings;  समुरुः happy, विभुरुरुः
placed in difficulties;  परुरुः a measure of corn,  &c.  This is
also added to  भृङ्—भृङ् a house,  यह्वा: a wife,  a house.

ऋच्—is added to द्वुच् not meaning 'to see' when a pronoun pre-
cedes it;  मद्+द्वुर्-अ=नाद्वुच्: like that; also when the words
समान and अन्य precede;  सद्वुच् like to,  अन्याद्वुच् like another.
It is added similarly;  सद्वुन्: कुरुवच् , सद्वुय &c

syllable त् after it    सुद्ग् is added to  the root अत्  after  निश्च्
and वच्; निश्च् वद्धुतुते विरुवुच्: ' one who speaks sweetly;  रुवुवुच्:
' subject to the influence of, obedient to the will of;—छु after
the words इस् , हिम् , यौत् , चित्तुं:—छुत् after ' doing action,'
&c.; भद्युक: causing fear, dreadful, अभद्युव:;—to  the root  गमु
after a subanta;  विदुगुमु: passing through  the sky,  a bird;
—to  the roots यू, दु, द्यु, नि, यूव्, रुट् , त्र्यु and वुद्यु when the
