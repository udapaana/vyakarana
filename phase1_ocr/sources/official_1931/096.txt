96                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 144-146

§ 144. The affix अति is added to तद् , यद् and किम् to show
number or measure e. g  तति 'so many', यति 'as many,' and
कति ' how many'. These are declined in the plural only, and
take no termination in the Nom and Acc. ; e. g. Nom. and
Acc. कति Inst. कतिभिः the rest like हरि.

        8—Reciprocal Pronouns.

§ 145. Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the
pronominal adjectives अन्य, इतर and पर; e. g. अन्योन्य, इतरेतर,
and परस्पर. These are generally used in the sing. and also
advertially e. g. परस्परेण स्पृहणीयदोभर्म &c. ( Rag. VII. 14);
परस्परं विबद्धन्ते &c. They are more commonly used as the first
members of compounds; as अन्योन्यमोभाजनवाद् बुधुत &c. (Ku.
I, 44, इतरेतरयोगाः (Sis. X. 24.) &c.

        9- -Possessive Pronouns.

§ 146. Possessive pronouns are formed by means of the
secondary affix ईय added to तद्, एतद्, अस्मद्, and युष्मद् ; and
अ and ईन added to भवत्, and युष्मद्, changed respectively
to माम्क and तावक् in the sing. and आस्माक and यौष्माक in the
plu. before these ; e. g.
