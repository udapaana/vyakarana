                      IRREGULAR BASES

§ 509.  The roots श्रन्थु, मन्थु, रम्भु. and स्वड़्जू drop their nasa
optionally before the terminations of the Perfect. श्रन्थु, श्रन्थु
रम्भु obey § 500, even before the strong terminations when thei
nasal is dropped.

  श्रेथु      श्रेथिव      श्रेथिम          मेथु       मेथिव       मेथिम
