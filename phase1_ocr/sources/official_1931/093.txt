§ 136   The following couplet ( Kārikā ) gives a rule for th
correct use of these pronouns :—

इदमुत्तु सन्निकर्षं सर्वपततवीति चैतद्रो रूपम् ।
अदस्तु विप्रकृष्टं तत्द्रेति परोक्ष विजानीयात् ॥

इदम् is used with reference to a person or thing near at hand
and एतद् to one nearer still ; अदस् is used of a person or thin
at a distance, while तद् is used of one that is absent.

§ 137.  The optional forms of इदम् and एतद् in the Acc
Inst. Sing. and Gen. & Loc. Duals are to be used when the
is anvādes'a* i. e. their proper forms have already been used i
a previous clause; e. g. अनेन व्याकरणमधीतं एनं छन्दोऽध्यापय '
has studied grammar, teach him prosody,' अनयोः पतित्रं कुरु
एनयोः प्रस्तं स्वस्व , 'The family of them both is pure, and the
wealth ample.'
