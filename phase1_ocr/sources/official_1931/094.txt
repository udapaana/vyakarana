§ 122. ( 1 ) दिशि to point, rule. This root gives rise to
three nominal bases differing in gender; the mas. base is दिश्
( Pan. III. 2. 61 ; वा० III. 2. 70 ) which forms its nom. sing. after
the analogy of युज्, viz. दिक् ॥ Thus वदिजः, प्रदिजः &c. ॥ The word
occurs in the sense of 'Lord' or 'ruler,' being a compound and
having a base in अच् ( Pan. VI. 1. 168 ).

( 2 ) Fem. दिश् is formed by the affix क्विप् by the
general rule of Pāṇini किजुगुखश्मुखाणां कृज्विप्रयोर्न्युडपः ( Pan. III.
2. 61 ), or by क्विप् by शिवादिभ्यश्च ( वा० III. 2. 77 ; Pan. VI.
1. 63 ). Before case-affixes beginning with a consonant this fem.
base becomes दिक् or दिग्; and ग् changes to ग् before the case-
affixes beginning with non-nasal mutes viz. ( दिग् → ) दिग्भिः or
दिङ्भिः, ( दिक् → ) दिग्भ्यः or दिङ्भ्यः, दिक्षु ॥ But optionally क् may
remain intact by virtue of the rule कृदात्तः किः which appears
to have been suggested by Vār., ( quoted in the Kās. on VI. 1.
63 ); and thus we will have दिग्भिः or दिङ्भिः or दिक्भिः; दिग्भ्यः or
दिङ्भ्यः or दिक्भ्यः ( similarly दिक्सु or दिक्षु ); so also in acc. pl.
दिशः or दिक्षः ॥ The word दिश् occurs in the सं० in the sense of
'side,' 'quarter' &c., and optionally takes an augment मुट् ( म्
