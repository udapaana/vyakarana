Chapter VIII.

FORMATION OF FEMININE BASES.

§ 305. Feminine bases are derived from the masculine by the addition of the affixes आ ( टाप्‌, हाप्‌, चाप्‌), ई ( ड़ीप्‌, ड़ीष्‌, छीच् ), ऊ ( ऊङ् ) and ति.

§ 306. Before the affix ई—

( a ) nouns ending in consonants assume that form which they take before the termination of Inst. sing.; प्रत्यञ्च्-प्रतीची, राजन्-राज्ञी, मृगवद्-मृघवी, श्वन्-सुनी, अर्यमद्-अर्यम्णी, विद्वस्-विदुषी, &c. There are some exceptions. अर्वाञ्च्-अर्वाची &c.
