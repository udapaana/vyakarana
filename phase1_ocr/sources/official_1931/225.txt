§ 366.    The following are the prepositions ( upasargas ) com-
monly prefixed to roots:—

अति—' beyond, over'; अतिक्रम: ' overstepping ' or ' going beyond,
        अतिसर्जन ' a gift, &c.'

अधि—' over, above, &c ;' अधिगम: 'going up, acquisition,'अधिकार
        ' high office or power,' अधिक्षेप: ' censure,' &c.

अनु—' after, behind, along, &c.' अनुक्रमण ' following,' अनुच्छोति·
        ' imitation,' अनुग्रह: ' favour,' &c.

अप—' away from, away.' अपनयनं ' the act of taking away,' अपह·
        ' to plunder, seize ' &c , अपकार: ' harm, wrong' &c.

आपि ( sometimes पि )—'near to,' ' over,' ' taking to,' &c.; अपिगम्
        ' to be resolved into or reduced to, * , अपिधानं or पिधानं
        ' a covering,' अध्यय: ' destruction,' &c.

This prep. is more commonly used as an independent adverb
having a cumulative force in classical Sanskrit.

अभि—' towards, near to,' &c; अभिगम् ' to go towards,' अभिजन:
        ' noble descent or family,'आभिमान: ' self-respect,' अभिरुच्
        ' to defeat,' &c.

अव—( sometimes व; see अपि and it, note )—'away, down,' &c.
    अव—or वगाह ' to plunge into,' अवतार: ' descent,' अवगतं
        ' reproached,' अवमन् to disrespect,' &c.
