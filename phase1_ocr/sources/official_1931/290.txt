290                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [ § 455-456

                 Seventh or रुधादि Class.

§ 455.  In this class the base is formed by inserting  न  between the radical vowel and the final consonant before the  strong and ण before the weak terminations.

§ 456.  ( a ) The original nasal of a root is dropped.

    ( b )  In the case of the root वहु , व is  changed  to  ने before consonantal strong terminations.
