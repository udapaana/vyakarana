§ 764  The Gerund अम् does not, in many  instances,  express
the notion of repetition.

§ 765.  This gerund or the one in स्या  is used with the  words
अचे, पुयुं and  पुञ्चं,  used  as prepositions, although the notion of
repetition is not  present in  these compounds; अत्रुभैसुख्वुव्युत्वा हा
स्त्रौयौ ' having taken to goes out;' so यथायुगोन or पुञ्चवा यृडु-
भोरिते or पुञ्चवा बुद्धुते.

§ 766.  The gerund in अम् of the root कु is used *

( a ) with the noun  governed by it, if it be compounded  with
it prepositionally when  censure  is  implied;  चौरुकाते  आकोष्युत्ते
( चौरुछुद्युय उत्याचियुथुर्यै: ) ' cries out ( he is ) a thief, a thief.' In this
case यु is added to the noun governed.

( b ) with the words स्वाद्, छुण, and  संस्युद् ,  यु  being  added to
these as before; आस्वात्ङुर स्वादु छुत्युवा थुकैं स्वाद्ङु-छुरूणं-संस्यदै—कारु थुकैं
'he eats having sweetened or seasoned his food.'

( c )  with the words अत्युया,  एव,  हुर्युं,  and  कुर्युं,  provided the
root य takes its sense; अत्युयाकारुं  हुत्तो  'he  speaks  in  a coherent
manner;' ' एवं कृत्वा the acts thus;'  किंकारुते,  कुथंकारुते  हुयुभुरामु ,
but शिनैत्युयुत्या हुत्वा मुङुकैं.

( d ) with the particles  युथुं  and  युथा  when an angry  reply
is given; युथुमुकारुं  शोध्युं  तुथुमकारुं  शोध्युं  किं  तवौनैन  ( Sid. Kau. );
'I will  eat  in  this  manner,  I will  eat in that manner,  what
have you do with that ?'
