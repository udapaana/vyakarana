§ 170.  Other derivatives from the numerals:—

( a )  by the Taddhita affix क which is added to numerals
ending in शत् and ति e. g. पञ्चकः bought for five ( rupees &c. )

( b ) *by means of the Tad. affix तय in the sense of " consisting
of parts" or "collection"; e. g. चतुष्टयं ( चतुष्टयी fem. ) consist-
ing of four parts or a collection of four; so पञ्चत्य ( fem. यी);
