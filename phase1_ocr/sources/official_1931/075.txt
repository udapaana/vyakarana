§ 93. Bases ending in च् , छ् , ज् , or झ् and ब्, प्, ह्.

§ 94. (a) च् or ज् is changed to क् when followed by a hard consonant or by nothing, and to ग् when followed by a soft consonant.*

  (b) The ending स्र् or ड्र् of root-nouns and the final of त्रक्ष्, भ्रज्, डुग्र्, यज्, यज्, राज्. and भ्राज् are changed to ग् when followed by any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel or by nothing†; the ग् is changed to ङ् or ङ् when at the end of a word, and to ड् when followed by a soft consonant. The ड् of परित्राज् is similarly changed to ङ् or ङ्.

  (c)  But the स्र् of the root-nouns द्विट् . इट् , स्फुट् and मुट्  is changed to क् as also the प् of द्युगु ' a bold or impudent man ' and the क्ष् of such words as विषक्ष् &c. The ड्र् of नड्र् is optionally changed to ङ् or क् , as also the क्ष् of तड्र् and गारक्ष् . The ड्र् of ऋत्विज् is changed to क्

* चोः कुः । Pán VIII 2. 30  † त्रक्षभ्रज्स्र्      गजराजभ्राजच्छशोषिः। Pān. VIII. 2. 36.
