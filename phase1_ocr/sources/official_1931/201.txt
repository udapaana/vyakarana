§ 314. * Prātipadikas expressive of colour, having त for their penultimate and ending in an Anudātta vowel, except आसित black and पलित grey, and the word पिञ्जा form their feminine in ई or आ; एता or एती 'variegated' from एत; रोहिता, रोहिती; पिञ्जूनी-पिञ्जूणी; but असिता, पलिता, श्वेता ( as the त here is Udātta ).

(a) † But if the Prātipadika has no त for its penultimate, it simply takes ई; कल्माषी variegated, चारुषी; but कृष्णा कपिला ( as the last vowels are not Anudātta ).

§ 315. The Feminine of नृ and नर् is नारी and that of words included in the Shāṅgaravādi ( शाङ्गरवादि०ण ) group†is formed by adding ई; as शाङ्गरवी. गौतमी, आतिथेयी, आशोकैयी, वैदी, एदी, &c.

§ 316. The feminine of words expressive of relationship is irregular; भ्रातृ-भञात्‍, पितृ-मातृ, &c.

§ 317. ¶ The feminine of पति is पत्नी meaning ' the sharer with her husband in the fruit of the sacrifices performed by him; but if it end a compound it may optionally remain unchanged except when preceded by समान, एक, बीर. पिण्ड, भ्‍, भानु, भद्, and पुत्र &c before which this change is necessary ; गृहपति or गृहपत्नी, the mistress of the house; or द्युहपति or द्युहपत्नी; द्रुपलपति or इषलपत्नी &c. but समानः पतिर्येस्या सा सपत्नी a co-wife, एकपत्नी, बीरपत्नी.
