§ 702.  ( a ) The roots  इङ् and  यङुच्  take  the affix वत्  like त्
in an active sense; इङ्दुचुर् ' one who extracted Soma juice;
वङुच्चुर् ' one who has offered a sacrifice;' अत् is added to यु
optionally  in  the  same  sense;  जीर्ण  or  जतुर्   ' one who has
become old' ( also जीविवत् ).

§ 703.  The participles in त् or  न  follow the  Declension of
nouns in अ.

§ The P. P. participles have not always a passive force:—

§ 704. *  The  affix  त्  added  to  roots  having the  sense of
'staying, ' or going' shows the place where  the  action usually
takes place; द्रु मुकुन्दुस्य आसितं ' this is the place where Mukunda
to sit; ' इदं गतं त्सुतार्ः ' this is the path by which the husband
of Rāmā used to pass; ' अत्रुकै एतत् अनतेस्य ' this is the place where
Anaṅta took his food, ' &c.
