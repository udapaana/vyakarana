§ 115. The Nom. and acc. dual उषसी, the nom. voc. and
acc. pl. उषसः are formed regularly by adding the case-affixes. So
also कर्तासौ and कर्तासः ( nom. pl. ) and कर्तासः ( acc. pl. ). Simi-
larly the dat., abl. and loc. dual of both these words will be
formed as follows: उषोभ्याम् and कर्तोभ्याम्, उषसोः and कर्तसोः ॥
Similarly also we have the ins. कर्तासा ॥ But in the ins. of
उषस् we have उषसा by virtue of the special rule वाष्प-अदभ्यष्प्राच्वराब्वि-
भ्यो निचा ( Pan. VI. 4. 116 ). So also we have उषसे, उषसः, उषसः
and उषसि, though the गुण ओ ought to be substituted for अ by
virtue of the general rule प्रत्ययस्य लुकि तद्धितलुकि च गुणः ॥ But
the गुण is not admitted here by virtue of the rule पच्यदात् of
Vār. ( quoted in the Kāsikā on VI. 4. 116 ). The Gen. dual should
have been उषोः by VI. 4. 116, but there being no counteracting
rule like पच्यदात् to the general गुण-rule, Pāṇini explains this
exception by suggesting an आदेश एकादेश of वृद्धि औ for अ of the
base + ओ of the सादेश ( Pan. VII. 3. 102 ). Thus we have उषौस्,
उषोभिः ( उषसः + भिः ), उषोभ्यः ( उषसः + भ्यः ), उषसाम् ( उषसाम् ),
उषःसु ; and so of कर्तासि we have in all cases where गुणोकार is
compulsory, namely ins., dat., and abl. sing., ins. dat. abl. pl.,
gen. dual viz. कर्तोसा, कर्तोसि, कर्तोसः, कर्तोभिः, कर्तोभ्यः, कर्तोसोः.
