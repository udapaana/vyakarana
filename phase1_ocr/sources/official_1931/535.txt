§ 932. ‡ With the particle युयुयु not joined with स्युयु, the Aor-
ist, the Imperfect, the Perfect or the Present may be used;
युयुयुनुयुयुङुनुयू युयूयुयु यवुयूदुयूरुयूयूदुयूयूर्युयुर्युयुनै। (Sid. Kau.) here formerly
