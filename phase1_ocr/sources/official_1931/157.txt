§ 225. Two adjectives may also be compounded into what is called the विशेषणोभयपदकर्मधारयः as युक्कृष्णः, छुरुणसरिग्रः.

(a), * Two participles expressive of two consecutive actions of the same individual may be compounded together, the one expressive of the previous action being placed first; आदौ स्नातः पश्चाद्धुलिप्तः स्नाताडुलिप्तः 'first bathed and then anointed'; similarly पीतोद्यूर्णिन्द 'first drunk and afterwards vomited out', पीतप्रतिवद्धः; यथीतप्रतिष्ठक्तः (Rag. II. 1. IV. 43), &c.

(b). In the case of the two sets of words given above (§ 222, g.) forming compounds with one another those beginning with पूर्व are placed first. In the case of एक compounded with वीर it may precede or follow it; as वीरैकः or एकवीरः; the former, however, is preferable. In the case of the first set of words viz. एक......केवल the one succeeding precedes the one preceding when compounded together; as पुराणजरद्, केवलदु-गाषम्, &c.
