§ 883. भुवुद् is used in the second person like 'you' in English
as a courteous form of address though it is to be regarded as a
pronoun of the third person and ought to be treated as such,
even as युयुयुयुदु &c.; भुवुद् is used in the sacred texts; भुवुद् अपि तवु प्रुच्छुयुट्
you may also go there.

(a) When respect is to be shown, अयुच् and सवु are prefixed to
भुवुद् according as the person with reference to whom it is used
is near, or at a distance or absent; अत्रुभुवुयुट् कुयुयुयुयुः the venerable
Kāshyapa (who is near); इतुयुयुतुनं अट्युकुत्तुरिवुच्युच्युयुवुयुट् may you.
occupy (lit. ornament) this seat; तवुभुवुतो द्रुयुयुवुतो lady Irāvatī
(who is not present); sometimes युयुट् is used with युवुरु to show
respect; as युत्युयुयुं विवुयुयुयुविवुयुं स भुवुयुट् निदुयुदुयुट् । Māl. Mād. I.

§ 884. The pronoun तद् has often the sense of 'well known,
renowned,' &c. तौ यार्वुतीपुरुदैनुभुदो those (well-known) Pārvatī and
Parameśvara; त्युयुच्युनैयु वनुस्युयूलुयुति those well-known forest sites.

(a) When repeated, this pronoun has the sense of 'various,
several;' तैयु तैयु रयुयुनीयुयु स्युयुनैयु in those various highly delightful
spots; छुटोयुति तैतैः युयुत्युनै: notwithstanding several efforts–कुयूहेतैः
स्त्युदुटुतुयुत्युन: &c (Bhag. VII. 20).

§ 885. The pronouns एकुच् and अयुयुर or अन्युयु are used in the
plural in the sense of 'some—others;' विपुवुयुयुयुः पुनुयुयुयुयुयुः सदुयूकुयु
इयुनैकं शाशुयुयुयूतिरुयुच्युद् द्युयुन्युयुनै कुलो निरुयुदुट् द्रुयुयुयुरु some think that widow-
remarriage is sanctioned by the Ś'astras, some say that it is pro-
hibited by them, while others hold that it was allowed in the
Kali age. कोऽयुट् may take the place of एकुनै.
