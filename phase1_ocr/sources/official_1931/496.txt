484                  SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                   [ § 815–817

§ 815.  * A  characteristic  attribute indicative of the existence
of a particular  ste'e  or  condition  is  put  in  the  Instrumental;
जटाभिः तापसः he is an  ascetic  (which is  apparent)  from  his
matted hair ( जटाभिः=प्रकृतितोद्भूतुर्यैजुट्युभिः Sid. Kan. ).

§ 816.  † The Instrumental  is  also used  to  express  the cause
or motive or the object of purpose  of  an action as distinguished
from the mere  instrument  of  it;  पुण्युनेनं  दृष्टो  हरिः  Hari  was  seen
by  (virtue  of)  merit;  तेनाऽपराधेनं  दुण्ड्युयोऽस्युति  thou  art  to  be
punished for that fault; अवुयुवुनैनं  वसति  dwells for the  purpose of
studying;  also  when  the  object  to  be  accomplished  is  simply
implied; अङुङु  अनेणु  away  with  your  efforts  i  e  they  will  not
succeed; श्रुङुयुमु  सायुर्थं  नास्ति  इन्युयुयुः (Sid  Kau  ).

§ 817  The Instrumental  is  used  with  words  expressing the
idea  of—

(a) excelling; हुनांच महाभाग  तुगुतिवेङुते  Oh fortunate one,  you
excel  your  ancestors  in  that  (devotion);  भाराविङुत्युयुयुति  भारं
सहुद्युयुयुशुः  (Mu  3. 17  )  he  surpasses.  by his lustre,  the  lustre
of the sun; दुहितुरु संदुट् युणेयुसुयुयुञ्यु वनह्नुगिः । S'ak. I.

(b) resemblance, likeness, equality; स्युरेणु पितरुयुट्युदुरुति  resembles
his father in vo:ce;  देदुवुन्युयुनं  स्युरेणु  च  रायुयुदुयुच्युदुट्युरुति  (Utta.  4.);
अस्य युयुं सादुट् हुनुनं होचुनं  वादुयुति  his face resembles that of his mother;
विष्णुयुनुयु  पड्युछुर्नो  नो  equal  to  Vishṇu  in  valour.

(c) swearing;  भरुतोनुत्युयुयुनु  चुयुर्दं  मूर्णु  I swear  by  Bharata  and
myself; सुर्पित्युवुरि  मम  जीविुतेनं I  conjure  thee  by  my life.

(d) rejoicing,  being  pleased; भक्त्युयुयु  पुयुरो  यथुयुमुङुकुरुयुयु  च  प्रीत्युऽऽस्युयु
I  am  pleased  with  thee  by  thy  devotion  to  your  preceptor  and
compassion  upon me; कुट्युडुलुयुः स्युल्युकुनुयुपि  तुयुयुति  a  low  person  is
satisfied  with  little.
