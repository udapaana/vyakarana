§  416.   Special rules of Sandhi of the  finals  of  roots  and the initial letters of terminations:—

    ( 1 )  The ending इ of  a  root  takes  its  Vṛddhi substitute when followed  by a consonantal  strong  termination ;  as इ+ति=नीति.

    ( 2 )  The final इ or उ. short  or  long, of a root  is  changed to इस् or उस् before a vowel weak termination.

    ( 3 )  The ending द् of roots is changed  to  ढ  when  followed by any consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, or by nothing; and that of roots beginning with द् to त् under the same circumstances.

    ( 4 )  The initial त  or  य of  a termination is changed to च after a soft aspirate ( 4th letter of a class ).

    ( 5 )  द् or य followed by स is changed to क्.
