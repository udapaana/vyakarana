Note:—Words of this class do not enter into further combination with other words.

(c)* with another, when they both imply a name (संज्ञा); there is an aluk of the Loc. term when the noun ends in a consonant or अ; as अरण्येतिलकाः lit 'wild sesamum yielding no oil, hence anything not answering to one's expectations;' similarly चनेकसरुकाः; स्वाचित्सारः 'a bamboo' (also स्वकुत्तारः, vide Si. IV. 61.). These compounds are nitya (obligatory) as remarked by Bhattoji (वावधेन संज्ञानवभमाद्रियसमाबोयम्).

(d) with potential p. participles in य implying an obligation; मासिदेयं (ऋण); पुत्रोदेयं (साम); the aluk by § 217 (b) below.

(e)† with p. p. participles when it expresses a part of the day or night; तद may take the place of the Loc.; as पूर्वाहे छन्नं पूर्वाहृछन्नं; अपराह्नेछन्नः तत्रश्च्छुत्कम् &c; but आह्ने इदं; and with the p. p. participles when reproach is implied, with an aluk of the Loc; as 'अवतसेनकुलास्येर्तं तवेतद् ' 'this thy action is similar to that of a mungoose on the hot ground.' अवतसैनकुलास्थितं is used to express the inconstancy of a person.
