§ 308. पूर्वपदप्रकृत्यैकत्वे ॥

When the first member retains its original form (प्रकृति), it is treated as one unit.

Obs. This principle governs how the first member of an Avyayībhāva maintains its basic stem form throughout the compound.

§ 309. पूर्वपदात् सञ्ज्ञायाम् अग्रहः ॥

From the first member, when it has a technical designation, there is no initial change.

Example:— प्रादुर्भाव 'manifestation' - here प्र retains its form as a recognized upasarga.

§ 310. समासान्तः पराः अव्ययीभावे ।

At the end of an Avyayībhāva compound, the later elements (पर) become fixed.

Obs. This means that once an Avyayībhāva is formed, it becomes invariable and indeclinable.

§ 311. विभाषा सुपो बहुलम् ॥

Optionally, and in various ways, case-endings (सुप्) may be treated differently.

Obs. This acknowledges that there are variations in how case-endings are handled in the formation of Avyayībhāva compounds, and that usage may vary.

Conclusion of Chapter IX - Avyayībhāva

The Avyayībhāva compounds form an important class of indeclinable compounds in Sanskrit. They are characterized by:
1. Being absolutely indeclinable (अव्यय)
2. Having neuter gender and singular number in form
3. The first member determining the meaning
4. Various case-relations being preserved in the sense though not in form
