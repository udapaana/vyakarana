COMPARATIVE AND SUPERLATIVE DEGREES.

§ 887 Adjectives in the Comparative degree are used with
the Ablative; वर्नुयुयुयुरुणों श्रेयुः protection (of one's subjects) is
better than aggrandizement. अर्जुनैनुयुयुयुवुयुयुयुरो ज्युयुयुयुयुट् Udhisthira
was older than Arjuna.

(:) Sometimes the comparative is used with the Instra-
mental; प्राणैं दियुयुरुः dearer than life. See also § 863 Note.

§ 888. The superlative may either be construed with the
Genitive or Locative; अयुयुतेनुयुयुनों एतैयुट् वा गरियुः युङुत्तुयुतो वा.

§ 889. The sense of the comparative and the superlative may
also be expressed by the particular case used; अस्युयु दुदुयुं यायुयुयुयुयुनुकु-
ठिनं his heart is harder than stone; छुयुयुयूयुर्णों छुयुनैदुट् वा चैदुयु: युयुयुदुट्:
Chaitra is the cleverest of all students.

§ 890. The words युरु and यवुरु when used in the sense of the
superlative govern the Genitive or the Locative; पुत्रु सर्वुनैत्तुयो युरुः
'a son is the best of things possessed of touch;' चतुयुयुयुदुयुनों गोः युवुरु
होहुरुयुनुः कुयुयुर्युनों वरु 'the cow is the best of quadrupeds and gold of
metals'; the neu. sing. of युरु is used (with a word expressive
of negation) in the sense of 'better and not, or but not;' अकुरुत्युयुयु-
न्युनुयुदुयुरुयुनै युर् doing any thing slowly is better than not doing
