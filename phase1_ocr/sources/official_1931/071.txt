*The Kau. does not specially mention this form. Just as stops after giving the Nom. forms in the case of words lik रै, गौ, स्यौ &c., so does it do in the case of सै also, implyin thereby that the Voc. forms of सै are also like the Nom. one But the Sutra एङ्-हृस्वात्सङ्ऊुदै: Pān. VI. 1. 69 (एङ्-ऋनात् हृस्वान् अङ्गात् हल् छुयते सम्बुद्धै: चर्) (Kau.) seems to apply in the prese case and by dropping the final स् we get the form सै.
