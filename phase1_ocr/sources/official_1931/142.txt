§ 206. Instances of the Aluk of the Instrumental† (i. e. in which the noun in the Inst. does not drop its case term.)—
अन्येन कृतम् अन्येसाकृतम् 'done honestly'; ओजसाकृतम् 'done with might'; एषाङ्कः 'one having an elder brother'; जङ्घाबन्धः 'blind from birth'; मनसाष्मा, मनसाङ्राप्री, when proper names, otherwise मनोष्मा, मनोञ्रापी, &c. आत्मन् followed by an ordinal number retains its case ending; as आत्मना पञ्चम आत्मनाप्रमः ।

§ 207 A noun in the Dative is compounded × —

(a) with another expressive of the material of which the thing expressed by the noun in the Dative case is made; as द्रूपाय द्रुः यूपद्रुः 'wood for a sacrificial post.'

(b) and with the words अर्थ, बलि, हित, इष्ट and रक्षित. The compound with अर्थ is a nityasamāsa and takes the gender and number of the noun it qualifies; हितार्थ अर्थं हितार्थं सूत्रः
