§ 331. * A Bah. ending in नख and मुख and used as proper names forms fem. in आ; सूर्पेणखा, गोर्मुखा; but ताम्रमुखा a girl having a ruddy face.

§ 332. † Names of the limbs of the body, preceded by words denoting a cardinal point in a compound, form their fem. in ई उद्वञ्जुषाँ, &c.

§ 333. A Bah. ‡ ending in पाद् ( substituted for पाद् ) optionally forms its fem. in ई before which it is changed, to पद्‌; व्यामस्य इव पादौ यस्याः सा द्व्याम्यपाद्-पदी, द्विपाद्-पदी and in आ when पाद means ' a foot of a Vedic verse '; द्विपदा ऋकु, एकपदा &c.

( a ) But if पाद् be preceded by one of the words कुम्भ, बूल, तुण, हस्त, काक, हुरुष्ण, द्रुकर, &c the fem is necessarily formed in ई-पाद् changing into पद् as before; कुम्भपदी ' a woman whose feet are as big as a pitcher.
