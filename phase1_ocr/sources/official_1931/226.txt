लक्ष्मीवद्‌, पशस्वद्‌. भारुद्वद, &c. ;* राजन्‌+वद्‌=राजन्वद्‌; as राजन्वाद् देशः ' a country having a good or just king'; ( cf. Rag. VI. 22. )=राज्ञवद्‌; as राज्ञवाद् देशः ' a country governed by a king'; उदक्‌+वद्‌=उदन्वद्‌ m. ' the sea'; उदकुवद्‌ as उदकुवान्‌ ( घटः: ' a jar ' ) containing water.

Exceptions :—म is not changed to व in the case of the words यव, रलिय, ऊमि, भूमि, छमि, कुञ्च्म्, ब्रश; इक्षु, भज्र, त्रजि, द्यरित्‌, कन्तुद्‌, इश्चुः and मनु, as यवमान्‌, ऊमिमान्‌, &c.

(b) † मत्‌ also becomes वत्‌ when added to words ending in any of the first four letters of a class ; विद्युद्वान्‌, तडिद्वाद्‌ m. 'possessing lightning' i. e. ' a cloud ', &c The न of विद्युद्‌ &c. is not changed to द्‌ as it is not at the end of a पद्‌.

( c ) and when the whole expresses a संज्ञा ( a name ); अहीवती; मुनीवती &c.

§ 343. When added to words denoting qualities मत्‌ is dropped; ‡ e. g. शुहौ धुणीऽस्य्रास्तीति शुक्लः पदः a white ( i. e. possessed of the white colour ) garment, so द्युष्णं. &c

य ( यद्‌ )—is added to रुप in the sense of ' a stamped coin, or beautiful '; रूप्य ' possessing snow, snowy', यूण्यः ' possessing merits '.

सुष—is added to ऊर्णां, शुभ्, अहं, and ञौ; ऊर्णसुः ' woolen ', शुभसुः ' possessing bliss, fortunate ', अर्हसुः ' proud '; द्रोषुः ' happy '.

र—is added to पाण्डु, सषु, इषि, ऊर्ध, नय, मुष्क, पाडु, स, मुस्‌,
