           ( h ) No affixes added to certain nominal bases.

§ 661.  A few nominal stems are used as verbs without the
addition of any special affix as given above. They are called
' कर्मण्यर्घ वर्णाः'

§ 662.  The notion of ' desiring ' is expressed by adding
a root to a root; e. g. ' to eat salt,' विष्कुप्यति = विप+यु. यु
meaning ' to take ' or ' to desire ', so पुष्पयति, विषगर्भयति =
विप+गृध्, similarly मुष्कर्षयति, &c.

§ 663.  Verbs may be formed optionally from the words,
स्फायति, चयायति, भूरि, and अथ by means of regular affixes, or
without the help of any affixes as given in the last para.
The special affixes are क्वच्, चङ्कृच् or णिङ्; so these four words
may be used in three ways:— 1. as nominal verbs with the
affixes added, 2. as verbs used transitively without affixes,
and 3. as nominal verbal bases not requiring any affixes for
their use. Thus स्फायि भाद्रये, स्फायति, स्फाययति,' to use salt.' so
from आत्मन् we have आत्मति, मितायति, आत्मायते respectively.
In the same way from other three also stems may be formed
Similarly from अम्भस्, पयस्, शुद्धे we get णिङन्ति, पयस्यति,
&c. In all these cases the root affix may be taken to be गुर्
or किर्.

§ 664.  By means of the suffixes कुय, क्रिय, णिङ् and कौय
the sense of reproach is conveyed; e. g. स्तेनङ्कुयति, स्तेनङ्क्रियति
means ' to call a person a thief, ' भृत्येयति means ' to call a
person a servant;' so किंकरेयति.
