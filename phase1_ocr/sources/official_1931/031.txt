   § 47.  Visarga ( substituted for स् and not र् ) preceded by short अ and followed by short अ or a soft consonant is changed to उ; शिवः+अच्च्यः=शिवउ+उ+अच्च्यः=शिवोऽच्च्यः= शिवोऽच्च्यः Śiva is adorable; ( see § 25 ); देवः+वन्दयः=देवो वन्द्य God should be saluted; but तिष्ठतु पय अ रे त्रिदग्न (where the अ after पय: is pluto); प्रातः+अत्र=प्राततरत्र ( for here the visarga is sub-stituted for र् ); so प्राततगच्छ &c.

   § 48.  Visarga preceded by भा is dropped necessarily when followed by a soft consonant, and optionally when followed by a vowel; it is also optionally dropped when preceded by अ and followed by a vowel except अ; when it is not dropped it is changed to ण in both these cases; देवा:+नम्ण्याः=देवा नम्ण्याः नेवा+हर=देवा हर, देवाऽविह !

   § 49.  (a) Visarga, preceded by any vowel except भ or अ and followed by a vowel or soft consonant, is changed to र् : हरिः+जयति=हरिर्जयति Hari conquers; so भानुरुद्ऽति the sun rises; गौरागच्छति a cow or bull comes.

   Exception :—The Visarga of the particles शैः, भगौः and अघौः obeys § 48; e. g. शौ+अच्युत=शौ अच्युत or शोऽयच्युत Oh Achyu-ta; भगो नमस्ते Bhago ! a bow to thee; अधो याहि Oh you go.
