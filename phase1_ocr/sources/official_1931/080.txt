§ 130. मघवन् ( epithet of इन्द्र ) follows the decl. of
युवन्, but with certain exceptions. Thus the forms of युवन् are
allowed to occur throughout its declension ( युवभ्यवत्मघवा Kāsikā
on VII. 1. 1 ) ; and, besides these, there are the following
permissible forms viz. मघवन्, मघवा, मघवन्, मघवानम्, मघवने &c.
The affixes of the nom. etc. dual and pl., viz. औ, ओस्, शस्, भिः
&c., are to be applied to the end of the base ( यस्यचतुर्थीयुतन्ता निर्देशः,
Pan. III. 2. 179 ), that is, these affixes are to be added without
dropping the न् of मघवन्, being preceded by the final syllable वन्
which is looked upon as an affix for this purpose. That is to
say, न् is not to be dropped by the rule नाम्यपरे ( Pan. VI. 4. 3 ),
and so we have the forms मघवन् + औ → मघवनौ → मघोनौ; so also
मघोनौ, मघवन् + ओस् → मघोनोः, मघवन् + आम् → मघोनाम्, मघवन् + भिः →
मघोभिः, मघवन् + भ्यः → मघोभ्यः, मघवन् + भ्याम् → मघोभ्याम्, मघवन् + सु → मघवत्सु ॥
In these forms the voc. of मघवन् is व्, and the termination अस्
( nom. pl. ) makes the form मघवनः, by dropping the न् of वन् and by
changing न् to anusvāra ( मघवन् → मघवन् → मघवनः ) ; and then this
anusvāra-न् may be changed to a parasavarṇa, which gives the
word मघोनः ॥ According to the Kāsikā, if the vowel of a case-
affix is dropped and is followed by a consonant, then by the
rule प्रथमयोरटचौ, विराग इदाघः ( Pan. VIII. 3. 1 and Vart. ) we have
this rule; viz: योऽयात् स्य:स्वाक्, being applicable; this rule would,
in the case of words ending in अन् and आन्, drop the न् of the
base before consonantal case-terminations, viz. भिः, भ्यः, भ्याम्;
and thus in the instr. pl. we would get मघवभिः, मघवभ्यः &c.
