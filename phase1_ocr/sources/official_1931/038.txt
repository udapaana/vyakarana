§24. ए, ओ, ऐ and औ, when followed by a vowel, are changed to अय्, अव्, आय्, and आव respectively†; e.g. हरे+ए=हरये for Hari; विष्णो+ए=विष्णवे for Vishnu; ने+अकः:=नायकः A leader; पौ+अकः:=पावकः the purifier i.e. fire,

(a) य or व at the end of a word and preceded by अ or आ is optionally dropped when followed by a vowel or a soft consonant§; e.g. हरे+एहि=हरयेहि or हर एहि; विष्णो+इह=विष्णविह or विष्ण इह; भिये+इयतः:=भियायियतः or भिया इयतः intent on getting money; प्रारौ+उत्कः:=प्रारावुत्कः or प्रारा उत्कः longing to see the preceptor.

Note—Two vowels brought together by the disappearance of an intervening consonant or visarga do not coalesce.

(b) ओ and औ, when followed by a termination beginning with य्, become अव् and आव् respectively ‡; e.g. गो+यं=गव्यम् produced from a cow as milk, ghee &c. गौ+यं=गाव्यम् accessible by a boat, navigable.

N.B. This rule holds good, in the case of roots, only when the ओ or औ is brought in by virtue of a termination, × e. g. लु+यं=लों+यं=लव्यम् fit to be lopped off. अश्वयल+यं=अश्वयलो+यं=अश्वयलव्यम् which ought necessarily to be cut off.
