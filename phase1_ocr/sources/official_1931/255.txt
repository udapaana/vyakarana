§ 404.  The base of the roots of the 2nd group of Conjugational classes undergoes many modifications with regard to which the terminations are divided into sets, one set is called ' strong ', the other ' weak.' The base taking the strong terminations may be calld ' The strong base;' and  that taking the weak ones ' The weak base'.

    ( a )  The strong terminations are:—

    The singulars of all persons of the Present and the Imperfect, the third  person singular  and all numbers  of the first person  of  the Imperative ,  in  the Parasmaipada, and all numbers  of  the  first  person  of  the Imperative in  the Atmanepada.

    ( b )  The rest are weak.
