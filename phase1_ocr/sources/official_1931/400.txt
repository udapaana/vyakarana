§ 638.  As the Parasm. Freq. base is used both in the sense
of frequency and of intensity, it has been said to denote the
former in the strong cases and the latter in the weak; i.e.
जोहूति, जोहवीति 'to invoke frequently'; जुहुती 'to invoke
repeatedly'. It is, however, not easy to make such a distinction
with regard to the other roots.

§ 639.  Paradigms:—The Third persons of the Parasmaipada
Frequentative of चृ ' to do,' पृच् ' to fill,' and कृष् ' to draw
furrows.' All those tenses of this Intensive in which the weak
form of the base is used ( vide §§ 485, 486 ) substitute पचृ for
चृ 'to do'; दृष् 'to fill up,' and कृषृ 'to draw furrows' respect-
ively. If in any weak form of an Intensive the root has a final
consonant, र् is inserted between the Guna of the root and the
termination ( see § 640 )
