                    Bases ending in ह् ॥

§ 124. There are very few bases ending in ह्; of these
are माही earth, and दुह् ( milk-giver ). As regards the decl. of
माही, वाह् ( bearing along, Lat. "veho" ) in the mas., वह्नि
( fire ) and words derived from roots of the class क्रय् ( to buy )
&c., final ह् after short vowel is generally dropped, along with
the nom. affix, the vowel being lengthened as its substitute, or
the vowel अ being added ( the substitute for the nom. affix
विसर्ग ). Thus we have from माही, the nom. सं० माही or माहः, from
वाह् we get वाः or वाट्, and क्रयि gives क्रयी or क्रयः ॥ In the voc. सं०
we get माही or माहि ( माह्, उष्-उष = माह्, Pan. VIII. 3. 44, 46 ), वाट्,
or वाहो ( वाह् + ओ = वाहः, Pan. VIII. 3. 44, 46 ), वह् or वाट् ॥
Similarly वह्नि ( fire, Lat. "ignis" ) makes its nom. सं०, वह्निः or
वह्निः by the guna-substitution for the final of the base ह्नि
( Pan. VIII. 2. 77 ). Before other cases, ह् → ढ् → ह् as
वह्निभ्याम्, वह्निभिः, वह्निभ्यः &c. ॥

§ 125. With regard to the roots ending in ह् which form
nouns in ह् ( fem. ) with affix क्विप्, such as मुह् ( a moment from
the root मुह् ) we have the nom. sing. मूढा or मूट्; or by VIII.
3. 41 मूढा; acc. sing. मूढम्, ins. मूढा, dat. मूढे, gen. मूढः, loc.
मूढि or मूढिं or मूढिस् ॥ Similarly we have अप्स्वाही ( नी० उह्-
व्याइ = वाही ) ॥ Similarly the forms of दुह् and of राह् ( lightning )
can be formed. They occur in the वेदे only.
