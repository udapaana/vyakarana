                        III. PARTICLES.

§ 374.    The particles are either used as expletives or inten-
sives; some of these are किल, खलु, च, ह, इ, वै, हि, &c.

§ 375. The following particles are used with certain words —

अह—अद्भुतं ' a wonder '.

का—कापुरुषः 'a bad man,' कौष्णं 'luke-warm,' काजलं 'a little water'.

कु—कुकृत्यं ' a bad deed '.
