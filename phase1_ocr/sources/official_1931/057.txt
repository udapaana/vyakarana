§ 95. (a) इ is changed to ट् when at the end of a pada or when followed by a consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel.  (b) In the case of roots beginning with इ it is changed to ट् under the same circumstances; (c) when forming part of the roots दुह्, लिह्, स्तह् and लिह् , it is optionally changed to ट् i. e. to ट् or घ् ; (d) and in the case of the root नह् it is changed to प्  under the above-mentioned circumstances.*

  (e) The ह् of डुर्णह f. a kind of metre is changed to क् when followed by a hard consonant or by nothing, and to ग् before a soft consonant ( Pān. III. 2, 59).

§ 96. When a mono-syllabic root (or root-noun) begins with ब् , ग् or ध् and ends with प्, ट्, ध्, भ् or ह्, the प्, ग् or ध् is respecti-vely changed to प्, द् or प्  before सु or ष्व् or when followed by nothing or when the final soft aspirate is changed to an unaspi-rate letter†.
