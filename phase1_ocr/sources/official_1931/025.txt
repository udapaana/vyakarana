(a) य and न, not at the end of a pada, are turned into an Anusvâra when followed by a consonant except a nasal or a semivowel or ह; आक्रम्+स्यते=आक्रंस्यते he will overcome; यशांस्+सि=यशांसि (nom. pl. of यशस् fame); but मन्यते he thinks, as न is followed here by य which is a semi-vowel; ग्रामान्+गच्छति=ग्रामान्स्च्छति as न is at the end of a pada (grammatical form).

(b) This change is optional in the case of न् when ह followed by य comes after it; e.g. किं+झलयति=किंझलयति and किन्ह्मलयति what does he shake?; but if this ह be followed by य्, and if by य् झ, ठ, the nasalised य्, व्, ल्, are optionally substituted for य्: e.g. कि+द्वोते=किंद्वोते or किंद्वोते what does he conceal? किं+ह्यः:=किंह्यः and किंह्यः what! yesterday! so किंह्मलयति, किंह्मलयति; किल्लादयति and किल्लैलादयति; but अहन्+आगतः:=अहमागतः।

§37. An Anusvâra followed by any consonant except म्, य्, व् or ह is changed to the nasal of the class to which the following letter belongs, necessarily when in the middle, and optionally when at the end of a word*; e.g. अनुक्+इतः:=अं+क्+इतः (by the preceding rule and then) अङ्क्तः marked (by this); so अनुच्+द्वतः:=अंचितः honoured; कृपितं obstructed; ज्ञान्तः calmed; ग्रप्फेतः woven; त्वम्+ करोषि=त्वं करोषि or त्वङ्करोषि thou doest; similarly संयन्ता or सयंन्ता a restrainer; संवर्षरः or संवत्सरः a year; यं लोकय् or यल्लोकम् to which people.
