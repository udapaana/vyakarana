§ 593.  ( a ) The roots स्तच्, जन्, तन्, and सच्, '
drop their च् and at the same time lengthen their
स्तन्यते, सोपते, &c.

( b ) शी 'to lie down' and चि form their bases as ह्यस्

( c ) ऊह् shortens its ऊ when a preposition is pref

( d ) दरिहा, दौषी and वेङी drop their final vowel befo

( e ) हू and अस् substitute हृच् and भ् for themselves
भञ्ज् also substitute अञ्ज् and घी respectively.
