§ 77. Root nouns in ई or ऊ :M. F. N.

Rule of Sandhi—(a) The ending ई or उ, short or long, of nouns derived from roots with the affix किप्.(o) and of ऋ, is changed to इय् or उव् before the vowel terminations*: the fem. nouns of this description are optionally declined like नदी in the Da. Ab. Gen. and Loc. singulars and the Gen. plu,

  (b) But if the ending ई or उ of a many-voweled noun having a root at the end be not preceded by a radical conjunct conso-nant or the root noun has a preposition termed गति (e. g. as added to the root ) or a word governed by the root preceding it, it is changed to ण or झ, except in the case of सुधी and nouns ending in स्त†.
