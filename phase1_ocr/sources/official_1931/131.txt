§ 217. Additional instances of the aluk of the Loc.

(a) Aluk is necessary in the case of the words—(1) गौ and शुभू. followed by विधर, as गावि विधरः, stationary in the sky, युवि विधर: arm in battle; (2) हृद् and दिव् followed by स्पृक्; हदिस्पृक्, दिविस्पृक् (हदयं दिवं च स्पृशतीति); (3) अन्त and मृध् followed by गज; मध्ये-गुरः; अन्तैगुरः and (4) words expressive of parts of the body except मुर्धन् and मस्तक and not followed by कारः; कर्णेकाल्क, डरसिलोम (having the hair on the breast: Bah.), but मूर्धे-मस्तक-शिखं; छुले कामो अस्य छुसकामः:.

(b)* When a noun in the Loc. is compounded with a verbal derivative and the whole is a name, aluk takes place in most cases; e. g. स्तम्बेरमः (स्तम्बे a kind of grass रमते अत्र) an elephant; कर्णेजपः one who whispers into the ear, a spy; स्तैचरः a heavenly being; पेंकर्हुं a lotus; कुशोशयम्, जलेशयः a fish; but कुरुचरा; सरोजम् or सरोजञ्ज &c.

(c) There is optional aluk of the Loc. of a word expressive of time when compounded with काल and words ending in the affixed तर, तम and तन; पूर्वाह-कु-काले; पूर्वाह-कू-सरे &c. पूर्वाह-कू तने.

(d)† When the compound is not a name, aluk is necessary when ज comes after प्रादु, घरद्, काल and दिवु; and optional when it comes after वर्ष, क्षर, घर and वर; as प्राद्यषि-घरदि-काले-दिवि-जः; बर्षेजः or वर्षजः produced in the rainy season, &c.

Exception†:—No aluk takes place in the case of verbal derivatives ending in इन् and the words सिद्ध, वद्ध, and स्थ in the ordinary language; as स्थाण्डिलञागी 'an ascetic,' सादुम्रस्थितिङ, चकबद्धः; समस्थः; but छणोऽस्यावरेश: &c.

। प्राह्दुशर्दुकालदिवौं जे । विभाषा वर्षेक्षरघरवरापु । Pān. VI. 3. 15.16.
‡ नेन्तिइद्धुबद्धुघातिषु च । स्थे च भाषायाम् । Pān. VI. 3. 19. 20.
