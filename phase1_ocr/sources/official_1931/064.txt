                FEMININE.

    The feminine of भगवत् is भगवती which is formed by
adding the taddhita affix ङीष् to the Prātipadika of भगवत्
( by rules वा० III. 2. 7, Pan. IV. 1. 6 ). It is declined like
नदी ॥ Similarly the fem. of विद्वत् is विद्वती ( विद्वती स्त्री, a learned
woman, विद्वता: कन्या a learned girl ); महत् ( great ) makes महती ॥
    Note.—The word सत् ( existent, good ) does not form its
fem. in ई , but has the irregular fem. सती which is as if it
were formed from सत् + ङीष् , the final त् of सत् being changed to
long ई ( Pan. VI. 4. 64 ), the lengthening not taking place
when the word signifies 'existent, real' ( Pan. Vart. to VII. 3. 114 ),
when सन्ती will be the fem. of सत् ॥

                Bases ending in न् ॥

§ 110. ( 1 ) Bases ending in न् drop that letter along
with the nom. sing. affix स्, if there be a short vowel before the
न् ; if that vowel be long, the dropping is optional ( Pan. VIII.
2. 7, 23 ); thus राजन् nom. sing. राजा; वरिमन् nom. sing. वरिमा ;
धनिन् nom. sing. धनी ; मेघिन् nom. sing. मेघी ; पथिन् nom. sing. पन्थाः or
