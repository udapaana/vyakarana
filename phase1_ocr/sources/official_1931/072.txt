§ 119. ( a ) In the nom. sing. of the fem. bases ending in
ऋ the substitution of सवर्णदीर्घ is optional, so स्वसृ makes स्वसा or
स्वसृ; and in the acc., nom., voc., and acc. dual र् is changed to
anusvāra regularly, and we get स्वसारम्, स्वसारौ; मातरम्, मातरौ &c.
So also before the case-termination of the gen. and loc. dual र्
is changed to anusvāra, and we get पित्रोः, मात्रोः, स्वस्रोः ॥

( b ) Bases ending in तृ or नृ, like कर्तृ ( doer ) नेतृ
( leader ), नृ ( man ) make the nom. voc. and acc. dual कर्तारौ,
नेतारौ, नरौ ॥ So also the fem. forms will be कर्त्री ( optionally
कर्तृ in nom. sing ), कर्तारम् and कर्तारौ; नेत्री, नेतारम्, नेतारौ; नारी,
नारम्, and नारौ. It may be noted that only such noun-bases ending
in तृ as are derived from roots by the affix अल, can also add
ङीष् ( fem. affix ) to the crude base before case-affixes ( वा० III.
2. 7 ; Pan. IV. 1. 6 ), when we get the fem. bases कर्त्री and
नेत्री which are then, like any other ई-ending base, declined
regularly. Similarly the fem. of नृ is नारी ( नृ + ङीष् = नारी )
which is also declined regularly. So also देवृ has the fem.
नारी, which may optionally be made देवरी ( from the बहुव्रीहि
प्रातिपदिक देवर ). The fem. of पितृ is formed by adding the affix
टाप् to the crude base, which gives us पितरी which is in general
use in the सं० ( पितर्, पितरम्, पितरौ &c. ). Such bases have
no form in ई ॥
