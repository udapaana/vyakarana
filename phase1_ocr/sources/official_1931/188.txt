§ 298. स्वरादिनि च ।

And स्थानिन् is understood when the compound begins with a vowel.

Example:— अध्यृषि 'in or regarding the ṛṣi'.

§ 299. वक्तव्यो व्युत्पत्तौ ॥

The word वक्तव्य 'should be stated' is used when explaining the derivation or etymology of an Avyayībhāva compound.

Obs. This is a general note about how to explain the formation of these compounds in grammatical analysis.

§ 300. तिष्ठद्गुः ॥

The augment गुक् is added to a word ending in ति when forming an Avyayībhāva compound.

Example:— अभीति 'fearlessness' becomes अभीगुति in certain formations.

Obs. This is a special phonetic rule for certain compounds.

§ 301. यथायोगं विकारस्तद्धितेषु ॥

Changes (विकार) occur according to context in तद्धित formations (secondary suffixes).

Obs. This general principle states that phonetic modifications in Avyayībhāva compounds follow the rules applicable to तद्धित affixes.

§ 302. न वृद्धयादेशाः पूर्वपदात् ॥

Vowel strengthening (वृद्धि) substitutions do not occur to the first member of the compound.

Example:— अनुकूलम् (not अनौकूलम्), प्रतिकूलम् (not प्रैतिकूलम्).
