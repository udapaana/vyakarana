        5—Reflexive Pronouns.

§ 140. The sense of the reflexive pronoun is, in Sanskrit,
expressed by the word आत्मन् 'self' which is invariably used
in the masculine gender and singular number, and by the
reflexive adverb स्वयम् e. g. यत्र दुर्योगात्मानं मया स्वयं वाह्मने:
"they all (the wives of Das'aratha ) saw in dreams that they
were guarded by dwarfs;" so म or सा छलाप्राप्तिम् आत्मानम्-
गच्छति । राजा स्वयं समरमुपि जगाम, &c

6- Indefinite Pronouns.

§ 141. Indefinite pronouns are formed by the addition
of चित् or चन or अपि and sometimes of किंत् to the various
cases of the pronoun किम् in all the genders; e. g. क:श्चित्, कश्चन
a certain one, कोऽपि, केनापि, कयाचन, कर्थापि, ख्यापित, &c.

§ 142. The terminations given above are also added to
interrogative adverbs in the sense of indefiniteness; as कदाचित्
at some time, क्वाचन, कतिचित् some, कोऽसत् somewhere, &c.

        7—Correlative Pronouns.

§ 143. Correlatives are formed by the affixes यत् added
to यद्, तद् and एतद् , and यत् added to इदम् and किम् in the sense
of 'as much as;' and ईश or इश in the sense of 'similar to';
तद्, एतद् and यद् become ता, एता and या respectively before
these, and इदम् and किम् इ and कि before यत् and ई and की
before ईश or इश ; e. g. तावत् ( तःपरिमाणमस्य ), ईयत् ( ईद् परिमाण-
मस्य ), ताइश like that, ईइश, like this, कियद् how much, &c.
