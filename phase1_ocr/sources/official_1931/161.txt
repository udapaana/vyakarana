§ 261-264 ]                    BAHUVRIHI.                    161

§ 261. *The words उरस्, सर्पिस्, डुपानह्, दाधि, मधु, ज्ञालि-ही and धूर्, अनडुह्, पयस्, नौ and लुरुसी when used in the singular, have a क added to them, when they form the latter member of a Bah. comp.; उध्वं (expanded, well-developed) डुरः यस्य उध्वोरस्कः; प्रियसर्पिष्कः 'one fond of ghee', &c. एकः एषाद् यस्य अर्तौ एकाषुर्कः 'having one man only', &c; when धूर् and the words following it are used in the dual and the plural they take क optionally. द्विड्माद् or द्विड्मकः &c.

(a) अर्थ takes the final क, necessarily when preceded by अन् and optionally in any other case; अनर्थकः 'useless'; but अपृथंयर्थं 'meaningless' वच.

§ 262. † A fem. Bah. ending in हन् takes this क necessarily; as बहुरेणीङका नगरी 'a city having many ascetics', बहुवागिमका सभा 'an assembly having many eloquent speakers in it'; but बहुमुणीङको ग्रामः (vide § 263 below), as it is mas.

§ 263. ‡ And as a general rule, when the final word of a Bah. comp. does not undergo any additions or alterations mentioned in the foregoing rules, it optionally takes the affix क. महायशस्कः-महायशाः 'of mighty glory'; but उत्तरपूर्वं, व्याघ्रपाद्, डुगन्धिः &c.

§ 264. But if the last word of a Bah. compound be a fem. noun ending in ई or ऊ not capable of being changed into इय or उव before the vowel case termination, or if it ends in ङ, क is necessarily affixed; ईश्वरः कर्त्र यस्य तद् ईश्वरकर्तृकं जगत्, बहुनदीको देशः, रूपवती वधू यस्य स रूपवद्धूकः, &c.; but सुधी: स्त्री is an exception; बहुस्त्रीकः, सस्त्रीकः &c.
