§ 329. * When a compound has for its second member a word denoting a limb of the body of an animal and having no conjunct consonant preceding its final vowel, the fem. is formed by the addition of आ or ई; अबिकेशा-केशी; ' a women having abundant hair,' इडुकेशा-केशी, चत्रुखसा-खौ; but सुघुरुका having beautiful ankles; सस्तनी or सस्तना ( स्त्रो वा प्रतिमा वाः ) ससुखा शाला ' an edifice with a beautiful front '.

( a ) † If the word, denoting the limb, however, be one of the words हौड, नख खुर, उखा, शीर्षा, बाल, शाफ, शुञ्ज, खुञ्ज, कर &c. or have more than two syllables, the fem. is formed in आ alone; कृष्णाण्कोड़ा ( अश्वानाहुः क्षोड़ा Sid. Kau. ), पृथुजघना, having large hips; चतुलनयना, &c.

( b ) ‡ Also when the first member is one of स, सद्‌. or विद्य-मान, the fem. is formed in आ necessarily; सकेशा, अकेशा, विद्य-मानाकिका, सह्नाकिका &c.
