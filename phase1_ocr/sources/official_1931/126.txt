Exception*.—But a compound is allowed in the case of the words यज्ञक one who employs as sacrificing priests, पूजक, परि-ञ्चारक, परिदेषक a waiter at meals, स्नापक a servant who bathes his master or brings bathing water for him, अध्यापक, उत्सादक one who destroys, हेतु, भर्तृ, when it does not mean 'a holder,' and others not very important: त्राक्षणयाजकः, देवपूजकः; राजपरि-चारकः &c.; अग्निहोता, स्योर्ता, &c. but वृत्रस्य भर्ता 'the thunder bolt-bearer'.

(b)† A specific Genitive (i. e from which a singling out is made) is not compounded with any other words, as वृणां द्विजः भेङः

(c)‡ A noun in the Genitive is not compounded with ordinal numbers, words denoting qualities, words implying satisfaction present participles, past participles meaning 'honoured or approved of' or showing the place of the act, verbal indeclinables and verbal nouns ending in तव; e. g. सर्वं पञ्चः त्राह्मणस्य झुङ्ञः (रन्तः); कांस्य काणर्व; फलानां सुहितः 'satisfied with fruits' (an Inst. Tat. is, of course, allowed in these cases); हितस्य कुर्वेँ कुर्णो वा किकुरः; सर्वं मत: esteemed by the good; राज्ञौं पुजितः इदर्ना आसितं (place of sitting) गर्तं सुक्तं वा; ब्राह्मणस्य छत्वा, नरस्य कर्तव्यम् &c.

‡ पुरायणाङुरहिताप्रस्सदव्यसमाना घेकरणेन । केन च पूजायां अभि-करणवाचिना च । Pān. II. 2. 11-13. The prohibition with respect to a word expressive of a quality (गुण) is not obligatory, remarks Dikshita; as Pān. himself uses such compounds as संत्राग्यमणाब &c. Hence compounds like अर्थेगौरवम्, झुद्धिमान्चम्, &c. are justified (अनिलोगे यणेन निषेध. तदृषेष्यं संत्राग्यमाणत्वादिदयादिनेदर्शात् । तेनार्थेगौरवं झुद्धिमान्यसेत्यादि सिद्धम् ।).
