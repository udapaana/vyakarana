Bhaṭṭi II. 7). नीरुद्‌; अभीरुद्‌, कुनीपद् overpowering an assailant परीतद्‌. But परिषणपद्‌ as नह् is not followed by Kuip.

(b) When followed by the aff. वल, and the whole is a name;-हुर्वीवलः: a husbandman.

( c ) Words containing more than two vowels when followed by मद् ( वद् ) except in the case of अजिर, सदिर, इठिन, ईश, कारण्ड and चकवाक and the whole is a name; अमरावती and इरावती which are names; but अजिरवती; झोतिषती, वलयवती which is not a name. Also in the case of शर्, वेश, धूम, अहि, केति, धुनि, धुस्ति, and हुव्; शरावती &c.

(d) When a verbal derivative with the aff. अ ( घञ् ) follows a preposition, in most cases, when the comp does not signify a human being, परिपाद्‌:-परीपाद्‌:; but निषादः: one of a mountain-tribe. Similarly प्रति-नी-वेश:; प्रति-नी-कार: &c.

(e) When काश follows a preposition ending in इ‌; वीकाश:, नीकाश:; but प्रकाशः

( f ) When अश्व is the first, and नर् the second member of a compound, and the whole is a name; अश्वाषम् gold, अश्वाषद्‌: a spider; but अश्वपद्‌:, विस्वातर: an epithet of Savitṛi.

(g) When स्मित्र is the latter member and the whole is the name of a rishi; विश्वामित्र:, but विश्वमित्रो माणवकः ।
