§ 733.  The roots छु and  दु  take  both  क्यप्  and  एयत् ; छुय्य,

§ 734. यञ्च् in the sense of ' to  be harnessed ' takes the  affix
क्यप् and changes its final to री ; पुयुय: गो: ' a bull to be harnessed
to the yoke;' in other sense it takes the affix त्; पुङो.

§ 735.  Roots ending in ऋ, and those ending  in  a  consonant
take the affix घ्यत् (य) in the same sense  as क्यद् . Before this  affix
the ending चु and व of a root are changed to वृ and  यु  respecti-
vely and the  final vowel and  the penultimate अ take Vṛiddhi
substitute; any other penultimate vowel generally takes  Guṇa.

छु—कार्युं 'what ought to be done,'  पू—पार्युं ' what ought to be
worn,' दा-देय: हृ-हार्युं; पद्-दुरु-पार्यू: ' what is to be thought of,' &c.;
वत्-वाच्युं ' what is meant in a sentence, ' पच्—पाच्युं  'what is  to
be cooked,' वुज्—भोज्युं ' what is t₫ be purified,' &c.
