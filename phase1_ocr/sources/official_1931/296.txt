    * The following couplet gives the endings of these roots and their number in each:—

       फरच्चजतृ दृपङ्गाचू गम्भाः नुमताः क्रमात् ।
       द्वेत्रीदशार्कपञ्चेरेको" गयलोदिशदुसतूनं ॥

The couplet gives the ending consonants  and thus enables the students to know at once which root is Set  and  which is Anit; e. g. कुट् may be at once known to be a set root  as  ट् does not occur in the first line.   The second line  gives  the  number of roots ending in each consonant. Thus  roots  ending  in  य in कुट् are कु i. e. one ( कु being the first  consonant ) in  number; roots ending in च are च i. e. 5, ( च being the 5th consonant ); roots ending in त are त i. e. one, in number, and so on.
