§ 326. † If prātipadika end in बन्‌ it changes its न to द् before ई; शक्वन्‌-शाकरी the strong one; पीवन्‌ पीवरी, शुबेन्‌-शुबेरो the night, इन्स्यन्नतिक्रान्ता अतिरुखरी, आतिथेवरी, &c.

Exception.—‡ But if a base end in बन्‌ applied to roots ending in a soft consonant; or if a compound has such a base for its latter member, the fem. is formed by adding आ only, before which the preceding अन्‌ is dropped, e. g. अवावन्‌+आ-अवाना a Brāhmaṇa woman ( ब्राहणी ) or a female thief; राजयुष्मा.

§ 327. § A Bahuvrīhi ending in अन्‌ forms its fem. in आ optionally; as इष्‌+पश्वन्‌=इषपश्वन्‌-ङी, बहु +यश्वन्‌ ज्चा, &c. but if the word ending in अन्‌ be such as drops its अ before the आ of the Inst, sing. it takes ई also optionally; e. g. बहु+राजन्‌—बहुराजन्‌-राजा-राज्ञी &c.

( a ) × If a Bah. ends in बन्‌, the न is optionally changed to द्‌; as बहुपीवन्‌-बहुपीवा or बहुपीवरो a town in which there are many fishermen.
