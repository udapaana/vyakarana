§ 391.  Roots of the tenth or चुरादि class* add अय before the personal terminations.  Before अय ( I ) the penultimate short vowel ( except अ ) takes the Guṇa substitute; and the final vowel and the penultimate अ not prosodially long, take their Vṛddhi substitute; e. g. चुर्+ति=चुर्+अय+ति=चोर्+अय+ति=चोरयति; धू+अय+ति=धो+अय+ति=धाव+अय+ति=धावयति; ताड्+अय+ति=ताड्+अय +ति=ताडयति; but दण्ड्+अय+ति=दण्डयति, &c.

§ 392.  ( a )† The preceding अ is lengthened before a conjugational termination beginning with a semivowel, a nasal or म् or य; e.  g. नयामि &c.

    ( b )  The final अ is dropped before terminations beginning with अ; नय+अन्ति=नयन्ति, &c.
