        ( b ) THE ADVERBIAL INDECLINABLE PARTICIPLE

                            OR

                    The Gerund in अम् .

§ 761.  There is another  Indeclinable Participle having the
same sense as that in स्या,  formed  with  the affix अम् .  Before
this the vowel of the derivative verbal  base  generally  undergoes
the same changes as it does before the इु of the  Pass.  Aorist
3rd sing.; नी-नयाम् 'having  carried  or  led,'  दा-दायाम्  'having
given,' यू-भावाम्, पिपु-भेदाम् , मह्-भाह्राम् , गम्-गमाम् , &c.

§ 762.  This Participle is generally used at the  end  of com-
pounds; य कोष्चारं हत: 'he was pelted to  death,'  बुन्दुयाहै  वुहीत
( Vīks. I. 1. ) She was taken a captive;' प्रुक्षायुं प्रसुनमाम ' having
( Bhaț̤ṭi. I. 2. ) 'He totally  exterminated his enemies, &c.'

§ 763.  The participles in स्या and  अम् * when repeated yield
the sense of repetition of the action  or  condition  expressed by
the verb; स्हृत्वा स्हृत्वा or स्मृत स्मारुयुं  'having  repeatedly remem-
bered; पीत्वा पीत्वा or पाय पायाम्  'having drunk  again  and again;'
similarly अच्युत्वाच्युत्वा or अचुयचुयाम् or भीजुत्पोजुत्य or  लोयुलयुत्यमनैत्, छुमुयु-छुमुयुम
आवुद्धु , गष्य-गन्त्वा  गन्त्वा or  गात गामुमु or गत गमुम् , छुत्युस-छुतुयुस्त्वा छुतुयुस्त्वा
or द्युमुं छुर्युमुं or छुमुं छुमुयुं , पुठुनुं पुठुनुम् , जाग्-जागुरं जागरुम् , † &c.
