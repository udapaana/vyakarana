                    CONJUGATION OF VERBS.

§ 378. There are in Sanskrit—

  ( a ) Two kinds of verbs, Primitive and Derivative.

  ( b ) Six Tenses ( काल: ) and four Moods ( अर्थ: ) which are as follow:—

TENSES   TECHNICAL NAME. MOODS TECHNICAL NAME.

अनद्यतनभूत: or Imperfect   लङ्     आशीः: or Benedictive आशीर्लिङ्
परोक्षभूत: or Perfect      लिट्    सत्ता  or Conditional लृट्
अनद्यतन-}
भविष्यत्  } or 1 st Future लृट्
भविष्यत् or 2 nd Future   लुट्

    हेट् or the Subjunctive is used only in the Veda and is therefore termed 'The Vedic Subjunctive.'

    Note:—The ten tenses and moods are technically called the ten Lakaras in Sanskrit grammars.

  ( c ) Three Voices ( प्रयोग: ), the Active Voice ( कर्तरिप्रयोग ) as सुतः, the Passive Voice ( कर्मणिप्रयोग ), as सूयते पक्ति यज-भक्त:, and the Impersonal Construction ( भावेप्रयोग ) तेषां गम्यते.

    * These technical names are given in the following Kārikā,

       लट् वर्तमाने लेट् वेदे भूते लुङ् लङ् लिटस्तथा ।
       विध्याशिषोर्लिङ् लिङ्लोटौ लुट् लृट् लृट् च भविष्यति ॥

    This terminology of Pāṇini, it will be seen, is artificial. Other grammarians use different names   The ten Lakaras of Pāṇini are according to their nomenclature as follow.—वर्तति ( हतिः: ), अवर्ततीत्, हत्ततीत्, परोक्षा, भविष्यती, भविष्यत्ती, पञ्चमी, ससर्गी, कियातिरूपि and आशीः:—
