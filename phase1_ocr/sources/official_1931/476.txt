क—(उरस्)—is added to हस्, पत्, पद्, स्था, घ्रा, त्वष्, हन्, कम्, गम्-
and द्रु and denotes the agent; शुस्—हास्क: sporting, glittering
पादुक: falling  घ्रा—भ्राजुक: happening. living; हन्—घातुक:
कम्-कामुक: amorous.

ट—(इच्युट्)—is affixed to चिद्, मिद्, and छिद्; चिदुट: who knows,
knowing; पिबेद्युट: breaking; बेध्युट्: cutting,' &c.

य—(स्य) is added to जाग् and the Pre. Bases of भज, जन
and दंश; जागर्म्युक ' watchful,' &c. ( vide Bhatti. II. 22, Rag.
XIV, 85. Sis. XX 36 ); पुत: पुन: अतिदेवेन ना यज्नसत्रिठः।
मायजुकै: ' one who performs sacrifices frequently' (see Bhatti.
II. 22. ); पुत पुन: अतिदेवेन ना जनतीति जनजुक: ' one who
makes to be born repeatedly, an ascetic;' पुन: पुन: भङ्तिदेवेन ना
दशतीति देवचुकः ' biting frequently, a serpent, demon ' ( vide
Bhatti I. 26 ).

क्न, किन् and विन—Derivatives are formed with these affixes
which are added to roots and then dropped; the difference
between the first two is that in the case of the latter
before the dropped क् and the root. इ, ए ends in a
short vowel.

क्त—is affixed to स्पृश preceded by a subanta; स्पृशस्पृश 'one who
touches ghee; मन्त्रस्पृश ' one who touches anything after recit-
ing a holy verse,' &c. except when the subanta means water:
उदकस्पर्श: ' one who touches water ' and not उदकस्पृश. The
following words are to be accepted as they are आग्रक्त,
(कर्तो कर्तो यज्ने) sacrificing regularly at every season;
a priest who officiates at a sacrifice; घुस्—दुघुष्—proud,
haughty; कुद्—कुद् a garland: दिश्—दिशा a direction; विद्—
हरिणु: name of a metre, words like चाट्वीत् derived from the
roots अञ्च् and the words पुच् and भ्रश्च ought to be considered
as derived by means of this affix.
