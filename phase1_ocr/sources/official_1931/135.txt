(a) *The qualified word is placed first and is always of the mas. gender if it denotes a class or species, when compounded with certain adjectives, e. g. इषोयूषत्तिः 'a young female elephant', आङ्ऱस्तौकः 'a small fire', उदुम्बरकतिपयम् 'butter-milk mixed with a little water', गोग्रात्िः 'a cow that has brought forth a calf for the first time', गोधेनुः 'a milch-cow (नवप्रसूतिका)', गोवंशा 'a barren cow', गोविवद् (गर्भेघातिनी गौः), गोवत्सपुर्णी 'a cow whose calf is one year old,' कट्यभौजिचः 'an agnihotrin Brāhmana of the Katha branch of the Yajurveda,' कट्याध्यापकः 'a teacher of the Katha branch,' † गौ-मताडिका-मन्चविका-प्रकुण्डं (also mas. according to some)—उद्रः (गवौद्र)-तद्रज 'an excellent cow'. The last mentioned five words, all of which denote excellence, retain their own gender invariably; as ब्राह्मणमतब्राडिका 'an eminent Brāhmana' &c.; but कुमारी मतडिका, as no class is meant here.

(b) ‡ The words कहार, सत्रो, खौड lame, काण, कुण्ड blunt, dull, स्वलति bald-headed, गीर, वद्ध, भिक्षुक भीग, भिंगल, तञ्ज, जरद् hard, stiff, बधिर, कुञ्ज and बर्बर, may optionally precede the words in apposition to them; as जैसमिनककहारः or कहारजैसमिनः Jaimini who is turned tawny on account of his having practised penance in the sun.' &c.
