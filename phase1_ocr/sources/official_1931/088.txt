                Bases ending in ष् ॥

§ 114. ( a ) उषस् ( dawn, light ) is one of the few bases
which have ष् for its final ; this word is one of those substan-
tives which possess only the singular number ( वचनान्तर इत्यु-
दयनः, Pan. I. 2. 25 Vart. ). There are other feminine bases end-
ing in ष् which are derived from the roots दुह् &c., viz. ( a )
