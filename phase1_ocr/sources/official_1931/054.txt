§ 62. Decline all nouns ending in अ mas. and neu. similarly.

(a) The Loc. sing. of words ending in अह् ( substituted for अहन् a day, at the end of a Tat. comp. ) is optionally formed like that of nouns ending in न्; e. g. ऽयह् has ऽयह्नि, ऽयनि or ऽयहनि. So ऽयह्नि, ऽयह्नि or ऽयहनि &c. See p. 73.

                    Nouns ending in आ Mas and Fem.

§ 63.  गोपा      mas.  a  cow-herd.

(a) Mas. nouns ending in आ take the general terminations. The final आ is dropped before a vowel termination beginning with the Acc. plu.

* The न् of इन् is changed to ण by § 41. Ins. sing of जन् is जनेन
