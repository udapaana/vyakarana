                        SECTION II.

                BASES ENDING IN CONSONANTS.

§ 87. Bases ending in any of the first four letters of a class and in थ्, र्, ष्, श्, ष्, प्, स् and ह्. The declension of these bases consists, for the most part, in adding the general case termi nations and observing the rules of sandhi.

§ 88. Bases ending र्, ल् , and ण् .

§ 89. (a) After final ल् the इ of the loc. is changed to उ.

  (b)  इ ( optionally changeable to इ ) may be optionally inserted between ण and सु.
