                Bases ending in शी ॥

§ 120. शी is a suffix which is added to roots and crude
nominal bases to form nouns and adjectives. ( द्वी० V. 4. 60, 65,
66 ; Pan. V. 2. 115, 118, 119 &c. ). Thus we have प्रजा-ईशी = प्रजा-
ईश = nom. प्रजेशः ( derived form praja; a lord of creatures );
काङ्क्षी ( craving for = काङ्क्षा-ईशी, derived from काङ्क्षा ); लोहित्यशी
( being desirous of लोहित्या ). Before the case-affixes शी becomes
इन्, thus काङ्क्षिन्; and in the fem., न् of शीन् being dropped, शी takes
the fem. affix ङीष्, ( वा० III. 2. 7 ; Pan. IV. 1. 6 ) and is declin-
ed after the analogy of नदी. Thus काङ्क्षी ( fem. ), काङ्क्षिणी,
काङ्क्षिणम् &c. The neuter forms are काङ्क्षि ( nom. voc. and acc.
sing. ), काङ्क्षीनि ( nom. voc. and acc. pl. ) &c. This declension
is similar to that of शी ( the pos. suf. of the mas. pres. part.
active in the शी conjugation ), शीन् making its nom. इ; and
will be subsequently explained along with the part. of this con-
jugation.
