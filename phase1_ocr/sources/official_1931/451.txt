§ 737.  ( a ) The roots  यञ्च् ,  याच् ,  रुच् ,  प्रवुच् , स्पृश् ,  and एच्
do not change  their  चु  or  ऋ  to  ऋ  or  यु  before  घ्यत् ;  शाय्युं
यात्युं; रोच्यं , प्रवत्युयुं ( प्रत्यशितोर्य: ), ज्ञेस्युं , स्पश्युं , गात्युं.

( b ) इन्धु does not change  its  चु  to  ऋ  before  घ्यत् when the
meaning is ' what ought  to  be spoken out,  speech, '  वाच्युं; but
वाक्युं; a sentence.'

( c ) पञ्च् when it means 'to go' does not change चु its to
ऋ :  पञ्चुर्युं ;  when it  means  ' to bend'  the चु  is  changed  to
ऋ :  पञ्कुवुं शरीरुम्

( d ) सू युज् when it is preceded by इु  and  हि,  takes  the affix एयत्
in  the sense of  'what  is  possible or capable of;'  and  does
not  change  its  ऋ  to  यु ;  प्रयुङ्कुर्   शक्युं  प्रयोज्युं;  तिवौर्ङुकुर्   शक्युं:
तिवौज्युं: शुङुय: !

( e ) भुज् has  भोजुं  meaning  ' food, '  and  भोज्युं  'what is fit
to be enjoyed.'

§ 738.  Roots ending in उ, short or long,  take the affix एलिम्
in the sense of  'what ought  or  must necessarily be done;'
छु—छातुं  'what  must  necessarily  be  cut  off,'  पाक्षु  'what
must  necessarily  be  cooked,'  दा to  be  with  आ—आत्तुम् ;  इु  ' to
mix,' यात्, &c.

( a ) The roots वत् ,  र्तु ,  छुयु ,  भृत्  and  वत्  also  do the same,
वाच्युं  'what must necessarily be on;' प्रवत्तुं  'what must be
spoken of distinctly;' छुत्युत् , भात्युत् , चात्युत् .

§ 739.  The  following  words  are  irregularly  derived  by
means of the affix घ्यत् ; आधातुरु: ' what ought  to be brought from
the Gārhapatya  i.  e.  the Dakshinā-gni;' ( fr.  नी  with  आ  )
विदिणामायिनौगै दुत्तुवुदु । य हि गाहुपुत्युयानुपात्रोउक्षिनौस्यु तत्सुतुप्रुत्र्यतोगाम् ।
( Sid.  Kau.  );  but  वायुं  in  other  senses  ' to  be  led,  &c.  ' नोपुनुमार्युं
( fr. नी with यु ) चोरु: यीनोवनुहै  डुर्यथुर्यै: ( Sid. Kau  );  ' disgusted
with  worldly  pleasures;'  as प्रणायुग्नैवोचारो  विरक्तो  डुर्युथुर्यै: । but
प्रणेयुं in other cases.
