§ 205. A noun in the Instrumental may be compounded—

(a) × with another expressive of an effect produced by what is denoted by the noun in the Instrumental case, and with अर्थ; as शङ्कुलेन सपाड. शङ्कुशालिषपडः severed by means of a knife; धान्येन अर्थः धान्यार्थः wealth obtained by means of grain.

(b) - with verbal derivatives when the Instrumental has thes ense of the agent or the instrument or means of the action; as हरिणा त्रातः हरित्रातः saved by Hari; नखैर्भिन्नः नखभिन्नः torn with the nails &c
