( 3 ) Neuter दिश् adds मुट् before the case-affixes of the
nom., voc., and acc. sing. ( पूर्वमुडगमः, Pan. VII. 1. 98 ), and thus
we have दिम् + क् । = दिम्क् or दिमुक्, but it is not in use in the सं०
॥ It is customary to use in this sense, the neuter उदक् ( 'water',
Lat. "udo") derived from the root उन्द् by the affix क्विप् ( उद्गति
उदक्, Pan. III. 2. 61 ). Before the case-affixes beginning with
a consonant this base becomes उदग्, and before case-affixes
beginning with non-nasal mutes, it becomes उदग् ( उदग्भिः or
उदङ्भिः, उदग्भ्यः or उदङ्भ्यः, उदक्षु ) By the special rule उदपस्सु ततो
लोपः सकारस्य च ( Pan. VIII. 3. 7 Vart. ) the स्-विसर्ग in the nom. pl.
is dropped, and we have उदञ्च्, उदञ् च or उदगि ( nom. plu. = northern
quarters). Similarly the acc. pl. is उदचः or उदङ्, &c.
