§ 654   The suffix य ( णयर् ) is added to a few nouns such as शूर
'male,' भृत् 'slow,' भूतिहं 'learned,' शुयस् 'famous,' in the sense of
उत्मनेष् 'agitated,' &c. in the sense of ' becoming what it was not
before,' or ' what it was not like to before,' शूर: भवति यूषायते 'what
was not mrch now becomes much,' उत्मनायति ' he ( who was not
agitated before ) becomes agitated,' similarly शुयनायते &c.
