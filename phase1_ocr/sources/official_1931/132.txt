2. Nañ or the Negative Tatpurusha.

§ 218. (a) The negative particle न changed to अ before a consonant and अन् before a vowel, may be compounded with any poun to form the Negative-Tatpurusha†; as न ब्राह्मणः अब्राह्मणः, one not a Brāhmana; न अश्वः अनश्वः, असद्, non-existent, or not good, &c.

§ 219. The न is not changed to अ or अन् in the following cases‡:-नभ्रादू 'that which does not shine,' 'a cloud;' नपादू(pre. p.)
