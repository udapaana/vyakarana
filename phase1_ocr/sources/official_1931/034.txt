(f) When* a preposition ending in अ is followed by a root having an initial ऋ; as उप+ऋच्छति=उपार्च्छति; प्राछति; but if the root be a denominative, the Vriddhi is optional; प्र+ऋषति=प्रर्षीयति or प्रार्षीयति acts like a bull; but उप+ऋकारीयति=उपकारीयति, as the ऋ is long; also प्र-लृकारीयति=प्रा-प्रल्कारीयति very much wishes for ल lkára; for ल is grammatically considered to be similar to ऋ.

§21. When अ or आ is followed by ए or ऐ and ओ or औ ऐ and औ are respectively substituted for both†; छृष्ण+एकत्वं=छृष्णैकत्वम् the majesty of god; सा+एवं=सैव she alone; भव+औषधं=भवौषधम् medicine against birth and re-birth; विद्या+औसुक्यं=विद्यौसुक्यम् longing for knowledge.

Exceptions:—

(a) । If a preposition ending in अ be followed by a root /beginning with ए or औ, ए or औ is substituted; प्र+एजते=प्रैजते quakes very much; उप+ओषति=उपौषति burns something near; but if the root be a denominative. vrddhi is optional; उप+एङ्कीयति=उपैङ्कीयति or उपेङ्कीयति; प्र+औघीयति=प्रौघीयति or प्रौघीयति.
