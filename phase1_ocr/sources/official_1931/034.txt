III Words ending in उ :—(a) Masculine, feminine, and neuter; m. गुरु a teacher, f. धेनु a milch-cow, n. मधु honey; in this class also adjectives end in उ; e.g. लघु light.

(b) Feminine words ending in ऊ do not differ in inflection from those ending in short उ except that they have ऊ in the Nom. Acc. and Voc. Sing.; e.g. वधू a bride (f.) and so गुरु (f.)

IV Words ending in ऋ :—(a) Masculine words such as पितृ a father, ending in ऋ may be divided into two classes; (1) those which are not derivatives and (2) those which are derivatives. The Nom. Dual and Plural of both classes end in आ. The former make their Nom. sing. in आ; as पिता a father: but the latter in आ or आ at will; e.g. भर्ता or भर्तः a husband, दाता or दातः a giver.

(b) Masculine words ending in तृ (properly derivatives formed by means of the affixes तृ, तृच् and क्तृ such as भर्तृ a master, वक्तृ a speaker and कर्तृ one who has made, may make their Nom. Pl. in रः with the tृ changed to री, or in रः with the medial ऋ changed to आ; e.g. वक्तारः or वक्तृः speakers.

V Words ending in ऋ;—Masculine; e.g. होतृ an invoker of the gods (root हु to sacrifice); they are derivatives.

VI. Feminine nouns ending in आ decline like नरः a man or like पितृ a father except in the Nom. Acc. Voc. Sing., where they take their own peculiar case-endings; माता mother, स्वसा a sister, दुहितृ a daughter.

VII Feminine nouns ending in ई take their inflectional ending in the same way as those ending in short इ; and those ending in ऊ in the same way as those ending in short उ; e.g. यातृ wife of the husband's brother, स्वश्रू husband's mother. कृऊ is an exception as it declines both as a feminine and a neuter: अमावास्या the night of the new moon declines like Dhenu; तनू the body and ऊर्वी the earth like पृथ्वी.
