हविःषु or हविषु ॥ The neuter nom., voc., and acc. pl. forms take
the augment सुट् before the case-affix स् ( न: सुटि, Pan. VI. 1. 110)
and the new combination gives rise to the form मनांसि and
तेजांसि ॥
