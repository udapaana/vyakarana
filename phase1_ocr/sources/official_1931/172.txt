172                                     संस्कृतव्याकरणम्

§ 303. न च संख्यापूर्वात् समासादेः ।

Nor does वृद्धि occur before a समास (compound) beginning with a numeral.

Example:— यथात्रिंशत् 'according to thirty' (not यथैत्रिंशत्).

§ 304. एकाचो द्वे प्रथमस्य ।

When the first member has only one vowel, it is doubled in certain Avyayībhāva formations.

Example:— सद्दः becomes सद्दद्दः in reduplicated forms.

Obs. This refers to reduplication rules specific to monosyllabic first members.

§ 305. अव्ययीभावे च ।

And this doubling occurs in Avyayībhāva compounds.

Example:— यथायथा 'according to each', सर्वसर्व 'every single one'.

§ 306. अचि र ऋतः ।

Before a vowel, ऋ becomes र.

Example:— This is a general sandhi rule applicable to Avyayībhāva compounds when ऋ of the first member is followed by a vowel in the second member.

§ 307. हलि च ।

And before a consonant also, ऋ may become र.

Example:— पुनः+कर्म = पुनर्करोति or पुनःकरोति.

Obs. This optional sandhi rule is mentioned for completeness in the context of Avyayībhāva formation.
