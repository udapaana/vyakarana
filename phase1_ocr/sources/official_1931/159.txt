§ 229-231 ]                    DWIGU COMPOUNDS.                    141

§ 229. In some Karmadhāraya compounds the last member of the first word which is itself a compound word is dropped*; e. g. शाकप्रियः पाण्डवः शाकपाण्डवः 'a king who likes vegetables;, देवपुत्रको ब्राह्मणः देवब्राह्मणः &c These compounds, though they should be properly called "Uttamapadalopl" are popularly designated "Madhyamapadalopl". Any compound requiring a similar explanation is classed under this group (शाकपाधिवादि) which is an आकलितगण.

DWIGU OR NUMERAL APPOSITIONAL COMPOUNDS.

§ 230. † When the first member of a Karmadhāraya com pound is a numeral it is called 'Dwigu'.

§ 231. A Dwigu compound is possible (a) under the circumstances mentioned in § 223 (a) i. e.—

(1) when a Taddhita affix is to be added to the compound so formed; षण्णां मातृणामपत्यं षाण्मातुरः 'the son of six mothers, Kārtikeya' (vide Ku. IX); पञ्चकपालः &c; or (2) when the compound itself becomes the first member of another compound, as पञ्चगावः पर्वं यस्यो पञ्चगवघनः; पञ्चनार्धिमयः &c.

(b) And when the compound denotes an aggregate; in this case it is singular; ‡ as त्रयाणां भुवनानां समाहारः त्रिभुवनम् the three worlds (taken collectively); as पञ्चपात्रम्, पञ्चगवम्, &c.

* शाकपाधिवादीनां सिद्धं उत्तरपदलोपस्योयसंख्यानम् । Vārt on Pān. II. 1. 60.
† संख्यापूर्वो द्विगुः । Pān. II. 1. 52.
‡ द्विगुरेकवचनम् । Pān. II. 4. I.
