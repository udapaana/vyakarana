§ 203.  The first variety consists of such Tatpurusha com-
pounds as have the attributive member in any one of the
oblique cases when dissolved.  There are six sub-varieties of
this corresponding to the six oblique cases

§ 204.  (a)  A noun in the Accusative case is compounded
with प्रित, अतीत, पतित, गत, अत्यक्त, प्राप्त. आपन्न, गमी, सुत्रुसु, and
others †; as कृष्णं प्रितः कृष्णाप्रितः one who has resorted to Kṛishṇa
( as his refuge ); दुःसमर्तीतः दुःस्वातीतः one who has overcome pain;
हुसं आपन्नः सुसापन्नः one who has obtained happiness. प्रामं गमी
प्रामगमी going to a village, a passenger; अन्नं इच्छुसुः अन्नेच्छुसुः अन्नेच्छुसुः &c.

N. B.   प्राप्त and आपन्न may also be placed first; as प्रासो जीविकां
प्राप्तजीविकः or जीविकाप्राप्तः one who has obtained livelihood ;
similarly आपन्नजीविकः or जीविकाप्राप्तः ; प्राप्तजीविका स्त्री, &c.
