In their usual senses they have हुसित,  स्पसित,  छगित, म्हेश्छित,

§ 700.  दा 'to give,' and दे,  form their P. P. Ptc. as दत्त;  the
व of दत्त is dropped  after a preposition ending in a vowel ; वर्-
वस्त; but in this latter case the preceding इ or उ is lengthened ;
नीत्, दत्त, &c.; इ may optionally be retained also ; मदत्, अवदत्,
डुदत्,* &c.
