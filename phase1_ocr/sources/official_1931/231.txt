                        I MASCULINE WORDS.

§ 345.        Verbal derivatives formed by the affixes अर् and आ,
and ई added to the roots दा and घा; e. g. पाकः, व्यापः, करः, मरः
any beverage or drink, poison, गोप्तर्: range, scope, यन्ता, विष:,
आधिः mental pain or anguish, निधिः a treasure, &c.

    Exceptions:—यास्था fem. and भव, लिङ्ग, and भग all neu.

§ 346. Words ending in ड and those having ऋ, इ, द, प, द,
स, ष, श, र, or स, for their penultimate; as प्रशु, भाद्य, इशु,
स्तवकः ' a bunch of flowers ' &c, घट:, पाषाण:, शोथ: ' a swelling,
फेनः, दीपः, स्तम्भः, रोम:, समयः, क्षुरः a razor,' अङ्कुर:, द्र:, वायस: &c.

    Exceptions:—Words ending in—

( a ) ड—घेडु, रज्जु ( except when it ends a compound, in which
        case it is both mas. and fem. ) डुड—द ' the last day of a
        lunar month on which the moon is invisible,' सरयु ' name
        of a river,' तदु, करेणु and विषेयु ' a kind of creeper,' which
        are all fem.; इमशु, जाडु, वडु ' wealth,' अश्रु, जतु ' lac,'
        ईय ' tin or lead', ताडु, दारु, मधु ' honey,' स्वादु ' relish',
        वस्तु and मस्तु ' sour cream ', which are all neuter.

( b ) क—चिबुक ' the chin', इत्लुक, प्रातिषद्धिक, अंशुक ' a garment,'
        उल्सक ' a fire brand.'

( c ) इ and ण—किरीट, इङुट, लङाट, गुग्गाट ' a place where four
        roads meet,' ज्ञण, लवण, पर्ण, उब्ध.

( d ) थ and न—काष्ठ, पृष्ठ, रिक्थ property left at death,' उत्कथ ' a
