§ 706  P. P. Participles have sometimes  the sense of  neuter
abstract nouns; as जातिसं 'speech', शासिसं 'sleep', ह्सित 'laughing,'
similarly स्थित, गत, etc.; See Bhaṭṭ  VII. 125.

§ 707.  The P. P. Participles of roots meaning 'to think or to
wish,' 'to know,' ' to adore,' and of the roots मून्, भा  etc.  have
the sense of the present Tense; राञुतं मतै:  ' he is honoured by the
king;' सत्तं इत्युनै: द्युप् पदं the root is kindled ;' so म्रि: etc

            (d) Past Active Participles.

§ 708  Past Active participles are derived  from  the  P.
Passive Participle in त् or न by changing it to them the affix वत्; e.g.

Roots.          P. P. Ptc.      P. Ac. Ptc.

भू  to be            भूत           भूतवत् one or that has been
कृ  to do            कृत           कृतवत् „     „      done
कू  to scatter       कीर्ण          कीर्णवत् „     „      scattered
छिद् to cut          छिन्न          छिन्नवत् „     „      cut
etc.                etc.           etc.                etc.

        ( e ) Participle of the Future Tense.

§ 709.  The Participle of the Simple Future,  Active as well
as Passive, is formed from the  third  person  singular form of
the Second Future  of  a  root.   The Parasm.   Ptc.  is formed
simply by dropping the final इ, and the Atm.  and  the Passive
one by substituting मान for the final इ; e. g.

Roots.  Par.           Atm.                  Passive.

भू—  भविष्यत्         भविष्यमाण           भविष्यमाण or भाविष्यमाण
