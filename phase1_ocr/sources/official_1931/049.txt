                Nouns ending in ऋ mas. fem. and neu.

§ 81. Nouns derived from roots with the affix तृ ( तृच् and तृन् Pán. III. 1. 133, and 2, 135 ), such as कर्तृ a maker, &c. and the words स्वसृ f; नसृ, नेदृ, क्षेतृ, होतृ, and प्रशासृ as also उद्रातृ change their ऋ to अर् in the Nom. sing. and to आर् in the first five inflections. The ऋ is lengthened in the Acc. and Gen. pl. and changed to उर् before the Ab and Gen. sing; the Voc sing. ends in अर्.
