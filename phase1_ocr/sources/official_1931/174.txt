§288. (a) उदक† becomes उद necessarily (1) when it forms a proper name, also when it is final; or (2) when followed by the words पूर्‌, वास, वाहन, and धि: उदमेघ: 'a particular cloud filled with water'; उदधि:, क्षीरोद: the sea of milk; उड्गणोद: &c.; उदवर्षं तिष्ठति, उदवास: standing in water; उदवाहन; उदधि: 'a vessel for holding water' घट:, सम्यदे तु पूर्णेण सिद्धम् Sid. Kau.;

(b) and optionally when followed by a word beginning with a simple consonant and denoting 'a vessel to be filled up with water', and by मन्थ, औदन, सक्तु, विन्दु, वज्र, मार, हार, शीर्षप् 'a yoke with a pan at each end for carrying loads' (काष्ठ Maráthi) and गार; उदकुम्भ: or उदकुम्भ; but उदकस्थाली (as स्थाली begins with a conjunct consonant), उदकपर्वत; उदमन्थ्य: or उदकमन्थ्य: barley-water; उदौदन: or उदकौदन: rice boiled with water,
