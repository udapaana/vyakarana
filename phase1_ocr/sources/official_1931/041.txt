II. HALSANDHI, OR THE COALESCENCE OF FINAL CONSONANTS WITH VOWELS _ ND CONSONANTS.

§28 When स् or a consonant of the dental class comes in contact with:—

(a) †च् or a consonant of the palatal class, the corresponding letter of the latter class is substituted for the former, and च for स; हरिन्+शेते=हरिंच्चेते Hari sleeps; रामः+चिनोति=रामश्चि नोति Râma collects; सत्+चित=सच्चित् Reality and knowledge; शार्गिन्+जय=शार्गिञ्जय Be you victorious, Oh Krshna.

Exception:–(1) Dentals coming after ग् are not changed into the corresponding palatals: e. g. विश्वः that which spreads lustre; यश्रः.
