VIII Feminine nouns ending in short इ or उ, when ending in इ or उ in the Nom. Voc. Sing. Dual, and Nom. Acc. Pl. change the इ or उ to ऊ; वेदविद् knowing the Vedas ends in द् in the Nom. Voc. Sing.; वेदवित्. So also चन्द्रवृत् the star Arundhatī; भागीरथी Bhāgīrathī; मही great (f.).

IX Words ending in ऐ and ओ:—This class contains, besides such words as गो an ox, नौ a ship ( both mas. fem and neu. ) such as रै property, ( mas. fem. and neu. ) and such compounds as ग्लै wise, mas.; विश्वानेव all-powerful, mas.

X Words ending in ऋ (a) Masculine and neuter in the form अस्थि a bone, अक्षि the eye.

(b) fem. in the form श्री beauty, धी intellect, भू earth, नी leading ( only in comp ), दुर a beam, द्रु a ladder. Feminines ending in ई or ऊ like श्री &c. change that vowel to इय् or उव् before a vowel; श्रीः a goddess of beauty makes श्रियं; धूः intelligence makes धियम्; भूः earth makes भुवम् ( § 75 ).

§ 55. The case-terminations technically called विभक्ति are as follows:—

Nom. स् or सु for the Singular ( एकवचन ), औ for the Dual ( द्विवचन ), जस् for the Plural (बहुवचन); so अम् for the Acc. Sing., औट्: ( with final ट् dropped ) for the Dual and शस् for the Plural; ट्या for the Inst. Sing., भ्याम् for the Dual, and भिस् for the Plural; डे (with the initial ड dropped) for the Dat. Sing., भ्याम् for the dual and भ्यस् for the Plural; डसि ( = सि ) for the Abl. Sing., भ्याम् for the Dual, and भ्यस् for the Pl; डस् ( ङ or स् ) for the Gen. Sing., ओस् for the Dual and आम् for the plural; डि (=इ ) for the loc. Sing., ओस् for the Dual, and सुप् (=सु ) for the Plural. These case-terminations are again distin-guished in Pāṇinian grammar into सार्वधातुक (like terminations of the Parasmaipada ) and आर्धधातुक ( like terminations of the Ātmanepada ).
