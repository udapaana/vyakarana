§ 753.  Roots of the 8th class ending in a  nasal,  except वत्
and the roots मन् , वन् and हुन् , drop their nasal necessarily; शुक् ,
तन्य ,  यत्य and  रुयु do it  optionally;  तन्  with  वि-विन्तर्य;  भुस् with
प्रति-प्रतिभुष्य; तन्य with वि-विन्तर्य or तिवुत्त्यु; रुय with वि—विरुय्य or
विरुत्य; मत् with यू—प्रणम्य or प्रणत्य, &c

§ 754.  The roots सन् ,  जत्  and  सन्  have  respectively  तिसत्युय-
तिसाय; प्रजन्य-प्रजाय; and  प्रसन्य-प्रसाय.

§ 755.  द्यषु lengthens its इ before the affix य  and  जागृ  changes
its final vowel to Guṇa;  प्रदीय;  प्रजायुं.

§ 756.  ( a ), च्युत्  and  छुयु  do  not  take Samprasāraṇa,  प्रत्युव्य;
उत्युत्य 'having become  old,'  उप्युत्य  'having  covered,'  but ऋ
with परि  or  संत् ,  takes Samprasāraṇa  optionally,  पुरिस्युत्य  or
परिदुर्य; संस्युत्य or संदीर्य.

§ 757.  The roots  mentioned  under  §  486  do  not  change
their आ to इङ; प्रयाय, भयाय , भमाय , &c.

§ 758.  Roots of the Tenth class and  Causals preserve  their
अय before य ; it being  separated by a  short  penultimate vowel;
not, it is dropped;  चोरुय-यचौर्य; बोषुय-यबोष्य; कु—cau. विकारुय;  नी
cau.  with आ—आनाय, &c ;  but  गृण-विगृण्य,  यूण्मुत्य,  प्रकल्युय;
प्रबोविदुर्य ' having again and again caused to be broken.'

§ 759. ( a ) आषु cau.  may  retain  its  अयु  optionally-गाम्य
or गामय.

§ 760.  Desiderative  bases  add  य  immediately  and  Freq.
bases  add  it  after dropping  their  यु,  when it is proceded by a
consonant and dropping the final अ  when  it is preceded  by  a
vowel; बुभु-यबुबुषिष्य; Fre. यबोबुघुय; भू Fre. यबोभुय &c.
