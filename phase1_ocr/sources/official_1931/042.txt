§32. The य् of स्था and स्तम्भ् is changed into य् when after the preposition उड्।;उड्+स्थानम्=उड्+ध्यानम्=उद्ध्यानम् and उड्ध्यानम् (see §20. a), and then उन्थानम्, उर्ध्यानम् rising up; similarly उत्स्भनम् and उर्ध्स्भनम् upholding.

§33 ह coming after any of the first four letters of a class is optionally changed to the soft aspirate (4th letter) of that class‡; वाक्+हरिः:=वाग्हरिः (see b. under§22), and by this वाग्घरिः (वाचां हरिः) the possessor of speech, Bṛhaspati.

§34. Any consonant, except a nasal or a semi-vowel, is changed to the first of its class, when followed by a hard consonant, and to the first or third when followed by nothing; वाक्, वाण्.

§35. ण् preceded by a word ending in any of the first four letters of a class and followed by a vowel, a semi-vowel, a nasal or ह, is optionally changed to ळ;– e.g. तत्+शिवः:=तल्+शिवः; and तड्+छिवः. and then तन्+शिवः, and तत्+छिवः by the above, and finally तंशिवः and तंच्छिवः. that Siva (sec. a. §28.). Similarly तच्छोकेन, तंच्छोकेन; but वाक्श्रोत्तति speech falters (as ण् is not followed by any of the letters mentioned in the rule).
