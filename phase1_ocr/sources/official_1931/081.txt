§ 131. The words ending in अन् or वन्, such as शुन् &c.
( learned ) and the words धनिन् ( rich ) make their ins., dat.,
and abl. dual and pl., gen. dual, nom. voc. acc. dual and pl.,
regularly by the application of the case-terminations to the end
of the base ( i. e., they take the affixes यस्येतुर्थीयुतन्तास्निर्देशः by Pan.
III. 2. 79 ) ; thus शुनौ ( nom. dual ), शुनाम् ( gen. pl. ) शुनि ( loc.
sing ), शुनिभिः ( ins. pl. ) विद्वनीनाम्, धनिनभिः ॥ In the ins. sing. and
in the nom. sing. न् is dropped, as विद्वान्, विद्वना; so also धनी,
धनिना ॥ The gen. voc. and loc. sing. and also the gen. sing.
are शुनिः, शुनिनः ॥ So धनिनः ॥ Before the affixes भिः, भ्यः,
भ्याम्, सु beginning with the consonants, the penultimate न् be-
comes a ण् by the rule नो णः ॥ Thus धनिणभिः, धनिणभ्यः, धनिणसु,
विद्वणभिः, विद्वणभ्यः ॥
