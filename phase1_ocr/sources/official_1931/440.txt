§ 696. ( a ) Roots ending in a nasal lengthen  their  penulti-
mate vowel  before  a  weak  termination  beginning  with  any
consonant except a nasal or a semivowel, or before the affix किन्
चर्-चार्न्, कर्-कार्न्, &c

( b ) Anit roots ending in a nasal,  the root वद् 1. P. and the
eight roots of the  8th  class-तन्  and others ( see p. 355 ) drop
their nasal before a weak  termination  beginning  with  any
consonant except a nasal or a semivowel:—

§ 697.  The roots हुन् , जुन् and सृन् , drop their nasal and at
the same time lengthen their vowel; सुस्, जास्, सास्.

§ 698.  A radical इ preceding or following a vowel is some-
times changed to ऊ before त् or न्;  when it is preceded by य it is
dropped; इङ्-गत, वुय; पुरीत्, पुर्ङ-पूर्ण, तिर:-एप्त, पि:-पूत,
यून ( See § 693, c. ).
