§ 617.  To this स् the augment इ is to be prefixed after Set
roots, optionally after Wet ones and not after those that are
Anit, subject to the following exceptions:—

(1)  Roots ending in इ or ई, ऋ and ॠ short, and the roots
मृज् and मृड् do not take इ; इ-इयिष् Vide § 618. ( 2 ); ऋ-ईर्य, &c.

Exceptions:—ऋ 'to go,' भृ A.  ' to repeat,' भृ 6 A. 'to hold,'
and भृ A. 'to purify,' admit of इ  ( see also 4 below )

(2)  स्मि, अञ्जर्, भञ्ज्, अञ्च् take इ necessarily.

(3)  ईड्, वृप्, वृध्, स्पृह् and कृत्स् do not admit of इ in the
Par.  ( see § 484 )   In the Atm. they admit इ, the last two do
so optionally; दाद्-विदृक्षति, विवर्तिषते, &c.

(4)  Roots ending in long ऋ and ॠ and the roots हरास्, त्रि.
जड्, ड, ष, ढ, स्, हू, कृष् ' to prosper,' कृष्, भर्ज, इङ् ( i. e. गम्, of
the 3rd cl. and optional Cas. Base of ज्ञा ), सृ ' to give,' पन्, पुन्,
टन, चुट्, हुर्, दुर्, and युक् ( Vide § 485. ) take इ optionally.

Exceptions:—कृ 'to scatter,' पृ  'to swallow,' take इ  neces-
sarily The intermediate इ is not lengthened in the case of these
roots; चिकाषिष् , &c.

(5)  The roots कृष्, गृह् and ढृ take इ in the Par. and reject
it in the Atm.

§ 618.  The radical vowels undergo the following changes
before स्—

(a)  The स् with इ is strong and without इ weak.

one case and does not in the other; दुष्-विदूषिष्,  विदुष्:
इ-दिदीषिष्, &c.
