§ 793–795 ]                SYNTAX.                    475

§ 793.  (a) When a pronoun is  used  relatively  with  its
noun,  it  must  agree  with  it  in  gender  and  number;  यः
पुरुषः स गच्छति that man goes, या  स्त्री  सा  गच्छति  that woman
goes, यत् फलं तत् फलम् that  fruit,  &c.

(b) When the nouns are of different genders,  the  pronoun
is put in the masculine  when  they  are  masculine  and  feminine,
and in the neuter when they are  masculine,  feminine  and  neuter.

Concord of the Relative with the Antecedent.

§ 794.  When  a  relative  pronoun  refers  to  an  antecedent,
it must agree with it  in  gender  and  number,  not  in  case:
यः पुरुषः स आगतः that man has  come  ( the  relative  and  the
demonstrative are here in the nominative );  येन  पुरुषेण  स  गतः
that man has gone ( the relative is in the  instr.  while  the  de-
monstrative is in the nominative  );  यं  पुरुषं  तं  पश्यामि  I  see
that man ( the relative is in the accusative  while  the  demons-
trative is in the nominative ).

§ 795.  When the antecedent is  of  the  dual  or  the  plural
number, the correlative  pronoun  in  the  principal  clause  may
be omitted, and  the  verb  put  in  that  number  of  which  the
antecedent is:  ये पुरुषाः तव सुता वा  वस्त्युकुटायुमुगुच्युछुन्ति  or  ये
पुरुषाः तव सुताः ते गुच्छुन्ति  those  men  who  are  your  sons  go
( in the first  sentence  the  correlative  pronoun  ते  is  omitted
and the verb, though  governed  by  an  antecedent  in  the  rela-
tive clause,  is  made  plural ).

                          SECTION II.
                         GOVERNMENT.
                  A.  GOVERNMENT OF CASES.

§ 796.  Government  is  that  influence  which  one  part  of
speech exerts over another, requiring  it  to  be  put  in  a  parti-
cular case.  It is mainly concerned with Cases,  viz.  "the  Govern-
ment of Nouns or of Cases by Verbs  and  Nouns"  and  "Govern-
ment by Prepositions."
