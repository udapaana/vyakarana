§ 279. अव्ययीभावेऽधिकरणे ॥

In the Avyayībhāva the first member of the compound is in the Locative case, when expressing अधिकरण (locus or position).

Examples:— उपकुम्भम् 'near the jar', अधिहरि 'over or regarding Hari', अनुवृक्षम् 'along the tree'.

§ 280. एकवचनम् ।

The first member of an Avyayībhāva compound must be in the Singular number.

Example:— यथाशक्ति 'according to power' (not यथाशक्तयः), अनुरथम् 'after the chariot' (not अनुरथान्).

§ 281. विशेषणञ्च ।

And an adjective (used as the first member) must also be in the Singular.

Example:— यथामहत् 'according to greatness' (not यथामहान्ति).

§ 282. अजातेरुपसर्गो गतिः । गत्यर्थः कर्मप्रवचनीयः ।
उपसर्गः क्रियायोगे ।

Obs. When a root having the sense of 'going' or 'motion' is compounded with an उपसर्ग (preposition), the latter is called गति. A word having the sense of गति is called कर्मप्रवचनीय. And any उपसर्ग used in connection with an action is so termed.

Examples of गति:— प्रविशति 'he enters', अभिधावति 'he runs towards'.

Examples of कर्मप्रवचनीय:— आमुखम् 'up to the face', अधिहरि 'over Hari', परिभूमि 'around the earth'.
