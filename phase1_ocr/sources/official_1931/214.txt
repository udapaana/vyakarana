SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                                        [ § 343.

SECTION III.

AFFIXES FORMING ADVERBS.

अक ( अकच्‌ )—is inserted before the final vowel of indeclinables without any change of meaning ; उच्चैः-उच्चकैः; नीचैः-नीचकैः:

एन—is added to nouns indicating ' a direction ' in the sense of ' not far from '; पूर्वेणा ग्रामं ' to the east of the village not far from it '; अपरेण ग्रामं, &c.

तस्‌ ( तसि )—has the sense of the Ablative; आदितः ' from the beginning,' मध्यतः, स्वरतः, वर्णतः &c.; sometimes this is added in the sense of the Gen ; as देवा अञ्जुनतोभवन्‌ ' the gods declared themselves on the side of Arjuna '; ( तसिद्‌)-परितः ' on all sides '; अभितः ' on both sides '.

ना—विना ' without ', नाना ' in various ways ', &c.

वत्‌—* is affixed in the sense of ' equally with ' or ' like to ' when the equality or likeness refers to an action ; व्राह्मणेणा तुल्यं ब्राह्मणवदुधोते; but पुत्रेण तुल्यः रथुलः and not पुत्रववद् रथुलः; similarly क्षत्रियवद्‌; or (2) ' as in the case of that मयूरायामिव मयूरावद्‌ लत्रे प्राकारः; चैत्रस्य इव चैत्रवद्‌ ( मैत्रस्य गावः ); (3) विधिमङ्गोति विधिवद्‌ पुज्यते.

इशद्‌-अल्पेश्रः ' little by little '; बहुशः &c.

स्मिन्‌-is added to nouns or indeclinables to express that a person or thing, not being like what is denoted by the base to which it is added, becomes or is made like it.

* तेन तुल्यं क्रिया चैद्यातिः । तत्र तस्येव । तदहुयम् । Pān. V. 1, 115-117. कुम्बसतिगोगौ संयक्करोति स्स्विन्‌ । Pān. V. 4. 50. अभूततद्भावः इति वक्तव्यम् । Vārt.
