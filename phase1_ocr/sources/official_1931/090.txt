2—Demonstrative Pronouns.

§ 135. The pronouns तद् 'that or he, sha, it,' एतद् 'this,'
इदम् 'this' and अदस् 'that'. The Nom. sing of the first two is
respectively सः and एषः mas. and सा and एषा fem. In other
cases they become त and एत and are declined like nouns in अ
except in the Nom. pl., D. Ab. sings , G. pl. and L. sing. the
terminations of which are ई, स्मै, स्मात्, इषाम् and स्मिन् mas.
and तौ, स्यै , स्यास् , सास् and स्याम् fem. respectively. All
pronouns ending in अ are similarly declined.
