§ 308. (a) A* prātipadika ending in कृ, except यत्कृ, तत्कृ, स्ितिकृ† and बहुकृ; स, सर, preceded by पुरः; अग्रतः अमे and पूर्व; and चर preceded by सेना, व्राश and words expressive of place; the words नद्‌, चोर, देब, मार, गर, प्लव, and नद्‌, nominal bases ending in पुय, nominal and verbal derivatives formed by the affix अ causing Vṛiddhi or Guṇa, such as औषधः, औक्ष्, कैभकारः. भारहारः; यादश, तादश; इत्यवरी, &c. and words ending in the Tad. affixes द्युत्र, दश, मात्र and इक ( with some exceptions ) and in the kṛit affix च्वर form their feminine in ई when they are not used adjectively; e. g. भोगकरी conducing to pleasures; एककरी &c.; पत्तेहौ, दित्तरी, &c ; अमेसरी, &c.; सेनाचरी, कुरुचरी, a woman of the Kuru country; मत्स्यचरी, &c.; नद्री, देव्री, सूद्री, &c.; सौर्णोयो, वैनतेयी, &c.; आक्षिकी, लावणिकी, &c., यादशी, सोष्ठयानि, इत्यवरी, evanescent, an unchaste woman &c.; गत्वरी &c.; but स्किकरी, बहुकुरूश्चरा नगरी.

( b ) ‡Also words ending in the Tad. affixes भ्, स्त्र, ईक, य ( causing Vṛiddhi ) and the words तरुण and तलुन; ज्ञेणि. पैञ्ज्नी fit for a man; शात्रकी, तरुणी, सेलुनी, &c. also words ending in the kṛit. affix अन causing a nasal to be inserted before it ; आढ्यंकरणी.

( c ) § Words indicative of the stages of life except the last take the fem. ई; कुमारी, किशोरी, बकैरी, वधूटी, चिरण्टी ( both meaning a ' a young woman ' ); but हुदा, स्थविरा &c. as these imply old age; कन्या is an exception.
