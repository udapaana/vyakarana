§ 773.  *A  root may  be used  in its gerundive  form  in  अम्
with its object prefixed to it if that object be a part of the body
which can be severed without fatal effects;  भुहुनोर्युं  कुषुयति ' nar-
rates contracting the eyebrows, but  हिमुरं नीदुदुयुरु ( not हिंस्राहुयुरु )
as the severance of the head would cause death.

( a ) Similarly when a part of  the  body  is  completely  hurt
or pressed in the action,  the  gerund  in  अम्  is  used  with  that
part; डुर-गुतितुर्युं  युष्युन्नै  i.  e.  कुलुसुयुदुर:  पीडुयुन्नै:  ' afflicting  the
whole bosom;' उद्गीनिंहिंसुवातं त्रुन्हिरिषु ग्राह्युं अनुर्युं:

§ 774.  †The roots विश्रु , युष् , पश्  and  स्कुन्द  are used in their
gerundive forms in अम् in the sense of ' complete  occupation or
pervasion or repetition'  with nouns  which  if not  compounded
would have stood in the Acc.;  गेहुगृहुगुर्युयायुते  गेहं  गेहुयुयुमुभुयुम् ।
स्कुन्दुम्युगौयुते  ।  so  गेहुदुयुयुर्युयुरु ,  गेहुदुयुनायुरु ,  गेहुदुयु-
स्कुन्दुम् , &c.

§ 775.  ( a ) ‡ The gerunds of  अम्  and युष्  are used with  nouns
denoting a period of time intervening  between the repetition or
performance of the action; द्वुवुहुरुचायुहं or द्वुवुहुभुवुवातुं  गां: पायुयति 'he
makes the cows drink once  in  every  two days  to intervene,
i. e  every third day'  अत् पायुवित्युवा द्युयुभुषूहुत्रुकुरुम्यु  पुन: पायुयतीतोर्युर्यै: ।
( Sid. Kau. ); similarly द्युहुत्युरुयु  or द्युहुतुरुरु .
