436                  SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                   [ § 723-728

                By means of the affix क्यप् ( य )

§ 723.  The roots इ 1  2. P.  'to go,' स्तु, शाष् , इ 5. P. A. हु,
डुष् , and roots having ऋ short for their penultimate, except कृष्
and वृत् , take the affix क्यप् ( य ) in the same sense as यत् . When
a root  ends  in  a  short  vowel,  त्  is inserted between the final
vowel of the root; e. g. इ+य=ए+य='worthy of being served ;'
स्तुय् 'deserving praise;' शाष्-शिष्य 'fit to be instructed;' वुद्-वुद्द्य:
इ-आइय; डुष्-डुष्य 'worthy of being served;' भृत्-भृत्य; दृष्-दृश्य
'fit to be increased as wealth, &c. ' but  कल्प ( कृष्+य )  'able;'
वुद्-वर्य ( वृद्+एयत् ) 'that ought to be told or said.'

§ 724   According to Vāmana, the roots सोत् , इकु and युह् take
इ यत्   According to Pāṇini the roots स्तु*-स्तु:य 'praiseworthy;' हकु-ह्कु:य; युह्-उह्य;
optionally they take the affix एयत् ( to be given hereafter ); सोत्य,
ह्वेत, यौहार्.

§ 725.  शुच् also takes  this affix optionally;  शुय्य 'what is fit
or ought to be cleansed;'  optionally  it takes एयत् before which
the final य is changed to प्र; शोच्य.

§ 726.  ( a ) भू when it has  a  Subanta  prefixed  to  it and is
without a preposition takes the affix  क्यप् impersonally;  ब्रह्मणौ
भाव: ब्रह्मभूयं 'identity with Brahman.' When no Subanta is used
prepositionally with it, it takes the affix यत् ; भव्य  or भूयम्.

( a ) वह् under similar circumstances takes either  क्यप् or एयत्
in a passive sense in the Veda, namely: वाह्युं or वहूवत् ' expounding
the Veda: ' ( वहु वेद: तस्य वृदत् Sid  Kau. ).

727.  खद् takes this affix,  but drops its  व and adds  ङ् to its
penultimate vowel खद्+य=खं+य=खङ्+ङ्+य=खेय  ' what is fit
or ought to be dug or excavated.'

728.  भृ 1 cl. takes this affix when the  participle  so derived
does not form a name; भृतुयाः 'those who  ought  to  be nourished
or maintained,' hence 'soldiers,' &c ;  but भारुयै: ( भृ+एयत् ) a
class of Kshatriyas'; when  सत्य  precedes भृ,  वत्  and  एयत्  are
added optionally संभुगुयाः or संघूषुते.
