## § 637. Conjugation of Parasmaipada Frequentative

In the Conjugational Tenses the Parasm. Frequentatives follow the conjugation of the special base of a root of the 3rd class. @deva[इ] is optionally prefixed to the terminations of the singulars of the Present, of the 2nd and 3rd singulars of the Imperf. and of the 3rd sing. of the Impera.; when @deva[इ] is prefixed to these, a penultimate short vowel does not take Guṇa; @deva[दा-दादाति] or @deva[दादेति]; @deva[दुह्-दोहेति] or @deva[दरिदोहेति] or @deva[दरीदोहेति] or @deva[दोहूतेति] or @deva[दरिदोहूतीति] or @deva[दरीदुदोहतीति], @deva[कृ-चर्केति] or @deva[चर्केतीति], @deva[चरिक्षेति] or @deva[चरिक्षेतीति], @deva[चरीक्षति] or @deva[चरीकृतीति].
