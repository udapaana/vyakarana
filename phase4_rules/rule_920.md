## § 920. Present for Past with @deva[नु]

The Present used with @deva[स्म] and @deva[हि] in the sense of the Past is equivalent to @deva[अकरोत्].

When the particles @deva[नु] and @deva[उत] are used, the Present may be optionally used:

**Examples:**

- @deva[कट्युयुकुयूर्युनै: किंयु—नं करोति] or @deva[चकार]
- @deva[उत करोति] or @deva[चकार]
