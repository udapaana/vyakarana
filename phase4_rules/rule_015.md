## § 15. @deva[गुण] and @deva[वृद्धि]

The vowels @deva[अ, ए, ओ] and the syllables @deva[अर] and @deva[अल्] are called @deva[गुण]; the vowels @deva[आ, ऐ, औ] and the syllables @deva[आर] and @deva[आल्] are called @deva[वृद्धि].[^1] The @deva[गुण] and @deva[वृद्धि] vowels and syllables that are substituted for the simple vowels will appear from the following table:

| Simple V. | @deva[अ] | @deva[इ] & @deva[ई] | @deva[उ] & @deva[ऊ] | @deva[ऋ] & @deva[ॠ] | @deva[ऌ] |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| @deva[गुण] | @deva[अ] | @deva[ए] | @deva[ओ] | @deva[अर] | @deva[अल्] |
| @deva[वृद्धि] | @deva[आ] | @deva[ऐ] | @deva[औ] | @deva[आर] | @deva[आल्] |

[^1]: @deva[वृद्धिरादैच् । अदेङ्गुणः । उरण् रपरः] Pāṇ. I. 1. 2; I. 1. 51.
