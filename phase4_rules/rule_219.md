## § 219. Exceptions to Nañ Transformation

The @deva[न] is not changed to @deva[अ] or @deva[अन्] in the following cases[^1]:—@deva[नभ्र] 'that which does not shine,' 'a cloud;' @deva[नपात्] (pre. p.) 'one who does not protect;' @deva[नवेद्यः] 'one who does not know,' @deva[नासत्याः] [@deva[न सत्याः असत्याः न असत्याः नासत्याः]][^2] the two Aśvins, the physicians of the gods. @deva[नहुष] [@deva[न हि श्वेतीति]] name of a demon slain by Indra; @deva[नकुलः] [@deva[न कुलम् अस्य], for the mungoose is supposed to belong to no particular species of animals]; @deva[नसद्] [@deva[न स्वम् अस्य] that which leaves no cavity, or does not go to heaven being burnt down with the dead body]; @deva[नक्षत्रम्] [@deva[न क्षरति इति]] that which does not move from its proper place, hence a star; @deva[नक्रः] [@deva[न क्रामति इति] that which does not go far off from water]; @deva[नाकः] [@deva[न कं अकं न अकं अक्रम्]]; @deva[नग] when it does not qualify an animal retains its @deva[न] optionally as @deva[नगः] or @deva[अगः] 'a mountain or a tree,' but @deva[अगः शूद्रः] 'a Śūdra that cannot move.'

**N.B.** It should be noted that some of these are Bahuvrihi compounds.

[^2]: @deva[अत्र बहुव्वचनसविवक्षिता । तेन 'नासत्यावश्विनौ मुक्तो' इति सिद्धम्] Tatvabodhini.
