## § 423. Augment इ in अन्, जक्ष्, रुद्, श्वस्, स्वप्

@deva[अन्] P. 'to breathe,' @deva[जक्ष्] 'to eat,' @deva[रुद्] P. 'to weep,' @deva[श्वस्] P. 'to sigh' and @deva[स्वप्] P. 'to sleep,' insert the augment @deva[इ] between their final and the terminations beginning with any consonant except @deva[य]; in the case of 2 and 3 sing. Imperf. they insert @deva[ई] or @deva[आ]; e.g.

### अन् P. 'to breathe'

| | Present | | | Imperfect | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[अनिमि] | @deva[अनिवः] | @deva[अनिमः] | @deva[आनम्] | @deva[आनिव] | @deva[आनिम] |
| 2. | @deva[अनिषि] | @deva[अनिथः] | @deva[अनिथ] | @deva[आनीः-आनः] | @deva[आनितम्] | @deva[आनित] |
| 3. | @deva[अनिति] | @deva[अनितः] | @deva[अनन्ति] | @deva[आनीत्-आनत्] | @deva[आनिताम्] | @deva[आनन्] |

| | Imperative | | | Potential | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | @deva[अनानि] | @deva[अनाव] | @deva[अनाम] | @deva[अन्याम्] | @deva[अन्याव] | @deva[अन्याम] |
| 2. | @deva[अनिहि] | @deva[अनितम्] | @deva[अनित] | @deva[अन्याः] | @deva[अन्यातम्] | @deva[अन्यात] |
| 3. | @deva[अनितु] | @deva[अनिताम्] | @deva[अनन्तु] | @deva[अन्यात्] | @deva[अन्याताम्] | @deva[अन्युः] |

Conjugate @deva[स्वप्], @deva[श्वस्] and @deva[रुद्] similarly; as @deva[स्वपिति] 3 sing. Pre., @deva[अस्वपीः-थः] 2 sing. Imperf.; @deva[अस्वपीत्-पत्] 3 sing. Imperf.; @deva[स्वपानि] 1. sing. Imp. @deva[स्वपिहि] 2 sing. Imp.; @deva[स्वप्यात्] 1. sing. Pot.; &c. @deva[श्वसिति] 3. sing. Pre.; @deva[अश्वसीः-थः] 2 sing. Imperf.; @deva[अश्वसीत्-सत्] 3 sing. Imperf.; @deva[श्वसानि] 1. sing. Imp. @deva[श्वसिहि] 2. sing. Imp. @deva[श्वसितु] 3. sing. Imp. @deva[श्वस्यात्] 1. sing. Pot., &c.; @deva[रोदिति], @deva[रुदिवः] @deva[रुदिमः] 1 Pre.; @deva[अरोदम्] 1. sing. Imperf. @deva[अरोदीः-थ], 2 sing. Imperf. @deva[अरोदीत्-दत्] 3. sing. Imperf.; @deva[रोदानि] 1. sing. Imp. @deva[रुदिहि] 2. sing. Imp. @deva[रोदितु] 3. sing. Imp.; @deva[रुद्यात्] 1. sing. Pot. &c.
