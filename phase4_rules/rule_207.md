## § 207. Dative Tatpurusha

A noun in the Dative is compounded[^1]—

(a) with another expressive of the material of which the thing expressed by the noun in the Dative case is made; as @deva[यूपाय द्रुः] @deva[यूपद्रुः] 'wood for a sacrificial post.'

(b) and with the words @deva[अर्थ], @deva[बलि], @deva[हित], @deva[इष्ट] and @deva[रक्षित]. The compound with @deva[अर्थ] is a nityasamāsa and takes the gender and number of the noun it qualifies; @deva[ब्राह्मणाय अर्थं] @deva[ब्राह्मणार्थं] @deva[सूपः] broth for a Brāhmaṇa; @deva[ब्राह्मणार्थं इदं] @deva[ब्राह्मणार्थं] @deva[यवागु] (water-gruel); @deva[ब्राह्मणार्थं इदं] @deva[ब्राह्मणार्थं] @deva[पयः]; @deva[भूतेभ्यो बलिः] @deva[भूतबलिः] an offering to the bhūtas (certain created beings); @deva[गवे हितं] @deva[गोहितम्] good for a cow; @deva[गवे इष्टं] @deva[गोइष्टम्]; @deva[गवे रक्षितं] @deva[गोरक्षितम्].
