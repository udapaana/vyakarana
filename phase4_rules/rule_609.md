## § 609. Roots dropping final vowel before अय्

@deva[दीपी], @deva[वेवी] and @deva[दरिद्रा] drop their final vowel before @deva[अय्]:

@deva[दीपयति-ते], @deva[वेवयति-ते], @deva[दरिदयति-ते].
