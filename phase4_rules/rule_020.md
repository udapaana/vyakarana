## § 20. Guṇa Substitution

When @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] is followed by @deva[इ, उ, ऋ, टृ], short or long, the guṇa letter corresponding to the latter takes the place of both[^4] e.g. @deva[उप + इन्द्र = उपेन्द्र:] Vishnu; @deva[परम + ईश्वर: = परमेश्वर:] the great lord; @deva[रमा + इच्छा = रमेच्छा] the wish of Ramā; @deva[यथा + इच्छित = यथेच्छितम्] according to wish or desire; @deva[हित + उपदेश: = हितोपदेश:] a friendly instruction; @deva[टत्प + ऊरु = टत्पोरु:] Kṛshṇa's thigh; @deva[गङ्गा + उदक = गङ्गोदक], @deva[मङ्ग + ऊरु = मङ्गोरु]; @deva[कृष्ण + ऋद्धि: = कृष्णर्द्धि:] Kṛshṇa's prosperity; @deva[महा + ऋषि: = महर्षि:] a great sage; @deva[तव + टृकार: = तवट्कार:] The ṭkāra pronounced by you.

### (a) Optional Dropping of First Consonant

If a consonant be followed by homogeneous consonants, except the nasals, the semivowels, and @deva[र], the first of them is optionally dropped;[^1] @deva[कुम्भ + कुदि: = कुम्भादि:] by the general rule; then @deva[कुम्भर् + उ + य + इ = कुम्भादि:] by this rule, the @deva[उ] being dropped; and also a third form @deva[कुम्भऋदि:] (see § 22, subsection d, below).

### (b) Optional Doubling

A consonant of the first five classes except @deva[य] coming after a semivowel is optionally doubled; so @deva[तत्वकार:] also becomes @deva[तत्व्वकार:, तत्त्त्वकार:] and @deva[तत्व्व्वकार:], 4 forms altogether.
