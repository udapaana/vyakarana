## § 50. स् Elision in Pronouns

### (a) Elision of स् in तद् and एतद् Nominative Singular

The @deva[स्] of the nom. sing. of @deva[तद्] and @deva[एतद्] masculine is dropped before a consonant when they do not end in @deva[क्] or are not used in a negative Tatpurusha;[^1] e.g. @deva[स पुष्णुः]; @deva[एष विष्णुः]; but @deva[एषः को रुद्रः] this Rudra; @deva[असौ + शिवः = असौशिवः] that is not Śiva (a negative Tatpurusha); @deva[एष + रुद्रः = एषोरुद्रः].

### (b) Poetic License for Vowel Combination

Sometimes in poetry the @deva[स्] of @deva[सः] and @deva[एषः] is considered as not existing when followed by a vowel other than @deva[अ], so that the two vowels may combine in order to meet the exigencies of the metre;[^2] e.g. @deva[तेषाम् + ऋषिः + इङ्कुर्धौ = पश्येत + य ईक्षीत] etc. Ṛg. II. 24. 1

@deva[स एष द्रव्यवान् रामः]
@deva[स एष राजा युधिष्ठिरः]
@deva[स एष कर्णो महात्यागी]
@deva[स एष भीमो महाबलः]
