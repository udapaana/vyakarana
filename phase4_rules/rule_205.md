## § 205. Instrumental Tatpurusha

A noun in the Instrumental may be compounded—

(a) with another expressive of an effect produced by what is denoted by the noun in the Instrumental case, and with @deva[अर्थ];[^1] as @deva[शङ्कुलेन सलितं] @deva[शङ्कुशलितम्] severed by means of a knife; @deva[धान्येन अर्थः] @deva[धान्यार्थः] wealth obtained by means of grain.

(b) with verbal derivatives when the Instrumental has the sense of the agent or the instrument or means of the action;[^2] as @deva[हरिणा त्रातः] @deva[हरित्रातः] saved by Hari; @deva[नखैर्भिन्नः] @deva[नखभिन्नः] torn with the nails etc.

(c) with the words[^3] @deva[पूर्व], @deva[सह], @deva[सम], @deva[ऊन] and words having the sense of @deva[ऊन], @deva[कलह], @deva[निपुण], @deva[मिश्र], @deva[स्पृश] and @deva[अवर]; as @deva[मात्रा पूर्वः] @deva[मातृपूर्वः]; @deva[मात्रा सहः] @deva[मातृसहः] like his mother; @deva[पितृसमः] equal to his father, @deva[मात्रा ऊनं] @deva[मातॄणः]; so @deva[माषविकलः] (less by one māsha, a particular weight); @deva[वाचा कलहः] @deva[वाक्कलहः] a quarrel (in words); @deva[आचारनिपुणः]; @deva[दधिमिश्रः]; @deva[आचारस्पृश्यः] 'thin by the discharge of religious duties;' @deva[मात्रेण अवरः] @deva[मासावरः] 'younger by a month.'

(d) With another noun signifying an article of food or something eatable, the noun in the Instrumental case denoting some kind of condiment used to season it or another article of food mixed with it;[^4] as @deva[दध्ना औदनः] @deva[दध्योदनः] rice mixed with curds; @deva[घृतेन धानाः] @deva[घृतधानाः] fried rice mixed with treacle.

(e) Sometimes @deva[स्वयं] may take the place of the Instrumental as @deva[स्वयंकृतः] done by one's self.
