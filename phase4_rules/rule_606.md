## § 606. Roots inserting इय् or च् or ज् instead of व्

@deva[ज्ञा] 'to pare, to sharpen,' @deva[स्था] 'to cut,' @deva[दो] 'to finish,' @deva[दे] P. 'to give,' @deva[अम्], @deva[कृष्], @deva[क्षिप्], @deva[ह्नुष्], all P. and meaning 'to injure,' 'to kill,' @deva[चत्] 'to kill,' @deva[कृत्] P. 'to honour,' @deva[ज्वद्], 'to shine,' @deva[ह्रद्], @deva[क्षद्], 'to shake, to move' P., 'to break' (10 Ā.), @deva[वष्], @deva[यो] 'to cook, to boil,' @deva[ह्रा] 'to kill, to gratify, to sharpen, to manifest,' @deva[चट्], 'to live or to be' (@deva[प्राचायति] in other senses), @deva[लट्] 'to sport, to loll the tongue,' @deva[वह्] 'to be poor, to reduce,' @deva[छत्], @deva[लद्], @deva[जद्], @deva[पृच्], @deva[कह्] 'to be crooked, to shine,' @deva[यद्], @deva[स्पञ्] and @deva[घट्] 1 P. 'to go,' 'to call,' @deva[ट्ये] 'to cover,' @deva[वे] 'to weave,' @deva[से] 'to waste away,' and @deva[पा] 'to drink' insert @deva[इय्] instead of @deva[व्]:

@deva[ज्ञापयति], @deva[स्तापयति], @deva[दाशयति], @deva[पाययति], &c.

(a) @deva[पा] 'to protect' and @deva[वे] when it means 'to shake,' insert @deva[च्] and @deva[ज्] respectively before @deva[अय्]:

@deva[पालयति] 'he protects,' @deva[वाञ्जयति] 'he shakes.'
