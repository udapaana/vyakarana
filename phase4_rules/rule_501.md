## § 501. Insertion of त After Reduplicative Syllable

@deva[त] is inserted after the reduplicative syllable in the case of roots beginning with @deva[अ] and ending in a conjunct consonant, in that of @deva[अह्] 'to pervade' and @deva[आस्] 'to go'; consequently @deva[अ] after @deva[त] is changed to @deva[आ].

e.g. @deva[अञ्ज्]: @deva[अञ्ज् + अ = आञ्ज् + अ + अ = आनञ्ज]

Similarly @deva[आनन्द्] 1 sing. of @deva[अन्द्], @deva[आनर्च्] of @deva[अर्च्], etc.
