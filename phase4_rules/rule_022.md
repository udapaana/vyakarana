## [^5] 22. Semivowel Substitution (Yaṇ)

When @deva[इ, उ, ऋ] and @deva[लृ], short or long, are followed by a dissimilar vowel, @deva[य्, व्, र्] and @deva[ल्] are respectively substituted for them[^1]; e.g. @deva[इति + आह = इत्याह]; @deva[बुधी + उपास्य: = बुध्युपास्यः] adored by the wise; @deva[मधु + अरि: = मध्वरि:] the enemy of @deva[मधु] i.e. Vishṇu, @deva[पातृ + अंश: = पात्रंश:] a portion of the creator; @deva[लृ + आकृतिः = लाकृतिः] a figure like that of @deva[लृ] &c.



### (a) Optional Doubling Before Consonant

A consonant except @deva[ङ्], preceded by a vowel, is optionally doubled, if no vowel follows[^1]; @deva[सुप्र + उपास्य: = सुप्पुपास्य:] and @deva[सुप्रम् + उपास्य:].

### (b) Soft Unaspirate Substitution

When a consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, is followed by a soft unaspirate or aspirate (3rd or 4th letter of a class) it is changed to the soft unaspirate (3rd letter) of its class[^3]; @deva[सुप्रम् + उपास्य: = सुद्दुपास्य:].

### (c) Optional Doubling of Semivowels

Semivowels, when preceded by a letter of the first five classes except @deva[म्] are optionally doubled[^4]; thus @deva[सुःम् + उपास्य: = सुःम् + उपास्य:] and @deva[सुःम्म् + उपास्य:]; @deva[सुद्म् + उपास्य: = सुद्म् + उपास्य:] and @deva[सुद्म्म् + उपास्य:]. Thus there are four forms ultimately, viz. @deva[सुःमुपास्य:, सुद्दुपास्य:] and @deva[सुद्दुरुपास्य:].

Similarly @deva[मधु + अरि: = मध्वरि:, मद्ध्वरि:, मद्धुवरि:], and @deva[मद्दुव्वरि:]; @deva[पाद् + अङ्क: = पादङ्क:] and @deva[पाद्दङ्क:]. In @deva[ल् + आकृति:] of course there is no possibility of any other form.

### (d) Doubling After र्/र्

A consonant except @deva[ङ्], coming after @deva[र्] or @deva[र्] preceded by a vowel is optionally doubled[^5]; e.g. @deva[हरि + अनुभव: = हर्य् + अनुभव: = हर्य्यनुभव:] by the general rule; and optionally @deva[हर्य्म् + अनुभव: = हर्य्यनुभव:] (Hari's experience) by this rule; so @deva[न हि + अस्ति = न ह्यस्ति] and @deva[न हय्यस्ति].

\[^1] @deva[इको यणचि] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 77.
