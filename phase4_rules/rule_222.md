## § 222. Adjective-Noun Karmadhāraya

An adjective may be compounded with the noun it qualifies in most cases[^1] as @deva[नीलं च तत् उत्पलं च] @deva[नीलोत्पलम्], a blue lotus etc.; @deva[शुण्डाश्वौ] @deva[सर्पेश्वरौ] @deva[शुण्डाश्वरौ] where the comp. is necessary; but @deva[जामदग्न्यौ रामः]. Such compounds are called @deva[विशेषणपूर्वपदकर्मधारयम्].

As a general rule in such compounds the qualifying word is placed first; but there are several exceptions which are given below.

(a) The qualified word is placed first and is always of the mas. gender if it denotes a class or species, when compounded with certain adjectives,[^2] e.g. @deva[युवहस्ति] 'a young female elephant', @deva[आखस्तोकः] 'a small fire', @deva[उदुम्बरकतिपयम्] 'butter-milk mixed with a little water', @deva[गोप्राचि] 'a cow that has brought forth a calf for the first time', @deva[गोधेनुः] 'a milch-cow (@deva[नवप्रसूतिका])', @deva[गोवंशा] 'a barren cow', @deva[गोविवत्] (@deva[गर्भघातिनी गौः]), @deva[गोवत्सतरी] 'a cow whose calf is one year old,' @deva[काठाभौजिकः] 'an agnihotrin Brāhmana of the Katha branch of the Yajurveda,' @deva[काठाध्यापकः] 'a teacher of the Katha branch,'[^3] @deva[गौमताडिका-मदिविका-प्रकुण्डम्] (also mas. according to some)—@deva[उद्रः] (@deva[गवोद्र])-@deva[तद्रज] 'an excellent cow'. The last mentioned five words, all of which denote excellence, retain their own gender invariably; as @deva[ब्राह्मणमताडिका] 'an eminent Brāhmana' etc.; but @deva[कुमारी मताडिका], as no class is meant here.

(b) The words @deva[कहार], @deva[सर्व], @deva[खञ्ज] lame, @deva[काण], @deva[कुण्ड] blunt, dull, @deva[स्फलित] bald-headed, @deva[जीर्ण], @deva[बद्ध], @deva[भिक्षुक] @deva[भीरु], @deva[भिङ्गल], @deva[तुञ्ज], @deva[जरठ] hard, stiff, @deva[बधिर], @deva[कुब्ज] and @deva[वर्वर], may optionally precede the words in apposition to them;[^4] as @deva[जैमिनिकहारः] or @deva[कहारजैमिनिः] Jaimini who is turned tawny on account of his having practised penance in the sun.' etc.

(c) Words expressive of the persons or things condemned or reproached are placed first;[^5] as @deva[वैयाकरणासत्] 'a bad grammarian,' 'one who has forgotten his grammar and therefore can make no use of his knowledge;' (@deva[पुत्रः सत् पुदर्ग वित्तम् आर्जयन् नु यं सुख्यति अध्यात्मवेत्युपत्ति एकद्युब्ध्यते] Tatv.); so @deva[मीमांसकाबुद्धः] 'a mīmāmsaka who is a sceptic' or 'an unbeliever', except in the case of the words @deva[पाप], @deva[अपक] and @deva[किम्]; as @deva[पापनापितः] 'a bad barber,' @deva[अपककुम्भकारः] 'a mean, silly potter,' @deva[कुत्सितः रामः] @deva[किंरामः], @deva[किंसखा] (@deva[कुत्सितः सखा]), etc.

(d) Words in apposition to @deva[इन्द्र], @deva[नाग] and @deva[कुञ्जर] are also placed first;[^6] e.g. @deva[नृपेन्द्रः] 'an eminent king,' @deva[तापसकुञ्जरः], @deva[मुनिनागः], etc.

(e) @deva[कतर] and @deva[कतम] are compounded only when they ask a question with reference to a class (@deva[जाति] family), and are placed first;[^7] e.g. @deva[कतर-कतम-काठः] 'which Brāhmana of the Katha branch'? @deva[कतर-कतम-कलापः] 'which Brāhmana of the Kalāpa branch'? But @deva[कतरः पुत्रः] 'which son'?

(f) The word @deva[कुमार] (and also @deva[कुमारी] changed to @deva[कुमार]) is placed first when compounded with one of the words @deva[श्रमणा], @deva[मन्त्रिता], @deva[कुलटा], @deva[गर्भिणी], @deva[तापसी], @deva[दासी], @deva[अध्यापक], @deva[पीवर], @deva[सुभग], @deva[मुखर], @deva[कुशल], @deva[चपल] and @deva[निपुण];[^8] as @deva[कुमारश्रमणा] 'a female mendicant,' @deva[कुमारमन्त्रिता] 'a young female ascetic'; @deva[कुमारसुभग]- 'a delicate boy or girl, @deva[कुमारगर्भिणी], @deva[कुमाराध्यापकः], etc.

(g) The words @deva[एक], @deva[सर्व], @deva[जरठ], @deva[पुराण], @deva[नव] and @deva[केवल]; and the words @deva[पूर्व], @deva[अपर], @deva[चरम], @deva[जघन्य] 'hindmost,' @deva[समान], @deva[मध्य], @deva[मध्यम] and @deva[वीर] are always placed first in a Karmadhāraya compound.[^9] @deva[अपर] when followed by @deva[अर्ध] is changed to @deva[पश्च]; as @deva[एकनाथः] 'sole master or lord', @deva[सर्वेश्वरः], @deva[जरठयोगविदः], 'an old logician,' @deva[पुराणमीमांसकः]; 'old mīmāmsā philosophers,' @deva[नवटीकाकुम्], @deva[पूर्ववैयाकरणाः], 'the old grammarians,' @deva[अपराध्यापकः] @deva[अपरस्यार्धम्] @deva[अपेक्ष] @deva[पश्चार्धम्] 'the hinder part or side of the body;' @deva[चरमराजः] the last king,' @deva[समानाधिकरणम्] 'being in the same category or predicament'; @deva[वीरैकः] 'a solo warrior; i.e. an eminent warrior', etc. The form @deva[एकवीर] (found used by great writers) also may be defended.

(h) The words @deva[सत्], @deva[महत्], @deva[परम], @deva[उत्तम] and @deva[उत्कट] are compounded with another noun when they are used as terms of praise or excellence;[^10] @deva[सद्वैद्यः] 'an excellent physician' @deva[महद्वैयाकरणम्]. etc. but @deva[उत्कटौ गौः] where @deva[उत्कट] means 'uplifted, or 'drawn out of.'
