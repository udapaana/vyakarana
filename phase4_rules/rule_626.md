## § 626. Exceptions to Frequentative Formation

**(a)** A Frequentative may be formed from @deva[अट्] 'to go,' @deva[इ] 'to go', @deva[अश्] 'to eat' and @deva[गुह्] 'to conceal,' though these begin with a vowel; and from @deva[वृत्] cl. 10 'to hint,' @deva[वृज्] cl. 10 'to string together', and @deva[वृष्] cl. 10, though they belong to the 10th cl.

**(b)** The Frequentative of roots signifying motion conveys the notion of tortuous motion and not of repetition. The Frequentative of roots @deva[छुर्] 'to cut,' @deva[सृ] 'to sink down,' @deva[वृ] 'to go' @deva[जप्] 'to mutter prayers,' @deva[जृम्भ्] 'to yawn,' @deva[दह्] 'to burn,' @deva[दंश्] 'to bite,' and @deva[गॄ] 'to swallow,' imply reproach on the manner of doing the act. @deva[छोछुप्यते] 'he cuts awkwardly,' @deva[सासृप्यते] 'falls down badly,' @deva[वावृत्यते] &c.
