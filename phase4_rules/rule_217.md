## § 217. Additional Aluk of the Locative

(a) Aluk is necessary in the case of the words—(1) @deva[गौ] and @deva[युधि] followed by @deva[विधृ], as @deva[गावि विधृ], stationary in the sky, @deva[युवि विधृ] firm in battle; (2) @deva[हृद्] and @deva[दिव्] followed by @deva[स्पृश्]; @deva[हृदिस्पृक्], @deva[दिविस्पृक्] (@deva[हृदयं दिवं च स्पृशतीति]); (3) @deva[अन्तर्] and @deva[मध्य] followed by @deva[गुह्]; @deva[मध्येगुहः]; @deva[अन्तर्गुहः] and (4) words expressive of parts of the body except @deva[मूर्धन्] and @deva[मस्तक] and not followed by @deva[केश]; @deva[कर्णे काकः], @deva[उरसि लोम] (having the hair on the breast: Bah.), but @deva[मूर्धे-मस्तके-शिखा]; @deva[छुले कामो अस्य] @deva[छुलकामः].

(b) When a noun in the Loc. is compounded with a verbal derivative and the whole is a name, aluk takes place in most cases;[^1] e.g. @deva[स्तम्बे रमः] (@deva[स्तम्ब] a kind of grass @deva[रमते अत्र]) an elephant; @deva[कर्णे जपः] @deva[कर्णेजपः] one who whispers into the ear, a spy; @deva[दिवि चरः] @deva[दिवाचरः] a heavenly being; @deva[पङ्के रुहम्] @deva[पङ्केरुहम्] a lotus; @deva[कुशे शयम्] @deva[कुशेशयम्], @deva[जले शयः] @deva[जलेशयः] a fish; but @deva[दिवाचरा]; @deva[सरोजम्] or @deva[सरोरुहम्] etc.

(c) There is optional aluk of the Loc. of a word expressive of time when compounded with @deva[काल] and words ending in the affixes @deva[तर], @deva[तम] and @deva[तन]; @deva[पूर्वाह्णे काले] @deva[पूर्वाह्णकाले]; @deva[पूर्वाह्णे उत्तरे] etc. @deva[पूर्वाह्णे उत्तने].

(d) When the compound is not a name, aluk is necessary when @deva[ज] comes after @deva[प्रादु], @deva[शरद्], @deva[काल] and @deva[दिवि]; and optional when it comes after @deva[वर्ष], @deva[क्षर], @deva[घर] and @deva[वर];[^2] as @deva[प्रादुषि-शरदि-काले-दिवि-जः]; @deva[वर्षे जः] or @deva[वर्षजः] produced in the rainy season, etc.

**Exception:**—No aluk takes place in the case of verbal derivatives ending in @deva[इन्] and the words @deva[सिद्ध], @deva[बद्ध], and @deva[स्थ] in the ordinary language;[^3] as @deva[स्थाण्डिले शायी] 'an ascetic,' @deva[शालूरस्थितः], @deva[चक्रे बद्धः] @deva[चक्रबद्धः]; @deva[समे स्थः] @deva[समस्थः]; but @deva[छणे अस्य अवरेशः] etc.

(e) When a noun in the Loc. case ending in @deva[अ] or a consonant and not denoting time is compounded with @deva[आश्रय], @deva[वास] and @deva[वास्तिन्] and with @deva[वन] aluk is optional;[^4] @deva[वृक्षे आश्रयः], @deva[ग्रामे वासः] @deva[ग्रामेवासः], @deva[ग्रामे वास्ती] @deva[ग्रामेवास्ती], @deva[वृक्षे अस्य वनम्] @deva[वृक्षेवनम्], but @deva[भूमिश्रयः], @deva[युद्धिवन्यः] etc.
