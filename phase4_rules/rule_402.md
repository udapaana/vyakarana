## § 402. Roots Belonging to Both 1st and 10th Classes

The following roots belong to the 1st and the 10th classes:

@deva[युज्], @deva[धूञ्] to unite, to restrain; @deva[अर्च्] to worship; @deva[ईर्] to throw; @deva[ली] to melt; @deva[हुञ्ज्] to abandon, to avoid; @deva[चु] to cover; @deva[जॄ], @deva[जि] to grow old; @deva[तिच्] to separate, to join; @deva[शिण्] to leave a residue; @deva[तण्] to burn; @deva[चुष्] to be pleased or satisfied; @deva[चुञ्च्] to kindle; @deva[चुण्], @deva[छुण्], @deva[धूम्] to kindle; @deva[धूष्] to fear; @deva[श्रथ्] to release, to kill; @deva[मी] to go; @deva[ग्रन्थ्] to string together; @deva[कील्क्], @deva[चील्क्] to endure; @deva[अर्द्] to kill; @deva[हिंस्] to kill; @deva[अर्द्] to worship; @deva[सड्] with @deva[आ] to go, to assail; @deva[शुच्] to purify, to cleanse; @deva[छुद्] to cover; @deva[जुष्] to satisfy, to guess, to kill; @deva[यी] to please; @deva[श्रन्थ्], @deva[ग्रन्थ्] to compose, arrange; @deva[घ्राण्] to obtain; @deva[तम्] to confide, to stretch; @deva[चन्] to confide, to hurt; @deva[बद्] to inform; @deva[वच्] to speak; @deva[मान्] to honour, worship; @deva[भू] A. to obtain (@deva[भवति] also according to some); @deva[गर्ह्] to censure; @deva[मार्ग्] to seek; @deva[कुण्ठ्] to grieve, to remember with regret; @deva[युज्] to clean; @deva[घुष्] to endure; @deva[धूष्] to brave, to overcome; @deva[जस्] to hurt, to injure; @deva[त्रिभ्] 10 A, 1. P, to torment, to beg; @deva[ढुक्ष्] to aver, and some others.
