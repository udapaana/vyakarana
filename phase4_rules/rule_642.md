## § 642. Changes Before क्यच् Affix

Before this @deva[य],

**(1)** final @deva[अ] and @deva[आ] are changed to @deva[ई]; @deva[पुत्र आत्मनः इच्छति—पुत्रीयति] (@deva[पुत्र+य=पुत्री+य+ति]) 'he wishes for a son.'

**(2)** final @deva[इ] and @deva[उ] are lengthened; @deva[कवि—कवीयति] 'he wishes for a poet.'

**(3)** final @deva[ऋ] is changed to @deva[री]; @deva[कर्तृ-कर्त्रीयति]

**(4)** final @deva[ओ] and @deva[औ] are changed to @deva[अव्] and @deva[आव्]; @deva[गो-गव्यति]; @deva[नौ-नाव्यति].

**(5)** a final nasal is dropped and the preceding vowel is changed like an original final vowel; @deva[राजन्-राजीयति] 'he wishes for a king.'

**(6)** in other cases the final consonant remains unchanged; @deva[वाच्-वाच्यति] 'he wishes for words,' @deva[दिव्-दिव्यति] (@deva[दीव्यति] according to some) 'he wishes for heaven,' @deva[समिध्-समिध्यति] 'he wishes for holy sticks,' &c.

**(7)** The Tad. affix expressing 'a descendant of' is dropped and then the changes mentioned above take place, @deva[गार्ग्य] (the son of @deva[गर्ग]) @deva[आत्मनः इच्छति गार्गीयति] (@deva[गार्ग्य+य+ति=गार्ग्+य+ति=गार्गी+य+ति]), &c.
