## § 643. Insertion of स् and अस् Before क्यच्

The consonant @deva[स्] and the syllable @deva[अस्] are inserted between any nominal stem and the affix @deva[य]; @deva[मधु आत्मनः इच्छति मधुस्यति] or @deva[मधस्यति] 'he wishes for honey'; so @deva[दृतिस्यति], @deva[दृष्यस्यति], &c. Final @deva[अ्] is dropped before @deva[अस्]; @deva[पुत्रस्यति].

**(a)** @deva[अस्] is inserted after @deva[वृष] and @deva[अश्व] when the sense to be conveyed is that of longing for them, after @deva[क्षीर] and @deva[लवण] when the one is desired to be drunk and the other to be licked; @deva[वृषस्यति गौः] 'the cow longs for the ox,' @deva[अश्वस्यति वडवा] 'the mare longs for the horse,' @deva[क्षीरस्यति बालः] 'the child desires to drink,' @deva[लवणस्यति उष्ट्रः] 'the camel wishes for licking the salt.' But @deva[वृषीयति] 'he desires to have a bull,' @deva[अश्वीयति] 'he wishes for a horse,' @deva[क्षीरीयति], @deva[लवणीयति].
