## § 227. Potentials and Similars in Karmadhāraya

Words ending in the terminations @deva[तव्य], @deva[अनीय], @deva[य] and @deva[तुल्य] and words having a similar meaning, may be compounded with any subanta not importing a particular kind;[^1] e.g. @deva[भोज्यम् अन्नम्] 'any hot eatable,' @deva[तुल्यश्वेतः] 'of a similar white colour,' @deva[सदृशश्वेतः], etc.; but @deva[भोज्यः ओदनः] as the latter word refers to a kind of food here.

---

[^1]: @deva[कृत्यतृत्यार्थ्या अजात्या] Pāṇ. II.1.59.
