## § 216. Locative Tatpurusha

A noun in the Locative is compounded—

(a) with @deva[शौण्ड], @deva[भक्त], @deva[किटव] a rogue, @deva[प्रशीण], @deva[संसृष्ट] adorned, invested with, @deva[अन्तर], @deva[आविस्], @deva[बद्ध], @deva[प्रणीत], @deva[कुशल], @deva[चतुर], @deva[निपुण], @deva[सिद्ध], @deva[पुष्ट], @deva[पक्व], and @deva[बन्ध्य];[^1] as @deva[अक्षेषु शौण्डः] @deva[अक्षशौण्डः] skilled in dice; @deva[ईश्वरे भक्तिः] @deva[ईश्वरभक्तिः] dependent on God (when compounded the affix @deva[इन्] i.e. @deva[भक्तिन्] is compulsory; so the compounded form is always with @deva[भक्तिन्]); @deva[आतपपुष्टः] dried in the sun; @deva[स्थालीपक्वः] cooked in a pot; @deva[चक्रबन्ध्यः] a kind of poetic composition; etc.

(b) with words meaning a 'crow' when contempt is implied;[^2] @deva[तीर्थे काकः इव] @deva[तीर्थकाकः] said in contempt of one who is very greedy; so @deva[तीर्थवायसः]; similarly @deva[मगरकाकः]:—@deva[वायसः] etc. etc.

There are a few Loc. Tat. compounds given in the list @deva[परमेश्वरादि] having a similar sense; e.g. @deva[कूपे मण्डूकः इव] @deva[कूपमण्डूकः] a well-abiding frog i.e. one who knows nothing of the world outside the pale of his own village; so @deva[कूपे उदुपान-मण्डूकः]; @deva[उदुम्बरमशि-मशकः] lit. an insect in the fruit of the Udumbara tree; metaphorically it means the same as @deva[कूपमण्डूकः]; @deva[कूपकच्छपः] etc. In some cases there is an aluk of the Loc.; as @deva[शैलूषः] 'a boasting coward', @deva[गेहेखिन्नाषः] 'a braggadocio'; @deva[गेहोनर्दी] 'a house-hero', 'a carpet knight', @deva[भोजने कुशलः] @deva[भोजनेकुशलः] 'clever in eating only' i.e. 'a good-for-nothing fellow', @deva[भोजने चतुरः] @deva[भोजनेचतुरः]; @deva[गोष्ठे कूरः] @deva[गोष्ठेकूरः]; @deva[गोष्ठे विद्वान्] @deva[गोष्ठेविद्वान्]; @deva[गेहे गर्ज्यः] @deva[गेहेगर्ज्यः], etc.

**Note:**—Words of this class do not enter into further combination with other words.

(c) with another, when they both imply a name (@deva[संज्ञा]); there is an aluk of the Loc. term when the noun ends in a consonant or @deva[अ];[^3] as @deva[अरण्ये तिलकाः] lit 'wild sesamum yielding no oil, hence anything not answering to one's expectations;' similarly @deva[वने कसेरुकाः]; @deva[कान्तारे सारः] 'a bamboo' (also @deva[कान्तारसारः], vide Si. IV. 61.). These compounds are nitya (obligatory) as remarked by Bhattoji.

(d) with potential p. participles in @deva[य] implying an obligation; @deva[मासि देयम्] (@deva[ऋणम्]); @deva[पुत्रे देयम्] (@deva[धनम्]); the aluk by § 217 (b) below.

(e) with p. p. participles when it expresses a part of the day or night; @deva[तत्] may take the place of the Loc.;[^4] as @deva[पूर्वाह्णे छन्नम्] @deva[पूर्वाह्णछन्नम्]; @deva[अपराह्णे छन्नम्] @deva[तत्राछन्नम्] etc; but @deva[अह्नि इदम्]; and with the p. p. participles when reproach is implied, with an aluk of the Loc; as '@deva[अवतसेनकुलस्य इव इदम् तव]' 'this thy action is similar to that of a mungoose on the hot ground.' @deva[अवतसेनकुलस्थितम्] is used to express the inconstancy of a person.
