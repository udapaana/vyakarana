## § 705. Active Sense P. P. Participles - Motion and Intransitive Roots

(a) The P. P. Participles of roots implying motion, of intransitive roots, and of the roots @deva[श्लिष्], @deva[वृत्], @deva[स्था], @deva[आस्], @deva[वस्] 'to dwell,' @deva[जन्], @deva[रुह्], and @deva[जृ] 4. cl. have an active sense:

- @deva[गतोऽहं मद्रासपुरम्]: 'I went to Madras'
- @deva[स्थानगतः बालः]: 'the boy has become languid'
- @deva[लक्ष्मीमालिङ्गितः हरिः]: 'Hari embraced Lakshmi'
- @deva[शेषशयितः]: 'slept on Sesha'
- @deva[वैकुण्ठवसितः]: 'dwelt in Vaikuntha'
- @deva[शिवसेवितः]: 'served Shiva'
- @deva[हरिदिनोदितः]: 'observed a fast on the day sacred to Hari'
- @deva[रामजात] @[jātaḥ]: 'was born after Rāma'
- @deva[गरुडासस्थः]: 'sat on Garuḍa'
- @deva[विच्चुन्नरुद्धः]: 'grown after this', etc.
