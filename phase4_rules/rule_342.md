## § 342. Affix Mah

**@deva[मह]** is added to the words @deva[पितृ] and @deva[मातृ] in the sense of 'the father of':

e.g. @deva[पितुः पिता पितामहः]: a paternal grandfather

e.g. @deva[मातामहः]: a maternal grandfather
