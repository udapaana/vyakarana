## § 636. Insertion of र् or ल् in Parasmaipada Frequentative

@deva[र्] or the syllable @deva[रि] or @deva[री] is inserted between the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable and the radical consonant of roots ending in short @deva[अ] or having it for their penultimate; similarly @deva[ल्] or @deva[लि] or @deva[ली] is inserted in the case of @deva[कृप्]; @deva[उत्=उरुत्-वरुत्] or @deva[वरिवृत्] or @deva[वरीवृत्]; @deva[छद्-चछद्], or @deva[चरिछद्] or @deva[चरीछद्]; @deva[कृप्-चकृप्], or @deva[चलिकृप्] @deva[चलीकृप्]; @deva[टुप्-दुटुप्], @deva[दरिटुप्] or @deva[दरीटुप्].
