## § 340. List of Common Taddhita Affixes - Section I: Miscellaneous Affixes

The following is a list of such of the Taddhita affixes as are commonly to be met with.

### SECTION I. MISCELLANEOUS AFFIXES

**@deva[अ]** is added in the sense of:

(1) 'the son of':

e.g. @deva[उपगोः अपत्यं पुमान् औपगवः]: the son of Upagu

e.g. @deva[वासुदेवः] from @deva[वसुदेव]

e.g. @deva[पर्वतस्य अपत्यं स्त्री पार्वती]: the daughter of the mountain, etc.

(2) 'the descendant of':

e.g. @deva[उत्सस्य गोत्रापत्यं पुमान् औत्सः]: a descendant of Utsa

e.g. @deva[उत्सस्य गोत्रापत्यं स्त्री औत्सी]: a female descendant of Utsa (vide § 311, 313)

(3) 'dyed with':

e.g. @deva[हरिद्रया रक्तं हारिद्रं वसनं]: a garment dyed with turmeric

(4) 'made of':

e.g. @deva[देवदारुविकारः दैवदारवः]: made of the fir tree

(5) 'belonging to, etc.':

e.g. @deva[देवस्य अयं देवः]: belonging to a god

e.g. @deva[शर्कराया इदं शार्करं]: of sand

e.g. @deva[ऊर्णाया इदं और्णं वस्त्रं]: a woolen garment

e.g. @deva[ग्रैष्मः]: belonging to the sultry season

e.g. @deva[नैशः]: nocturnal

e.g. @deva[सांवत्सरः]: yearly, etc.

When added to @deva[हेमन्त] the final @deva[त] is dropped, @deva[हेमनः]: autumnal (vide Śiśupālavadha VI.65; Kirātārjunīya XVII.12). @deva[हैमन्तः] means 'agreeable in the autumnal season'.

(6) 'lord of':

e.g. @deva[पृथिव्या ईश्वरः पार्थिवः]: the lord of the earth

e.g. @deva[पञ्चालानां नृपतिः पाञ्चालः]: the king of the Pañcālas

e.g. @deva[ऐक्ष्वाकः]: the king of the Ikṣvāku race

(7) 'a collection of':

e.g. @deva[काकानां समूहः काकं]

e.g. @deva[बकानां समूहः बाकं]: a flock of cranes

Similarly @deva[मायूरं] from @deva[मयूर] 'a peacock', @deva[कापोतं] from @deva[कपोत] 'a pigeon', @deva[भैक्षाणां समूहो भैक्षं]; @deva[गर्भिणीनां समूहो गार्भिणं], etc.

(8) 'knowing or studying':

e.g. @deva[व्याकरणं अधीते वेद वा वैयाकरणः]: one who studies grammar, grammarian, etc.

(9) and to form abstract nouns:

e.g. @deva[मुनेः भावः मौनं]: silence

e.g. @deva[युवन्] → @deva[यौवनं]: youth

e.g. @deva[सुहृद्] → @deva[सौहार्दं]: friendship

e.g. @deva[पथोर्भावः पार्थवं]: greatness, width, etc.
