## § 921. Present for Future with Interrogatives

With interrogatives the Present is often used in the sense of the Future and has also to be regarded as implying @deva[किम्]:

**Examples:**

- @deva[किं करोमि] i.e. @deva[करिष्यामि] What shall I do?
- @deva[कुत्र गच्छामि] i.e. @deva[गमिष्यामि] Whither shall I go?
- @deva[एतेषां कतमं] or @deva[एतेषां किमर्थं भोजनयसि] (i.e. @deva[भोजयिष्यसि] or @deva[भोजयितासि]) which of these persons will you feed?

So @deva[को वै वदति यातेति], &c.; but @deva[कः यातं यदित्याति].

**(a)** It is also used in the sense of the Future in conditional sentences as implying a condition of the occurrence of the desired object:

**Example:**

- @deva[रोकनं दुयूयूति] (@deva[राति दास्यति] वा) स स्युनै याति (@deva[मातु यास्यति] वा) he, who offers or (will offer) food, goes (or will go) to heaven (i.e. if one offers &c.)
