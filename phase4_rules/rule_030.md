## § 30. Nasal Substitution

If a consonant, except @deva[र्] or @deva[ह्], coming at the end of a word, be followed by a nasal, the nasal of its class is optionally substituted for it[^2]; @deva[एतत् + मुरारि: = एतन्मुरारि:] and @deva[एतत्मुरारि:] (see § 22 sec. b.) this Murāri; @deva[षट् + मासा: = षण्मासा:] and @deva[षट्मासा:] six months.




---

[^1]: N.B.** If the nasal belong to a termination the change is necessary; @deva[तन्मात्रम्] that alone; @deva[चिन्मात्रं] mere knowledge. @deva[वाक् + मय = वाङ्मय]. The word @deva[कड्कूवक] (see Rag. IV. 22.) is irregularly formed.
[^2]: @deva[यरोऽन्नासिकेऽन्नासिको वा] Pāṇ. VIII. 4. 45.
