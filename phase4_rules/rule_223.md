## § 223. Karmadhāraya with Cardinal Points and Numerals

Names of quarters and numerals form a Karm. compound with any subanta (a declinable word) but only when the whole compound is an appellation;[^1] e.g. @deva[सप्तर्षयः] 'Name of a constellation of stars,' 'Ursa Major;' @deva[पञ्चजनाः][^2] etc., @deva[पूर्वशालाकुम्] 'Name of a town in the east;' @deva[उत्तरा कुरवः] 'Northern Kurus; @deva[पञ्च ब्राह्मणाः] etc. The compound is forbidden in other cases.

(a) But words denoting a cardinal point or numerals may be compounded with any other noun when a Taddhita termination is to be affixed to the compound so formed (or it conveys in addition to its sense as a Karm a derivative meaning such as is usually denoted by a Tad. aff.) or when the compound itself becomes the first member of another compound or when the compound forms a proper name;[^3] @deva[पूर्वां शालाम्] @deva[पूर्वशालाम्] @deva[पूर्वशालाम्] @deva[शालायां प्रद्] @deva[पौर्वशालः] 'being in the eastern hall' from @deva[पूर्वशाला] + @deva[अ] (i.e. Tad. aff. @deva[अ] added by @deva[द्विपदपूर्वपदात् संख्यायां अण्] Pāṇ. IV.2.107); so @deva[षट्] + @deva[मातृ] = @deva[षण्मातृ] 'six mothers' + Tad. @deva[अ] = @deva[षाण्मातुरः] the son of six mothers; @deva[पूर्वां शालां प्रियां यस्य स] @deva[पूर्वशालाप्रियः] where @deva[पूर्वशाला] is a @deva[पूर्वपद] and not used independently, @deva[उत्तरद्वुस्तः], @deva[दक्षिणद्रुवः] etc. which are names.
