## § 411. Root ज्ञा - न Not Changed to ण्

In the case of the root @deva[ज्ञा] the @deva[न] of @deva[ना] is not changed to @deva[ण्].

e.g. @deva[ज्ञानाति], @deva[ज्ञानीताः], @deva[ज्ञानन्ति], &c.
