## § 202. Shortening of Feminine Endings in Compounds

The final vowel of a feminine noun ending in any of the affixes @deva[आ], @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] and forming the second member of a compound used adjectively is shortened; the @deva[आ] of @deva[गो] is changed to @deva[उ] under the same circumstances.[^1]

e.g. @deva[प्राप्त] + @deva[जीविका] = @deva[प्राप्तजीविकः] (Tat.); @deva[अतिमालः] (Tat.); @deva[पञ्चगुः] something bought for five cows; @deva[वृद्धगौ] @deva[नाञ्चौ] @deva[प्रचिकित्सः] @deva[बहुनाडीः] many-veined @deva[देहः]; @deva[चित्रा गावौ यस्य] @deva[चित्रगुः] having variegated cows, etc.; but @deva[कल्याणपञ्चमीकः] as the @deva[ई] is not final.

(a) If the final @deva[ई] or @deva[ऊ] be not a feminine termination, it remains unchanged; @deva[बहु] @deva[धीः] @deva[सा] @deva[बहुधीः], @deva[बहुतन्त्रीर्वीणी].
