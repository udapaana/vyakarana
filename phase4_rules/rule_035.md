## § 35. Optional श → छ Change

@deva[श] preceded by a word ending in any of the first four letters of a class and followed by a vowel, a semi-vowel, a nasal or @deva[ह्], is optionally changed to @deva[छ्],‡ e.g. @deva[तद् + शिव: = तद् + छिव:]; and @deva[तद् + छिव:], and then @deva[तत् + छिव:], and @deva[तत् + छिव:] by the above, and finally @deva[तच्छिव:] and @deva[तच्छिव:] that Siva (see subsection a, § 28.). Similarly @deva[तच्छ्लोकेन, तच्छ्लोकेन]; but @deva[वाक्श्रोतृति] speech falters (as @deva[श] is not followed by any of the letters mentioned in the rule).
