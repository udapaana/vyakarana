## § 211. Genitive Compounds: Restrictions

(a) It is not compounded with verbal derivatives[^1] ending in @deva[तृ] and @deva[अक] and denoting the agent of the action; @deva[अर्थं दातृ] and not @deva[अर्थदातृ]; @deva[घटस्य कर्ता], @deva[ओदनस्य पाचकः] etc.; but @deva[इष्टकानां भक्षयिता] as the @deva[अक] here does not denote the agent.

**Exception.**—But a compound is allowed in the case of the words @deva[याजक] one who employs as sacrificing priests, @deva[पूजक], @deva[परिचारक], @deva[परिवेषक] a waiter at meals, @deva[स्नापक] a servant who bathes his master or brings bathing water for him, @deva[अध्यापक], @deva[उत्सादक] one who destroys, @deva[हेतु], @deva[भर्तृ], when it does not mean 'a holder,' and others not very important:[^2] @deva[ब्राह्मणयाजकः], @deva[देवपूजकः]; @deva[राजपरिचारकः] etc.; @deva[अग्निहोत्रा], @deva[सोमभर्ता], etc. but @deva[वज्रस्य भर्ता] 'the thunder bolt-bearer'.

(b) A specific Genitive (i.e. from which a singling out is made) is not compounded with any other words,[^3] as @deva[वृक्षाणां द्विजः] @deva[खगः].

(c) A noun in the Genitive is not compounded with ordinal numbers, words denoting qualities, words implying satisfaction, present participles, past participles meaning 'honoured or approved of' or showing the place of the act, verbal indeclinables and verbal nouns ending in @deva[तव];[^4] e.g. @deva[सर्वेषां प्रथमः] @deva[ब्राह्मणस्य श्रेष्ठः] (or @deva[श्रेष्ठतरः]); @deva[फलानां तृप्तः] 'satisfied with fruits' (an Inst. Tat. is, of course, allowed in these cases); @deva[हितस्य कुर्वन्] @deva[कुर्वाणो वा] @deva[हितकुर्]; @deva[सज्जनैः मतः] esteemed by the good; @deva[राज्ञा पूजितः] @deva[इदं आसितं] (place of sitting) @deva[गतं] @deva[सुप्तं वा]; @deva[ब्राह्मणस्य दत्वा], @deva[नरस्य कर्तव्यम्] etc.

**N. B.** Compounds like @deva[राजपूजितः], @deva[राजमतः], etc. ought to be considered as Instrumental Tatpurushas.

### Exceptions

(1) If the word denoting a quality ends in @deva[तर] it does combine with a noun in the Genitive, and the @deva[तर] is dropped; @deva[सर्वेषां श्वेततरः] @deva[सर्वेश्वेतः] whiter than all others; similarly @deva[सर्वेषां महान्तरः] @deva[सर्वेमहान्] etc.

(2) The words @deva[द्वितीय], @deva[तृतीय], @deva[चतुर्थ] and @deva[तुर्य] are compounded with nouns signifying the whole of which they are parts, and are optionally placed first,[^5] @deva[द्वितीयं भिक्षायाः] @deva[द्वितीयभिक्षा], @deva[भिक्षाद्वितीयम्] half of the quantity of alms; but @deva[द्वितीयं भिक्षायाः] (begging alms a second time) @deva[भिक्षुकस्य].

**Note**—Compounds like @deva[द्वितीयभिक्षा], @deva[पूर्वकायः] (see further on) etc. are not strictly Gen. Tat, as the case of the first word determines the name, but mere Tat.; some call these Nom. Tat (@deva[प्रथमातत्पुरुष]).

(d) A noun in the Genitive, used as an object in a sentence, when the agent and object of the action denoted by the nouns formed by krit affixes are both used, is not compounded,[^6] as @deva[आश्चर्यं गवां दोहनं अगोपेन] 'the milking of cows by one, not a cow-herd, is a wonder.'
