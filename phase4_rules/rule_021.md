## [^4] 21. Vṛddhi Substitution for ए/ऐ and ओ/औ

When @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] is followed by @deva[ए] or @deva[ऐ] and @deva[ओ] or @deva[औ], @deva[ऐ] and @deva[औ] are respectively substituted for both;[^2] @deva[छुप्य + एकन्त = छुप्यैकन्त्य]; @deva[देव + ऐश्वर्य = दैवैश्वर्य] the majesty of god; @deva[सा + एव = सैव] she alone; @deva[भव + औषध्य = भवौषध्य] medicine against birth and re-birth. @deva[विद्या + औत्सुक्यं = विद्यौत्सुक्यं] longing for knowledge.

### Exceptions

### (a) Preposition + Root beginning with ए/औ

If a preposition ending in @deva[अ] be followed by a root beginning with @deva[ए] or @deva[औ], @deva[ए] or @deva[औ] is substituted;[^1] @deva[प्र + एजते = प्रेजते] quakes very much; @deva[उप + ओषति = उपोषति] burns something near; but if the root be a denominative, vṛddhi is optional; @deva[उप + एककीयति = उपेककीयति] or @deva[उपैककीयति]; @deva[प्र + ओषीयति = प्रोषीयति] or @deva[प्रौषीयति].


### (b) Indefinite Sense

When @deva[अ] is followed by @deva[एत्र] in an indefinite sense, @deva[ए] is substituted for both: @deva[क + एत्र = केत्र भोक्ष्यते] where will you dine to-day? (The place being not definitely known); but @deva[तत्रैव] (why, I shall dine at your house).

### (c) Before ओम् and आ

@deva[अ] followed by @deva[ओं] and @deva[आ] (prep.) is dropped e.g. @deva[शिवाय + ओं नमः = शिवायों नमः]; @deva[शिव + एहि] (@deva[आ + इहि]) @deva[= शिवेहि].

### (d) Optional Vṛddhi before ओष्/ओष्ठ

The vṛddhi is optional when the ending @deva[अ] of a word is followed by @deva[ओष्] a cat, or @deva[ओष्ठ] the lower lip, in a compound: @deva[स्थूल + ओष्ः = स्थूलौष्ः] or @deva[स्थूलोष्ः]; @deva[विम्बौष्, विम्बोष्ः].

### (e) Elision in Special Compounds

The final vowel or the final consonant with the preceding vowel is dropped in the case of the following words when followed by certain words in a compound:[^4] @deva[शाक] the country of the Śakas @deva[+ अम्बु] a well @deva[= शाकम्बु]; @deva[कम्बू] name of a country @deva[+ अम्बु = कम्बम्बु]; @deva[कुल + अटा = कुलटा] one who moves from one mansion to another, a woman of bad character; @deva[सीमन् + अन्तः = सीमन्तः].

\[^1] @deva[वृद्धिरेचि] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 88.
