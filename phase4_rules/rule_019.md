## § 19. Similar Vowel Combination

If a simple vowel, short or long, be followed by a similar vowel, short or long, the substitute for them both is the similar long vowel* e.g. @deva[देव + अरि: = देवारि:]; @deva[यज्ञ + आसीत् = यज्ञासीत्]; @deva[पद्म + अभवत् = पद्माभवत्]; @deva[विद्या + आतुर: = विद्यातुर:] eager to gain knowledge; @deva[इति + ईश = इतीश]; @deva[अपि + ईश्वते = अपीश्वते]; @deva[श्री + ईश: = श्रीश:]; @deva[भानु + उदय = भानूदय:]; @deva[साधु + ऊरु: = साधूरु:]; @deva[चमू + ऊर्जी: = चमूर्जी:] the strength of the army; @deva[कवि + ऋजु: = कवीर्जु:], @deva[छृ + ऋकार: = छॄकार:]; @deva[होतृ + टृकार: = होतॄकार:] the ṭkāra pronounced by the sacrificer (since there is no long @deva[टृ] long @deva[ऋ] is substituted for both).

### (a) Optional Substitution for ऋ/टृ

If @deva[ऋ] or @deva[टृ] be followed by a short @deva[ऋ] or @deva[टृ], short @deva[ऋ] or @deva[टृ] is optionally substituted for both;[^1] @deva[होतृ + ऋकार: = होतृकार:], and also @deva[होतर्कार:] (see § 23, subsection b); so three forms altogether @deva[होतृकार:, होतकार:] and @deva[होतर्कार:]; @deva[होतृ + टृकार: = होतृटृकार:] and also @deva[होतृट्कार:].

\* @deva[अकः सवर्णे दीर्घः] Pāṇ. VI. 1. 101.
