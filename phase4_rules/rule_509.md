## § 509. Roots Dropping Nasal in Perfect

The roots @deva[प्रच्य], @deva[मच्य], @deva[क्रच्य], and @deva[स्वञ्ज्] drop their nasal optionally before the terminations of the Perfect.

@deva[श्रञ्च्], @deva[ग्रञ्च्], @deva[दंश्] obey § 500, even before the strong terminations when their nasal is dropped.

### @deva[प्रच्य]

| Person | With nasal | Without nasal |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 sing. | @deva[प्रप्रच्य] | @deva[प्रेय] |
| 1 dual | @deva[प्रप्रच्यव] | @deva[प्रेयिव] |
| 1 pl. | @deva[प्रप्रच्यम] | @deva[प्रेयिम] |
| 2 sing. | @deva[प्रप्रच्यिय] | @deva[प्रेयिय] |
| 2 dual | @deva[प्रप्रच्युः] | @deva[प्रेयुः] |
| 2 pl. | @deva[प्रप्रच्य] | @deva[प्रेय] |
| 3 sing. | @deva[प्रप्रच्य] | @deva[प्रेय] |
| 3 dual | @deva[प्रप्रच्युः] | @deva[प्रेयतुः] |
| 3 pl. | @deva[प्रप्रच्यः] | @deva[प्रेयः] |

### @deva[मच्य]

Similarly conjugated: @deva[जमच्य] / @deva[येय], @deva[जम्रच्यव] / @deva[येयिव], @deva[जम्रच्यम] / @deva[येयिम], etc.

### @deva[दंश्] P. and @deva[स्वञ्ज्] Ā.

Forms include @deva[दंश] / @deva[देम], @deva[दंशिव] / @deva[देमिव], @deva[दंशिम] / @deva[देमिम], etc.

**N. B.**— The final @deva[ह्] of the roots @deva[दुह्], @deva[मुह्], @deva[स्निह्], and @deva[स्नुह्] is changed to @deva[घ] or @deva[व] when followed by any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel or by nothing.[^1]

---

[^1]: This is referenced in the text and applies to roots like @deva[दुह्], @deva[मुह्], @deva[स्निह्], and @deva[स्नुह्].
