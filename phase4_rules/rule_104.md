## § 104. Bases derived from the root अञ्च् (to go, to worship)

Nouns derived from the root @deva[अञ्च्] ('to go', 'to worship') by means of such affixes as @deva[क्विप्], @deva[किप्] etc., such as:

- @deva[प्राञ्च्] (@deva[प्र-अञ्च्]): eastern
- @deva[प्रत्यञ्च्] (@deva[प्रति-अञ्च्]): western
- @deva[सम्यञ्च्] (@deva[सम्-अञ्च्]): a companion
- @deva[तिर्यञ्च्] (@deva[तिरद्-अञ्च्]): going away
- @deva[सम्यञ्च्] (@deva[सम्-अञ्च्]): going with or well
- @deva[विष्वञ्च्] (@deva[विष्वब्-अञ्च्]): following
- @deva[देवयञ्च्] (@deva[देव-अञ्च्]): adoring a deity
- @deva[सर्वयञ्च्] (@deva[सर्वे-अञ्च्]): going everywhere
- @deva[उदञ्च्]: northern
- @deva[अन्वञ्च्]: following

### Declension Rules

(a) In the Nom. sing., @deva[अञ्च्] becomes @deva[अङ्]. The nasal of @deva[अञ्च्] is dropped before all terminations beginning with the Acc. pl., and the preceding semi-vowel is changed to its corresponding long vowel before the vowel terminations, after which @deva[अ] is dropped. When @deva[अञ्च्] is not preceded by a semi-vowel, its @deva[अ] is changed to @deva[ई] if not preceded by a long vowel; otherwise it remains unchanged. @deva[तिर्यञ्च्] becomes @deva[तिरश्च्] under similar circumstances. In other respects these words are declined like words ending in @deva[च्].
