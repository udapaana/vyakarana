## § 413. Root गृह् - न Changed to ह्

The @deva[न] of @deva[गृह्] is changed to @deva[ह्].

e.g. @deva[गृह्णाति]; @deva[अगृह्णाम्], @deva[अगृह्णीत], @deva[अगृह्णीम], &c. Imperf. 1st. pers.
