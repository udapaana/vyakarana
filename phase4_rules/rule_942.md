## § 942. Simple Future for Wonder

When the idea of wonder is to be expressed and the words @deva[काच्], @deva[मान] and @deva[यदि] do not occur in a sentence, the Simple Future should be used:

@deva[परान्धन्योऽधन्योऽपि हरिं पश्यति हरिं पक्ष्यति] it is a wonder that a blind man sees Hari.

**(a)** The Simple Future is also used when the particles @deva[नु] and @deva[आपि] expressing a doubt are used:

- @deva[नु दण्डः पतिष्यति] will the stick fall?
- @deva[आपि कापारामति द्वारं] will he close the door?

**(b)** This Future is also used when the particle @deva[अहम्] meaning sure or able is used:

@deva[अहं हतुं निश्चय] Kṛishṇa is sure or able to kill the elephant.
