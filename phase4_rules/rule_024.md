## [^5] 24. Aya-Ādi Substitution

@deva[ए, ओ, ऐ] and @deva[औ], when followed by a vowel, are changed to @deva[अय, अव, आय], and @deva[आव] respectively[^3]; e.g. @deva[हरे + ए = हरय] for Hari; @deva[विष्णो + ए = विष्णव] for Viṣṇu; @deva[ने + अक: = नायक:] A leader; @deva[पो + अक: = पावक:] the purifier i.e. fire.

### (a) Optional Dropping of य/व

@deva[य] or @deva[व] at the end of a word and preceded by @deva[अ] or @deva[आ] is optionally dropped when followed by a vowel or a soft consonant[^1]; e.g. @deva[हरे + एहि = हरयेहि] or @deva[हर एहि]; @deva[विष्णो + इह = विष्णविह] or @deva[विष्णु इह]; @deva[श्रिये + उपत: = श्रियायुपत:] or @deva[श्रिया उपत:] intent on getting money; @deva[गुरो + उत्क: = गुरावत्क:] or @deva[गुरा उत्क:] longing to see the preceptor.


### (b) Before य Termination

@deva[ओ] and @deva[औ], when followed by a termination beginning with @deva[य], become @deva[अव्] and @deva[आव्] respectively[^4]; e.g. @deva[गो + य = गव्यम्] produced from a cow as milk, ghee &c. @deva[नौ + य = नाव्यम्] accessible by a boat, navigable.


### (c) गो before Consonants

The @deva[ओ] of the word @deva[गो] is also changed to @deva[अव्] when followed by [consonants].
