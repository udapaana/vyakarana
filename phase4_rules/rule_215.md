## § 215. Aluk of the Genitive

Instances of the Aluk of the Gen. The noun in the Genitive may retain its case ending—[^1]

(a) when reproach is to be implied, as @deva[चोरस्य कुलम्], but @deva[ब्राह्मणकुलम्]. Also when @deva[देवानाम्] is followed by @deva[प्रिय] and the whole means a fool; otherwise @deva[देवप्रियः].

(b) when the words @deva[वाचस्], @deva[दिवस्] and @deva[प्रत्यक्षतस्] are followed by @deva[युक्ति], @deva[दृष्ट] and @deva[हर] respectively; as @deva[वाचस्युक्तिः] 'skilful speech,' @deva[दिवोदृष्टः] 'a particular appearance of stars in the shape of a staff in the sky,' @deva[प्रत्यक्षतोहरः] 'one who robs another in his very presence while he is looking on,' hence 'a goldsmith, a rogue.'

(c) in the following cases:—@deva[दिवोदासः] 'a king of Kāshi,' @deva[दिवस्पतिः] 'Indra,' @deva[वाचस्पतिः] lord of speech, Brihaspati; @deva[शुनःशेफः], @deva[शुनःपुच्छः] and @deva[शुनोलाङ्गूलः] 'sons of Ajigarta.'

(d) and optionally when followed by @deva[पुत्र] and censure is implied;[^2] @deva[दास्याः पुत्रः] or @deva[दास्योपुत्रः] a bastard, a knave; but @deva[ब्राह्मणपुत्रः].

(e) When a word ending in @deva[इक] is compounded with another and there is the relation by blood or some kind of learning, the aluk is necessary; and optional when it is followed by @deva[स्वसृ] and @deva[शिष्य].[^3] The initial @deva[स्] of @deva[स्वसृ] is optionally changed to @deva[ष] when preceded by @deva[मातुर्] or @deva[पितृ] and when there is aluk, and necessarily when there is no aluk in a compound; e.g. @deva[होतुः स्वसा], @deva[होतृशिष्यः] a pupil of the hotri; @deva[मातुः स्वसा], or @deva[मातुष्वसा] and @deva[मातृष्वसा]; so @deva[पितुः स्वसा], etc. (but @deva[मातुः स्वसा], @deva[पितुः स्वसा] when uncompounded); @deva[स्वसुःपतिः] or @deva[स्वसृपतिः]; but @deva[होतृधनम्].
