## § 618. Vowel Changes before the Desiderative Affix

The radical vowels undergo the following changes before @deva[स]—

**(a)** The @deva[स] with @deva[इ] is strong and without @deva[इ] weak.

The usual rule as to Guṇa substitute holds good in the one case and does not in the other; @deva[इष्-विदत्सिते], @deva[विदत्स]; @deva[इ-विपत्सि], &c.

**(b)** The final @deva[ऋ] and @deva[उ] and the penultimate @deva[अ] of @deva[इष्] and @deva[मृ] (substituted for @deva[म्] 2 Par. 'to go,' and for @deva[स्] with @deva[अधि] 'to remember' or 'to study') are lengthened and final @deva[अ], short or long, changed to @deva[ई], or to @deva[उ] after labials or @deva[य] when the @deva[स] is unaugmented; @deva[जि-जिगीष], @deva[इ-इयुष], @deva[छ-चिकीर्ष], @deva[नृ-तितीर्ष], @deva[प्र-धरर्ष], @deva[प्र-उपर्ष], &c.

**(c)** The roots @deva[रुह्], @deva[विद्] and @deva[द्रप्] do not change their vowel to Guṇa; and the roots @deva[ग्रह्], @deva[स्वप्] and @deva[प्रच्छ] take Samprasāraṇa; @deva[रुरुहिष], @deva[विविदिष], @deva[दुद्रपिष], @deva[जिघृष], @deva[सुषुप्स], @deva[पिपृच्छिष].

**(d)** Roots with an initial consonant, and having @deva[इ] or @deva[उ] short for their penultimate, and ending in any consonant, except @deva[न्] or @deva[म्], change their vowel to Guṇa optionally when @deva[इ] is prefixed to @deva[स]; @deva[उप-दिघृतिष], @deva[दिघोतिष]; @deva[उद्-दुद्रविष] or @deva[दुद्रोविष] &c.
