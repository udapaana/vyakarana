## § 630. Insertion of म् in Ātmanepada Frequentative

When a root ends in a nasal preceded by @deva[अ] and the nasal is not dropped, @deva[म्] (changeable to an Anusvāra or the nasal of the class to which the letter following it belongs) is inserted between the reduplicative @deva[अ] and the first radical consonant; the @deva[अ] of the reduplicative syllable remains unchanged (against @ref[§ 629 b]).

@deva[यम्-यम्य, पयम्य=येयम्यते] or @deva[यैयम्यते], @deva[जन्-जन्य-जञ्जन्य=जेजन्यते] or @deva[जञ्जन्यते], but when @deva[जन्=जाप्], the A. Freq. base is @deva[जाजाय], 3rd sing. @deva[जाजायते].

**(a)** The same rule applies to the roots @deva[चर्], @deva[फल्], @deva[जप्], @deva[जभ्], @deva[दह्], @deva[दंश्], @deva[भ्रम्] and @deva[पर्]. @deva[चर्] and @deva[फल्] after inserting @deva[र्] change the @deva[अ] of the following syllable to @deva[उ]; @deva[चर्=चर्य-चचर्य=चेचुर्य] or @deva[चञ्चुर्य=चेचुर्यते] or @deva[चञ्चुर्यते] by @ref[§ 394]. @deva[फल्=फल्य=पफल्य=पेफुल्यते] or @deva[पम्फुल्यते]; @deva[दह्=दह्य-ददह्य=देदह्यते] or @deva[दन्दह्यते] @deva[जप्—जेजप्यते] or @deva[जञ्जप्यते].
