## § 225. Double Adjective and Participle Karmadhāraya

Two adjectives may also be compounded into what is called the @deva[विशेषणोभयपदकर्मधारयः] as @deva[युवकृष्णः], @deva[सुरुणसरस्वरः].

(a) Two participles expressive of two consecutive actions of the same individual may be compounded together, the one expressive of the previous action being placed first;[^1] @deva[आदौ स्नातः पश्चात् अनुलिप्तः] @deva[स्नातानुलिप्तः] 'first bathed and then anointed'; similarly @deva[पीतोद्वान्तम्] 'first drunk and afterwards vomited out', @deva[पीतप्रतिबद्धः]; @deva[यथावितप्रतिष्ठक्तः] (Rag. II.1, IV.43), etc.

(b) In the case of the two sets of words given above (§ 222, g.) forming compounds with one another those beginning with @deva[पूर्व] are placed first. In the case of @deva[एक] compounded with @deva[वीर] it may precede or follow it; as @deva[वीरैकः] or @deva[एकवीरः]; the former, however, is preferable. In the case of the first set of words viz. @deva[एक]...@deva[केवल] the one succeeding precedes the one preceding when compounded together; as @deva[पुराणजरद्], @deva[केवलसर्वम्], etc.

(c) A past participle is compounded with another with the negative particle prefixed to it;[^2] '@deva[कृताकृतम्],' what is done and not done, i.e. 'partially done'.

(d) The word @deva[युवन्] (m. f.) enters into a Karm. comp. with the words @deva[खलित], @deva[पलित], @deva[वलित] having wrinkles, and @deva[जरती], and is placed first;[^3] i.e. @deva[युवा] + @deva[खलितः] @deva[युवखलितः] 'a young bald-headed man'; @deva[स्त्री] 'a young bald-headed woman'; @deva[युवजरती] 'a young woman looking old'; @deva[युवपलितः] 'a young man whose hair is grey', etc.
