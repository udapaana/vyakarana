## § 701. Irregular P. P. Participles

The following roots form their P. P. Participles irregularly:

| Roots | P. P. Ptc. | Roots | P. P. Ptc. |
|-------|------------|-------|------------|
| @deva[अनुड्] with @deva[अव] | @deva[अवनुड], @deva[अनूड] | @deva[ध्या] 'to instruct' | @deva[ध्यात] |
| with @deva[सम्] | @deva[सनुड], @deva[सनूट] | @deva[दो] 'to cut' | @deva[दुन्न] |
| with @deva[वि] or @deva[नि] | @deva[व्युण] | @deva[दुह्] 'to be bulky or strong' | @deva[दुह] |
| with @deva[अभि] in the sense of 'coming near' | @deva[अभ्युण्ण] | otherwise | @deva[दोहित] |
| in other cases | @deva[अबिद्धत] | @deva[अद्] 'to eat' | @deva[अन्न] |
| @deva[ऊर्ण्] 1. A. 'to weave' | @deva[ऊत] | @deva[पाच्] 'to cleanse' | @deva[पीत] or @deva[पाचित] |
| @deva[कृच्छ्] 'to be difficult or painful' | @deva[कृच], @deva[कृछ], @deva[कृट], @deva[कृत], @deva[कृच्छ्राण]. The grammar of this root is very difficult; @deva[कृच], @deva[वर्ण], etc. | @deva[पे] 'to suck' | @deva[पीत] |
| but @deva[कृचित], @deva[महीमाम्] 'gold rubbed on the touchstone' | | @deva[पच्] 'to cook' | @deva[पक्व] |
| @deva[कुल्य] 'to be lean' | @deva[कृत] | @deva[पा] 'to drink' | @deva[पीत] |
| @deva[क्षिव्] 'to be intoxicated' | @deva[क्षीव] | @deva[पूय्] 'to stink, to putrify' | @deva[पूत] |
| @deva[ज्ञाप्] 'to stink' | @deva[क्षाव] | @deva[फल्] 'to expand' | @deva[फुल्ल] |
| @deva[चात्] 'to shake' | @deva[चाग] | @deva[बन्ध्] 'to bind' | @deva[बद्ध] |
| @deva[छो] 'to be thin' | @deva[छाम] | @deva[मा] 'to measure' | @deva[मित] |
| @deva[गै] 'to sing' | @deva[गीत] | @deva[मृछ] 'to barter' | @deva[मीत] |
| @deva[छा] 'to split' | @deva[छात] or @deva[छिन] | @deva[मुर्च्छ्] 'to faint' | @deva[मूत] or @deva[मूर्च्छित] |
| @deva[त्रिह्] 'to go, to become dry' | @deva[त्रुट] | @deva[स्मृ] with @deva[उत्] | @deva[उद्धृत] 'convalescent' |
| @deva[ह्राद्] 'to delight' | @deva[ह्रेष्ट] | @deva[स्था] with @deva[प्र] or @deva[वि] 'a master' | @deva[प्रस्थीत] or @deva[प्रस्तीत] |
| @deva[व्या] 'to cook' (also @deva[व्यञ्] cau. of @deva[वृत्]) | @deva[बत] 'boiled' (when it qualifies @deva[क्षीर] or @deva[पयस्]) @deva[व्यञ्जन], @deva[अपक्त] | with @deva[परि] | @deva[परिद्र] or @deva[परिद्रुहित], etc. 'grown, increased' |
| @deva[स्मर्] with @deva[प्रति] and @deva[वि] | @deva[प्रतिस्मुर], @deva[विस्मर्] (where the @deva[र] is not changed to @deva[ऋ]) | @deva[स्ञा] with @deva[नि] | @deva[निङ्गीत] 'clever' |
| @deva[स्फाय्] 'to grow' | @deva[स्फीत] | with @deva[नव] | @deva[नवीष्ठा] 'skilful, experienced, clever' (lit. one who knows the dangerous spots in a river) |
| @deva[स्पृद्] with @deva[प्र] | @deva[प्रस्पीत] or @deva[प्रस्तीत] 'sounded' | | but @deva[निवेवात], @deva[नवीस्मात] in other cases |
| @deva[शो] 'to sharpen' | @deva[शात] or @deva[शित] | | |
