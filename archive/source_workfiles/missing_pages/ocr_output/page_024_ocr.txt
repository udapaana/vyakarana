18                    SANSKRIT GRAMMAR.                    [§ 22, 23

(a) A consonant except र्, preceded by a vowel, is optionally 
doubled, if no vowel follows*; गुरु + उपाध्याय = गुरूपाध्याय: and 
गुरुपु + उपाध्याय:—

(b) When a consonant, except a nasal or a semivowel, is 
followed by a soft unaspirate or aspirate (3rd or 4th letter of 
a class) it is changed to the soft unaspirate (3rd letter) of its 
class†; गुरु + उपाध्याय: = गुरुडुपाध्याय

(c) Semivowels, when preceded by a letter of the first five 
classes except र् are optionally doubled§; thus गुर् + उपाध्याय: = 
गुर्र् + उपाध्याय: and गुर्रुपु + उपाध्याय:; हृदय + उपाध्याय: = हृद्दय + उपाध्याय: 
and हृद्धयुपु + उपाध्याय: ! Thus there are four forms ultimately, 
viz. सुधषुपाध्याय:, सुडडुपाध्याय: and सुद्धयुपाध्याय: !

Similarly वाच् + अति: = वाचति:, वाच्चति:, वाच्छति:, and वाज्जति: 
वादु + अंश: = वाडश: and वाडडश: In छ + आकृति: of course there 
is no possibility of any other form.

(d) A consonant except र्, coming after र् or ष् preceded by a 
vowel is optionally doubled†; e.g. हरि + अनुभव: = हर्यु + अनुभव: = 
हर्यानुभव: by the general rule, and optionally हर्ययु + अनुभव: = 
हर्यानुभव: (Hari's experience) by this rule; so न हि + अस्ति = न 
ह्यस्ति and न ह्यास्ति !
