Sch, of G.—A mixture of इन्द्रवसा and उपेम्द्रवज्ना gives rise to
the metry called उवजाति. It is said to have fourteen different
varieties, For examples 666 Rag. II, &c. Kum. Ill. Kir, XVU.
Bhatti, IT. &e.

When other two metres are mixed in one stanza, the mixture
is still called Upajati, as in the following verse from the
Sigupalavadba which is a combination of बज्ञस्यविल and इन्धरवेश्ा-

इत्थं रथाभ्ेभनिषादिनां प्रगे गजे वृपाणामथ तोरणाद्वहिः ।
