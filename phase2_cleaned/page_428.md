§ 702. (a) The roots इ and यज् take the affix वत् like त्
in an active sense; इत्वत् 'one who extracted Soma juice;'
यजत्वत् 'one who has offered a sacrifice;' अत् is added to जु
optionally in the same sense; जीर्णः or जग्वतः 'one who has
become old' (also जग्मिवत्).

§ 703. The participles in त् or न follow the Declension of
nouns in अ.

§ The P. P. participles have not always a passive force:—

§ 704. * The affix त् added to roots having the sense of
'staying,' or 'going' shows the place where the action usually
takes place; अत्र मुकुन्दस्य आसितं 'this is the place where Mukunda
to sit;' इदं गतं रामस्य 'this is the path by which the husband
of Rāmā used to pass;' अत्रुहौ एतत् अस्य 'this is the place where
Anaṅta took his food,' &c.
