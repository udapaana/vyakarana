नु—with आ* is Ātम. आनुते 'he praises.'

मञ्ज्ञू*—with आ is Ātम. in the sense of 'taking leave of'—
"आनुच्छस्त तिरस्सुसुमयु" Meg. 10. 'take leave of this thy dear
friend,' also with the preposition सम् when used intransitively,
सङुच्छते 'he ascertains.'

भुज्—is Ātम. except in the sense of 'protecting,' शोदाय हुङ्के
'eats food,' भुयुते दृशिवरीयाः इचिरीषेय कैलाश 'the protector of
the earth enjoyed (experienced the pleasure from the possession
of) the earth alone;' पुड्ड्डो जनां दुस्सरसतिरि हुङ्के 'old people suffer
hundreds of miseries;' मही शुयक्ते 'protects the world.'

मुच्—with परि is Par.; परिमुच्छति 'endures;' but आइुयते 'he
touches.'

यम्‡—with आ is Ātम. either when used intransitively or has
for the object a part of the agent for its object; स्वर्गं वायव: 'the
tree spreads;' आयुयते पानिं 'stretches forth the hand;' but
आयच्छुते कुगारुड्भू 'draws up the rope from the well;' preceded
by सम्, वृट् and आ it is Ātम. except when it has a literary
work for its object; वस्त्रं आयुयते 'puts on a garment;' सारुड्भुय्यते
