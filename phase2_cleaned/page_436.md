§ 732. The following words are derived from the root यह्
with the affix क्यप्—अवयह्यः; प्रयह्यः, पदे 'two technical terms in
grammar;' पयुक्तः 'birds restrained from free motion, such as
parrots, &c.' प्रवायुरेव्येन परोहत्राक्ता उपक्षये। Sid. Kau.
ग्रामयुद्धे सेना 'an army stationed outside a village;' आर्युवाहास्ते
आयुयुद्धे: तिर्व्यामुद्धीव उत्यर्थः (Sid. Kau.) 'siding with the noble.'
See Rāg. II. 43.

§ 733. The roots कृ and दृ take both क्यप् and एयत्; कृत्यः,
कार्यः; दृत्यः; दार्यः 'what ought to be chosen, best,' &c.

§ 734. युज् in the sense of 'to be harnessed' takes the affix
क्यप् and changes its final to ग्; युग्यः गौः 'a bull to be harnessed
to the yoke;' in other sense it takes the affix त्; युक्तः.

By means of the affix घ्यत्.

§ 735. Roots ending in ऋ, and those ending in a consonant
take the affix घ्यत् (य) in the same sense as क्यप्. Before this affix
the ending चु and व of a root are changed to ऋ and यु respecti-
vely and the final vowel and the penultimate अ take Vṛiddhi
substitute; any other penultimate vowel generally takes Guṇa.

कृ—कार्यं 'what ought to be done,' धृ—धार्यं 'what ought to be
worn,' दा—देयः; हृ—हार्यं; पद्—दृदु—पार्यः 'what is to be thought of,' &c.;
वच्—वाच्यं 'what is meant in a sentence,' पच्—पाच्यं 'what is to
be cooked,' वुज्—भोज्यं 'what is to be purified,' &c.

§ 736. The root पद् when preceded by अमा 'with' takes the
affix घ्यत् before which the penultimate अ is optionally changed
to Vṛiddhi; अमा रुङु गुवातोऽव्या चन्द्रा:शो अवतिब्धुवा or वाश्या 'the day
on which the sun and the moon are with each other' i. e. are in
conjunction.'

(a) युज् when preceded by पाणिं or the preposition समत् takes
the affix घ्यत्; as पाणिस्यो डुयुयोऽ पाणिगुम्योऽ यृज्यः; similarly समवसुयुर्यं.
