§ 481. इ is to be prefixed, or not, or optionally to the terminations given above, according as the root is Seṭ or Aniṭ or Veṭ. Before the terminations the final vowel and the penultimate vowel take their Guṇa substitute.

§ 482. In the Conditional the augment अ is prefixed to the root as in the Imperfect.
