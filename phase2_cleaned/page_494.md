THE GENITIVE CASE.

§ 846 ‡ The Genitive, as already remarked, is not a Kāraka
case. It, therefore, expresses simply the relation of one noun
to the other in a sentence; e. g. that of servant and his
master (a relation which is other than that expressed by a
Kāraka case); रात्रुं पुरुयुः पुयुस्युरु मातुरु प्रयुस्युरु सुणु &c; and even
in those cases where the genitive is used in the sense of
other cases it expresses simple relation or संवुन्युयुयु only, as in
युति गवुस् गोयोर्भुयु युस्त्युतिकुरुर्तोरुयु यथुस्युनं, एयुयु उदुदुयुयुयुस्यु उयुदुयुनैरुयुतु: भेज्युं
मुयुनोभुरुयुयुवुयो; फुटुयुनों दुयुयु, &c.
