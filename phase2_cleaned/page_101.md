5—Reflexive Pronouns.

§ 140. The sense of the reflexive pronoun is, in Sanskrit, expressed by the word आत्मन् 'self' which is invariably used in the masculine gender and singular number, and by the reflexive adverb स्वयम् e.g. गुप्तं ददृशुरात्मानं सर्वाः स्वप्नेषु वामनैः "they all (the wives of Das'aratha ) saw in dreams that they were guarded by dwarfs; " सः or सा कृतापराधमिव आत्मानमवगच्छति । राजा स्वयं समरभूमि जगाम, &c.

6--- Indefinite Pronouns.

§ 141. Indefinite pronouns are formed by the addition of चित् or चन or अपि and sometimes of स्विद् to the various cases of the pronoun किम् in all the genders; g. कश्चित्, कश्चन a certain one, कोपि, केनापि, कयाचन, कस्यापि; कस्विद्, &c.

§ 142. The terminations given above are also added to interrogative adverbs in the sense of indefiniteness; as कदाचित् at some time, कदाचन, कतिचित् some, क्वचित् somewhere, &c.

7 - Correlative Pronouns.

§143. Correlatives are formed by the affix वत् added to यद्, तद् and एतद्, and यत् added to इदम् and किम् in the sense of as much as; and दृश or दृश् in the sense of 'similar to'; तद्, एतद् and यह become ता, एता and या respectively before these, and इदम् and किम् इ and कि before वत् and ई and की before दृश or दृश्; e. g. तावत् (तत्परिमाणमस्य ), इयत् ( इदं परिमाणमस्य ), तादृश like that, ईदृश, like this, कियत् how much, &c.
