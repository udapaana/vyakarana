§ 590. There are three kinds of Passive Verbs:—(1) the
Passive properly so called (कर्मणि प्र०), as राघेण क्रयं दीयते;
(2) an Impersonal Passive (भावे प्र०), as गच्यते 'it is gone;'
and (3) a Reflexive (आत्मनेपदी प्र०), as ओदनं पच्यते 'rice
is cooked'

CONJUGATIONAL TENSES.

§ 591. Formation of base:—

(a) य is added to the root, which is weak i. e. no Guṇa or
Vṛddhi substitute takes place before it; नी—नीय, चित्—चिद्य.

(b) Before य roots undergo the same changes as they do
before the Âtmanepadi terminations of the Benedictive; चि—चीय, कृ—
क्रिय, स्तृ—स्तर्य, ऋ—अर्य, कृ—कार्य, पु—पूय, वच्-वच्य, (but नित्य-
निर्य), वच्-उच्य, यह्—यह्य, &c.

(c) The final आ (original or substituted) of दा 'to give,'
दे, दो, धा, मा, ह्लादा, मा, घे, प्रा 'to drink,' पा and हा 'to abandon' is
changed to ई in other cases it remains unchanged; दा and हा—
दीय, घेघीय, हा— ह्रीय; but दा 'to cut, to purify'—द्याय, हा—
ज्ञाय, पा—यायात्.
