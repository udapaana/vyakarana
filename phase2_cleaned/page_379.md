§ 619 The formation of Desiderative bases of causals and
of roots of the Tenth class does not differ from that of
primitive roots

Art § 550. should be attended to in forming Desideratives
from Causals and roots of the Tenth class.

§ 620 Roots in their Desiderative forms take the same
terminations, Parasmai or Ātmane, that they do in the primitive.
The roots वृ, मृ, हु and टनु take Ātm. terminations in the
Desiderative.
