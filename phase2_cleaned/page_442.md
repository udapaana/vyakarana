2. Indeclinable Participles formed with य.

§ 751. When a root is compounded with one or more pre-
positions or words prefixed to verbs like prepositions, the
indeclinable participle is formed by affixing य immediately to
it; the य is changed to त्य after a short radical vowel (even
when it combines with the final vowel of a preposition to a
long vowel); e. g.

    दा with आ—आदाय              प्र with प्र—प्रेत्य
    मिद् with विश्—विज्ञाय           कृ with सम्—संस्कृत्य
    जि with परा—पराजित्य          कृ with विश्—विभक्त्य
    नी with वि—विनीय             वद् with नि—निवाच्य
    भू with अनु—अनुभूय            मद् with उत्—उत्पद्य
    इ with अभि—अभीत्य           &c.    &c.    &c.

§ 752. The rules given at §§ 394, 395, 459, 502 and 587
apply to the roots also in the gerund in य:—

    विद् with प्र—प्रवीद्य         अच् with प्र—प्राच्य
    कृ with अव—अवकीर्य         वस् with प्र—प्रोष्य
    युज् with आ—आयुज्य         शङ्ख् with वि—विशङ्ख्य
