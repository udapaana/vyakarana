§ 832 * The object of roots implying motion (i. e. the
place to which the motion is directed), if it be not a road, is
put in the Accusative or the Dative, when actual motion is
meant; ग्रामं मायुयु वा गच्छुति 'goes to a village;' but मनसा हरिं
भुजुति, पुरुस्युयुं गच्छुति.

THE ABLATIVE CASE.

§ 833. The principal sense of the Ablative case is अयुयुदुयुनं or
'motion away from it;' hence the noun from which the motion,
real or conceived, takes place is put in the Ablative case;
ग्रामुत्युयुगुच्छुति 'comes from a village;' युयुयुत्युट् 'falls' Ablative case;
from a running horse; सदुयुजुयुत्युरुयु भुयुते.

(a) ‡ Words having the sense of जुयुयुयुयु, विरास or 'cessation,'
'pause,' and यवुयुयुट् 'swerving from,' also govern the Ablative
case; यापुयुट् युयुट्युयुयुते 'he hates sin;' न नमुः अघुयुमुयुछोऽदुयुयुरिस्युरुकुर्मों
विरुरुयुतु (Reg. VIII. 22) also new monarch, steady in
action, did not desist from efforts until they bore their fruit';
युयुनोत्युयुयुयुयुति 'swerves from duty'; स्युरुविकुत्युयुयुयुयुनुः (Meg. 1.)
'careless, failing in the discharge of the duties (of his office)';
so प्रयुयुस्युहुयुनि; यदुयुशोयु निवुरुतुत संवुनुरुस्युरु यद्युशुयुयुट् (Manu Smr.
V. 49.), &c.

युट् with यु in the sense of 'to be careless about' is used with
the Loc. also; जुदुयुयुद्युच् प्रयुयुद्युचिन यमुदुयुट् विषुयुक्तुयुः । (Manu Smr.
II. 213) on this account wise men are not careless about
their wives.

§ 834. § In the case of words expressing fear or protection
from fear, that from which or whom the fear proceeds is put
in the Ablative case; चोरुदुट्युभेति is afraid of a thief; भूत रणो
युनेत्युनुयुत् is not afraid of the while-horned one i. e. Arjuna~
