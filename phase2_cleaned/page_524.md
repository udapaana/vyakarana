ऋदिदुयूनुयूं अायु स्युयूति वा यास्युयूति शीघ्रुयुं बस्युयूयुयूः if a shower were to
come quickly we would at once sow corn.

§ 940. When the word युयूच् is not used with roots meaning
'to remember,' such as स्युट्, छुदु, &c, the Second Future is used in
the sense of the Imperfect; स्युरुयूति छुयूयूयू गोकुलुयूनै यस्युयूयूयूः Kṛishṇa,
do you remember that we dwelt in Gokula?

§ 941. * When disbelief in or intolerance of an action is
intended to be expressed, and especially in the form of a ques-
tion, the Simple Future is used optionally in the sense of the
Potential; न तं कुयुयूयूयूमि or न समुयूयुनै भुवुयूरु हरिं निन्युदैत or निनिन्युयूयूति
I never believe, or cannot tolerate it, that you would or should
speak ill of Hari; कुः or कुरुः कुतुयुः हुरिं निन्युदैत or निनिन्युयूयूति who
will speak ill of Hari (I do not believe that any body will,
&c.); कै दुयूयूनुयुं भुवुयूरु यावुनैदुच् or याज्युयूष्युयूति, &c.; when the word
किंकुदुयुनुयूनुयुं † (a particle, showing great anger) and roots having the
sense of 'to be' precede, the Simple Future only should be used;
स नं तेवुयूरुयूयो भुवुयूरु युयूदुयू रायूयुयूनुनै सुयूयुवुरुयूनुति I do not believe
or like that you should make a Śudra perform a sacrifice; so
अतुतुं भुवुयूतै विपुदुयुं वा भुवुयूरु इयुकुनै याज्युयूयूष्युयूति.

§ 942. When the idea of wonder is to be expressed and
the words युयूच्, युयूनु and युदि do not occur in a sentence, the
Simple Future should be used; युरुयूदुन्युोऽदुयुन्युोऽपि हरिं युयूयुरुयूति हरिं युयूक्ष्युयूति it is
a wonder that a blind man sees Hari.

(a) The Simple Future is also used when the particles
उनु and आपि expressing a doubt are used; उनु दुयूयुयुयुः युतियुयूयूति will
the stick fall? आपि कुयूयूरुयूनुयूयूति द्युयूरुं will be close the door?

(b) This Future is also used when the particle अड्युयू mean-
ing sure or able is used; अहुयुयुं हुतुयूयु निश्युयूयु Kṛishṇa is sure
sure or able to kill the elephant.
