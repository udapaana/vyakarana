THE SECOND OR SIMPLE FUTURE.

§ 936. * When the close proximity of a future action is
intended, the Second Future or the Present may be used; कुयु गमि-
ष्युयूसि when will you go? एयु गच्छुयूमि or गमिष्युयूमि I shall just go.

§ 937. † When there is the idea of hope implied in a
conditional form, the Aorist, the Present or the Simple Future
may be used in both the clauses to denote a future time,
दैयुयूयूदुयूरुयूल्युयूदु नुयूति वरुयुत्युयूति वा यायूरुयूयूयूस्युयू बुसुयूयूः वस्युयूयुः वा। (Sid.
Kau.) Ifit were to rain it would it would sow corn.

§ 938 The Simple Future is sometimes used as a court-
eous way of command; युयूयूरुयूयुयुयुयूरु स तिष्ठुयूसि (Vik. V.) thus
you will go (i e. please then go) to the lake, &c.
