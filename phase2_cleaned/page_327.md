§ 515. अद्, कृट् and हथे admit इ necessarily before थ्.

§ 516. इ 'to go' lengthens its reduplicative इ before
weak terminations.

For the forms see p. 311.

§ 517. The base of इ with अधि 'to study' is अधीज्.

1. अधिजगे          अधिजगिवहे         अधिजगिमहे
2. अधिजगिषे        अधिजगाथे          अधिजगिध्वे
3. अधिजगे          अधिजगाते          अधिजगिरे

§ 518. ऊर्ण् forms its base as ऊर्णुव्. Its vowel is optionally
not guṇated before a strong termination when it takes इ.
