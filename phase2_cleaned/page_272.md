§ 429. चकास्, P. 'to shine,'—चकास्, जक्ष्, जाग्र्, दरिद् and व्याध् drop the न of the 3rd pers. plu. termination when added to them. In the Imperf. they take उस् as 3. pl. termination. Imp. 2 sing. of चकास् is चकास्दि-धि.
