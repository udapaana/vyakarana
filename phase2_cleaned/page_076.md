§ 109. The Nom. sing. of अनेहस् time, पुरूहस् name of Indra, and उशनस् name of Śukrāchārya is अनेहा, पुरूहा and उशना respectively; the Voc. sing. of उशनस् is उशनस, उशन् and उशन; the other forms are regular.

§ 110. To feminine nouns ending in स् simply add the terminations.

                भास् f. splendour.
N.     भाः        भासौ        भासः
I.     भासा       भाभ्याम्     भाभिः
L.     भासि       भासोः       भासु:

§ 111. Obs. The सा of उक्थशास् uttering hymns, becomes स before the consonantal terminations, except that of the Nom. sing.; e.g. Nom. उक्थशाः sing. उक्थशासौ dual.; Instr. sing. उक्थशासा; dual उक्थशोभ्याम्; Loc. plu. उक्थशा:सु.

                Irregular bases.

§ 112. जस् that falls, ध्वस् a destroyer, हिंस् one who kills well, जिघांस् desirous of killing. The first two change their स् to त् before consonantal terminations while the last two change their स् to द् under the same circumstances, the preceding nasal being dropped.
