§ 32. The स् of स्था and स्तम्भ is changed into ष् when after the
preposition उद्;उद्+स्थानम्=उद्+ष्थानम्=उद्ष्थानम् and उद्स्थानम्
( see. § 20. a ), and then उद्धथानम्, उच्छ्यानम् rising up; similarly
उत्+स्तम्भम् and उत्स्तम्भम् upholding.

§ 33. स् coming after any of the first four letters of a class is
optionally changed to the soft aspirate ( 4th letter ) of that
class†; वाक्+हरि:=वाग्हरि: (see b. under§ 22), and by this वाग्हरि:
( वाचो हरि: ) the possessor of speech, Bṛhaspati.

§ 34. Any consonant, except a nasal or a semi-vowel, is
changed to the first of its class, when followed by a hard
consonant, and to the first or third when followed by nothing;
वाक्, वाग्.

§ 35. श preceded by a word ending in any of the first four
letters of a class and followed by a vowel, a semi-vowel, a nasal
or ह्,is optionally changed to छ्,‡- e.g.तद्+शिव:=तद्+छिव:; and
तद्+छिव:. and then तत्+छिव:, and तत्+छिव: by the above, and
finally तच्छिव: and तच्छिव: that Siva ( see. a, § 28. ). Similarly
तच्छ्लोकेन, तच्छ्लोकेन; but वाक्श्रोतृति speech falters ( as श is not
followed by any of the letters mentioned in the rule ).
