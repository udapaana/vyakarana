                       SECTION I.

       PARTICIPLES (Declinable and Indeclinable)

           1. Participles (Declinable)

       (a) Participles of the Present Tense

§ 667. The participle of the present Parasm. is formed by
the addition of the affix अत् to that form of a root, primitive
or derivative, which it assumes before the third person plural
termination of the Present Tense. If the base ends in अ, the अ
is dropped; e g.

भू (1 cl.)—भव+अन्ति Pre. 3rd pl. भव+अत्=भवत् Pre. P being
तुदा (I cl)—तुद+अन्ति „        तुद+अत्=तुद्वत् „, standing
द्विष् (2 cl.)—द्विष्+अन्ति „        द्विष्+अत्=द्विषत् „, hating.
    similarly from
अद् (2 cl.) अद्यत् eating.       रुज् (7 cl.) रुज्यत् preventing.
या (2 cl.) यात् going            कृ (8 cl.) कुर्वत् doing.
हु (3 cl.) जुहुवत् sacrificing    तन् (8 cl.) तन्वत् stretching, &c.
दिव् (4 cl.) दीव्यत् playing.      क्री (9 cl.) क्रीणत् buying.
सु (5 cl.) सुन्वत् extracting.    चुर् (9 cl.) चुर्णत् stealing, &c.
तुद् (6 cl.) तुद्यत् giving pain.   चोर् (10 cl.) चोरयत् stealhng.
या cau of कृ, बोधयत् causing to know.
विबोधिषु desid. of बुध्, बुबोधिषत् desiring to know.
दित्सु desid. of दा, दिदासत् desiring to give.
चेक्षिपु freq. of क्षिप्, चेक्षिपत् throwing again and again.
    &c.        &c.        &c.        &c.        &c.

(a) The form. अत् is optionally changed to वस् when added
to the root विद्; विदत् or विद्वस् 'knowing.'

(b) The affix अत् when added to सृष् and सु 'to extract Soma
juice in a sacrifice' has the sense of an agent; as शत्रुवत् m. 'an enemy.'
पदं सनं सुन्वन्तो वृत्रायत: यः । Sid. Kau. 'all who sacrifice in a sacrifice.
