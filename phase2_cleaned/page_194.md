§318. The words अन्तर्वत् and पतिवत् form their feminine in ई but have a न् prefixed to the ई, अन्तर्वत्नी 'a woman big with a child'. पतिवत्नी 'a woman whose husband is living'. But if the word पति means 'a lord or master of' ई only is added; as पतिमती पृथ्वी (the earth having a king).

§319. Adjectives ending in इ short or long, have no other form for the feminine; as शुचिः शुचीः &c.

§320. Adjectives ending in उ not preceded by a conjunct consonant, except स्वक्, take ई optionally; e.g. मृदुः-मृदुः-दी. पटुः-वी. बहुः-वी:; but स्वकः 'a girl who chooses her husband' (पतिवरा कन्या Sid. Kau.); पाण्डुः and not पाण्डुई as the उ is preceded by a conjunct consonant; आयुः as it is a noun.

§321. A prātipadika ending in उ not preceded by य and denoting mankind forms its feminine in ऊ:; e.g. कुरुः 'a woman of the Kuru country'; but अध्वर्युः 'the wife of an Adhvaryu'; also when it does not denote animal-kind; as अक्षाहुः. कुक्कुटुः; except in the case of रज्जु and हनु; रज्जूः; हनूः.

(a) Obs. A prātipadika ending in बाहु and used as a proper name, and the word पञ्चगु also form their feminine in ऊ; as भद्रबाहुः 'a woman of that name'; but वृत्तबाहुः 'a woman having well-rounded arms'; पञ्चगूः !
