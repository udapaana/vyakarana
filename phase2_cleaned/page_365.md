§ 598. Roots of the tenth Class—

(a) The अय् (i. e. अय् with the final अ dropped) is optionally
dropped in the General Tenses, except the Perfect. The Aorist
forms, except that of the 3rd sing., are made up by adding the
Âtmanepada terminations that of the Fifth Form; दृ-चोरयाति,
चोरयामास Perf; चोरयिष्यते चोरितास्यत्, 1st Fu. चोरयिष्यते चोरयिष्यते
2nd Fu.; अचोरयिषि, अचोरयिषि, Aor. 1st sing., &c.

(b) Roots which do not lengthen thier penultimate अ (see
also § 603) lengthen it optionally in the general Tenses of the
Passive, except in Perf., when अय् is dropped; कम्-अकम्यिषि,
अकाम्यिषि, Aor. 1 sing., &c.

(c) The 3rd per. sing. of the Pass. Aorist is formed by drop-
ping अय्, necessarily and adding इ; चोर-अचोरि-अचोरायि; पीड़-
अपीड़ायि; &c.—अपारि, अपारिषि Aor. 3 sing; यह्—अपारि, अपार्षि
अपार्षि Aorist, 3rd sing.
