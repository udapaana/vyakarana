§ 576 Roots of the 8th class ending in न् or ण् drop their
nasal and substitute optionally यास् and न् for the terminations
ष्ठाः and ष् of the 2nd and 3rd person sing. Âtm. तृण्
lengthens its vowel after dropping ण्, Ātm.

§ 577. The vowel of ऊर्ण् takes Vṛddhi optionally in the
Parasm, i. e. optionally takes Guṇa substitute; also it option-
ally remains unchanged before इ (Vide §§ 466, 518).

§ 578. The अ of कृत्त् is optionally dropped in the Aorist;
consequently it takes the Sixth and the Fifth Varieties.

(6) THE BENEDICTIVE (आशीर्लिङ्ग).

§ 579. The Parasmaipada terminations of this mood may
be obtained by prefixing यास् to those of the Imperative after
dropping its final स् before स्त् and त् of the 2nd and 3rd per.
sings.; and the Ātmanepada ones by prefixing स् to the
terminations of the Ātm. Potential and also to the त् and थ्
occurring in them. These, therefore, are:—
