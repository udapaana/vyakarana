§ 494. The Parasmaipada singular terminations are strong; the rest weak. Before the strong terminations the penultimate short vowel takes its Guṇa substitute. The final vowel and penultimate अ take Vṛddhi necessarily in the third and optionally in the first person singular. In the 2nd person singular the final vowel takes Guṇa and the penultimate अ remains unchanged.

§ 495. *Special rules about the admission of the intermediate इ before the terminations व, म, वहे, से, वहे, महे; see (§ 457).

(a) All Seṭ roots admit it, whether seṭ or aniṭ, except ई, ऊ, ऋ, लृ, हु, वृ; वृ, ग्रु, क्षु, permit इ or अ; But इ with सम्, and वृ admit it only as सचक्रिव, ववरिव.

(b) Aniṭ roots ending in short अ, except अव्, reject it before व; as तस्थिव from स्था; but आरिव from ऋ.

(c) Aniṭ roots with a final vowel or with a penultimate अ admit it optionally before व.

§ 496. When initial इ or उ of a root takes Guṇa or Vṛddhi substitute, the reduplicative इ or उ is changed to इय or उव; otherwise the two vowels combine to form long ई or ऊ; as इ→इयेश=इयेश, ऊ→उवोच=ऊचोच; ईविध, &c.

§ 497. The final ऋ, short or long, is changed to अर before a vowel weak termination and ई to इय or य as it is preceded by a conjunct consonant or not; as ऋ→अर→इरुव ! dual; तितिर्+इरे=तितिरिरे, लिभिर्+इरे=लिभिरिरे, &c.
