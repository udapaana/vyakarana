INDECLINABLE PAST PARTICIPLES OR GERUNDS.

§ 909. The past irdeclinable participle in Sanskrit denotes
the prior of two actions done by the same agent and corres-
ponds to the perfect participle in English; हुतं उदुयूयु विरुयूरुयु
having said this he stopped; तुयूरु युयुयुयुयुरियुयु नलुहुयूयुं नोत्युयु यवुयूसति
having seated them on his back, he carried them to the lake
and ate them up.
