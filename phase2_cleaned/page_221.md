Before this the ending अ or आ is changed to इ, but not that of indeclinables; इ or उ is lengthened and हु changed to रू; final स् and the ending consonant of अक्षस्, मनस्, चक्षुस्, चेतस्, वहस्, and रजस्, are dropped and then the preceding rules are applied to the penultimate vowel. After this the termination is dropped and the verbal or other forms of कृ, भू and अस् are added to the base regarded as a preposition; अकुञ्जनः कुञ्जनमस्य सम्पद्यते अकुञ्जीकरोति शाकुनम्, जलसम्पद्यते जलीभवति; similarly रङ्गीकरोति मांसम् i. e. 'roasts it';—( 4 ) सत्यः सत्याकरोति भाण्डं वणिक् ' settles its price '; ( 5 ) and to onomatopeic words not followed by इति and containing more than one vowel, the words being reduplicated; पटच्,—पटपटाकरोति, ' utters the words पटच्, पटच्.
