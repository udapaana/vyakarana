§ 308. (a) A prātipadika ending in कर, except धक्कर, तक्कर, स्किकर and बहुकर; भ. सर, preceded by यत्; अमर: अमे and तुद्; and खर preceded by सेवा. द्वाप and words expressive of place; the words नद, चोर, देव, भाइ, भर. व्छय, and नह्, nominal bases ending in ण्य, nominal and verbal derivatives formed by the affix अ causing Vṛddhi or Guṇa, such as ओढनः, ओसन:, कुम्भकारः, भावहारः, पाठ्यः, ताइम:, &c. and words ending in the Tad. affixes इयस्, दुह, माच and इक (with some exceptions) and in the kṛit affix स्वर form their feminine in ई when they are not used adjectively; e. g. भोगकरी conducing to pleasures; एककरी &c.; पतिसी, सिनसी, &c.; अविसरी, &c.; सेनाखरी, कुरुखरी, a woman of the Kuru country; मत्स्यखरी, &c.; नदी, देवी, सटी, &c.; सौपर्णी, वैनतेयी, &c.; ऐन्द्री, औरसी, &c.; कुम्भकारी, नगरकारी, &c.; उरुद्यसी-दुही-माचां measuring or reaching as far as the knee; &c. आशिथकी, छागणकी, &c.; पाटसी, ताइसी, स्वरी, evanescent, an unchaste woman &c., गल्वरी &c., but स्किकरा. बहुकुरारा नगरी.

(b) Also words ending in the Tad. affixes न, स्न, इक्. य (causing Vṛiddhi) and the words नदुन and नठुन; धोणी. गोत्नी fit for a man; शाकोकी, तरुणी, नठुनी, &c. also words ending in the kṛit. affix अन causing a nasal to be inserted before it; आह्वेमकरणी.

(c) Words indicative of the stages of life except the last take the fem. ई; कुमारी, किशोरी, बक्री, बष्टी, बिरण्टी (both meaning a 'a young woman'); but वृद्धा. स्थविरा &c. as these imply old age; कन्या is an exception.
