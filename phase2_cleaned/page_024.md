(b) *When अ is followed by एष in an indefinite sense, ए is
substituted for both: क + एष = कैत्र भोक्ष्यते where will you dine
to-day ? ( The place being not definitely known ); but तत्रैव
( why, I shall dine at your house ).

(c) † अ followed by ओम् and आ ( prep. ) is dropped e. g.
शिवाय + ओं नमः = शिवायों नमः; शिव + एहि ( आ + इहि ) = शिवेहि.

(d) †The vṛddhi is optional when the ending अ of a word
is followed by ओष् a cat, or ओष् the lower lip, in a compound
स्थूल + ओष्ः = स्थूलौष्ः or स्थूलोष्ः; विम्बोष्, विम्बोष्ः.
