§ 672. The participle of the Pre, Passive is formed by adding
मान to the Passive base in यु; e g. बुध्यमान 'who or what is
known,' अद्यमान 'what is eaten,' दीयमान who or what is given,
चीयमान 'who or what is gathered,' क्रियमाण 'what is done,'
कृ—कीर्यमाण 'what is scattered,' चोर्यमाण 'who or what is
stolen' बुध्—cau. Pass. बोध्यमान 'who or what is caused to
know,' Des. Pass. बुबोधिषयमाण 'who or what is desired
to know,' &c.

§ 673. The participles formed according to § 662 are de-
clined like nouns ending in अ m f and n

       (b) Participles of the Perfect.

§ 674. The terminations of the Perf. and the Past parti-
ciples are weak, and therefore the radical vowel does not take
its Guṇa substitute before these. The penultimate nasal is
generally dropped before these (See § 534)

§ 675. The participle of the Perf. Par, and Ātम. is formed
by adding वस् and आन generally to that form of the root which
it assumes before the termination of the 3rd per. plural. If this
form consists of one syllable only or when the root ends in आ,
वस् has the intermediate इ added to it. इ is optionally prefixed
to वस् in the case of the roots गम्, इय्, हुव्, विश् and विद्य् (6 cl.);
चक् and स्पर्, and गुह् and हृत् when they do not take इ, form this
participle from that base which they would assume before the
2nd per. sing. termination.
