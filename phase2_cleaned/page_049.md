§ 73. Compound nouns ending with पति such as भूपति &c.
are declined like हरि regularly. त्रिपत्रि ( त्रिय: ऋव: यस्य यस्या वा )
is declined like हरि mas. & मति fem.; the Gen. plu. is optionally
like that of त्रि; परमत्रीणाम्, or °व्यपाणाम्.

§ 74. Obs. (a) औडुलोमि a descendant of Uduloman ( उदुलोम:
अपत्यं पुमान् ) is declined like हरि in the sing. and dual and like
ब्राह्म in the plural, the original word उदुलोम being substituted;*

N. औडुलोमि: औडुलोमी, उदुलोमा: &c.

(b) Words of similar derivation (see Pāṇ.- II. 4. 62. 63. 65.
66 and IV. 1. 105 ) also substitute their originals in the plural,
e.g. गार्ग्यस्य अपत्यं गार्ग्य: N. गार्ग्य: गार्ग्यौ गार्गा: &c.
