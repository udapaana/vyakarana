NON-CONJUGATIONAL TENSES AND MOODS.

§ 595. (a) The Reduplicative Perfect of a root in the
Passive is formed like that of the Active, except in that the Auxili-
ary verb भू is used instead of अस्; e. g. क्रीयते—क्रीयां बभूव &c.

(b) The Periphrastic Perfect of the Passive does not differ
from that of the Active, except in that the Auxiliary verb भू—
चकार is used; e. g. क्रीयते क्रीयां चकार, पूज्यते—पूजां चकार and before
it is used.

§ 597. (a) The Passive of the Aorist of roots belonging to
the first variety is formed by dropping त् and the आ and append-
ing the Ātmanepada terminations to the base; स्-अधायि; कृ-
अकारि; धा-अधायि; पच्-अपचि; दिश्-अदेशि; क्षिण्-अक्षेशि, &c.

(b) Roots belonging to the first, second, third and sixth
Varieties take the fourth, fifth or seventh Variety in the Passive,
in accordance with the general rules; स्था-अस्थायि 1 sing.; स्था-
अस्थायि; इ-अषाटि; इ-अगायि; चि-अचायि; चर्-अचेषि; नश्-अनशि, &c.

(c) The third per. sing. of the Aorist Passive of all roots is
formed by adding इ—

(1) Before this इ the penultimate (prosodially) short vowel
takes its Guṇa substitute and penultimate अ except that of वद्
and of Seṭ roots ending in अल् except वद् with आ, कृष्, and स्तृ,
and of the short vowel retain their Guṇa; छिद्-अच्छेदि; छुर्-अच्छोदि;
but निन्द्—अनिन्दि; तुर्-अतारि; छर्-अकारि; सृ-अस्रारि; पस्-
अपारि; but वद्-अनेक्षि; गम्-अगमि; but वद्-अवदि; &c. वद्
with आ—आवेदि; कृष्-अकारि; &c. शी-अशायि; स्तु-अस्तारि;
लू-अलारि; कृ or कृ—अकारि.

(2) Roots ending in आ original or substituted (i. e. of roots
in आ, औ, ऐ) first य् before this इ; दा—अदायि; घे—अधायि; धे
अधायि; शो—अशायि, &c.

(3) स्तम्भ्, जन्य् and स्तज् insert a nasal before their final consonant,
so that their penultimate अ cannot take Guṇa or Vṛddhi
substitute; अस्तभ्ङ्क्ष्, अजङ्क्ष्, अस्तञ्ज्.

(4) क्रम् without a preposition does the same optionally and-
with a preposition necessarily; अक्राम, अचक्रमि; but आ-अक्राम.

(5) भृज् 'to break' forms अभार्जि or अभ्रष्टि; शाच्-शाचयि and
अवाचयि in the sense of 'to observe (10 Â.).'

(6) यच् takes Vṛddhi; यच् lengthens its vowel; अयाचि, अयाचि.

(7) इ 'to go' has अगामि; with अधि Â., अध्यगामि or अध्यषामि.
