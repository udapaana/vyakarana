(c) When the agent of the action denoted by a verb cannot
be other than an animal, the causal of the verb takes the Par.
even when the action refers to the agent as कृष्णः स्वपिति 'Krishna
sleeps,' गोपी कृष्णं स्वापयति 'the cowherdess lulls Krishna to sleep,'
but फलं पतति 'the fruit falls down,' but वातः फलं पातयति 'the wind
causes the fruit to fall down,' &c.

(d) In the case of verbs meaning 'to eat,' except अद्, and
'to move,' their causals take the Parasm. even when the fruit of
the action refers to the agent; जिगारयति, आशयति 'makes another
eat,' चालयति, क्रमयति, 'he shakes' &c.

Exceptions to (c) and (d)—to (c) शम् 'to pacify,' आस् with
आ 'to draw in' यत् with आ 'to endeavour,' परिष्ठहु 'to faint,'
रुच् 'to shine,' वद् 'to speak,' वस् 'to dwell', and पा 'to
drink;' to (d) स्वप्-यु 'to drink,' नृत् 'to dance;' in the case
of these roots the usual rule holds good; शमयते, शमयते;
सुमयति-ते &c.
