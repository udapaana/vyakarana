PERIPHRASTIC PERFECT

§ 526. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed by adding आम् to
the root and then adding the forms of the reduplicated Perfect
of कृ, भू, or अस् as terminations; when the forms of कृ, are
added, a Parasmaipadi root takes the Parasmaipadi forms and
an Ātmanepadi one takes the Ātmanepadi forms.

§ 527. Before आम् the final vowel and the penultimate short vowel,
except that of विद्, take their Guṇa substitute.
