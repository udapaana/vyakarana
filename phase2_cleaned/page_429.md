§ 706. P. P. Participles have sometimes the sense of neuter
abstract nouns; as वचितं 'speech', शयितं 'sleep', हसितं 'laughing,'
similarly स्थितं, गतं, etc.; See Bhaṭṭ. VII. 125.

§ 707. The P. P. Participles of roots meaning 'to think or to
wish,' 'to know,' 'to adore,' and of the roots हन्, भू etc. have
the sense of the present Tense; राज्ञा मतः 'he is honoured by the
king;' सत्त्रैष आरुत्रेः द्युतः तथा the root is kindled;' so भूतः etc.

(d) Past Active Participles.

§ 708. Past Active participles are derived from the P. P.
Passive Participle in त् or न by changing it to them the affix वत्; e.g.

Roots.          P. P. Ptc.      P. Ac. Ptc.

भू to be            भूतः           भूतवत् one or that has been
कृ to do            कृतः           कृतवत् „     „      done
कृ to scatter       कीर्णः          कीर्णवत् „     „      scattered
दिद् to cut          दिन्नः          दिन्नवत् „     „      cut
etc.                etc.           etc.                etc.

(e) Participle of the Future Tense.

§ 709. The Participle of the Simple Future, Active as well
as Passive, is formed from the third person singular form of
the Second Future of a root. The Parasm. Ptc. is formed
simply by dropping the final इ, and the Atм. and the Passive
one by substituting मान for the final इ; e. g.

Roots.  Par.           Atम.                  Passive.

भू—  भविष्यत्         भविष्यमाणः           भविष्यमाणः or भाविष्यमाणः
