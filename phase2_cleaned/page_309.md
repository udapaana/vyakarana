‡ The व of the terminations ध्वस्व, स्वम्, and महे of 2nd per. pl. of the Benedictive, the Aorist and the Perfect, is changed to ढ् when preceded by any vowel except अ or आ or by य, र, व, ल or ह्. When the aforesaid terminations are preceded by the vowel ई, it is changed to इ, if ई be preceded by one of the consonants given above.
