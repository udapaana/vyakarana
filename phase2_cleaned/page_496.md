§ 850. * The root ज्ञा meaning 'to have an incorrect know-
ledge of' governs the Genitive; तेलं सर्पिषो जानीते supposed oil to
be ghee; but सर्पिर्युजुयुनोति.

(a) Verbs implying 'to think of, to remember,' such as स्युट्, ट्
with अवि, 'to be master of,' such as हुशुट्, यु with विवु, &c. and to ha·e
compassion upon such as दुयु, &c, govern the Genitive as to the
object; वाटिचुट्युदुङुः स्युरुति (Meg. 50) dost thou remember the lord?
स्युयुरुट् राजुवुवुस्युयुणुयुति विवुयुयुयुङु राजुवुनुयुयुस्युयुः Rām. VI. 60. 3. अत्युयुनुदि तवु
छुट्युयुणुः (Bhaṭṭi VIII. 119.) Lakshmana. remembers thee; यभुवुते
मित्रुस्युयु हुन्युयुवुकुयुयुनुयुयु मद्युयुनुयुयुः (Mal. Mād. I.) the great king now
master over his daughters; युदि ते पेयुयुयुनुयु आत्युयुनो युयुयुविछुयुयुयुयु
(Uttar.) if after I see him I shall have control overmyself;
मायुराम्यो अङ्गुयुनोऽस्युयु चुङुतुनुः (Ś'ak. II.) I have lost all power over
my limbs; कुष्युचुचुरीवुयु मनसो वुचुदुट्: (Kum. III. 64.) with great
difficulty they very well control their minds; कैयुदुयुस्युर्नों निषुयुयु न
येर्णों ब्रुजुन्ति तेषो दुयुरुनं न प्रुस्युयुयुनु (Bhaṭṭi. II. 33.) why dost thou
feel no compassion for those whose wealth does not see
the morrow? रामुस्युयु दुयुयुयुनुनुः (Ibid. VIII 119) taking pity
on Rāmā.

(b) कुच् meaning 'to impart additional properties' governs the
Genitive; युट्युयुयुयुयुङु स्युवुयुभुयोयुहु 'fuel imparts heat to water,' युयु
कुरुयुचिन्युयुयुयुकुच्युयुयुयुः Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 119.
