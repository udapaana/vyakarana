§ 314. Prātipadikas expressive of colour, having न for their penultimate and ending in an Anudātta vowel, except आसित black and पलित grey, and the word पिङ्ग form their feminine in ई or आ; एता or एती 'variegated' from एत; रोहिता, रोहिती; पिङ्गी-पिङ्गा; but आसिता, पलिता, श्वेता ( as the त here is Udātta ).

(a) But if the Prātipadika has no न for its penultimate, it simply takes ई; कल्माषी variegated, सारङ्गी; but कृष्णा कपिला ( as the last vowels are not Anudātta ).

§ 315. The Feminine of नृ and नर is नारी and that of words included in the Shārṅgaravādi ( शार्ङ्गरवादिगण ) group is formed by adding ई; as शार्ङ्गरवी, गौतमी, आतिथेयी, आश्रौकेयी, वैद्यी, पुत्री, &c.

§ 316. The feminine of words expressive of relationship is irregular; श्वशुर-श्वश्रू, पितृ-मातृ, &c.

§ 317. The feminine of पति is पत्नी meaning 'the sharer with her husband in the fruit of the sacrifices performed by him; but if it end a compound it may optionally remain unchanged except when preceded by समान, एक, चोर, पिण्ड, त्व धात्र, मह, and पुत्र &c. before which this change is necessary; गृहपति or गृहपत्नी, the mistress of the house; or इन्द्रपति or इन्द्रपत्नी; उषलपति or उषलपत्नी &c. but समानः पतिर्यस्याः सा सपत्नी a co-wife, एकपत्नी, चोरपत्नी.
