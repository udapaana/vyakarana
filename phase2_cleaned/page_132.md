Exception*:—But a compound is allowed in the case of the words याजक one who employs as sacrificing priests, पूजक, परिचारक, परिवेषक a waiter at meals, स्नापक a servant who bathes his master or brings bathing water for him, अध्यापक, उत्साहक one who destroys, होतृ, भर्तृ, when it does not mean 'a holder,' and others not very important; ब्राह्मणयाजकः, देवपूजकः, राजपरिचारकः: &c.; अधिहोता, क्षुमर्तृ, &c. but वज्रस्य भर्तृ 'the thunderbolt-bearer'.

(b)† A specific Genitive (i.e. from which a singling out is made) is not compounded with any other words; as द्विजानो द्विजः श्रेष्ठः

(c)‡ A noun in the Genitive is not compounded with ordinal numbers, words denoting qualities, words implying satisfaction present participles, past participles meaning 'honoured or approved of' or showing the place of the act, verbal indeclinables and verbal nouns ending in तव्य; e.g. द्विजेषु पञ्चमः, ब्राह्मणस्य शुक्लः (हस्तः); काकस्य कार्ष्येयः; फलानां तृप्तितः 'satisfied with fruits'. (an Inst. Tat. is, of course, allowed in these cases); द्विजस्य कुर्वन् कुर्वाणो वा किंचित्, सतां मतः esteemed by the good; राज्ञां पूजितः; इदमेषां आसीदिति (place of sitting) गतो दृष्टं वा; ब्राह्मणस्य छन्दा, नृस्य कर्तव्यम् &c.
