I MASCULINE WORDS.

§ 345. Verbal derivatives formed by the affixes अच् and घञ्, and ई added to the roots धा and पा; e. g. पाकः, व्यापः, करः, मरः any beverage or drink, poison, गोचरः range, scope, यन्ता, विषः, आधिः mental pain or anguish, निधिः a treasure, &c.

Exceptions:—यास्था fem. and भव, लिङ्ग, and भग all neu.

§ 346. Words ending in उ and those having क, ट, ण, थ, प, ष, ख, घ, र, or स, for their penultimate; as प्रभुः, भानुः, इक्षुः, स्तबकः 'a bunch of flowers' &c; घटः, पाषाणः, शोथः 'a swelling, फेनः; दीपः; स्तम्भः; रोमः; समयः; क्षुरः a razor,' अङ्कुरः, धरः, वायसः &c.

Exceptions:—Words ending in—

(a) उ—धेनु, रज्जु (except when it ends a compound, in which case it is both mas. and fem.) कुहू—हू 'the last day of a lunar month on which the moon is invisible,' सरयु 'name of a river,' तनु, करेणु and विषेणु 'a kind of creeper,' which are all fem; इक्ष्वु, जाठु, वसु 'wealth,' अश्रु, जतु 'lac,' त्रपु 'tin or lead', तालु, दारु, मधु 'honey,' स्वादु 'relish', वस्तु and मस्तु 'sour cream', which are all neuter.

(b) क—चिबुक 'the chin', इत्लुक, प्रातिपदिक, अंशुक 'a garment,' उल्मुक 'a fire brand.'

(c) ट and ण—किरीट, इकुट, ललाट, चतुष्पट 'a place where four roads meet;' कृण, लवण, पर्ण, उष्ण.
