क )—is affixed (1) to words expressive of countries in the sense of 'born or produced in'; माद्रकः 'born or produced in the Madra country';—(2) to पीत in the sense of 'dyed with'; पीतकः 'dyed yellow';—(3) in the sense of 'belonging to'; माद्रकः, स्वकः, &c.—(4) 'bought for' पञ्चकः 'bought for five rupees';—(5) in the sense of 'acting' शीतकः 'one who is cold' i. e.
