§ 22. When इ, उ, ऋ and लृ, short or long, are followed by
a dissimilar vowel, य्‌, व्‌, र्‌ and ल्‌ are respectively substituted
for them*; e. g. इति+आह=इत्याह; बुधी+उपास्य:=बुध्युपास्यः
adored by the wise; मधु+अरि:=मध्वरि: the enemy of मधु i. e.
Vishṇu, पातृ+अंश:=पात्रंश: a portion of the creator; लृ+आ-
कृतिः=लाकृतिः a figure like that of लृ &c.
