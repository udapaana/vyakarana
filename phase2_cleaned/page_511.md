§ 901. The past passive participle is used as a neuter sub-
stantive; गतं departure; दुत्तुं a gift, युयुतं an excavation, अन्युनुयुं,
युयुनुयुं, &c.

§ 902 The past passive participle active and passive, may be
used with the auxiliaries अस् and भू in any tense, the mean-
ing of the participle changing accordingly; गतोऽस्युयु I have or am starting;
I have or am gone; so गतवुयूनुभुवं or गतवुयूनुयूनुयुं or गतोभुवुयुं I had
or was gone; इच्युच्युन्तुयूस्तु. गतो वनु युयुयो यविष्युत्युति रायुः that Rāma
is to go to the forest to-morrow; संबुसुः कोपिमुनुटुयुयुयुं भविष्युयुति
thou wilt obtain great glory, &c.

THE FUTURE PARTICIPLES.

§ 903 The future participle denotes that a person or thing
is doing, or is about to do the action of or undergo the condi-
tion, expressed by the root; करिष्युयुच् going or about to do; करिष्युयु-
युयुणु about to do or what is about to be done.

§ 904. Besides showing simple futurity participle expresses
intention or purpose; अनुयुयुस्युयुच् मुतितुनुयुयूरु wishing to follow
the daughter of the sage; दुयूस्युयुच् wishing to give, वुन्युयुयुरु
हरिकुयूनुनु intending exercising, as it were, to tame the wild beasts.

POTENTIAL PASSIVE PARTICIPLES.

§ 905. The Potential passive participle is used in the sense
of 'what should or ought to be done;' besides this, this parti-
ciple yields the sense of 'fitness, obligation, necessity, capacity,'
&c., the agent being put in the Instrumental case; विसुयुनोयुकुन्तो-
विष्टुं किं कुरुनुयुनं क्युयुयुयुयुनु (Devi Bhāg. IV. 7. 1.) he considered in
