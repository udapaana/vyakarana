वह्—is both Par. and Ātम. but with य is restricted to the
Par; प्रवहति.

विद्—* to know' (2nd cl.) with सम् is Ātम. when used
intransitively in the sense of 'knowing of, being aware of,' and
adds इ to इ optionally in the 3rd per. plural; सविदते or सविदन्ते
'they know well;' cf. ब्रह न सविदन्त मायानेगमादिमियो यो:सुवा Bh. VIII.
11.; 'who do not know that the mountain Mainaka is the friend
of Yāyu); but सविदन्त: संमृद्धुभूतौ तत्स्वात्तौ पर:दूष्य:णों Bhaṭṭi. V. 37.
as it is used transitively. It is also Ātम. with अपि in the sense
of 'recognising;' as सविद्वै.

विद्§†—with वि is निविदति; किल्विदनानि न्यविदम्न &c. Bhaț.
VI. 143. Also when अभि is prefixed to वि; आभीमानदेते सन्मार्गं
(Sid. Kau.) 'takes to a good path;' see Bh. VIII, 80.

शप्‡—meaning 'to reproach or to abuse' takes the Ātम.
even when the fruit of the action does not accrue to the agent;
कृष्णाय शपते ।

शिक्ष्—takes the Ātम. in the sense of 'wishing to study;'
यडौषे शिक्षते 'wishes to study archery.'

श्रू—|| 'to hear' with सम् is Ātम. when used intransitively;
समवेत 'hears well;' भुक्ताम्य दुख Bhaț. VII. 12,; 'Listens with
heed; oh monkey;' cf. श्रुताव थ: संश्रुयुते स किं मधुः Kir. I. 5; but
शब्दं संश्रुणोति 'he hears the sound.' This root takes the Ātम. in
the Desiderative, except when आ or प्रति precedes it, शुश्रूषते; but
आ—प्रति—शुश्रूषति.

स्था—¶ with सम्, अव, य and वि is Ātम., सतियते; सुद्धौ परिच्चम्धायाडो
