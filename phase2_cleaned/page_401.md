When the root has its original sense, the Par. is used and
ल् is not inserted: कुसुमानि अपकीर्ति स्त्री 'the woman scatters
flowers;' अरण्युपति मृग scatters it

कृत्*—when not preceded by a preposition is conjugated in both
the Padas. But it is used in Ātm. by itself when the meaning is
'free movement, energy, development or increase, '&c,. ऋग्विय क्रमते
बुद्धिः 'his intellect moves freely in (i. e proves very powerful in
mastering) the Rigveda;' शत्रुमध्येऽपिक्रमते 'moving unobstructed in
the assembly of the enemy' (Bh. VIII. 22); अध्ययनाय क्रमते 'shows
ability or energy for studying;' न तस्याय क्रमते जडानाम्। Vikra.I.16
क्रमन्तेऽस्मिन् शास्त्राणि 'the Śāstras find enlargement in him or are
satisfactorily mastered by him.' In the same sense it is Ātm.
when preceded by उद् and परा only (i. e. is Par. if preceded by any
other preposition) उत्क्रामति, परक्रीडति i. e. is Par. if 'सामर्थ्य (showed
might); परिक्रामसुरातते (made bold) यास्ञ्जीषु यस्य क्रमते (किंचुट । Bh.
VIII. 22–23; but क्रमनिते (साकेबृद् बुद्धिः). When preceded by आति
is Ātm. in the sense of 'the ascending or the rising of a heavenly
body;' आक्रमते सूर्यः 'the sun rises;' but आकामति धूमो उस्त्रीतेऽसात् 'the
smoke issues forth from the upper terrace.' Also when preceded by
वि, in the sense of 'a graceful movement of the feet, or ascending,'
साधुशु विक्रमते वाजी 'the horse moves gracefully;' but विक्रामति संधिः
'the joint splits,'—and by य and उय in the sense of 'beginning,'
यक्रमते; as in संक्रान्ते तिर: प्राक्रमनेवनूत्र । Kum. III. 2. 'thus began to
talk with him in private;' but प्रक्रामति 'goes,' उपक्रामति 'comes near.'

क्री†—'to buy' is Ātm. when the prepositions अद्, परि and वि
are prefixed to it; e.g. अदुक्रीत्ते वं means 'to sell'), अपक्रीणीते, परिक्रीते
cf. Bh. VIII. 8. छुनेत्ोपह्युतं वाणिज: परिक्रीत्णातम्बुयितवत् ।
