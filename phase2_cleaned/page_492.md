§ 841. * The ablative is used with—

(a) The prepositions अयु and परिंङु meaning 'away from,
without' and meaning 'as far as' or 'including, comprehend-
ing'; यदुनुनुयुयु हरिकिभुयो द्युंङुयुते रतोरुतुयुरुतुः (Bām. I.) now that he
dwelt in Laṅkā, in terror, away from the worlds; अयु हरेः
सत्तुरुः Samsāra exists outside Hari; अयु ङुतिगुर्तुयो द्युयुयुं देयुः it
rained everywhere, except in the country of Trigarṭa. Similarly
परिंङु हरेः सीमुयुयुयुः परिङु युट्युदुयुयुष्युंङु द्युयु: (Vop.) &c. आयुकुनं संकुयु
आरुकुछुयुयुद्युट् ब्रद्युयु Brahman pervades every thing; आ यतोयुयु शुत्रोयुर्णो
until the learned are satisfied.
