## Footnotes

* The following couplet gives the endings of these roots and their number in each:—

फरच्चजतृ दृपङ्गाचू गम्भाः नुमताः क्रमात्।
द्वेत्रीदशार्कपञ्चैरेको नवलोदशदुसतून्॥

The couplet gives the ending consonants and thus enables the students to know at once which root is Seṭ and which is Aniṭ; e. g. कुट् may be at once known to be a seṭ root as ट् does not occur in the first line. The second line gives the number of roots ending in each consonant. Thus roots ending in य are कु i. e. one (कु being the first consonant) in number; roots ending in च are च i. e. 5, (च being the 5th consonant); roots ending in त are त i. e. one, in number, and so on.
