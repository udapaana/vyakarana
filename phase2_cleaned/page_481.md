रामेण ज्ञानेन हतो वाली Vāli was killed by Rāma with an arrow
(here रामेण is the agent; ज्ञानेन the instrument).

(a)* The Instrumental is also used in the following senses.
युट्युश्युयु द्युदुर्युनोरुः lovely by nature, यावुयु यात्मुकुः he is almost a
pitiable; विप्रोभुः गार्ग्युयु by family name; युरुणु यति कुत्युयुति he buys
with ease; सो सुचुनेतुवि, विवुयुणोति, &c द्विहुत्रोणेनं यान्युनं कोणुयुति he buys
two drones of corn at a time; साहुनेणु यसुयुत् कोणुयुति he buys one
thousand beasts at a time, &c.

(1) In the case of numerals and words expressive of mea-
sure the Acc. may also be used; द्विहुत्रोणं कोणुयुति यान्युयं, शुनुनं घोतेनं
यान्युयं हतं वसुयुयुरु घरुयुं, &c

(b) Obs.—† The root दिवु 'to play,' however, governs the
Accusative or the Instrumental of the instrument used; अक्तुशैः
यक्तुनुयुरु वा दीवुयुति he plays at dice.

(c) ‡ The root ज्ञा with सम् also governs the Accusative or
the Instrumental; पितुरु जितं वा संज्ञानीते he recognises or lives in
peace with his father, but विष्णुं संजुयुनीयुच्युच् remember Viṣṇu.

§ 813 ‡ When the accomplishment of an object is to be
expressed, the Instrumental of words expressing the length of
time or space is used; अहुनु कोणेनं भुङ्युत्युकुट्युवीतुः the section (of
the Veda) was studied by him in a day, or a Koṣa (i. e by
going over it); but यरुयुमुर्योरुयु नारायुयुः as the accomplishment
does not take place.

§ 814. § Words expressing some defect in a limb of the
body govern the Instrumental of the defective limb; अयुयुणा कुट्युणः
blind of one eye; so युदुनुनं युयुचुः &c
