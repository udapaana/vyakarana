§ 307. A Prâtipadika ending in अ and the words included in the Ajâdi group form their feminine in आ; as भुञ्जान-भुञ्जाना ; अज-अजा, एडका, अभा, चटका, मूषिका; बाला, बत्सा, होडा, मन्दा, विडाला, all meaning 'a young girl'; (of these the first five form an exception to § 313, and the second to § 308, a°); आ is also added to फल when compounded with सत्, भद्र, अर्जुन, पद्म and पिण्ड; to पुष्प when compounded with सत्, words ending in अच्, to काण्ड, प्रान्त, शत and एक; to शूद्र not preceded by महत्, when class is implied, and to मल preceded by अ; संस्फला; भद्रफला, पुणयफला, &c all 'kinds of creeper'; सत्-पाक्-काण्ड-प्रान्त-शत-एक-पुष्पा, 'kinds of creeper' शूद्रा 'a woman of the शूद्र class;' अमला.
