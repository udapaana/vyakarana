(4) The Perfect (लिट्)

§ 488. There are two kinds of Perfect, Reduplicative and Periphrastic.

§ 489. The Reduplicative Perfect is formed of all monosyllabic roots beginning with consonants, as well as of those beginning with the vowels अ or आ, and ऋ, ऌ; and ए short.
Exceptions—ऊह्, अस्, कास्, आस्. These take the Periphrastic Perfect necessarily.

§ 490. The Periphrastic Perfect is formed of all roots beginning with any vowel except अ or आ which is naturally or prosodially long, and of roots of more than one syllable (roots of the 10th class and other derivative roots included). Exceptions:—ऊड् and चक्ष् which take the Reduplicative Perfects.

§ 491. The roots उप्, विदू, जाग्, पै, ई, घू, व, and दरिद्रा take both the perfects.

The Reduplicative Perfect.
