स्था—is Ātम. in the Desiderative; तुस्थूषते.

हू—is Par. in the causal; ज्ञापयति.

स्वृ—with सम् and आ )is Ātम., संस्वरते 'roars so as to terrify;'
हुरं संस्वरतीदाङ्कृत्यः Bhaṭṭi. IX. 28; आरस्वरते 'utters a loud sound.'

स्पृश्†—with आ is Ātम. when used intransitively or when it has a limb
of the body of the agent for its object is Ātम.; अपृश्ते 'kills or
dashes down;' स्पर्शित आप्नोति 'strikes his own head;' but परस्य किर
आतस्पति (Sid. Kau.)

हृ‡—with अनु is Ātम. in the senses of 'following the habits
of, acquiring the natural quality of;' प्रेमस्यम् प्रतुलं 'horses
always follow the gait of their progenitors;' so भान्तं भावे-
अनुहरते; in the sense of 'acquiring a new quality by imitation'
it takes the Par. तिरुगारमनुहरति 'imitates his father.'

हे§—उस्, preceded by उस्, वि, नि, and सम् and used intransi-
vely is Ātम. उस्—वि—नि—सं—हुद्वते; with आ it is Ātम. in the
sense of 'challenging;' कृष्णं:छापुगाजुर:द्यातं 'Krishna challenges
Chāṇūra to battle;' आहृते चेविराणुसुर्मौ Sis. XXI. 1; but
घुमाहृति.
