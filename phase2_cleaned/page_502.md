ज्युयुकुरुणै one by whom grammar is studied; यद्युयुनो यदुयुस्कुदुयुङु by
whom the six Angas were mastered, &c.

The words सायुयु and अस्युयुयु govern the Locative of that with
reference to which they are used; मायुयु सायुयु छुयुयुयुयो मातरि 'well be-
haved towards the mother, अस्युयुयुयुसु ill-behaved towards his
maternal uncle.

(b) * The object or purpose for which any thing is done
is put in the Locative case when the thing desired is intima-
tely connected with that on which the action takes place; युर्मुयुनं
होरिणुनै हरिं sacrifices a goat; भीरुनुयुयुनं हरिं यात्युति पुयुयुकुल्युयो वुयुनुयुनं
(Mb.). (Man) kills the tiger for his skin, the elephant for his
tusks, the Chamary deer for her hair and the musk-deer for his
musk. If there is no intimate union the Dative is used.

Obs,—Sometimes the Instrumental is used to denote the
object for which any thing is done, चेतेनं (i. e wages) भार्युयुं
भुरुति. Sometimes the Loc. is used to denote the object in
general; यथुयु दुड्युतोनै यावु कुर्मुहुं तेकुच् तिकुच् since you are created by
the creator to do duty, fulfil it.

§ 865. † The Locative or the Genitive is used with the
words स्युयुमो a master, हुयुयुरु, अयुनुयुयुत a lord, वुयुयुयुट् a heir, सायुयुनुयु,
यतिवु a ball, and युनुयुयुवु born for; युवुयुयुयो गोयुयु वा स्युयुयुमो the master of
कोयुयु or प्रुयुयुयुयुयु or युयुवुरुयुः owns the ball from the ten kings; प्रुयुयुयुयु
आयुनुयुट् युयुनुयुयुतिः the lord of villages, similarly तेदुयुनुस्युयु निवुदुयुनै वा
यायुयुद्युरै, युनुयुवुर्नों यथुदुयुत्युस्युयु वा शाकुंतो, दुयुनैनं दुयुनुस्युयु वा यायुकुनुयुः (surety
for appearance in a court); गोयुयु गवुयुयो वा यदुयुयु. गोयुयुः a cowherd
is born for cows.
