(6) THE BENEDICTIVE (आशीर्लिङ्ग).

§ 579. The Parasmaipada terminations of this mood may
be obtained by prefixing यास् to those of the Imperative after
dropping its final स् before स्त् and त् of the 2nd and 3rd per.
sings.; and the Ātmanepada ones by prefixing स् to the
terminations of the Ātm. Potential and also to the त् and थ्
occurring in them. These, therefore, are:—

1. यासम्     यास्व     यास्म            1. सीय      सीवहि     सीमहि

3. यात्       यास्ताम्  यासुः            3. सीष्ट     सीयाताम्   सीरन्
