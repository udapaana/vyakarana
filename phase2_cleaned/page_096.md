2—Demonstrative Pronouns.

§ 135. The pronouns तद् 'that or he, she, it,' एतद् 'this,' इदम् 'this' and अदस् 'that'. The Nom. sing of the first two is respectively सः and एषः mas. and सा and एषा fem. In other cases they become त and एत and are declined like nouns in अ except in the Nom. pl., D. Ab. sings., G. pl. and L. sing. the terminations of which are ए, स्मै, स्मात्, एषाम् and स्मिन् mas. and स्यै, स्यास्, स्यास्, साम् and स्याम् fem. respectively. All pronouns ending in अ are similarly declined.
