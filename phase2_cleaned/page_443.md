§ 753. Roots of the 8th class ending in a nasal, except यम्
and the roots मन्, यन् and हन्, drop their nasal necessarily; तक्,
तन्य, यम्य and रुम् do it optionally; तन् with वि—वितत्य; म्रुष् with
प्रति—प्रतिम्रुष्य; तन्य with वि—वितन्य or विततुम्; रुम् with वि—विरुम्य or
विरत्य; मन् with प्र—प्रणम्य or प्रणत्य, &c.

§ 754. The roots सन्, जन् and सन् have respectively निसत्युम्—
निसाय; प्रजन्य—प्रजाय; and प्रसन्य—प्रसाय.

§ 755. द्विष् lengthens its इ before the affix य and जागृ changes
its final vowel to Guṇa; प्रदीय; प्रजाय्य.

§ 756. (a), च्युत् and छुध् do not take Samprasāraṇa, प्रच्युत्य;
उच्युत्य 'having become old,' उप्युत्य 'having covered,' but ऋ
with परि or समु, takes Samprasāraṇa optionally, परिस्युत्य or
परिदुर्य; संस्युत्य or संदीर्य.

§ 757. The roots mentioned under § 486 do not change
their आ to इ; प्रयाय, प्रमाय, प्रमाय, &c.

§ 758. Roots of the Tenth class and Causals preserve their
अय before य; it being separated by a short penultimate vowel if
not, it is dropped; चोरय—परचोर्य; बोधय—परबोध्य; कृ—cau. विकारय; नी
cau. with आ—आनाय, &c.; but गृण—विगृण्य, प्रकृपुम्य, प्रकल्पय;
प्रबोविदुर्य 'having again and again caused to be broken.'

§ 759. (a) गम् cau. may retain its अय optionally—गम्य
or गामय.

§ 760. Desiderative bases add य immediately and Freq.
bases add it after dropping their य, when it is preceded by a
consonant and dropping the final अ when it is preceded by a
vowel; बुबू—परबुबुधिष्य; Fre. परबोबुध्य; भू Fre. परबोभूय &c.
