Another variety of उद्वुता is mentioned where the first
quarter has भ, न, भ. ग instead of भ, न, ज, ल, ग.

  Other kinds of metres in which every quarter
differs in the number of syllables, are included under the
same name ' Gāthā ' The same name is applicable to a stro-
phe consisting of any number of quarters other than four.

Metres regulated by the number of syllabic instants.

§ 14. The most common variety of metres regulated by
syllabic instants is आर्या. It is of nine kinds:—

         पथा वियुला चपला मुखचपला जयमयचपला च ।
         गीत्युपगीत्युद्वुरीतय आर्योभीतिद्यु नवश्रयाः॥

Of these the last four are generally used & described here.

                      आर्या.

  Def. यस्याः पादे प्रथमे द्वुादशा मात्रास्तथा तृतीयेऽपि ।
       अष्टादश द्वितीये चतुर्थैकै पंचदुश सार्धं ॥

The first and third quarters have each 12 mātrās or syllabic
instants, the second 18 and the fourth 15.

  Ex. येनाभन्दं नरेन्द्रै दलदरविन्दै दिनान्युनायिषत ।
       कुट्जे खल्वु तैनैहं तैनैहं मधुकैरेण कथम् ॥
       H. S. G. 96
