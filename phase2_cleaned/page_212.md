त्यक् (त्यक्) is added in the sense of 'living in, belonging to,' &c. to the words क्षत्रिया, पश्चात्, उत्तर, अमात्य, वृद, कृ, शरद्, भवत्, and indeclinables ending in तस्; दाक्षिणात्यः a southerner, पाश्चात्यः, पौरस्त्यः, an inhabitant of the east; अमात्यः; one who accompanies a king, a minister, वृदत्यः कृत्यः; शरत्यः, भवत्यः &c.; also to the preposition नि, नित्यः 'eternal'.

त्यक (त्यकट्) is added to उप and अधि; उपत्यका land at the foot of a mountain, अधित्यका 'a table-land.'

त्र—is affixed to गो only; गवां समूहः गोत्रा f. 'a herd of kine'.

त्व—forms abstract nouns; गोत्वं.

दृश, इयस् and मात्र* (दृशच्, इयसच्, मात्रच्,) are added in the sense of 'measuring as much as'; जानु प्रमाणं अस्य जानुदृश्-इयस्-मात्रे उदकं 'water reaching as far as the knee', &c.

न and स †(नञ्, सञ्)—are added to स्त्री and पुंस् respectively in different senses:—स्त्रैणः 'womanly, feminine, suited to women, (n.) womanhood' &c.; पौंसः 'manly, heroic, fit for a man, (n.) manhood, virility'.

पाष—is added as a depreciatory termination; भिषक्पाषः 'a bad doctor, a quack'; वैयाकरणपाषः &c.; added to केश it has the sense of 'a collection of', as केशपाषः (पश and पस्य are also added to केश in the same sense)

मय (मयट्)—§ is added in the sense of (1) 'made of'; मृदः विकारः मृन्मयं 'made of earth,' काष्ठमयं 'made of wood' &c.; and (2) in that of profusion or excess' यस्मिन् हुतं प्रचुरं
