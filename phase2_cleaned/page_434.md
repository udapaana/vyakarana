By means of the affix क्यप् (य)

§ 723. The roots इ 1. 2. P. 'to go,' स्तु, शास्, इ 5. P. A. ह्व,
दुह्, and roots having ऋ short for their penultimate, except कृष्
and वृत्, take the affix क्यप् (य) in the same sense as यत्. When
a root ends in a short vowel, त् is inserted between the final
vowel of the root; e. g. इ+य=ए+त्+य='worthy of being served;'
स्तुत्यः 'deserving praise;' शास्—शिष्यः 'fit to be instructed;' दुह्—दुह्यः;
इ—आइत्यः; दुह्—दुह्यः 'worthy of being served;' भृ—भृत्यः; दृश्—दृश्यः
'fit to be increased as wealth, &c.' but कल्पः (कृष्+य) 'able;'
वद्—वर्यः (वृत्+एयत्) 'that ought to be told or said.'

§ 724. According to Vāmana, the roots स्तु, ह्व and वह् take
इ. यत्. According to Pāṇini the roots स्तु*—स्तुत्यः 'praiseworthy;' ह्वे—ह्वेत्यः; वह्—वह्यः;
optionally they take the affix एयत् (to be given hereafter); स्तव्यः,
ह्वायः, वाह्यः.

§ 725. शुच् also takes this affix optionally; शुच्यः 'what is fit
or ought to be cleansed;' optionally it takes एयत् before which
the final च् is changed to त्; शोच्यः.

§ 726. (a) भू when it has a Subanta prefixed to it and is
without a preposition takes the affix क्यप् impersonally; ब्रह्मणि
भावः ब्रह्मभूयं 'identity with Brahman.' When no Subanta is used
prepositionally with it, it takes the affix यत्; भव्यः or भूयम्.

(a) वह् under similar circumstances takes either क्यप् or एयत्
in a passive sense in the Veda, namely: वाह्यं or वहयेत् 'expounding
the Veda;' (वहतेवेदः तस्य वेदित् Sid. Kau.).

§ 727. खद् takes this affix, but drops its द् and adds ट् to its
penultimate vowel; खद्+य=ख+य=खट्+य=खेय्यः 'what is fit
or ought to be dug or excavated.'

§ 728. भृ I. cl. takes this affix when the participle so derived
does not form a name; भृत्याः 'those who ought to be nourished
or maintained,' hence 'soldiers,' &c.; but भार्याः (भृ+एयत्) 'a
class of Kshatriyas;' when सम् precedes भृ, क्यप् and एयत् are
added optionally; संभृत्याः or संभार्याः.
