§ 6. In the case of metres regulated by syllables each line
is divided by groups of three syllables called gaṇas or
syllabic feet, named as यू, नु, यू, यू; जु, तु, यू, and स. The names
and the Schemes of these are given in the following stanza.

युयुनुयूछुयूनै:विदुयूयूनै नुद्युयूनै युयूविदुयूयूयू पुरुयूलिड्युयूयूयुयूनुयुः।
जो युयूयुयुस्युयूयुनुर्णो रट्युयूयूरुयुः युौयुस्युयुरुयुयूयूः कुयिनुयूयूल्युयूयुट्युयूयूः ॥

i. e. यू has all its syllables long; नु has all its syllables short; भु
has its first syllable long, यु has its first syllable short; जु has
its middle syllable long; रु has its middle syllable short; सु has
its last syllable long and तु has its last syllable short. †

The symbol ∪ stands for a short or light syllable; the sym-
bol—denotes a long vowel or a heavy syllable. Symbolically
represented these Gaṇas will stand as under:—

यू—— जु∪——
नु∪∪∪ र—∪—
भु—∪∪ सु∪∪—
यू∪—— तु——∪

Similarly the letter युयु is used to denote a short syllable and
यू a long one at the close of a line.

§ 7. In the case of metres regulated by syllabic instants
each line is divided into groups of four mātrās called the Mātrā
Gaṇas; one युयूत्रुयू or instant is allotted to a short vowel and two
to a long one. The Mātrā gaṇas are five in number. These
may be symbolically represented thus:—

यू——, नु∪——, भु∪∪∪, सु∪∪∪, तु∪∪∪,
