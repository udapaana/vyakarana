अ—forms abstract nouns from derivative bases; दु—विकौकुंत्युं a:
desire to do; पुत्युक्युमुच्चुत् a desire to have a son, &c. Also from
roots ending in a consonant having a long vowel for their
penultimate, इत्युद् desir. आयुतै; चुदुष्युत्युं—to युरु reasonable, &c.

युदु—similarly forms abstract nouns; it is added to roots marked
with an indicatory युं and to निश्च् and others; च्—चरुत् old age;
वुयु—वुयुरु shame, &c.; निश्च्—निशुद् distinction; separation; विचुद्—
चिन्तुं contemplation, anxiety; युच्—युच्चुं cleansing &c.—to कुयु
which changes its युट् and युट्—अत्युरुः mercy; to roots giving
a preposition or the words युरु and अत्युरु precede, यू—युञुत् giving
gift, भू—भूयुरु lustre, &c. यू with अरु—अरुयुरु faith; with अत्युरु—
अत्युघै disappearance.

इुवु—(इुरु, पुरुदु, युच्चु, द्युयु, छुमुरु)—

इुरु—is added to रुच्छुयु; रुवुकुः 'a washerman,'

इुर्—to affixed to all roots and denotes the agent of the action;
छु—कारुकुः one who makes, acting, &c,; पुद्—पायुकुः one who
cooks; युदुरु—यूरुरुदुकु: दू—वायुकु:, धुत्त्यु—धोयुकु, &c; it is added to the
युरुयु and other roots of that group, but in this case the preced-
ing vowel is not lengthened युयुर्—युयुकुयु, दुयु—दुरुयुकु, युयुकुत्युरुः
a killer, अयुकुः a father, &c. Added to some roots it forms
the name of diseases; इुच्छु विछुयुद्युंक्तुं vomiting; यदु—पनायुड्युंक्तुं
dysentery; diarrhœa; युड्—दुर्शुत्यु:ड्युंक्रा itch, scab, &c; sometimes
it is added to denote the meanings of roots आवुद्—आतिड्युक्तुं
sitting, शो—चायिकुं sleeping, &c.; sometimes it has the sense
of futurity, छुष्यो दुशैकै यूवुते he goes desirous of seeing
Krishna; नरुर्युं युरुक्रु &c.

त्युरु—is added to निन्युद्, विदुष्, किदुयु, युयुद्; नन्युरु with ति, किदुयु with पति
रुरु., भद्यु, नै, नारु and इतौ in the sense of the 'agent,' or 'in the
habit of,' निन्युद्—निन्युकुः 'one who blames or is in the habit of
blaming or censuring others,' हिंस्—हिंसुकुः; किंदुयु—कैदुयुकु: &c.;
—to विदुष् and इुच्छु preceded by आ; आविदुक्रुः 'a gambler,' आकोक्षुकुः
'one who calls out or vociferates, reviler.'
