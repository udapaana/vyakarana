§ 405. Before strong terminations the penultimate short and the final vowel of the base take their Guṇa substitute.

Fifth, Eighth and Ninth classes

§ 406. * नु and उ are added on to the roots of the 5th and 8th classes respectively before the terminations.

§ 407. The final नु of the base is optionally dropped before म् and व् if it is not preceded by a conjunct consonant. It is changed to ण before a weak termination beginning with a vowel, if preceded by a conjunct consonant and to न in other cases. The हि of the Imperative 2nd per. sing. is dropped after नु not preceded by a conjunct consonant.

§ 408. † In the ninth class न is inserted between the root and the terminations. न becomes ण before the weak terminations beginning with a vowel, and नी before the weak terminations beginning with a consonant.

§ 409. (a) The penultimate nasal of a root is dropped before न &c. as युनक्ति, युञ्जत, युञ्जन्ति &c. from युज् 'to arrange, put together.'

(b) In the case of roots ending in a consonant, the imperative second person singular termination is आन instead of हि; as सुनान from सु 'to press out juice'.
