THIRD VARIETY OR FORM

§ 545. Terminations:—

The same as those of the 2nd Variety.

§ 546. Roots of the tenth class, causals, some derivatives,
the root कम् and the roots भी, हु, and घू when expressing fear or
gent, take this Variety necessarily, and the roots घे and चि take
it optionally.

§ 547. (a) The root is first reduplicated and then the aug-
ment अ and the terminations are added as in the 2nd Variety.
(b) Final इ and उ are changed to इय् and उव् before अ and
final आ is dropped.
