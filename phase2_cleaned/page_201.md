(c) In the case of derivatives form compound words sometimes the initial vowel of the second word takes its Vṛiddhi substitute, and sometimes the Vṛiddhi is double; पुरेवार्षिकः 'belonging to the last year', similarly उपविशालकः &c. सौहार्दम् fr. सुहृद्, सौभाग्यम् from सुभगम्, &c. When these terminations are added to a Dva. comp. both the words of which are the names of deities, Vṛiddhi is substituted for the initial vowel of both, आग्निमारुतं कर्म 'a sacrifice offered to Agni and the Marut deities,' &c.

(d) If the initial vowel of a word be preceded by the य् and व् of a preposition the य् or व् is first changed to इय् or उव् before Vṛiddhi substitute can take place; as व्याकरणम्+अ=वियाकरणम्+अ=वैयाकरणः; स्वप्+अ=सुवप्+अ=सौप्तः; similarly सौवस्तिक from स्वस्ति; सौवरम् fr. स्वर, &c.

(e) Before consonantal Tad. affixes, a final न् is generally rejected; the final न् with the preceding vowel is sometimes dropped before vowel terminations and before such as begin with य्; युवन्-युवकम्, राजन्-राजकम्, &c. आत्मन्-आत्मय-आत्मीयम्. There are various exceptions to this latter part of the rule; e.g. राजन्यः fr. राजन् &c.

N. B.—Other changes the student will easily note from the instances given.
