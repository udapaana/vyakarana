§ 303. The न of वाहन is changed to ण when preceded by a word denoting an object that can be borne; पुष्पवाहणम्; but इन्द्रवाहनम् (इन्द्रस्वामिके वाहनमित्यर्थः: Sid. Kau.).

§ 304. The न of पान is changed to ण necessarily when the compound implies a country and optionally when it signifies merely the act of drinking; as सुरापाणः: (पाण्डयः); क्षीरपाणः: (द्वायणर्देशः); but क्षीरपानम् or पाणम्.

Chapter VIII.

FORMATION OF FEMININE BASES.

§ 305. Feminine bases are derived from the masculine by the addition of the affixes आ (टाप्, डाप्, चाप्), ई (ङीप्, ङीष्, ङीन्), क (कङ्) and ति.

§ 306. Before the affix ई—

(a) nouns ending in consonants assume that form which they take before the termination of Inst. sing.; प्रत्यच्-श्-प्रतीची, राजन्—राज्ञी, भवत्—भवती, श्वन्—शुनी, अर्यमन्—आर्येणी, विद्वस्—विदुषी, &c. There are some exceptions, अर्हन्—अर्हती &c.
