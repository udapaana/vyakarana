क्त—this is added to a root with or without any preposition
prefixed to it. इ or इट् are inserted before it. The words
दिव्युट् and दुरितट् receive this affix but not any preposition.
दिश्—दुष्टुक्त a powerful enemy; पच्—पक्त earth full of water;
पुट—पुतित diving into water; बोध—बोधित one who consecrates
sacred fire; पुर (पुरःसत्त्व)—पुरेबुद्ध: who gets to
the top of the highest Himalaya before all; but also अपुरितत्,
पुरुष्टुट्, &c.
