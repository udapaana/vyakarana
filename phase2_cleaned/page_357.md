§ 581. The final इ or उ is lengthened; final ऋ short is
changed to रि, and final ऋ changed to ऋर् or to ऊर् when preceded
by a labial or व् before the Par. terminations of the Benedictive,
and the य of the Passive. चि—चीयात्, स्तु—स्तुयात्, कृ—कुरियात्,
लृ—लीरियात्, वृ—वूरियात्, &c.

§ 582. Final ऋ preceded by a conjunct consonant and the
root ऋ are changed to Guṇa under the same circumstances;
स्तृ—स्तर्यात्, ऋ—अर्यात्.

§ 583 Roots capable of taking Samprasāraṇa take it. शास्
substitutes इ for its vowel.

§ 584. A penultimate nasal is generally dropped. Some of
the roots dropping their nasal thus are:—भञ्ज, अञ्ज, वञ्ज, रञ्ज,
सञ्ज, रुञ्ज, युञ्ज, मुञ्ज, उञ्च, स्कन्द्, स्यन्द्, इञ्ज, वृञ्ज, स्तम्भ,
सिञ्ज, शुङ्ग, शुम्भ, अञ्च and तुङ्ग.

§ 585. दा, यम् and roots assuming these forms, धा, स्था, पा,
मा, 'to drink', पा, 'to abandon' and शो, change their final to ई.
Final या (original or substituted; see § 459), if it be preceded
by a conjunct consonant, is changed to ई optionally: दा—दीयात्,
यम्—यीयात्, पा—पीयात्, निशा—निशीयात्, निर्शीयात् &c.; but पा to
'protect, पायात्.

(b) Roots ending in ऋ preceded by a conjunct consonant, the
