§ 470. All these terminations are strong. The final vowel and the penultimate vowel of a root therefore take their Guṇa substitute before them.

§ 471. Aniṭ roots with a penultimate अ change it to आ optionally before a strong termination beginning with any consonant except a nasal or a semi-vowel; सू—सोतासि, सोतासि &c.

Irregular Bases.

§ 472. The roots भू, कृष् 1 Â., श्रु, धिय and वद् admit इ optionally in the First Future; e. g. 1 sing. भवितासि, भविष्यासि, श्रुतासि, शेष्यासि, सृजिताहे, शोचाहे; लोभितासि, लोप्स्यासि; रवितासि, रेष्यासि; वदिष्यति, वक्तासि.

§ 473. कुप् is optionally Parasm. in the First Future and when so it rejects इ: कोपितासि, कोप्तासि; कोपिताहे 1 sing. &c.

§ 474. The augment इ as added to सह् is long in all Non-conjugational Tenses, except in the Perfect; सहीतासि 1 sing.
