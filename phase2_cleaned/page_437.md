§ 737. (a) The roots युज्, याच्, रुच्, प्रथ्, स्पृश्, and एच्
do not change their ऋ or व to वृ or यु before घ्यत्; योज्यं
याच्यं; रोच्यं, प्रथ्यंयुम् (प्रत्यशीतव्यः), ज्ञेय्यं, स्पश्यं, गाच्यं.

(b) वच् does not change its चु to ऋ before घ्यत् when the
meaning is 'what ought to be spoken out, speech,' वाच्यं; but
वाक्यं; 'a sentence.'

(c) पञ्च् when it means 'to go' does not change चु its to
ऋ; पञ्च्यं; when it means 'to bend' the चु is changed to
ऋ; पञ्कुवं शरीरं.

(d) युज् when it is preceded by प्र and वि, takes the affix एयत्
in the sense of 'what is possible or capable of;' and does
not change its ऋ to यु; प्रयुङ्कुम् शक्यं प्रयोज्यं; निवौङुकुम् शक्यः
निवौज्यः; शङुयः!

(e) भुज् has भोजं meaning 'food,' and भोज्यं 'what is fit
to be enjoyed.'

§ 738. Roots ending in ऋ, short or long, take the affix एलिम्
in the sense of 'what ought or must necessarily be done;'
कृ—कर्तुं 'what must necessarily be cut off,' पाक्तुं 'what
must necessarily be cooked,' दा to be with आ—आत्तुं; प्र 'to
mix,' यात्, &c.

(a) The roots वच्, प्रथ्, द्युत्, भृत् and वद् also do the same,
वाच्यं 'what must necessarily be on;' प्रथ्युत् 'what must be
spoken of distinctly;' द्युत्युत्, भात्युत्, वात्युत्.

§ 739. The following words are irregularly derived by
means of the affix घ्यत्; आहात्यं 'what ought to be brought from
the Gārhapatya i. e. the Dakshiṇā-gni;' (fr. नी with आ)
विद्युणामाविनांगे दृत्युतृद। य हि गाहपत्युगाम्यानात्रोऽक्षिनां स्य तत्संतप्रत्र्यूतोगाम्।
(Sid. Kau.); but नायं in other senses 'to be led, &c.' मोपुनुमार्यं
(fr. नी with मो) चौरः मेनोवनुहे डुर्युथुर्येः (Sid. Kau.); 'disgusted
with worldly pleasures;' as प्रणायुयूनैवोचारो विरक्तो डुर्युथुर्येः। but
प्रणेयं in other cases.
