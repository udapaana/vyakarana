§ 859. * In the case of Potential passive participles the agent
of the action is put in the Genitive or in the Instrumental case;
मया मम वा हेयुयो हरिः Hari ought to be served by me; राक्तुयुसुनुयु-स्युयु
नुयुयुरुमु तृयुतुरुयुः (Bhaṭṭi VIII. 125) the forest together with the
lord of the demons ought to preserve must be destroyed by me;
गन्तव्युयु ते वसुतिरुटुकुयु हे. (Meg.) Thou should go to Alakā.

§ 860. † In the case of words denoting equality or likeness,
such as तुल्युयु, सदुशु, &c., the person or thing with whom or which
any object is compared is put in the Genitive case, except in
the case of तुल्युयु there is no compulsion; युयुणुस्युयु कृष्णुस्युयु वा
equal to or like Kṛṣṇa; कोऽन्युयोऽस्युत सदुशुयो मम who else is equal
to me? but छुयुयुस्युरु तुल्युयो उयुयुयु वा नारिंङु (Sid. Kau.)

Obs.—The words तुल्युयु and उयुयुयु, however, are found used by
good anthers with the Instrumental against Pāṇini's rule;
तुल्युयो यवुरुयुहेनं वनुयुवुयुसु (Kum. V. 34.) which rises to the high
position of being equal with your lip; स्तुनुयुयुर्युं अतिरुक्तुनं
शुङुबुनु (Siṣ. I. 4.,) clearly deserving to be compared with Śambhu
white with ashes; see Rag. VIII. 15.

§ 861. ‡ The words आयुयुयुर्युं, मङुंङु, भुङु, कुयुशुलं, इयुनं, अयुयुः and हित
and words having the same sense, when used in a sentence con-
taining a benediction, govern the Dative or the Genitive case;
आयुयुयुर्युं चिरुञ्जीवितं छुयुयुयुयु छुयुयुयुस्युयु वा युयुरुयुट् (Sid. Kau.); may
Kṛṣṇa live long; similarly मङुं, भुङु, कुयुशुलं, निरुयुयुर्युं, इयुनं, हो, अयुयुः-
युयुयोजुनुनं. हित. पुयुर्युनं वा युयुरुयुट् (Sid. Kau.).
