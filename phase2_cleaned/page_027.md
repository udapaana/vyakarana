§ 24. ए, ओ, ऐ and औ, when followed by a vowel, are changed to अय, अव, आय, and आव respectively†; e. g. हरे + ए = हरय for Hari; विष्णो + ए = विष्णव for Viṣṇu; ने + अक: = नायक: A leader; पो + अक: = पावक: the purifier i. e. fire.

(a) य or व at the end of a word and preceded by अ or आ is optionally dropped when followed by a vowel or a soft consonant§; e. g. हरे + एहि = हरयेहि or हर एहि; विष्णो + इह = विष्णविह or विष्णु इह; श्रिये + उपत: = श्रियायुपत: or श्रिया उपत: intent on getting money; गुरो + उत्क: = गुरावत्क: or गुरा उत्क: longing to see the preceptor.

Note—Two vowels brought together by the disappearance of an intervening consonant or visarga do not coalesce.

(b) ओ and औ, when followed by a termination beginning with य, become अव् and आव् respectively ‡; e. g. गो + य = गव्यम् produced from a cow as milk, ghee &c. नौ + य = नाव्यम् accessible by a boat, navigable.

N. B. This rule holds good, in the case of roots, only when the ओ or औ is brought in by virtue of a termination; × e. g. छृ + य = छो + य = छव्यम् fit to be lopped off. अवच्छपट् + य = अवच्छपौ + य = अवच्छपाव्यम् which ought necessarily to be cut off.
