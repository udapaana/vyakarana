Imperfect.

§ 393. The augment अ is prefixed to roots in this tense. This अ is replaced by आ in the case of roots beginning with a vowel*; this आ forms Vṛddhi with an initial vowel; e. g. अ+ईक्ष्+त्=आ+ईक्ष्+अ+त्=ऐक्षत्; similarly ईक्ष्—ऐक्षत्; उक्ष्—औक्षत्; ऊह्—औहत्; ऋच्छ्—आर्च्छत् &c.

(a) When a preposition (उपसर्ग) is prefixed to a root, the augment अ or आ comes between the prep. and the root; e. g. हृ with प्र, प्राहरत्.
