(2) Participles in य (यत्, क्यप् and घ्यत्).
By means of यत् (य).

§ 714. The affix य (यत्)* is added on to roots ending in a
vowel in the sense of 'fit to be, or ought to be.' Before
this य the radical vowel undergoes Guṇa substitute and final
आ (इ, ऋ and ओ changeable to आ) is changed to ए.

    दा—देयः what is fit or ought to be given.
    पा—पेयः       „       „       sucked.
    गै—गेयः       „       „       sung.
    भो—भोयः      „       „       cut.
    ग्रथ्—ग्रैयः      „       „       collected.
    नी—नेयः       „       „       led or carried.

§ 715. Roots having अ for their penultimate and ending in
a consonant of the labial class take the affix य; शाप्—शाप्यः; दुष्—
दुष्यः; रप्—रप्यः, &c.

(a) When वध् is preceded by अपि, य् (changed to इ) is inserted
between इ and प्; अपिवधो—आपेधयः 'what ought or is fit to be
killed;' य is also inserted when द्रुष् is preceded by उप if the
meaning be 'to praise;' उपद्रुष्यः साधुः 'a Sādhu ought to be
praised;' but उपकुष्यं धनं 'wealth ought to be acquired.'

§ 716. The roots हस् 'to laugh at,' भाष् 'to kill,' पृच् 'to
go on,' or 'ask,' यत् 'to strive,' जय्, भक् and शक् take the
affix य; हस्यं 'what is fit to be laughed at,' भाष्यं 'what ought to
be killed;' &c.
