(a) Similarly a Bah. ending in द्वायन्, and हायन् denoting age, and having a numeral for its first member takes ई in the fem.; द्विहायनी, त्रिहायनी 'a girl two years old;', त्रिहायणी, &c; but द्विहायना शाला a building built two years since.

N. B.—the य of हायन् is changed to ण after द्वि and बहुर् only when it takes ई; बहुहैयणी बाला; but द्विहायना, बहुहैयना शाला.

§ 329. When a compound has for its second member a word denoting a limb of the body of an animal and having no conjunct consonant preceding its final vowel, the fem. is formed by the addition of आ or ई; अतिकेशा-शी; 'a women having abundant hair;' सुकेशा-शी; चन्द्रमुखा-खी; but सुगुल्फा having beautiful ankles; हस्तनी or हस्तना (आ वा प्रतिषेधा वा;) हट्टका शाला 'an edifice with a beautiful front'.

(a) If the word denoting the limb, however, be one of the words कोष, नास् जङ्घ, उस्रा, शिखा, वाल, शाफ, शुक, भुज, कर &c. or have more than two syllables, the fem. is formed in आ alone; काश्यपात्कोषा (अद्रिमाचूर्णः कोषा Sid. Kau.), पृथुजघना, having large hips; बहुलनयना, &c.

(b) Also when the first member is one of स, सह, or विष्वमात्, the fem. is formed in आ necessarily; सकेशा, अकेशा, विष्वमात्नासिका, सहनासिका &c.
