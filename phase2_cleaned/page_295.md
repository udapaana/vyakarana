II. General or Non-Conjugational Tenses and Moods.

§ 457. In the General Tenses and Moods and in the formation of verbal derivatives generally, the augment इ is prefixed (necessarily or optionally) to terminations beginning with any consonant except य in the case of certain roots. Such roots as take the augment इ necessarily are called Seṭ (स+इट् i. e. with इ); such as take it optionally are called Veṭ (वा+इट्), and those that do not, are called Aniṭ (अन्+इट् i. e. without इ).

§ 458. (a) Roots of more than one syllable, derived roots and roots of the 10th class are always Seṭ.

(b) Of all monosyllabic roots ending in a vowel those given in the following couplet (Kārikā) are Seṭ and the rest Aniṭ.

"ऊदूनौयौगेधिदेयूज्ञोविदुद्राबिहारिभिमिभिः।
इडङ्ढुळभ्यां च विनेकाच्योऽजन्तेषु निघाताः स्थूताः॥

i. e. roots ending in long ऊ and long ऋ, the roots ग्लै, धू, ध्यै
