Words ending in ओ and औ.

§ 86. Vṛddhi ( औ ) is substituted for the ओ of words ending
in ओ in the first five inflections except the Acc. sing.; आ is
substituted for ओ in the Acc. sing. and plural.* Nouns ending
in औ are regularly declined.
