अ—is added to roots having इ, उ, क, or छ for their penulti-
mate, and to the roots युट् and कू and denotes the agent; लिख—
लिखः 'one who writes,' क्षिप्—क्षिपः 'one who throws,' कुप्—कुपः &c.
अ—is added to roots ending in आ with one who soothsays; it is also
added to roots ending in आ with or without any preposition,
the final आ being dropped; ज्ञा—ज्ञः or प्रज्ञः one who knows,
wise; ह्वे—ह्वेः or आह्वेः one who calls; also when a subanta is
used prepositionally with it; दुह्—गोदुहः one who gives cows or
cuts the hair; हस्ति—हस्तिः (अरतिहस्ति) an elephant; when
added to वह् it has various meanings; समुदः happy, विवुद्धः
placed in difficulties; परुदः a measure of corn, &c. This is
also added to भृङ्—भृङ्ः a house, वह्वाः a wife, a house.

अच्—is added to दृश् not meaning 'to see' when a pronoun pre-
cedes it; मत्+दृश्—अ=मादृशः like that; also when the words
समान and अन्य precede; सदृशः like to, अन्यादृशः like another.
It is added similarly; सदृशः कारवः, सदृयः &c.

त् and तु—Before these the अ of the noun forming the object
of the root with which it is used prepositionally inserts the
syllable त् after it. सुदत् is added to the root अद् after निष्च्
and रव्; निष्च् वद्धुते विरुवुच्: 'one who speaks sweetly; रुवुवुच्:
'subject to the influence of, obedient to the will of;—छु after
the words इष्, हिम्, यौत्, चित्तुः:—छुत् after 'doing action,'
&c.; भयुकः causing fear, dreadful, अभयुवः;—to the root गम्
after a subanta; विदुगुमः passing through the sky, a bird;
—to the roots यू, दु, द्यु, नि, युव्, रुट्, त्र्यु and वुद्यु when the
whole is a name of the Supreme God; युयुते: in the name of the Sama
Veda; युतिवुत्तुं a bird who chooses her husband; चुङ्घुरुणुः an
elephant, कर्णुरुः name of a mountain; एवुत्तुः name of a
king; आदेवु name of a king;—to घुर् after वाजु, वायुवुयुस्:
one who restrains his speech for the observation of a vow;
—to परु: the roots युत् and युट् अत् are all the words युट् respec-
tively; सर्वुच्छुवा the earth, सूर्युत्तुः Indra;—to कूत् after the words
रुत्, छुत्, अत्यु and क्तुत्त्यु; रुत्युकुः all destroying, all powerful;
