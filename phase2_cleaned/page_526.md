§ 945. When time after a युयुहुयूर्तुं (nearly equal to an hour) is
expressed, the Imperative is used; छुहुनोयुयूयुनुयूतुयूयुं स्युयु offer the sacri-
fice after an hour.

§ 946. The Imperative with the particle स्युयु is used when a
request is courteously expressed; यालुयूयूयूयूयू स्युयु please teach
the child.

§ 947. The Imperative has sometimes the force of the pre-
sent when used in combination with the particle युयु; युयु भुवुदु no,
it is not so; युयु च तै तियुयुयुः शुयूच्युयुन्युयुयूयुवुयुदु पुयूथिवु।

§ 948. इच्छुयूमि युयूयूहुयूजीतुयू or इयुकुयूयू I wish you should dine.
See § 952.

§ 949. * There is a peculiar use of the Imperative which
ought to be noticed. The Imperative second person sigular is
repeated when frequency of an act is indicated and the whole
used with the root in any tense; युयूहि युयूहि हुति युयूति (Sid. Kau.)
he goes every now and then; यकुयूहि युकुयूहि युयूक्षुयूयुं युकुयूहि (Sid Kau) he
युीदुट्; अयुयोयुयूयूयुनैयुनैयुयूयूते be studies steadily. The Imperative
second person is also used when several acts are described as
done by the same person; शुकुयुच्छु रिरु, यानुयुः सुदुचुयूयुयूयूहुयूरुयूति (Sid.
Kau.) he takes his meal, now eating fruit, now eating fried
rice; similarly अयुकुनुयुं वुयुदैयु दुवियुकुयूयूयूयूवुदुयुयुयुयूयुवुयुरुयूते (Sid. Kau.).
