§ 663. The Causals of the roots कृष्, सुद्, जल्, इ with अभि, भृ,
हु, and हू are Parasm; कोषयति पदवं, घोषयति काव्यादपि, मासयति
दूषकं, जनयति डुवक, अध्यापयति, भावयते भारणवेदस्वर्थे: (Sid.
Kau.); ज्ञापयति विज्ञाप्यर्थेस्वर्थे: (Sid. Kau.). ज्ञापयति स्तम्भवर्थेस्वर्थे:
(Sid. Kau.).

§ 664. The following is an alphabetical list of the roots that
change their proper Pada after certain prepositions under the
circumstances given in each case.

अद्—when preceded by a preposition is conjugated in either
Pada; अध तिरस्यति-ते.

इ with अभि-in the causal takes the Parasm. अध्यापयति.

ऊह्—when preceded by a preposition is conjugated in either
Pada; पापं अवोहति-ते 'he destroys sin;' उदूहति 'discards; संगृ-
ह्णति-ते 'he gathers together.'

ऋ with सम् is Ātm.; समारन्त समासीष्टा: Bh. VIII. 16; 'all
my desires have come to me i. e. are gratified.'

ऋच्छ with सम्—is Par. when used transitively, and Ātm.
when used intransitively; समच्छति 'he collects;' समच्छते 'is
collected.'

कृ—without a preposition admits of either Pada. It is
Parasm, with सम् and परि; समीकरोति वेदम् (Kādā.),
तौ सीदयामाबरातुङ्गेत &c. (Bhaṭṭi. VIII. 9.); it is Ātm. with
preposition in following senses:—(1) हिंसन † or 'hurting,' 'kill-
ing,' as उत्करुते 'informs against (with a view to injure)'
(2) अवमानन or 'censuring, overcoming;' उदाकुरुते श्येनको गर्तिको:स्य
