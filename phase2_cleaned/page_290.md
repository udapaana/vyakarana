§ 454. हā 'to abandon' assumes the forms जहि or जहो before consonantal weak terminations except in the Pot. and जह् before vowel terminations and those of the Pot. The Imp. 2 sing. forms are जहाहि, जहिहि and जहोहि.

Seventh or रुधादि Class.

§ 455. In this class the base is formed by inserting न between the radical vowel and the final consonant before the strong and ण before the weak terminations.

§ 456. (a) The original nasal of a root is dropped.

(b) In the case of the root भुज्, व is changed to उ before consonantal strong terminations.

Paradigms.

         Present.                       Imperfect.

         Imperative                    Potential.
