Potential.

    Par.                          Ātm.
S.      D.       P.           S.       D.        P.
1.  ईयम्    ईव       ईम           ईय       ईवहि      ईमहि
2.  ईः      ईतम्     ईत           ईथाः     ईयाथाम्   ईध्वम्
3.  ईत्     ईताम्    ईयुः         ईत       ईयाताम्   ईरन्

Note:—Terminations beginning with vowels may be called Vowel terminations; those beginning with consonants, consonantal terminations.

Formation of the base of the roots of the first group:—

§ 388. अ (शप्) is added on to the roots of the First or भ्वादि class* before the terminations. Before this अ the penultimate short and the final vowel of a root take their Guṇa substitute; e. g.

बुध्+ति=बुध्+अ+ति=बोधति; जि+अ+ति=जे+अ+ति=जयति, &c.

§ 389. य (श्यन्) is added on to the final of a root of the Fourth or दिवादि class before the terminations; the radical vowel remains unchanged; e. g. तुष्+ति=तुष्+य+ति=तुष्यति.

§ 390. अ is added on to the roots of the sixth or तुदादि class before which the penultimate vowel remains unchanged, and the final इ, उ, short or long, ऋ and ॠ are changed to इय, उव, रिय
