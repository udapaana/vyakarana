IRREGULAR BASES

§ 509. The roots श्रन्थु, मन्थु, रम्भु. and स्वञ्ज् drop their nasal
optionally before the terminations of the Perfect. श्रन्थु, मन्थु and
रम्भु obey § 500, even before the strong terminations when their
nasal is dropped.

## Footnotes

* The final ह् of the roots दुह्, मुह्, स्निह्, and स्तुह् is changed
to घ् or ढ् when followed by any consonant except a nasal or a
semi-vowel or by nothing.
