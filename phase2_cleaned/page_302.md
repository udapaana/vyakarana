§ 485. The roots कृत्, वृत्, गृध्, वृध् and वृश् take इ optionally when followed by an Ārdhadhātuka (Non-conjugational termination) beginning with स except in the Aorist; कृत्—कर्तिष्याम, कर्त्स्याम; अकर्तिष्यम्, अकर्त्स्याम Cond. &c.

§ 486. In the case of इ with अधि, गा is optionally substituted for इ in the Conditional and the Aorist. इ is substituted for the final vowel of the verbs च्या 3 P. Â. 1 P., प्रा, दो, ते, धे, मा, स्था, भा (substituted for इ 2 P. and स्था with अधि,) म्ला, मा, and को before a consonantal weak termination. All terminations added to मा substituted for इ are weak.
