§ 144. The affix आति is added to तद्, यद् and किम् to show number or measure e. g. तति 'so many', यति 'as many,' and कति ' how many'. These are declined in the plural only, and take no termination in the Nom. and Acc.; e. g. Nom. and Acc. कति Inst. कतिभिः the rest like हरि.

8—Reciprocal Pronouns.

§ 145. Reciprocity is expressed by the repetition of the pronominal adjectives अन्य, इतर and पर; e. g. अन्योन्य, इतरेतर, and परस्पर. These are generally used in the sing. and also adverbially e. g. परस्परेण स्पृहणीयशोभौ &c. ( Rag. VII. 14); परस्परं विवद्न्ते &c. They are more commonly used as the first members of compounds; as अन्योन्यक्रोधाजननात् बभूव &c. (Ku. I, 44; इतरेतरयोगात् (Sis. X. 24.) &c.

9- -Possessive Pronouns.

§ 146. Possessive pronouns are formed by means of the secondary affix ईय added to तद्, एतद्, अस्मद्, and युष्मद्; and अ and ईन added to अस्मद्, and युष्मद्, changed respectively to मामक् and तावक् in the sing. and आस्माक् and यौष्माक् in the plu. before these; e. g.
