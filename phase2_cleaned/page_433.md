§ 718. The roots वच् implying reproach, पण् meaning 'to
transact business,' and वृ 9. Atम. not denoting limit or restraint,
take the affix य; इः य is prefixed to वच् if it hints reproachingly or
demnation; but अनुप (अन्+वच्+क्यप् i. e. य) गुरोः 'the name
of a preceptor ought not to be uttered (out of respect),' पण्यः
गौः 'a cow is saleable;' but पाण्यः (पण्+एयत् i. e. य) ब्राह्मणः 'a
Brāhmaṇa deserving praise;' वर्यः (that can be chosen or sought
after)' a title of विष्णु. शतेन वरा 'hundred men (i. e. any one) can seek
the hand of a girl;' but वार्या (वृ+क्यप् i. e. य) कन्या 'to be married
by a particular man.'

§ 719. The root वह् not denoting an instrument for carrying,
and ऋ in the sense of 'a master or a Vaishya' take य; वह्यं 'a
carriage or a vehicle;' but वाह्यः (वह्+एयत्) 'what can be borne,'
आर्यः 'a master or a Vaishya;' but आर्येः (ऋ+एयत्) 'fit to be
approached or adored.'

§ 720. भू with जम् in the senses of 'being conceived' takes the
affix य; as उपुरग्नः गौः संभावयमानां तेन गर्भेण संभोज्यते (Sid.
Kau.) but उभूसंभावनीयः (उभू+एयत्) कादाचिः साधुस्य उपक्षये (Sid. Kau.).

§ 721. जृ takes this affix with the negative particle prefixed
to it and forms अजर्यं 'what cannot grow old;' but this must
qualify संगतं 'friendship;' cf. तेन संगतमेण रामयोरयं कथं जरति।
Bhaṭṭi. VI. 53. In अत्रैरर्जर्यं जरसंबोध्यमानेन नित्यसंगत्वात् Aṅg.
XVIII. 7, the word अजर्यं can be construed as an adverb; but if
does not qualify संगतं, it will take the affix य; as अजरिता कन्या.

§ 722. हन् takes the affix यत् optionally, before which वध् is
substituted for it; हन्+य=वध्यः='what ought to be killed,'
it optionally takes एयत्, in which case घात् is substituted
for it; घात्यः.
