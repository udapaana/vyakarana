Exceptions:—

(a) ज्ञा, जाग्, स्था and आस्य take इ; the final vowel of the
first two takes Guṇa substitute, and the last two drop their
final; ज्ञायित, जागरित; स्थित, आस्यदित.

(b) पत् takes इ though it admits of इ optionally in
Desiderative; पतित.

(c) The Anit roots वस् and श्लिष् admit इ before त्;
उषित, शुषित.

§ 685. Set roots (subject to § 684), and all derivative bases
admit of इ; roots of the Tenth Class and causals reject their
final अय, Par. Frequentatives their final अ, and Ātम.
