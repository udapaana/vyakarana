क—(उरस्)—is added to हस्, पत्, पद्, स्था, घ्रा, त्वष्, हन्, कम्, गम्
and द्रु and denotes the agent; हस्—हास्कः sporting, glittering;
पादुकः falling; घ्रा—भ्राजुकः shining, living; हन्—घातुकः;
कम्—कामुकः amorous.

ट—(इच्युट्)—is affixed to विद्, भिद्, and छिद्; विदुट् who knows,
knowing; भिदेट् breaking; भेदुट्; cutting, &c.

य—(स्य) is added to जाग् and the Pre. Bases of भज, जन
and दंश; जागर्य्युक 'watchful,' &c. (vide Bhatti. II. 22, Rag.
XIV. 85, Sis. XX. 36); पुन: पुन: अतिथैर्भज्यते यज्ञसत्रिकः।
भायज्युक: 'one who performs sacrifices frequently' (see Bhatti.
II. 22.); पुनः पुन: अतिथेन जन नीति जनज्युक: 'one who
makes to be born repeatedly, an ascetic;' पुन: पुन: भशुन्देरव ना
दशतीति दशज्युक: 'biting frequently, a serpent, demon' (vide
Bhatti I. 26).

क्न, किन् and विन्—Derivatives are formed with these affixes
which are added to roots and then dropped; the difference
between the first two is that in the case of the latter
before the dropped क् and the root, ए, ए ends in a
short vowel.

क्त—is affixed to स्पृश preceded by a subanta; स्पृशस्पृक् 'one who
touches ghee; मन्त्रस्पृक् 'one who touches anything after recit-
ing a holy verse,' &c. except when the subanta means water;
उदकस्पर्श: 'one who touches water' and not उदकस्पृक्. The
following words are to be accepted as they are आत्रक्त,
(कर्ते कर्ते यज्ञे) sacrificing regularly at every season;
a priest who officiates at a sacrifice; दुस्—दुर्घ—proud,
haughty; कृद्—कृत् a garland; दिश—दिशा a direction; विद्—
हरित्: name of a metre, words like चाट्वीत् derived from the
roots अञ्च् and the words पूर्व and भ्राङु ought to be considered
as derived by means of this affix.
