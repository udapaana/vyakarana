§ 643. The consonant यु and the syllable अस् are inserted
between the nominal stem and the affix य; so मधु आदायं इच्छति
मधुस्यति or मधुस्यति 'he wishes for honey'; so सुधिस्यति, सुधस्यति,
&c. Final अ is dropped before अस्; पुत्रस्यति.

(a) अत् is inserted after मृग् and अश्व when the sense to be con-
veyed is that of longing for them, after क्षीर and लवण् when the
one is desired to be drunk and the other to be licked; मृगस्यति गौः
'the cow longs for the ox,' अश्वस्यति वड्वा 'the mare lngs for the
horse;' क्षीरस्यति बालः 'the child desires to drink,' लवणस्यति ओष्ठः
'the camel wishes for licking the salt.' But मृषीयति 'he desires to
have a ball,' अर्यीयति 'he wishes for a horse,' क्षीरायति, लवणायति.

§ 644. The affix य is not added to nouns ending in स् and to
indeclinables: स्वरिच्छति, स्वरिच्छति (he longs for heaven).
