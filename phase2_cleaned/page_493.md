§ 842. * When a word expressive of 'a debt,' is merely
intended to be stated as a cause, it is put in the Ablative case;
शुतात्युदुवुदुङु द्युयु a thing mortgaged for a hundred rupees, कुट्युणुयुदुवुदुङु
इयु tied down as it were by the debt he owes.

§ 843. (a) The Ablative often denotes the cause of an action
or condition and may be translated by 'on account of, by reason
of,' &c; भौनुयुन्युयुयुदीः गणुयुते one is considered a fool by reason of
his silence (if he keeps silence); गोमानुप्युयुयुणों क्तुयुयुट् (H.) on
account of my killing cows and men.

(b) The Ablative is used to make a causative assertion or to
advance an argument; यवुतो भूद्युयुयुयु यमुयुयुट् । The mountain is
fiery (has fire on it); because there is smoke सत्युयुरुणुयुरु-
युयुकुदु हेति चुन्युयुत्युयुयुयुच्युयुयुनुवुकुयुयुयुदोनुयुयुनोयुयुयुट् (Ved, Su II I. 1.) the
disputant says—if you say that our argument is liable to the
fault of giving no scope to your smṛitis then we reply: this your
argument will not hold; for in that case other smṛitis will
have no scope given to them.

(c) The Ablative is used with comparatives or words having
a comparative sense; भुक्तुयुयुङुर्युंट् ज्ञानुयुगुर्णों श्रुयुयुयुट् the way of
knowledge is more efficient than that of devotion; अणुयुरुयुणुयुणोचुयुच्युरु
smaller than an atom; अयुयुनैनुयुसुहुदुभुयुस्युः सत्युयुयुवुनोतुरिच्युयुनं truth
alone is superior to a thousand horse-sacrifices; वैयुवुरुयुयुयुरुयुयुङुनो not
inferior to Chaitraratha.

§ 844 † The indeclinables पृथुकु, विना and नाना govern the
Ablative, the Accusative and the Instrumental cases; पृथुकुच्
रामुयुट्–रायुनं-रायुणुनं वा different from or without Rāma; so नाना रामुट्.
