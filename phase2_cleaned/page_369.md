§ 605. (a) भू 'to throw,' पीड् 'to destroy,' भू 'to perish,' भू 'to
conquer,' and हृ 'to buy,' also insert व्, but after changing their
final vowel to आ; भावयति, पीडापयति, जापयति, क्रापयति.

(b) The roots ले, भक् or पे 'to cook' and भा (mit.), shorten
their vowel necessarily, and प्ले and दा optionally, before the
inserted व् when not preceded by prepositions; क्लृपयति, &c.क्लूपयति,

रम्भूपयति, वर्पयाति, स्वापयति; but प्रदापयति, प्रम्भूपयति only.
