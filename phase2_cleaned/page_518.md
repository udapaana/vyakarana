§ 921. *With interrogatives the Present is often used in the
sense of the Future and has also to be regarded as implying कुयु युयु
i. e. (कुरिष्युयूमि), कं गच्छुयूमि i. e. (गमिष्युयूमि) What shall I do?
Whither shall I go? एतुयूयुयुः कुतुनुयुं or एतुयूयुयुयो किमुयुयुं भोजुनुयुयुरि (i. e. भो-
ज्युयुयुयुयुयुरि or भोजुयूयुयुयूरुयूनुरि) which of these persons will you feed?
so को दु युदुयुयोति युयूयूनुति, &c.; but कुः युयूयुयुनं युनुदुयुयुयूति।

(a) It is also used in the sense of the Future in conditional
sentences as implying a condition of the occurrence of the
desired object; रोकुनं दुयूयूति (रात्युयु द्युयुस्युयूति वा) स स्युयुनै यात्युति (मातु
यास्युयूति वा) he, who offers or (will offer) food, goes (or will go)
to heaven i. e. if one offers &c.

§ 922 With the words यावुट्, तावुट् and others having a
similar sense, the Present is sometimes used in the sense of the
Future perfect; यावुस्युरु त्युयुयुनों न युयुयूति तावुयुदुयूयुयूनुरु before he sees
you, move away (before he shall have seen you, &c.).

(b) † With the particles युयुयु and यावुयु the Present has the
sense of the Future when certainty is indicated; यावुयूयूते साराभिदुयूट्
स्युयुरुयू (Rag. X. 25.) it will endeavour to accomplish your
object. युयूयु वसुदुयूरोयो जयुति बहुयूयूयु (S'ak. VII. 33) he will
conquer the earth consisting of seven continents. युरुयूहुयूते तवु
वुच्युकुनै नमुः। Kir. VIII. 8.

§ 923.‡ The particle स्युयु when used with the Present converts it
into a Past tense; प्रतिच्छुन्युर्णेति युविषुयूयुवुयुनों युवुत्तुरुयूयुयु तत्युयु भुयूत्युयूदुरोयो निषुयू-
