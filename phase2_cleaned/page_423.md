दितः or प्रस्वेदितः चैत्रः 'Chaitra has begun to perspire;' प्रस्वेदितं or
प्रस्वेदितमनेन 'he has perspired,' &c.; स्वेदु 4. cl. has स्वेदित also.

N. B. When the participle is not used in the sense given
above these roots reject इ; स्पृष्टः—स्पृष्ट; 'perspired, sounded
inarticulately,' &c.

§ 687. (a) अञ्च् in the sense of 'to worship' takes इ; अञ्चित
(b) गम् 'bent' not in the sense of worship गुप्तः with सम्—समूढः (समुङ्गिरे शबरीति पदै).

(b) युज् and शुष् reject इ when they express the idea of 'immo-
deration' or 'rudeness;' युष्टः 'rude,' विषृष्टः 'ill-mannered;' but युजितः
'overpowered, trifled with,' &c., विश्रुषित 'tormented or ill-treated.'

§ 688. न* is substituted for त् when it immediately follows a
final इ or ऋ; this final इ is also changed to ई; शि—शीन, ऋ—ऋण;
पुल्—पूर्ण (See § 693).

Exceptions:—

(a) शीग् has शीतं when it means 'a part, a portion,' शीनं in
other senses.

(b) विङ् 6. P. A. takes त् in the sense of 'fit for enjoyment or
famous,' विक्तः 'wealth, property,' विकः 'famous'; विनः in other cases.

(c) भृङ्, पूर् and चुस्छु; भृतः, पूर्णः 'filled,' (also पूतः when it means
the same as अत्), चुस्छुः.

§ 689. Roots ending in ऋ† (ऋ, लृ and ओ changeable to आ)
and beginning with a conjunct consonant containing a semi-
vowel also substitute न् for त्; ह्रु 'to run, to sleep,' प्राणः; म्लै 'to
fade,' म्लानः; स्रु—स्त्राणः 'collected to a mass,' &c.

Exceptions:—ग्रै 'to name,' &c., स्वै 'to contemplate,' ग्राणः and
प्लै; स्वितः, स्वानः, ग्रातः and प्रातः.

§ 690. *The roots given at § 414 and ज्ञा substitute न् for त्.
