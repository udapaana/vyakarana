आ—'up to, towards, all round, a little,' &c.; आच्छाद् 'to cover all round,' आकारः 'form or shape (within due bounds), आकाशः 'that which shines all round' i. e. 'the ether'; आकम्प 'to shake a little,' &c.

उद्—द्—'upon,' &c.; उद्गम् 'to go up,' उद्यमः 'industry,' उत्सर्गः 'pouring out, hence a gift, a general rule,' &c.

उप—'near to, towards, by the side of,' &c.; उपगा 'to go near to,' उपकृति f. 'an obligation,' उपरति f. 'death,' उपस्थानं 'praise, worship,' उपमिति f. 'comparison,' &c.

दुस्—स्—'bad, hard to be done,' &c.; दुराचारः 'bad conduct,' दुष्कर 'hard to be done,' दुःसह 'difficult to be borne,' &c.

नि—'in, into, great, opposed to,' &c.; निड् 'to insult,' निकेत 'a house,' निचय 'a heap, a great collection,' निपीत 'drunk in,' निदेश 'a command,' &c.

निस्—स्—'out of, away from, without,' &c.; निःसृ 'to issue out,' निर्गम 'a passage out,' निर्दोषः 'out of i. e. free from blame,' निः शंक 'without doubt,' &.

परा—'away, back, opposed to,' &c.; पराकृ 'to reject, to despise,' पराक्रम 'to act bravely,' परागत 'gone away,' पराञ्च 'turn back,' पराजय 'what is opposed to victory, defeat,' &c.

परि—'all round, about,' &c.; परिधा 'to place all round i. e. to put on or wear,' परिधिः 'a wall &c. that surrounds,' परिणामः 'ripening, maturity,' परिगणना 'counting all round, i. e. a complete enumeration,' &c.

प्रति—'towards, back, in return, in opposition to,' &c.; प्रतिगम् 'to go towards,' प्रतिभाषण 'a speech in return, an answer,' प्रति—वि—कारः 'an act in opposition to i. e. a remedy' &c.
