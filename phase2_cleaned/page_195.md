(b) कङ् and कमण्डलुः if used as proper names take ई; कङ्गी: 'a woman of that name,' कमण्डल्वी:; but कमण्डलुः कुः in other cases.

§ 322. If a compound has for its last member ऊरु and for its first, words signifying a standard of comparison, or one of the words सहित, शक्, क्षमण, वाम, महित and सह, it forms its feminine in ऊः; रम्भोरुः रम्भे इव ऊरु यस्याः plantain-thighed; करभोरुः having thighs ( beautifully tapering ) like the fore-arm or the trunk of an elephant; दृढितोरुः having well-turned thighs; शक्तो खुरौ वातृविव संनियच्छत्यादूरु यस्याः सा शक्तोरुः; [ हितेन सह सहितो ऊरु यस्याः सा ] सहितोरुः; [ सहते इति सहो ऊरु यस्याः सा ] सहोरुः 'a woman whose thighs are capable of enduring great fatigue or pain'; or 'one having excellent thighs.'

§ 323. The fem. of उशकपि, Vishṇu or Shiva, आर्धि, कुसित or कुसिद 'a usurer' is formed in ई before which the final vowel of these is changed to ऐ; उशकऐपयी, भगायी, कुसितायी or कुसिदायी.

§ 324. The feminine of मनु is optionally formed by adding ई before which the final उ is changed to ओ or ऐ; मनावी. मनायी or मनुः ।

§ 325. A prātipodika ending in short क or द forms its feminine by the addition of ई; कर्क-कर्की, दण्डयु-दण्डयुनी, स्वनी, मञ्जनी, परिदेवयु-परिदेवनी; &c.

N. B. Words like स्वसु, ननान्दु, विधु, हरितु, स्वश्रु &c. being themselves feminine do not take this termination.
