§ 656 The affix य is added to लोहित and some other words
and to words ending in the affix आ (शट्) and the nominal
base thus formed is conjugated in the Parasm. and Ātmane.;
e g लोहित—लोहितायति-ते 'becomes red.' पटयायते 'utters the
sound Paṭpaṭ.'

    (f) By means of the affix इङ् (किङ् and गाङ्)
            and conjuga'ed in Parasm. and Ātm.

§ 657. Verbal bases are formed from the nouns given below
by the addition of the affixes इङ् (किङ्), and इङ् (गाङ्) with vari-
ous senses; those derived by means of किङ् are conjugated in
the Ātm. and those by गाङ् in the Par; e. g पुच्छ with उद्, देव,
and धट्-शपुच्छुङ्गते 'raises the tail,' उद्भुच्छुङ्गति 'he raises the load:'
धटे 'collects together the vessels;' सुरेश्वरते विष्णु. 'drinks सुरा
cooked in pots,' कनीषकति घर् 'causes to sprinkle,' &c
§ 658. इङ् is changed to आकिङ् when added to सत्य, अर्थ and
वर्ण. सत्यं करोति आत्मङ् वा सत्याकिङ्गति: अर्थाकिङ्गति, वर्णाकिङ्गति.

§ 659. इङ् is further added in the following cases;—काणेन अभि-
यान—आभीकणाकिङ्गति; लाताभ अदुभाट—अदुभाकिङ्गति; काणेन उपगायत
—उपकाणाकिङ्गति, भूतिके शूपस्तोति—उपभूतिकाकिङ्गति; तट्ट यूव्वाति—तट्यति,
पाणीज्-पाणीजीकिङ्गति; निवातवते-निवाते 'clears the air of vapours.';
चूर्णाकिङ्गति; एनीयाजेडे—एनयते (declares her variegated); &c., &c.

The various changes taking place here before the affix य the
student will easily notice as irregularities.
